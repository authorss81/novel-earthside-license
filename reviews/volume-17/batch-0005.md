## 1. The canon conflict, which needed an outline decision and got one

**The review's finding 1 is the most important thing in this file and it was not a prose fault.** `outline/volume-17.md`
contradicted itself in six places about whether Adrian Vale stands on that stair on day 846.

| where | what it said |
|---|---|
| §3 | he is in five chapters and **not in the room on any of the last seven mornings of the volume** |
| §6.9 | after the tenth hour of 843 **he is not on it on any of the seven mornings that follow** |
| §9 item 9 | **he is not on that stair again on any of the seven mornings that follow** |

access; §6.9 renders it in this manuscript's own form as a man who stops coming up a stair; and the three
statements that carry the act all say the stair is finished to him at the end of 843. **The count lost.**

**The batch that wrote 0846 took the day map's cell, wrote the chapter from it, and the chapter is now committed.**
That is what changed this pass's answer. `reviews/volume-17/batch-0004.md` §6 met this same row, found that
adding a sixth appearance or moving one of the five would change the plan of record, and declined to do either and
wrote the concern into the open threads for the volume owner. That was the right call on the finding that pass had,
which was that the protagonist is in one chapter of a ten — a design concern about a number. **This finding is not
that: one of the two sides silently cancels an act that another file reserves, and deferring it has now cost a

**So the decision is four, and it was taken in the plan in six cells and printed in the plan's own words:**

- §4.1 is **THE FOUR**, publishes four rows, drops the 0846 row, counts three failures and a fourth that is not a

**0844, two faults in one chapter and both of them load-bearing.** The register was shut on the shelf behind the

**0849, the weather.** The chapter said Wednesday came in after two days of rain. Rain falls on 846 and 847; 848
