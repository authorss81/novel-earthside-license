## The findings, and what was done to each

### 1. `chapter-0218.md:7` — a count that does not check, resting on a negative no chapter states. **Repaired, and the negative turned out to be positive.**

The line read *"it is the second Tuesday the queue has run since the forty-ninth day of the Long Month."* The review called it an unstated negative, since nothing says the queue did **not** run on the forty-ninth. Checking it against the page settled it the other way: **`chapter-0204.md:3` opens *"About a hundred and forty people were in the yard of the store on the upper road at about the sixth hour of the morning on the forty-ninth day of the Long Month, and the queue had formed along the wall in the ordinary course."** The queue did run, and the forty-ninth day is a Tuesday by the founding rule, so the queue has now run on three Tuesdays counting from it — the forty-ninth, the fifty-sixth and the sixty-third — and *"second"* was wrong.

**The line now names its own count and its own negative:** *"It is the third Tuesday the queue has run on counting from the forty-ninth day of the Long Month, which is the one it formed on, and the fifty-sixth day of that month was the second, and this is the first one it has run on since there was salt in that chest instead of a wage."* Every figure survives, the day anchors survive, and the sentence is now checkable by a reader with nothing but the three chapters.

### 2. `chapter-0218.md:15` — *yesterday* was a Monday and the sentence is about two Tuesdays. **Repaired.**
