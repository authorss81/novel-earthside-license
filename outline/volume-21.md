## 1. Central pressure

**A thing said in that room is the only record this world has that survives a morning without a page, and that is exactly
why nothing in it can be checked.**

**Volume 20 spent an instrument and it worked.** From day 975 a question goes on a page before it is put to anybody, the
person it is about reads it, answers it, and **nothing is written down after that.** On day 997 the woman of about
sixty-nine with her tin asked her sixth question in this manuscript, the man of about fifty-seven read it twice and
answered, and what he said **went out of that room in his own mouth and down that stair and along that row and out past
the store's end of it**, and there is no leaf on either of those two books with a scratch of it on it and no board in
that market and no chalk anywhere in this city that carries one word of it. **That was the whole of what was left off the
page, and it was left off on purpose, and it was the right thing to do.**

**AND IT IS THE ONLY THING IN THIS WORLD THAT KEEPS MOVING AFTERWARDS.** It went down that stair on day 997 and by day
1000 it had not come back, and on the fifth morning of this volume it does: a thing that somebody in that room said on an
earlier day **comes back into that room in words that the person who said it does not use.** Not in a mouth that is
anybody's. **In words.** The people carrying
it have not lied to anybody. They were told something, they were right about having been told it, they have put it into
other words because that is what people do with a thing they have been told, and **by the time it comes back the room
cannot tell whether it is hearing its own speech or somebody else's, and there is no instrument in four hundred miles
that could tell it.**

**THE PRESSURE IS NOT THE MISQUOTING. The pressure is that the only two ways this world has of settling a disagreement
about what somebody said are both already spent.** The first is a page, and a page is what Volume 19 spent fifty days
refusing to do to anybody and what Volume 20 spent fifty days learning to do on purpose. The second is to ask the person,
and asking is the act that puts a person on a page before they have opened their mouth and never on one afterwards.
**A room can have a record it can check or speech it can keep. Volume 20 chose. This volume lives inside the choice.**

**AND THE THING THAT MAKES IT NOT A CHAPTER ABOUT MISQUOTING: the people who are misquoted are not the interesting part.**
The interesting part is that **the room's own people know it is happening, on the mornings it happens, and go on with
what they were doing.**
