## Volume number and shape

Month*, the Nth being day less 315. Day 801 is a Thursday and the four hundred and eighty-sixth day of that
month. Day 850 is a Thursday and the five hundred and thirty-fifth. **No double day, no gap, no day twice, and
no chapter may write another chapter's day.** A room over a market gave up its number on day 775, was wrong
