the Nth being day less 315. **Day 951 is a Sunday and the six hundred and thirty-sixth day of that month. Day 1000 is a
Sunday and the six hundred and eighty-fifth.** No double day, no gap, no day twice, and no chapter may write another

**AND THE TWO ENDS OF THIS RANGE ARE THE SAME WEEKDAY FORTY-NINE DAYS APART, FOR THE THIRD VOLUME RUNNING, AND IT IS

## 1. Central pressure

**A question is the oldest instrument this manuscript has and the only one nobody has ever written down.**

Volume 16 spent a number said aloud in a room. Volume 17 spent a bar in two sockets on a top step. Volume 18 spent a
person, and found that the cheapest instrument this world has for a person is somebody standing up. Volume 19 spent a
line, and found that a line in a book is the only instrument that survives the morning it was made in and that a room
which has learned to write a person down has not thereby learned to ask that person anything. **Volume 20 spends the
asking.**

**And the asking is not free, and this volume is about the bill.** To ask a person something out loud, in a room, in
daylight, with a date in chalk on the outside of the door behind the asker, is a thing this manuscript has done on
perhaps a hundred pages across nine hundred and fifty chapters. **Nobody has ever written the question down.** The
question went out of the room with the person who asked it and it was answered or it was not answered, and either way
**there is now no way on any surface in this city to say what was asked, or of whom, or by whom, or on what day.**
The consequence arrives on the second morning of this volume and it is the whole of it: **the person who asked and the
person who was asked remember two different questions, neither of them can prove anything, and neither of them will
ever find out.**

**KEPT IN THE VOLUME-LIST LINE'S OWN TERMS, as every reconciled line since Volume 10 has kept it: a thing made by
somebody else is going to be used about the people it is about, and the people it is about are not in the room. Here
about a question, and not about a book, and not about a line, and the people it is about are the ones who are not in
that room, and there are about four hundred of them at the foot of that stair.**

**AND THE PRESSURE IS NOT THE ASKING. THE ASKING IS THE FIFTH MORNING OF IT, the way the book was the fifth morning of
Volume 19.** The pressure is that a room which has gone fifty days without asking anybody anything is now going to ask,
and **that the cheapest way for a room to be sure of what it asked is to write it down, and that writing it down is the
act Volume 19 spent fifty days refusing to do to anybody, and that the two acts are the same act wearing different
clothes.** A question that is written down cannot be misremembered, and cannot be asked twice by accident, and cannot be
used against the person it was asked of — **and it puts that person on a page before they have opened their mouth.**
