# Volume 06, Batch 0001 — Chapters 0251–0260, days 251 to 260

**Ten chapters, and here is why ten and not twenty.** A first batch of this volume has to do four things that cannot be split and cannot be deferred: it has to put a Crown instrument on a page as a **correct** document and not a threat, it has to get **Regent Solenne Dary's name into a room, in daylight, with a date on it, by a person who has been in one**, it has to get **Hettis Roke onto a page as a man who refuses things on a body's behalf**, and it has to **put the fifth date form on a page**, because the month runs out on day 255 and days 256 to 300 have no form to write a date in at all. **Ten chapters, one chapter to one day, days 251 to 260, ending on a Tuesday**, and the hinge falls on the sixth of them, which is why this batch is these ten chapters and not any other ten.

**The mapping is one chapter to one day for all ten. There are no double days in this batch and there are no double days in this volume, and `outline/volume-06.md` §14.2 says in writing why a war volume does not get them here.** The volume's four movements are 12, 13, 13 and 12 and this batch is the first ten days of the first movement.
