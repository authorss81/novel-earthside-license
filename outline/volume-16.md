## 1. Central pressure

**A room over a market has about nine people in it every morning of this flood, and the number has never been
written down anywhere, has never been carried out of that room, and has been said out loud in that room more
often than anything else in it. It is the only thing that room has ever said about itself. It says how many and
it never says who, which is why nobody in four hundred miles has ever been able to use it against anybody, and
it is going to be taken out of that room on day 775 by a man with a board under his arm who has been right about
a page in about four seconds in volumes running and who is thanked by nobody.**

The volume's subject is **a count that is safe because it names nobody**, and what is lost when a room gives it
up: not the ability to say how many, but the ability to say *since when*. A room that knows how many of its own
people there were this morning can notice an absence the moment it happens and can date it. A room that cannot
say how many can still notice that somebody is not there, and can never say since which morning, and never will.

**KEPT IN THE VOLUME-LIST LINE'S OWN TERMS, as every reconciled line since Volume 10 has kept it: a thing made
by somebody else is going to be used about the people it is about and the people it is about are not in the
room — here about a number and not about a figure, and about the forty days of mornings that the number was
saying and the room is now going to stop saying.**
