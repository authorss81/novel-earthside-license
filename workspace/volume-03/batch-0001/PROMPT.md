## Where the story is

**Chapter 0101 opens on day 101, the Thursday, the eleventh day of the month after this one.** Day 100 was the hundredth day of the flood and the close of Volume 02. **The date the batch is built on is the nineteenth day of the month after this one, and about nine people are going up a nine-mile road on it, in a month that is not March, because anybody may pick the work up.** The Registry hearing is about eight weeks off and there is one hearing and one clock.

Eight things are on the page:

1. **The undertaking of the Lantern Concord is void** on the ground that its own condition was not met, and the condition was in its own first sentence, and it took about nineteen minutes, and nobody was convicted, and the paper is still on a nail, unmarked, with the string untouched, and the only place the voidness exists is a notary's record. **It is also in a wooden case in Aurel, unsigned, and the office has not been told, and the hearing is about eight weeks off.**
2. **A page ended it, and the page is spent, and nobody knows why.** It can be used once and only once, only on a thing that claims to be the work of a person, and no other ground. Nine witnesses, one of them named by herself. No second copy. The inside was folded twice. **The man who carried it into the room did not read it out and said why in fifteen words, because the inside of it is not his.** **No chapter in this batch may explain it, may show it working, and may not have anybody produce a mechanism.**
3. **The shape has two names and they are in ordinary use.** *Single Witness* is the patch. *First Grammar* is what carries it. Ivenn Marrow said both in a converted salt store on the first day of the month after this one, in daylight, with a date on it, in his own hand, **four days early instead of in the four months he had promised, in a room that was not his, without asking anybody to open it, and asked for all three of those things to be entered.** He is not convicted of anything, is not going to be, and is not made useless in any scene. **He may not explain either name, may not answer for either, and no character may guess either aloud who has not been given it.** His room is in about fourteen weeks and is not due in this batch. He has still not mentioned the letter to Hattie Park and the count is sixty days at day 100.
4. **The eighth column of a quarterly description is a question on a river.** It went down in a wooden case on the eighth day of the month after this one. It asks which gate, in which year, on whose authority the count of eight was continued after that gate came off the roll, and what the eight names in it are. **A clerk of that office may not ask a question and may not refuse one, and a person named in a question is not told the question exists, and no person is named in it.** One of the eight is cut on a stone nine miles up the upper road in a Hearthguard's grandfather's hand about a hundred and forty years old. **Seven of the eight have never been looked at. Nobody living can say where the rest went.** A boy of about nine carried a cost column eleven miles in the wrong weather in Volume 02 and can be sent with anything.
5. **The licence is in force and weaker in the only part of it that was doing any work.** The four words *nobody has asked them* were a refusal for twenty-four days and a refusal is not entered anywhere; they are now on a page with a heading and a date, and **there is a column for where the seventeen are with a space under the date for an officer's hand nine hundred miles away.** Nobody undid it and nobody could. The door is shut and has been opened twice in a hundred days and the hundredth day was not one of them. **The seat of holder of the pull is vacant and the instrument says nobody and has said nobody five times.**
6. **Two Wayhouses and one Open Hand exchange stand under local law and none of them is Adrian's.** A bed and a bowl, open to a list of nobody, closable by its own household on a date on the page and not asked why twice, at a price of a quarter of a pound of salt and an hour of washing. A cooperative with no share, no interest, and a right of refusal that cannot be bought out. **A ledger nobody asked for, with rates in it and a refusal on it and no names, and nine people trading on it in the morning, and it is not a roll and is not going to Aurel.**
7. **Three debts are owed on a page in a stone and none of them is asked for**, and the third of them is now entered as unasked **on purpose, with a date on it, so that in four months nobody can say it was forgotten.** A woman of forty-eight in a fourth house at Nine Elms has been clearing a drain by herself every morning since the twenty-ninth day of the new month and **does not know he exists. No chapter in this batch may ask her, may go to her door, may name her in a room where it could reach her, and may not have anybody go and stand outside the fourth house at Nine Elms.**
8. **The nineteen shapes are in four slates and eight of the nineteen are different and seven of the eight have never been looked at, and the nineteenth day of the month after this one is on a slate and it is the only thing on it, and about nine people are going up a nine-mile road on that date.** A woman of about sixty-one who cannot read read them aloud to a docker who has a slate on the hundredth day of the flood, because anybody may pick the work up. The meaning is unwritten and nobody has written anything on the wall.

## Hard constraints — carry these from Volume 02 unchanged
