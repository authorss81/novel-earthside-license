## HOW YOU WRITE THEM

**The twenty-two items of `outline/volume-20.md` §17 are binding and you do not need them restated here.** Four of them
will bite this batch harder than any other batch has been bitten and they are named so you know to look for them:

1. **§17.18, the one about the protagonist.** On 0953 and 0959 he is in the chapter and in neither of them may he be
   useful to anybody, and on 0953 his hands are on a thing he is not touching and on 0959 they are on a worn place in a
   step. **A chapter may not carry its stillness in a chain of *nobody* clauses standing in for a scene.** The count of
   *nobody* across the fifty files of the volume behind this one is 166 in 44 bodies and it is the single most available
   word in this manuscript; the ceiling is three sentences beginning *nobody* in a row and it is a ceiling.
2. **§17.5, the rule about handles.** §4.1 gives both of his descriptors in full on first appearance in a chapter and
   **no attribution in either chapter may restate a person's whole handle every time that person speaks.** The volume
   behind this one cut its three long descriptors from twenty-two occurrences to seventeen for exactly this reason.
3. **§17.17, the closing paragraph.** Each closing states something not stated anywhere else in that chapter, in
   different words from the chapter's own body, and no two chapters of your ten may close on the same construction.
   **Ten shapes are off limits and two of them are new to this volume: not a question and not the answer to one, and not
   the grit and not a line in it.** The third new one is not the tin.
4. **§17.21 and §17.22, the two about your own record.** Your record may not publish a compliance figure it has not
   measured with an instrument in the same run, may not publish the word *zero* over a count it reached by reading rather
   than measuring, and may not assert an absence it has not measured. **A count that must include your own prompt, your
   own record and your own summary is the only count of the held things worth publishing, because a scope that omits
   them is not a measurement of where the held things are — a review found that fault in this repository already.**

**And the mean word count is a consequence and not a plan.** `outline/volume-20.md` §17.4 says so and no card in this
file sets a target for it.
