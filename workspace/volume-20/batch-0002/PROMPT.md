## THE FOUR FACTS OF THIS REPOSITORY THAT THE FIRST BATCH GOT WRONG AND THAT YOU MUST NOT REPEAT

1. **THAT STAIR HAS ELEVEN STEPS IN IT AND NOT NINE.** The ninth step down from the top is neither the bottom step nor
   the top step, and there are two steps under it before the flags. The top step, where the bar lies out of its two
   sockets, and the trough on the flags at the top of that step, are both a long way above the ninth. **The first batch
   called it the nine steps in eighteen places, which made its own ninth step and its own bottom step the same step and
   made two of its chapters unreadable. Do not count that stair. Do not put a number on it.**
2. **§17.5 BIT THE FIRST BATCH TWICE.** A card may not make an attribution restate a person's whole handle every time
   that person speaks. In batch 0001 the keeper said two things on 957 and the man of about thirty-eight said two on
   0960, and both attributions carried the full descriptor both times. **On any day of yours where one person speaks
   twice, the second attribution is the second handle and not the descriptor: the keeper, the tradesman, the stallholder,
   the carrier, the man of about thirty-eight.** A descriptor belongs beside the age and the trade on first appearance and
   never instead of them.
3. **A SCENE'S STATE IS PAST TENSE AND A GENERAL TRUTH IS NOT.** The narration of this volume is past tense, and the
   gnomic present belongs to how a thing is made, how water behaves, what a boot does on a wet step, and what a hole
   does after a nail has been in it a long time. **"The outside of that door is made of two planks" is a general truth
   and reads; "the two sockets on the far side of the door are empty" is a fact about this morning and does not.** The
   first batch had eight of the second kind and a repair pass converted them.
4. **A CHAPTER MAY NOT BE HELD TOGETHER BY *NOBODY* CLAUSES.** §17.18 and the volume behind this one both bind it. The
   ceiling is three sentences beginning *nobody* in a row. **Your 0965 and your 0969 are the two days in this batch where
   the pressure is that nobody is thanked and nobody can check, and those are exactly the two days where that clause
   will try to carry the chapter on its own. Give both of them a person doing a piece of work.**
