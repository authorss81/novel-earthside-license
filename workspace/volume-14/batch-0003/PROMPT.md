## 3a. The four fields, and what they are for

**This is the section that did not exist when the batch behind you was written, and its absence is why that batch is ten sketches. A chapter is a want somebody holds, met by something that resists it, and turned by a thing that happens in the room rather than in anybody's head. Without the four fields you get a sequence of observations with a person in it, and a person in it is not a scene.**

| Field | What it holds | What it may not hold |
|---|---|---|
| `want` | One sentence, the thing a named person in that chapter wants by the tenth hour, and it has to be a thing a person could want. It may be small. *"The board to sit level before the sun comes round."* | It may not be a decision of record, it may not be a document, and it may not be the volume's question. |
| `resistance` | One sentence, the thing in the chapter that will not give it. Usually a person, sometimes a surface or a piece of weather. **It is the reason the chapter is not over in its first paragraph.** | It may not be a villain and it may not be an obstacle that exists only to be overcome. Nobody in this volume is defeated. |
| `turn` | One sentence, the moment the want and the resistance meet and the situation is not what either of them expected. **It happens in the room, in daylight, and at least one other person sees it happen.** | It may not be a revelation, an answer to a forbidden question, or a change in anybody's understanding. Nothing on this volume is understood by the end of it. |
| `close` | One sentence, the thing somebody **does** in the last line. It is an action in a body, not a summary of a morning. | It may not be a thesis, it may not restate what the chapter found, and it may not be one of the five off-limits positions in §7. |

**A card may not supply a want, and it may not move a day, and it may not add a cast member, and it may not widen a prohibition — and none of those four is changed by this section. The fields say what the chapter is about, and the four prohibitions above still bind them.**

4. **Write the sentence you would write if only one chapter of yours existed.** Then check whether any other chapter is carrying it. This is the whole of the fix and it takes a reading, not an instrument.
