## What this phase is for

**Volume 05 has fifty chapters of prose and no close record. `state/volume-01-close.md` through `state/volume-04-close.md` are the model, and `state/volume-04-close.md` is the closer one because Volume 06 inherits from it. A batch summary is about a batch; a close record is about a volume and is the one page a writer reads before planning anything.**

**And this volume needs its close record more than the last four did, because of the one thing that is true about it and is not true about any other volume in this repository:**

**FIFTY CHAPTERS, FIFTY DAYS, AND NONE OF THE FIVE BEATS IN `outline/series.md`'s ONE-PARAGRAPH LINE FOR VOLUME 05 HAS STARTED.** That line promises Nora and the Crossworld Families Union demanding return, compensation and evidence; Adrian's sister finding that missing players come back with altered memories and cannot prove they are the same people; Adrian spending a scarce passage privilege to evacuate stranded players and taking public blame for the forced departure; a limited two-way return protocol and a medical quarantine; and Celia Rusk as a credible late antagonist. **None of those five is in any of the fifty chapters, no character from that paragraph is on a page in this volume, and `outline/volume-03.md` is missing with fifty chapters on disk as well.** Batches 0002, 0003, 0004 and 0005 each saw this, each recorded it as a failure rather than a compliance, and none could act on it, because acting on it means writing an outline.

**You cannot write that outline. A close phase is not an outline phase. What you can do — and what is the whole point of this phase — is put on one page, in the first place anybody will look, what Volume 05 actually is, so that the next volume's outline phase starts from a record and not from fifty chapters of days.**
