## Batch pacing

Chapters 41–43 are aftermath, a date, and an ordinary tide. Chapter 44 is the batch's turn: the Single Witness logic is named, in a room, by a man who is not yet able to name it. Chapter 45 is the discovery — a distributed working is possible and its price is a crew. Chapter 46 is the batch's midpoint, in which the crew is asked and the condition that makes the whole thing plural comes out of Renna Ord's own rule about a shift. **Chapter 47 is the batch climax and the commitment** — the longest chapter, the sentence at last, the instrument entered, and nothing performed.
