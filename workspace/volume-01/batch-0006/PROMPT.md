- **Resistance:** **three things have to be true at once and each of them is somebody else's victory.** The **Narrow Mark** is re-inspected by the four who signed the Chapter 10 inspection — Tamsin Quill, Nell Bray, Ovin Sarr, Hettis Roke, with Ottoline Vask's hands on the stone — and **narrowed to a two-witness route, and the reduction from four to two per opening is the volume's argument that a working can be made cheaper without being made private**, and the two are the count required *per opening* while the four remain the quorum that authorised the route. **Owen's burial term is settled in the record** and it is four pages and it is not shortened and **nobody may write *he died because***, and a person tries and is stopped by a woman of twenty-four, and the fourth column of the loft wall comes off on the eleventh day of eleven and **who fills it is an event.** And the cohort and the second group are named, with **no land rights, no class privileges and no workway**, and about ninety people in the yard want a stipend out of it and the yard is told, in the language of the room, that there is not one and why.
- **Turn:** **the licence has to say who answers for the next test, and the room reaches for the only man in it everybody can be angry at.** Adrian is offered the seat again — a small one, a real one, a standing one, with a name on it and a review date — and **this time it is not refused in a yard and it is not accepted in a yard, and the instrument says nobody, and he says the sentence himself, and what he says is that a licence with his name on the top of it is a document that only this city has ever been frightened of.** **Marrow is in the room and is the most useful man in it and says nothing wrong, and there is a moment in which he could have said one thing and does not, and the reader must not be able to tell whether that was choice or luck.** And **Sabra Vint comes down two hundred steps for the third time and says a number to a child**, and the number is nineteen, and the child does not know what it is.
- **Consequence:** **the licence is signed, sealed, and dated, with a quorum, a cost record, a right of review, a right of refusal, a right to be asked, and a page that says what happens on the day the crew does not turn up.** The first cohort and the second group are named in it, separately, with what they have and with what they do not have, and the list of what they do not have is the longer one and is entered as a term and not as a favour. **Adrian signs it having read it, and he signs it last, and his hand is not the notary's and the page is not a seat.**
- **Power:** nothing gained. The volume's only gain is the knowledge that he is the reason, and it is not a use, and the licence is not a use either.
- **Ending:** the final image, which is fixed in `outline/volume-01.md` and may not be paraphrased: **the repaired lock opens only for a single witnessed return mark; Tamsin writes the next inspection date in a public ledger while the ledger entry naming Adrian as the route's first witness rests on a piece of ordinary river stone; a child asks whether the next person will be a player; Adrian looks toward the dark water and has not yet found an answer that does not make the child smaller.** **The box in the last column of the tide table is not empty, and it is not going to be his, and the volume ends on a word somebody else wrote.**
