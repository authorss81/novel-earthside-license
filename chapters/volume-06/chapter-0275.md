# Chapter 0275 — Four Sentences

A Hearthguard of forty-four came into a room over a market in a market town nine hundred paces past the last named house on the twentieth day of the Short Month, a Wednesday, and there was a date in chalk on the door of that room and about nine people in it, and the man who had put the date there had put it there because a piece of paper with a question in somebody's own words on it had gone out to four places on the Saturday four days before, and nobody who had received it had been asked whether the day suited them. The light came in on the north side and went off the low ground by about the second hour of the afternoon, and the cold was out of the wind and in the shutters, and what was in that room was a slate, a piece of chalk, a spade, and a bootful of drain silt, and the man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall was not in it and had not sent anybody.

---

He stood up before he said anything, which he had not been asked to do, and about four people in that room have said since that a man of forty-four who stands up before he refuses is a man who has decided that the refusal is going to be a thing said standing up in a room in daylight with a date on the door and not a thing said in a lane.

"I have read it and I have had four days and I am going to say no, and I am going to say it on the Hearthguard's behalf, and the Hearthguard's share of the first way is the whole of the Hearthguard's share and I sign for it."

"You have not asked whether the day suits anybody."

"I have not been asked and I did not ask, and about four people in this city have worked out since the fourth day of that month that those two things are not the same thing, and I have not come here to say the second one is wrong." He had his hands at his sides and he had not put them on the table. "**That instrument has been used twice in this city and on two different days, and a question that has gone to everybody in writing four days early cannot be changed afterwards, and that is the whole of what it is for.**"

The woman of about twenty-four had a slate flat on the table in front of her and the chalk in her hand and she wrote nothing on it, and about four people in that room have said since that a woman holding a chalk over a slate for four sentences and writing nothing at all is a woman who has understood that what is being said is not a thing to be entered, and about nine people in that room could not have said what else she should have done.

"**The first time this city used it, it stopped the man who wrote it, in front of two hundred and forty people, and the man who wrote it is standing in this room, and I have read the four conditions and I am not going to be on the other side of that table again.**"

Nobody in that room said anything. The man of about thirty-six had his boot in the silt at the north end of that market and he took it out and stood on the boards, and about four people in that room have said since that a man who takes his boot out of a hole in the floor of a room in which a Hearthguard is refusing a route has stopped working and is not going to start again that morning.

"**The second time it worked and what it worked at was me, and it produced a no out of a man who had not been asked anything, and about four people in four hundred miles have said since that that is the only time anything anybody has written has ever worked on anybody in this flood.**"

He stopped there. The woman of about twenty-four put the chalk down on the table and turned the slate round so that the writing side was away from him, which she does, and about four people in that room have said since that she turned an empty slate, and that nobody in that room has worked out why a woman with nothing written on it needs to turn it.

"That was three sentences."

"That was three and I have one more, and the fourth is the refusal and it binds." He did not sit down. "**I refuse the first way on the Hearthguard's behalf, and the refusal binds the company, and it is dated to that instrument and not to today, and I would like the date on the door outside to stay the date on the door outside.**"

"And the reason for the route."

"**A rack that has not been worked does not come off, and a rack stands in a channel, and a channel takes its water from a gate, and eleven weeks of a channel without a household's water is eleven weeks of a salt season that is not recovered in this flood or the next one.**" He said it at the same speed as the other three and he did not stop at the end of it. "**That is about a hundred and forty years old and it is the only sentence about a gate that anybody in this room can check, and I have not improved on it and I am not going to.**"

---

A woman of about thirty-seven who had come up that road with him and had not said one word in two hours got up off the bench at the far end of that room, and about four people in that room have said since that a woman who has been in a room for two hours and has not said a word gets up at the moment the refusal is finished and not a moment before it, and that about nine people in that room could not have said when she had decided to speak.

"He said it for the company."

"He said it for the company and he said it for the company without asking the company, and that is what a company is, and about four people in this room have said since that an hour and a half ago this morning two people in it worked out that a body cannot be asked and neither of them has left the building."

"Did you know that when you came up that road."

"I knew a body could not be asked and I did not know it had been proved in a market on the Tuesday, and I would like it entered that I came up this road to keep a road and not to be proved right about anything, and that I have not been proved right and I have not been proved wrong, and I am going to go back down it at the fourth hour with the two of us and nothing on us." She did not look at the man of about thirty-four who was not in the room. "**I have been nineteen years in a body and there are four of us at the outer ward and I have watched a man get up and say no in a room four times in about nine days, and I am not going to be the one who tells him he should have asked us first, because he has been asked four times and said no four times and I have been one of the four.**"

The bench she had got up off was not hers and she was not on it and she had not been given it, and about four people in that room have said since that a woman who gets up off a bench she has not been given in a room where a refusal has just happened is a woman who has decided that the room is a room and not a house.

He sat down at about the eighth hour of the morning. The room did not follow him and about four people in that room have said since that a man who has refused a route for a body and then sat down has left nine people in a room with a season, and that about nine people in that room had come for a road and had got one.

The man of about thirty-six went out of the door at about the ninth hour and did not come back until about the second hour of the afternoon, and when he came back he had a bar in his two hands and mud up to the axle outside the north end of that market and he stood the bar on the near side of the road and came in and sat down on the bench at the far end of the room without saying anything, and about four people in that market town have said since that a man who carries a bar into a room where a route has just been refused and puts it down and says nothing is a man who has decided that the bar is the only part of the morning he is still responsible for.

"I am not going to say you are wrong."

"**Do not.**" Adrian Vale had not stood up and had not sat down either. "You have not got a piece of paper in your coat and I have got one, and the one with four conditions on it is mine and the one with the question on it is not, and I have had four days with it and I have not been able to hand it to anybody in four places out of the four, and I would like that entered in this room and I would like it entered now while the man who refused me is sitting in it."

"That is not a thing anybody enters."

"It is a thing a person says out loud in a room in daylight with a date on the door and about nine people in it, and about four of them heard me say it and about nine of them are going to be asked about it by somebody who was not in this room, and that is the whole of what a room is." He had his hands on the table. "A question in a person's own words has to be written by a person and it has to go to everybody who will be in the room, and three of the four places I put it in are a body and a household and a stall, and I could not do it in any of the three, and I did it anyway. It went to a man who keeps a road, and he has just told me no in a room in front of a date. **That is correct and I am not going to stand here and tell anybody in this room that it is not.**"

The woman of about thirty at the stall had a needle and a net in her hands and had not looked up once through the whole of the four sentences, and about four people in that room have said since that a woman who sewed through four sentences of a refusal and then looked up is a woman who has been waiting to find out whether the fifth sentence was going to be about the road, and that it was.

"The fifth sentence was about the road."

"**The fifth sentence was about the road and about a hundred and forty years and about a season, and there are about nine people in this room who have heard a season given a number of weeks for the first time in this flood, and I am one of the nine and I do not know how many of us there are.**" She drew the needle through. "You did not have to say the word. You have not said the word once since the fourteenth day of that month and you have said it on a gatepost and about four of us heard you and the rest of us got it off a cart of kindling, and about four people in this market have said that the word is in three villages and is not in this room and that is where it should be."

---

At about the fourth hour of the afternoon he was in a yard behind that market with nothing in his hands, and he was alone, and about four people who have been in that yard have said since that a man standing in a yard at the fourth hour of the afternoon with a piece of paper in his coat is either working out something or waiting to be told, and that nobody in that market came out and told him anything, and that he was out there about half an hour and that he worked it out.

He had built the instrument out of one thing, and the one thing was that a household is the only thing in this flood that can hand its own days back and that nobody can do it for a household. He had taken the four conditions and put them in a piece of paper and given it four days' notice, and the four days were not there so that anybody would be asked. The four days were there so that the days would be back before anybody had been asked. He had built that out of the one true fact he had, and he used the fact to take the thing without a household ever agreeing to it, and that is the same act, done faster, by a man who has never done it faster before.

Nobody in that yard told him that. Nobody in that room on that Wednesday told him that, and the Hearthguard of forty-four who refused him had refused him on an instrument and not on that, and the man of about thirty-six who had gone out and put a bar down had said one sentence and it had not been that sentence, and the woman with the needle had said that the word was in three villages and not in that room, and the woman who had come up the road with him had said that a body could not be asked and had not said who had found it out.

He stood in that yard with the paper in his coat and did not take it out. About four people who have been in that yard have said since that a man who has understood the shape of his own instrument does not take it out of his coat, because taking it out would be doing something about it and there was nothing to be done with it that week. He had four conditions in his own hand and a question in somebody else's and four places it had gone to, and one of the four had answered, and about four people in that market town have said since that the correct answer from one of four is not a result and is not a datum and is not about the number four, and that a man standing in a yard at the fourth hour of the afternoon on a Wednesday is a man who has lost a season and has not yet worked out that what he lost was a season and not a plan, because the plan had been correct and so had the refusal and about four people in that yard have said since that two correct things in one room on one morning is the ordinary way of a morning and about nine people in that town have been in a room on a morning like that and have not had one since the first week of this flood.

Nobody in that yard said whether he was right. Nobody asked him and nobody in that yard offered, and about four people who have been in it have said since that a man who works something out on his own in a yard is a man who will work out the next thing on his own as well, and that this is not a fault and is not a virtue and is the whole of what can be said about it in a yard behind a market in a market town.

Three villages lost eleven weeks of drying that Wednesday, and eleven weeks is a figure in words and not a number of days and about nine people in that market town have converted it into a number of days in their own heads and have not said so out loud to anybody, and nobody in four hundred miles has said how many people are in those three villages, and the man of about thirty-six has not been asked about any of it and has not said a word about it, and about four people in that market town have said since that a season is a season and is not a figure, and that the man who said no in that room was right, and that being right is not a discount on the cost.
