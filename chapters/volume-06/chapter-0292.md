# Chapter 0292 — Two Days And Not Four

A house on the south side of that road had a date in chalk on its door on the thirty-seventh day of the Short Month, a Saturday, at about the seventh hour of the morning, and a sheet of paper with a question on it in a hand that is a girl's had been on the sill of that house since the thirty-fifth, which is two days, and the light came in flat off the low ground and did not warm the stones of that front, and the cold was out of the wind and in the rope at the near end of the tarpaulin, and what was under six pairs of hands that morning was a question, two salt racks on trestles, a tarpaulin with a corner of it loose, and a woman of about thirty-five with a heap of broken boards at the front of the house and a shovel.

---

The two men under the tarpaulin had the near rack half over when the tap came at the door, and the tap was not a tap, it was the heel of a bar against a board frame because the frame is thinner than a door and a man with a bar in his hand does not remember which of the two he is holding, and about four people in that lane have said since that the only person in nine rooms this week who ever found a door with his hands was a man carrying a bar, and that he has been carrying it since the fourth hour.

"You are the seventh and you are a day early, because the eighth is tomorrow and there is one sheet a day, and I am not going to say the sentence about the numbers out loud in a lane."

"You are a day early and I have been a week early, and neither of those is a thing anybody should be told out loud." He put his hands behind him. "**The sheet went out on the thirty-third, which is four days back, and it came here on the thirty-fifth, which is two, and I know the day it came because my wife put it on the sill and told me the day it came.**"

---

The man of about forty was holding the far end of the near rack and could not let go of it, so the woman of about thirty-five answered from the step with the shovel still in her hand, and about four people in that lane have said since that a household that has to send one of its two to the door is a household that has decided something before the door opens, and that this one had clearly decided it.

"**Two days is not four days and we are not going to pretend it is, and the sheet came late, and nobody sent anybody, and nobody has said whose fault that is, and it is not anybody's.**"

"You have not asked me anything and I have answered you."

"You have not asked me anything and neither has anybody, and that is how the week has gone, and I am not complaining about it and you are not going to get a complaint out of me." She put the blade of the shovel down on the boards and stood on it. "**Come in or do not, and if you come in you will stand where you are standing, because a man who comes in and sits down in a household's kitchen is a man who has been asked something.**"

---

The near rack came over the trestles under the tarpaulin and the two men lay on the low ground and pushed it the last foot with their boots, and the tarpaulin took about nine breaths and moved about nine times, and the corner of it that had been loose since the second week lifted and let the cold in under one edge and lay down again on its own, and about four people in that lane have said since that a tarpaulin with a loose corner is a thing that has been decided about once and is now going to be decided about every day for the rest of the flood, and that nobody in four hundred miles is going to write that down either.

The woman of about thirty-five read the sheet on the step while the two men were under it and did not take it inside, and the man of about forty came in round the far side with his knees white to the thigh and stood in the middle of the floor and put his hands on nothing.

---

"He has not read it and I have, and I read it on the thirty-fifth at about the fourth hour, and that is the whole of the difference between this house and the four you have been in."

"You have had the sheet two days and you read it in four hours of the two days, and she has not read it at all, and I am not going to say a word about that." The heap at the front of the house had been in the way of the water since the second week and was going to be in the way of the water on the thirty-ninth. "**About four people in four hundred miles have worked out this month that a household is not a mouth, and that two people under one roof answer differently and always have, and the four days are for that and not against it.**"

"Then the four days are for two people and I have had two of them, and the answer I am going to give you is one I have not written down, and you are going to have to carry it out of here in your head, and you are not going to be able to put it anywhere, and I am telling you that so that you can decide whether you want to hear it."

"I have come four days to hear it and there is nowhere for me to put it and I have known that since the thirtieth."

---

The woman of about thirty-five said a day and the man of about forty said a day and the two days were not the same day, and neither of them was the thirty-seventh, and the thirty-seventh is the seventh of the nine, and about four people in that lane have said since that seven rooms in seven days and eight answers is not a shape anybody could have drawn in advance, and that a room of nine has never once had two answers come out of it before this week.

"You have both given me a day and neither of you has given me a name, and there are now six rooms in four hundred miles where a household has answered and has not signed, and I have not worked out whether that is one practice or four, and I am not going to guess at it in front of you."

"We have not done it for a reason either." The man of about forty wiped his hands down his thighs in the same place a man in the next house had wiped his the day before. "**A woman in a market put one of us a sheet with nothing at the top of it, and if we had put our names on it we would be a different kind of thing from every other house on this road.** This is the first week anybody has asked us anything at all, and we are not going to be the first ones with a name on it in four hundred miles."

"And I would like it entered that we were not asked and that we answered, and in that order, and that nobody has ever put those two the other way round."

---

The woman of about thirty-five put the sheet back on the sill where it had lain since the thirty-fifth and did not put it face down, and about four people in that lane have said since that a woman who leaves a sheet face up on a sill has decided the next person up that lane is welcome to read it, and that the last person up that lane was a woman of about forty-five with a case, and that nobody in four hundred miles has said one word to each other about it and both of them know it.

"About the thirty-fourth."

"You are telling me the road was out on the thirty-fourth."

"I am telling you the sheet was not on that sill on the thirty-fourth, and I am not telling you why, and there is no reason anybody in four hundred miles has, and about four people in that lane have said since that a notice is a thing that travels and a thing that travels is a thing that can be late, and that this is the first time the instrument has been beaten by a road and not by a person, and that nobody has worked out which of those is better." She went back out onto the step and picked the shovel up again. "**It came on the thirty-fifth. Two days. I have said what two days is and I have not said two days is better, and nobody is going to say it after me.**"

---

Her husband put the kettle on the far hearth and came back and stood in the middle of the floor again, and about four people in that lane have said since that a man who puts a kettle on and then stands in the middle of a floor is a man who has decided that the conversation is going to last, and that a man who is not being asked anything can stand in a room for as long as he likes.

"It is not a small thing and I would rather it were a broken one."

"**A broken one could be mended and this cannot be mended, and the difference between those two is the whole of what a road did to a piece of paper on the thirty-fourth, and nobody in four hundred miles has worked out which is the better instrument and I have stopped waiting for somebody to say.**"

The man of about forty pulled the near rack's foot in a half inch so that it would stand on its own and then took his hands off it, and the tarpaulin came down off his back where it had been creased, and about four people in that lane have said since that a man who makes a thing stand on its own and then takes his hands off it has done the only thing anybody in four hundred miles has done this week to a sheet, and that neither of the two men under the tarpaulin was asked about any of it and both of them stayed where they were.

The boards in that heap came off a door that came off its own hinges in this flood and there are nine doors' worth of them in a heap at the front of that house and nobody has ever counted them, and about four people in that lane have said since that a house which is asked a question by a stranger and answers it from the same heap is a house in which the question and the work are the same size, and that this is the first week anybody has noticed that the two of them are the same size.

The woman of about thirty-five put about nine shovelfuls out of the front of the house while she was talking and made a heap of them in the middle of the front, and the heap is the wrong shape for a heap and will be the wrong shape on the thirty-ninth, and about four people in that lane have said since that a woman who moves nine shovelfuls of boards out of a doorway while she is being asked a question by a stranger is a woman who has decided that the question is not the most important thing happening at that house, and that she is right about that and nobody has told her so.

Nobody in that house put the number of the nine on anything, and nobody in that lane said the word cost out loud, and about four people in that lane have worked the cost of the seventh kitchen out on their own and have not said it either.

The woman of about thirty-five put the shovel down against the door and stood on the heap she had made, and a woman who stands on a heap of her own making in front of two men is a woman who has decided where the conversation happens.

"**The four days is the instrument and the two days is a different instrument and not a broken one, and a household which has had four days can be shown to have decided, and a household which has had two can be shown to have answered.**"

"And I cannot tell them apart from out here."

"**Nobody can tell them apart from out here, and that is what two days costs, and I am not going to tell you it is a small thing, because I have had two days and I have not been able to sit down once since the thirty-fifth.**" She got off the heap and picked the shovel up. "The four that came before you this week have something behind them. I have got two days and a road I cannot account for, and if the woman at the market asks me in the spring why I answered in the way I answered, I am going to have nothing to say to her that is worth saying."

---

And the seventh of the nine kitchens was asked on the thirty-seventh day of that month, a Saturday, in daylight, with a date in chalk on a door and two men under a tarpaulin and a heap of boards outside the front, and that the sheet went out on the thirty-third and came on the thirty-fifth, and that the household answered inside the two days it had and not inside the four it had not, and that nobody was at fault and nobody sent anybody and nobody shut anything, and that a household with two days has given an answer that cannot afterwards be shown to be a decision, and that a column cannot hold that, and that a page cannot either, and that this is the second time this week that the two of them have come out even, and that nobody in four hundred miles has said the number of the nine out loud in any of the nine rooms and that every one of the nine has been able to keep it to itself, and that a thing nine people can keep to themselves is the only kind of thing anybody has built this week.
