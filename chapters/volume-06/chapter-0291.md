# Chapter 0291 — One Sheet, Two Households

A date went on a door in chalk on the thirty-sixth day of the Short Month, a Friday, at about the seventh hour of the morning, and a sheet of paper with a question on it in a hand that is a girl's had come to that door on the thirty-second, which is four days ago, and the light came in off the low ground behind the houses and did not get past the second board, and the cold was in the stone of the sill and out of the wind and in the tallow, and what was under six pairs of hands in that house that morning was a door that does not shut, a length of chain, a bar of tallow, a brass hinge, a pot, and a question with nothing at the top of it.

---

The door had not shut for four months and the hinge was going on at about the eighth hour with a chain round the post to hold it while the tallow cooled, and the woman doing it had her knee in the doorway and had a man holding the door against her back so that the shavings would come off it and not off the floor.

"You are the sixth."

"I am the sixth of nine and I did not number myself, and the number is in a room nine hundred paces from here with a date at the top of it and nothing under it, and I came up this lane because the sheet said this door." He stood on the step with his hands behind him, which is what he did in kitchens. "It went out on the thirty-second. That is four days."

"**It came on the thirty-second and it has been on that shelf since the thirty-second, and I have had four days with it, and there are two of us in this house and it did not say which of us it was for.**"

---

The man of about fifty put the door down against his back where the chain would hold it and wiped his hands down his thighs, and a man who has been holding a door for nine minutes has both arms full of a thing that is not the conversation and gets nine minutes of it for free.

"She has been in this house since the winter before the water and I have been in it longer, and we are two households and we have not become one household and nobody is going to make us one, and a sheet with nothing at the top of it cannot tell the difference and neither can a door." He looked at the pot. "**The four days is the instrument and the instrument goes to a room, and there were two households in that room, and there were four days and two households, and there is no way to divide four days between two households.** So neither of us has had four days and I have been waiting since the thirty-third for a room to arrive in that has only one household in it."

"That is the fifth sentence anybody has said in this house today and three of them are mine."

"Then we are agreed and we can both go to the pot, and about four people in that lane have said since that two households in one house is a thing this flood has made about nine times and has never once written down, and that a sheet goes to a place and a place with two households in it is a place, and that nobody asked whether a place was a household."

---

Nobody said the second household's name and nobody asked for it, and about four people on that row have said since that a house with two hearths in it is a house and a house with two households in it is a place, and that the whole of what a sheet can be this week turns on those two words, and that the two words have never been said in that order by anybody before this morning.

The woman of about twenty-seven came off the step with the shavings off her and put the hinge-knife down on the boards inside the door and squared the pot, and a woman who squares a pot in front of a stranger is a woman who has decided the stranger is in the room.

"**He read it on the thirty-second and I read it on the thirty-third and neither of us read it in front of the other, and that is the whole of what is wrong with this house, and it is not anybody's doing.** Four days of notice is four days for a person to find out who else will be in the room and we both found out on the wrong day." She put her hand flat on the top half of the sheet where it lay on the board. "**And I am not going to be asked whether the thirty-sixth suited me, and I suited it, and that is the whole of what your four days did and you did not do it.**"

---

The man of about fifty did not take the sheet and did not look at it and went to the far hearth and put the kettle on it, and about four people in that house know that a man who goes to the far hearth while a stranger is in the room has decided which of the two households he is in and has said so with his feet.

"You have not asked me a question."

"I have not asked you a question and I have not been going to, and I have been in four rooms this week where the two people asking were the two people nobody asked anything, and that is not a courtesy and it is not going to change." The hinge-knife was on the boards and he stepped over it. "**I am the one who carries it and I am not one of the people it is about, and a person nobody asks anything is a person who cannot be put in a column, and that is the only reason I am standing here and it is not a reason anybody can put on a page.**"

"So put nothing on the page, and that will be the first thing anybody has said to me this month that I can use."

---

He got up off the settle without hurrying and took the box off the shelf and did not open it in front of the stranger and read two lines out of it with his head down and put it back, and it was two lines and a day and a mark at the bottom that was not a name, and the day was not the thirty-sixth and was not the day the other one had given.

"**I have been in this house thirty-one winters and I have never in my life given a day to a stranger, and I have given one to a sheet, and the sheet has no name on it and that is the only reason I did it.**" He sat back down on the settle. "You can take it upstairs and read it and take it back down and it will say the same thing, and there is no way anybody is ever going to know that I said it."

She wrote for about as long as it takes to fill a pail and she wrote with the heel of her hand over the top half of the sheet so that whatever was under it stayed under it, and then she read it out, and it was two lines and a day and a mark at the bottom of them that was not a name, and the two lines said yes, and the day was not the thirty-sixth.

"That is the fourth one of the nine that has not given me one of the nine."

"That is the fourth one, and there are four rooms you have not been in and not one of them is going to give you one of the nine either, and the day is mine and I am telling you what it is so that you can put it on something, and you are not going to be able to put it on anything, and I have known that since the thirty-second." She put the sheet back on the shelf face down. "**A hand is not a page. I said it before the thirtieth in a kitchen in that market and I am saying it in my own house now and it does not get better by being said twice.**"

---

The man of about fifty brought the kettle and put it on the boards between the two hearths and did not sit down, and about four people in that house have said since that a man who puts a kettle in the middle of two hearths is a man who has worked out that the room is one room and that the kettle is the only thing in it that has to be shared, and that he did not say a word about it and the kettle said it.

"The other one has not answered."

"The other one has answered in the same two lines in the same hand and it is in the box on this shelf and it is a different hand and it is a different day." She did not look at the shelf. "**Two households, two answers, one sheet, four days, and nobody in four hundred miles has a column that will take two of anything.** You came here expecting a room and you have found a place, and the two of them are not the same size, and I have had four days to work that out and you have had the walk."

---

The chain round the post had gone slack by then and the door had begun to stand about a finger's width open on its own weight, and the hinge-knife was still on the boards where it had been put down, and the shavings where they had come off the door had been swept into the corner of the room with the back of a hand and were still there, and about four people in that house have said since that a thing that is carried somewhere and finished is the only kind of thing there is, and that a question which is put down on a board and picked up again can be used again, and that a sheet cannot be picked up again, and that this is the only difference anybody has found this week.

The man of about fifty put his hand on the shelf and did not take anything off it, and a man who puts his hand flat on a shelf where two answers are lying face down has counted them without picking either up, and about four people in four hundred miles have worked out this week that a document which cannot be produced and cannot be enforced is a document that can only be obeyed, and that nobody in this flood has ever obeyed one, and that the shelf in that house is the first place in four hundred miles where two of them are lying.

"You will not take them and you cannot show them and you are going to be asked to do it again."

"I am going to be asked to do it again and I am going to say the same thing in the same two lines, and about four people in four hundred miles have worked out this month that the instrument is a question and everything else about it is a way of carrying the answer, and that a way of carrying an answer that can be taken away is not a way of carrying an answer." He put his hand on the shelf a second time and took it off. "**And neither of us is going to be thanked, because the four that came before you this week did not get thanked either, and a sheet with nothing at the top of it has no way of saying thank you to, and I have noticed that nobody has tried.**"

---

The woman of about twenty-seven put her shoulder against the door and shut it and the hinge held, and she stood with her back to it for a moment longer than a person stands against a door that has just started to work.

"**It shut.**"

"It shut and it will shut tomorrow and the day after, and that is not what I came for and I am not going to thank you for it, and you are not going to thank me either, and we have both been in this room for about two hours and neither of us asked the other a single question."

Nobody in that house said the number out loud and nobody in that house wrote the number of the nine on anything, and about four people in that lane have said since that the sixth of the nine is a place and not a kitchen and that the instrument has never once asked itself what a place is, and that a sheet went to a place on the thirty-second and there were two households in it and the four days were for finding out who would be in the room, and that nobody could find out, and that nobody sent anybody to find out, and that nobody in four hundred miles has blamed anybody for it, and that a household which has not had four days has still got an answer, and that a household with an answer and no four days is the first thing this week that is going to cost somebody something and that nobody has worked out what.

And about four people on that row have said since that the sixth of the nine kitchens was asked on the thirty-sixth day of that month, a Friday, in daylight, with a date in chalk on a door and a door being hung at the same time, and that it was a place and not a household and it had two of them in it, and that neither household had been asked whether the thirty-sixth suited it and both suited it, and that the four days had gone to a place and could not be divided, and that nobody sent anybody and nobody was at fault, and that a number that was nine all week stopped being a number on the thirty-sixth and has not been made nine since.
