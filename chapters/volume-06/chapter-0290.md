# Chapter 0290 — A House And Not A Place

A room over a market in this city had a date on the door in chalk on the thirty-fifth day of the Short Month, a Thursday, and a carter of about thirty-five came into it at about the ninth hour of the morning with nothing in his hands, and a woman of about twenty-nine who kept a public register had her second slate on the table with the heading on it and nothing under it, and the light came in on the south side and went off the floor by about the middle of the afternoon, and the cold was out of the wind and in the shutters, and what was in that room was a second slate, a piece of chalk, an inch of tar on a thumb, and a man of about forty-one in a very good coat who put his hand inside the coat and found nothing in it and left it there.

---

"You went out on the thirty-third and you are in here on the thirty-fifth and there is no sheet anywhere in this building, and I want to know whether you were turned out."

"I was not turned out and I have not been refused, and about four people in this city have said since that a man who comes back from a door with nothing in his hand and was not turned out of it has been given something worse than a refusal, and that nobody has worked out what it is yet." He put his hand on the rail of his own cart, which was not in the room, and then took it off the rail he had not brought. "**I gave the question on Tuesday afternoon at about the fourth hour, and there was no sheet, and I was two days early for the day it was to be asked on, and about four people in that lane have said since that a question which turns up two days early is a question that has arrived like a person, which is to say at somebody's convenience and not on anybody's plan.**"

"You gave the question a day late because there was no sheet for you to give."

"There was no sheet for me to give because a sheet goes to a place and not to a house, and about four people in that lane have said since that the road has about nine places on it and about nine houses off it, and that the eight houses that were reached are eight of the nine places, and that the ninth place has one house at the end of a lane past two gates and has had one house on it for as long as anybody can remember." He did not sit down. "**And I have walked the four days late, and the four days are the instrument, and a man who asks a question with nothing of the four days in it has not got a different instrument. He has got the same instrument four days warm.**"

---

The woman of about twenty-nine turned the second slate round and did not write anything on it, and about four people in that room have said since that a woman who turns a slate round towards a man who has brought her nothing and then does not write on it has decided that there is a thing she could write and has decided not to write it, and that about four people in this city have worked out that the decision not to write is the whole of what she has done this week.

"He answered you."

"He answered me and he said a day, and the day is his and I am not going to say what it is because I was not asked what it is and I have not got a sheet to write it on." The carter of about thirty-five turned his left thumb over and looked at the tar on it. "**An answer with nothing of the four days behind it is an answer to a question nobody can prove was ever put, and this is the first time in about a hundred and forty days that anybody in four hundred miles has answered something they cannot prove was put to them.** About four people in this city have worked out that this is worse than a refusal and is not a refusal."

"That is the first of three things I am going to say, and the second of them is that nobody sent you."

"Nobody sent me. A man in a good coat said in a room in this city on a Tuesday that an office which gets a form back with a place empty does not send the form again, and about four people in that room worked out what that meant, and nobody in that room wrote it down, and a carter was the person who went." He put his hand on the edge of the table and did not touch the slate. "**A page is refused by being ignored. A person is not. I was sent because a person cannot be ignored, and I have come back with an answer that nobody can file, and about four people in this city have said since that the two of those are the same mistake and that the mistake is not the fault of the man who walked.**"

---

"I am going to say the third of three things and the third of them is the one I have been turning over since Tuesday morning." She put her hand flat on the second slate, on the heading, and did not move it. "**The four places on that page are a date, a gate, a holder and the days, and I have been saying for about a month that none of the four of them will take what is coming back, and I have been wrong about one of them.** A date will take it, because I have a date on a slate for every one of nine days, and the other three will not. A woman who says she has been wrong about three-quarters of a thing she has been saying for a month is a woman who has finally read her own page, and about four of them have said that this is the first time in about a hundred and forty days that anybody in this city has admitted a page was right about something."

"Which of the four is right."

"The date is, and that is all, and about four people in this room have said since that the one place a Crown's page can take from a household is the one place a household is least able to keep to, because a day is the only one of the four that everybody would remember differently, and about four of them have said that this is the whole reason the third place is empty and is going to stay empty, and that about four people in four hundred miles have said that a document that can be filled in from a date is a document that can be filled in from anywhere."

"You have walked four days for a sheet that was already on a table, and about four people in this market have said since that the cheapest thing anybody has done in four hundred miles in about a month is the walk, and that about four of them have said that it was not cheap and that it is going to be cheap for about four more days."

The carter of about thirty-five wiped his thumb on his own belt and the tar came off onto the leather, and about four people in that room have said since that a man who wipes a stranger's tar off his own hand onto his own belt is a man who has decided that what was on his hand is now his, and that about four of them have said that this is the only way anything has been acquired in that room this month, and that about four people in this city have worked out that a page cannot be acquired this way at all.

---

The man of about forty-one in a very good coat had a hand inside the coat that had nothing in it and had been in there for most of the morning, and about four people in that room have said since that a man who has been sitting in a room for four hours with his hand in a coat that is empty has been waiting for somebody to put something in it, and that about four people in this city have worked out that what he has been waiting for is a name.

"Nine kitchens on nine days and the first of the nine was on the thirty-first, and the fifth is today, and there is one of the nine that is a house at the end of a lane past two gates." He took his hand out. "**The ninth of the nine is the thirty-ninth, which is a Monday, and a sheet went out of this room this morning for that day, and the ninth of the nine is going to be asked by somebody who has not got a sheet either, and nobody has worked out who.** About four of them have said that this is a thing a room can be told about and cannot act on."

"You can enter the day."

"I can enter the day and I am going to, and I am not going to enter the answer, and I entered a day and an asking and an answer on the twenty-eighth of that month and I did not enter the answer then either, and about four people in this city have said since that a register which enters nine days and no answers is a register which can be shown to have been kept, and that about four of them have said that a register which can be shown to have been kept is the only instrument anybody in this city has that an office nine hundred miles off cannot ask to have produced." She put the chalk down and left it on the slate. "**And the cost, which I name first, because that is what I do, is that a register with nine days in it and nothing under them is a register in which nine households have answered and nobody in four hundred miles can prove it.** That is the first time a person has had that and the first time anybody has had to decide whether it is a good thing."

"It is a good thing, and it is the only good thing in this flood that cannot be taken back, and about four people in four hundred miles have said since that a person who is never asked anything can be entered in a column and a household that answers into a room with nobody in it cannot, and that about four of them have said that these ten days have worked that out by accident and that nobody in four hundred miles put it there."

---

Nobody in that room said the word schedule out loud and nobody in that room wrote the number of the nine kitchens on anything, and about four people in that market have said since that about four people in that room worked out the whole of the next four days before anybody said one word of it, and that about four of them have said that the room did the arithmetic and the road did not, and that about four people in four hundred miles have said that this is the first week in a hundred and forty days that the arithmetic and the road have come out the same.

The ninth sheet went out of that room at about the fourth hour of the afternoon for a room on the thirty-ninth day of that month, which is a Monday and which is four days off, and the carter of about thirty-five went out of the door with nothing in his hands again and about four people in that market have said since that a man who walks into a market with empty hands twice in three days is a man who has been shown what an empty hand is for, and that about four of them have said that the ninth of the nine is four days away and that nobody in that room has said what will happen on it.

And about four people in this city have said since that a household at the end of a lane past two gates was asked on the thirty-third day of that month, a Tuesday, in daylight, by a man with nothing in his hand, and that it was two days before the day it was to be asked on, and that there were no four days behind it, and that it answered, and that nobody sent anybody and nobody turned anybody away and no form was filled in anywhere in four hundred miles, and that about four people in that market have said since that this is the first failure of the asking in nine days and that it failed because it was a road and not because it was a form, and that about four people in this city have said since that a form has never yet turned anybody away from a door in four hundred miles, and that a road has got one household out of nine this week, and that about four of them have said that this is the first time in a hundred and forty days that the cheapest thing in this flood has been the one that worked, and that nobody is going to be able to say afterwards which of the two of those is the instrument, and that about four people in that market have said since that not knowing is the cheapest thing in the book, and that about four of them have said that a page which comes back with nothing in it has not been anywhere, and that a person who comes back with nothing has been in a room, and that those are the only two things anybody in four hundred miles can say about the difference and that neither of them is a name.
