# Chapter 0363 — None Of The Four Is A Person

A room over a market had the forty-eighth day of that month in chalk on its door at about the seventh hour, a Sunday, and about nine people in it by the middle of the day, and the light came in over a step at the back with no name on it and lay along a bench that nobody was sitting on, and the cold was out of the floor and in the grain of a board, and what was under about four pairs of hands that morning was a form, a slate, a lump of chalk, a pen with the nib ground narrow and cut down with a knife, a second slate on a shelf behind the table with a date on it and nothing under it, and four places laid out on the bench.

---

There were three of the four in that room and there were four places laid out on the bench, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who keeps a public register laid out four places for three people on a Sunday morning and did not say so and did not do it by accident, and that nobody in that room said one word about the fourth place, and that a party of four is four and that this is what four looks like when it is not being said out loud.

The woman of about twenty-one had walked nine places in three days and had written nothing on the ninth of them, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a person who has stopped waiting to be told what she is for is the only person in this city who is not owed anything by anybody, and that she had the pen in her hand and had had it in her hand since the seventh hour and had not put it down and had not used it.

The man of about twenty-five had nine words in the inside of his coat that he had written himself and had not said in two rooms, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has said yes in his own words in a room with a date on the door and then written himself nine words and put them in his coat has a page of his own that nobody in this city can ask to have produced, and that he has not opened the coat in front of about nine people and is not going to.

The man of about forty-eight came in and sat down, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has been in a city since before the water and has not been asked anything in a long time has learned to sit down the moment he comes into a room, and that standing about is what a person does when they are expecting to be asked.

Nobody asked anybody whether it would go. About four people in that room have worked out since that a room in this city has been running on the rule that it does not ask anybody anything, and that the rule is not a courtesy and is not a form, and that three people in this room said yes in their own words in three rooms on three different days in words that nobody in this city can produce a copy of, and that the rule has held through all of it.

---

The form was on the table with three of its four places in a condition and one of them in a decision, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a document which has been read out in a room in daylight with the day on the door has now been in three rooms and has not been filled in in any of them, and that a document that goes back is the ordinary end of a document and not a defeat, and that this one is not going back yet.

The man of about thirty-nine was against the wall by the step at the back where the light does not reach, with his own slate on his knee and his right hand flat over the face of it, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has been at the back of three rooms in a fortnight with a slate of his own and has not shown the face of it to anybody has decided that this room is the fourth one and that a thing a man does on the fourth occasion is the first thing anybody can call a habit.

She wrote the question out before she asked it, which she does, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who writes a question down before she asks it has decided that the answer is going to matter afterwards, and that the only person in the room who had noticed that she was going to do it was the man against the wall with his hand over his slate.

"Which of the four is a person."

"**None of the four. The second one counts them.**"

She said it without lifting the pen off the bench, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who has walked nine places in three days and written nothing on the ninth of them has just answered a question about a column correctly, in one breath, before anybody had finished asking it, and that nobody in that room marked the moment.

---

Nobody marked the moment. That is the whole of what happened at about the eighth hour on the forty-eighth day of that month, and about four people in that room have worked out since that nine people have just been told the truest thing anybody has said in this room since a word came off a slate in a cloth, and that the room went on, and that the man at the back did not move his hand, and that the man of about twenty-five did not open his coat, and that the woman of about twenty-one put the pen down on the bench and picked it up again about a quarter of an hour later.

"You have not written it down."

"**I have written the day and nothing under the day, and I did that in front of nine people in a room with the day on the door, and you were not in that room, and I am not going to start now because a woman in this room has just told me what a form is and she is not going to tell me twice.**"

Nobody improved on that. About four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who has kept a public register for nine years has just said out loud, in front of nine people, that she is not going to put a thing somebody else said on a page because it was correct, and that the price of that is that a clerk four hundred miles off is going to ask her for it in about nine years and she is going to have to say that she does not have it, and that she has said the price before the thing happened, which is the only thing anybody in this city has got that a form has not.

The man of about thirty-nine said nothing at all, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has been at the back of three rooms in a fortnight and has not been asked one question about any of it has heard the answer to the only question anybody in this city wanted answered on the fortieth day of that month, and has not been asked whether he agrees with it, and is not going to be, and that nobody in that room was going to ask him.

---

The man of about fifty-seven had the length of new rope nobody had used over the back of his chair, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has carried a new rope in this flood and never used it has had it over the back of a chair when he means to be in a room for a while, and that he has been in nine rooms in about a fortnight and has not been asked where the line runs, and that this morning he was not asked it either, and that the not-asking is now ten rooms and a fortnight and is not an oversight.

Somebody opened the window to say something to somebody at the back and it shut again on its own inside a minute, and about four people in that room have worked out since that the window on that side has not done that in about a fortnight because a man of about forty-eight fixed the catch with a knife in a room with a date on the door, and that about nine people in four hundred miles know it and that about nine of them are in this room.

"That window has shut twice since I was last in here."

"**It has shut every time, and I did it in about a quarter of an hour with a knife that does not spring, and I am not going to be thanked for it, and nobody has thanked me, and I would like it noticed that I fixed it and that the fixing of it is the only thing I have done in about a fortnight that anybody in this city could have done for themselves.**"

Nobody said anything about that either, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has been one of the four people who said yes in their own words has just told nine people that the single useful thing he did in a fortnight was a thing they could have done themselves, and that it is the same sentence the man of about thirty-four says about a line on a wall, and that the two of them have never been in a room together and are not going to be.

---

The room emptied at about the fourth hour and the form was on the table where it had been all morning and about four people who came down that stair have worked out since that a room in this city has just had the truest thing said in it in about a fortnight and did not do one single thing about it, and that the third of the four places under a heading is still empty on purpose and the fourth is still a household's, and that nobody was asked whether any of the four of them is going anywhere, and that the fourth of the four is not in this city and is not going to be asked about anything by anybody, and that whatever the fourth of the four said she said in a room with a date on the door and that is where she said it, and that nobody in this room is going to say it again or count anything.

The light went off the bench nobody was sitting on, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman of about twenty-one has answered a question about a column and has not been thanked and has not been asked a second question, and that a pen with the nib ground narrow and cut down with a knife went back into her apron with nothing on it, and that the page this city has been trying to make for about a month has just been described by the only person in it who was ever likely to be able to describe it, and that she was not asked to.
