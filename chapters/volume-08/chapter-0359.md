# Chapter 0359 — A Sheet On A Wall, And A Man Who Does Not Stop At A Gate

A sheet of paper with nothing at the top of it came out of the room over a market at about the sixth hour of the morning folded twice with a thumb and not with a bone, and a stone off a doorstep went on top of it, and a satchel with a flap went over the shoulder of the man who was carrying it, and what was under about four pairs of hands that day was that sheet, that stone, that satchel, and about nine people in four different places in this city.

---

The sheet goes out tonight and the room it is for is on the forty-eighth day of that month, and those are four days, and the four are the Thursday, the Friday, the Saturday and the Sunday, and about four people in four places in this city have worked out since that a room with a day on its door and a sheet that went out four days before it is the only instrument this flood has that has never once been late.

A place with about nine households in it is a place, and a sheet cannot be divided between nine households, and about four people who have come out of doors on that lane have worked out since that this is the whole of what a notice is in this city, and that everybody who has ever wanted a notice to be something more particular than that has wanted an instrument this flood does not have.

He had copied the four lines out in her hand the evening before and had folded it and had not opened it since, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who copies a thing out in somebody else's hand in order to carry it is doing the only part of the asking that does not require him to know what the asking is, and that he does not know, and that he has told two people on two days that he does not, and that a man who is the only instrument in four hundred miles nobody can ask to have produced is a man who does not open what he is asked to carry.

"**You have not read it.**"

"**I copied it out in your hand and did not read it before I copied it and have not read it since, and that is the ninth thing I have carried in this flood that I have not opened, and I am saying the number out loud so that you know I have counted.**"

"You do not have to count."

"**The day a man who carries things stops counting is the day he has stopped noticing, and I have noticed every one of the nine and none of them was mine.**"

---

Four lines had been ruled on a list on the table behind him and he had not looked at them, and about four people in that city have worked out since that four lines on a list in a room over a market on a Wednesday is four people written into a room four days from now, and that they are four people and not a company and not a body, and that there is no word in this city for what four people on a list are, and that a room which has run out of words about a thing has not run out of the thing.

Three of the four said yes in their own words. The first of them on the fifteenth day of that month, in a room, in words of her own, and the second of them on the sixteenth, and the third of them on the seventeenth, and about four people in that city have worked out since that a yes a person says in their own words in a room with a date on the door is the only kind of agreement in this flood that nobody can be asked to produce a copy of, and that the fourth of the four is not on the list in a room on this Wednesday and is not going to be asked about it by anybody, and that the fourth of the four said what she said in a room with a date on the door on the fourteenth of that month and that is where she said it.

He had four more walls to get to before the light went and about two hours of light, and about four people in this city have worked out since that a man with a list of walls and a stone off a doorstep has the only trade in four hundred miles that this flood has improved, and that nobody has ever asked him whether he wants it, and that he would not know what to do with the answer.

He carried the sheet to the end of the lane past two gates and put it against a wall under a stone off a doorstep and put the stone back on it, and he did not stop at the door, and about four people in that lane have worked out since that a man who puts a paper against a wall and walks away has done the whole of the asking in about nine seconds, and that the nine seconds is the instrument, and that nothing he did in them was a decision.

---

Four of the nine households at the end of that lane had had a sheet with four days behind it, and four of them had said yes in their own words on the thirty-ninth day of the Short Month, and about four people in that lane have worked out since that those four are a number of households and are not the four on a list in a room over a market, and that a page that puts those two fours in one sentence has confused a lane with four people, and that nobody in this city is going to be asked a second question inside a fortnight about a thing they have already answered in their own words.

The nine of them were not watching him and had not come out, and about four people on that lane have worked out since that a sheet under a stone against a wall on a Wednesday evening is a piece of paper that has been delivered and not received, and that the difference between those two is the whole of what four days of notice buys, and that four days buys a paper being on a wall and nothing else at all.

He put the satchel's flap down where the nine of them could see it do it.

"**I am not going to explain that to you, and the whole of what I am is a man who puts a thing against a wall and does not stand there being asked about it.**"

"You have not been asked."

"**Then there is nothing to explain and we are both standing on a lane for no reason, and I have four more of these to put on walls before the light goes and none of them is for a house.**"

---

He came back down the lane at about the eighth hour of the evening and the light was off the ground and there was a shut gate at the end of it, and the woman of about fifty who keeps that gate was standing on the inside of it with a piece of chalk in her hand and a brass key on a tarred cord in her apron pocket, and the forty-fourth day of that month was on the post above the forty-third with nothing rubbed out, and about four people on that lane have worked out since that a woman has written a date on that post every morning through a flood with no months in it and has never rubbed one out and has not been asked anything by anybody in all of it.

Then the marked voice changed, and there was nobody on the lane to notice it.

He did not stop at the gate. She did not speak to him. He went past it at about the pace a man goes past a thing he has been asked about at that gate before and has learned what it costs, and about four people who watched from the second house have worked out since that a man who carries things for a living has learned what a gate costs from being stopped at one, and that a carrier is the only person in this city that anybody has ever written that down about, and that nobody has.

"**The forty-fourth day of that month.**"

The gate went and the bar went across and about four people on that lane have worked out since that a woman who has not been asked anything in about a month said four words to a post and to nobody, and that the four of them were a date, and that the date was on the post before he got there and will be on the post tomorrow, and that a woman who says a date out loud to a gate every morning is not asking for anything and is not waiting for anybody and is not going to be asked, and that she has not been asked.

---

He went home with the satchel's flap down and about four people on that road have worked out since that nine things have gone through his hands in this flood and that he has not opened one of them, and that five of the nine were sheets of paper with nothing at the top of them, and that four of the nine have been on a wall in this city inside about a week, and that not one of the four had a name at the top of it, and that the price of that is that nobody can do anything for you, and that he has not complained about it once in nine things.
