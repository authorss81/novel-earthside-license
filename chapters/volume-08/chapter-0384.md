# Chapter 0384 — The Four Places Read Out Loud, And A Share That Is A Household's

A room over a market had the sixty-ninth day of the Bare Month in chalk on its door at about the seventh hour, a Sunday, and the light came in over a step at the back with no name on it and lay along a bench that three of them were sitting on and stopped in the middle of the table, and the cold came up out of the floor at about the fourth hour and stayed, and what was under about four pairs of hands that morning was a form, a book open at a ruled line, a slate, a lump of chalk, a second slate on a shelf behind the table with a date on it and nothing under it, and a woman's hands with nothing in them.

---

She ruled it herself. There is no form in four hundred miles with a room on it, and there is no form in four hundred miles with a heading on it either, and a woman who has kept a public register for nine years has been writing both of those things out by hand since before the water and has never once improved on either.

"There is a heading and there are four places under it, and I am going to read all four of them out loud in the local language, and I have not read the fourth one out loud in this room before and I have read the other three in about nine different rooms, and the reason I am doing it this morning is that the fourth one is going out on somebody else's calendar and nobody in this room has ever heard what it says."

The book was open on the table in front of her at a ruled line. It has three columns and the first is for what went out and the second is for what came back and the third has been empty on purpose since a Thursday in that month. Her forearm was not across it this morning. She had both hands on the table.

---

"A district. That is the first one and it has nowhere to start, and it has had nowhere to start since the winter before the water, and I am not going to pretend this morning that a room has fixed that."

She went on. A population. A number, and the man who sends it is the man who works boards at the bottom of this city and is not in this room and has not been asked what the number is. A rota. She read the word and stopped for a moment and then went on, and about four people in that room worked out since that the third of the four places was empty and has been empty, and that a word read out in a room stops being a word on a piece of paper the moment it leaves somebody's mouth in the local language, and that nobody in that room was going to be the one who said so.

Then she read the fourth one.

"**A share.**"

The room did not make a sound. A share is a figure. A share is a number a man puts in a place on a page four times a year, and a man who has been putting numbers in that place for about nine years has never once gone to find out what a share is, and he said so out loud in this room a fortnight ago and nobody asked him why.

"**And that is the whole of what is on the form, and I have said the fourth one out loud in the local language in a room with the day on the door, and about nine people have just heard a word they have been reading for a month, and a share is a figure, and I would like it said out loud by somebody in this room that a share is not a figure.**"

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall had his hand flat on his own inside breast pocket and did not take it out, and he had been in a room where a share was read out before and had said nothing about it then and said nothing about it now, and about four people in that room worked out since that the two of them had not looked at each other and were not going to.

---

"A share is a figure."

"**A share is a figure and a share is a household's, and those are not the same thing, and about nine people in this room have been reading the fourth place as a number for a month because a number is what the other three of them are.**"

The man of about thirty-nine was against the wall by the step at the back where the light does not reach, with his own slate flat on his knee and his right hand over the face of it. He had been at the back of nine rooms in a fortnight without being asked one question in any of them, and a woman has just read a word out loud nine feet off him, and he did not say a word about it and nobody in that room was going to ask him whether he thought a share was a figure or a household.

The man of about forty-eight had come in and sat down the way he does, and the man of about twenty-five had his hands in the pockets of his coat, and the woman of about twenty-one had her hands in her apron with the pen in the apron, and there were three of the four of them in that room and there were four lines ruled on a list behind the woman with the book, and nobody in that room said one word about the fourth line.

---

Nobody in that room asked the household. That is the whole of what happened at about the ninth hour, and nobody argued about it, and a man of about thirty-four who says a thing out loud before he is asked for it said it in one breath and gave the reason with it.

"**Nobody is going to go to that household. She said no on a step before anybody had asked her, and there is no version of this flood in which anybody asks her again, and if the fourth place is a household's then the fourth place stays empty, and it is going out empty, and it goes out correct.**"

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register put the pen down flat on the table beside the book.

"**Then the fourth place goes out empty and it goes out with a heading on it, and about four people in this room have worked out since that a return that goes out correct is the only thing in four hundred miles that this city has ever been able to do, and I would like it said out loud that the cost of it is that the reason the fourth place is empty is a woman on a step and not a column, and no office nine hundred miles off is ever going to be able to tell the difference.**"

Nobody improved on that either. A room over a market has now read out loud, in the local language, in daylight, with a date in chalk on the door, the whole of a form that is going out on somebody else's calendar, and has established in front of about nine people that the fourth place is a household's and that the household is not going to be asked, and both of those are a thing a form cannot carry and only a room can, and a room is not a thing anybody four hundred miles off can ask to have produced.

---

Then somebody at the back said the other thing, and it was said by the man of about thirty-nine, and it was the first time in nine rooms in a fortnight that he had said a whole sentence that was not a question.

"There are two empty thirds in this room. The third of the four places on that form has been empty, and the third column of that book has been empty on purpose since a Thursday in that month, and they are not the same empty and I am not going to sit here and be asked whether they are."

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register did not argue with it and did not look at him. She has been in about nine rooms in a fortnight and has said nothing in five of them, and the not-saying is not a position and is not going to be described by anybody in that room as one, and about four people in that room worked out since that the man at the back had just said a true thing out loud and that not one of them was going to be the second one to agree with him.

---

The room emptied at about the fourth hour and the form was on the table with the first place having nowhere to start and the second one in a condition and the third one empty and the fourth one empty, and the book was shut and went back on the shelf it lives on. The second slate had been on the shelf behind the table with a date on it and nothing under it all day and nobody went near the shelf.

Three of the four of them came down those nine steps and the fourth of the four is not in this city and is not going to be asked about anything by anybody, and a party of four is four, and the reading of a form out loud in a room on a Sunday has changed exactly one thing, which is that about nine people in this city now know what the fourth place is, and the knowing of it has not put one mark on the page, and the page is going out on somebody else's calendar with the fourth place empty and no name at the top of it, which is the only thing about it that this city decided and did not take back.
