# Chapter 0355 — Four Places Under A Heading, Read Out In The Local Language

A room over a market had the fortieth day of that month in chalk on its door at about the seventh hour, a Saturday, and about nine people in it, and the light came in over a step at the back with no name on it and lay along a bench that nobody was sitting on, and the cold was out of the floor and in the grain of a board, and what was under about four pairs of hands that morning was a form, a slate, a lump of chalk, a second slate with a date on it and nothing under it, and about nine people who had come up a stair they had all climbed before.

---

She wrote the day on the door before she started and not after, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who keeps a public register writes the date on the door first because a date written afterwards is a record of what happened and a date written first is a promise about what is going to, and that this city has known the difference for about a month and has used it eleven times.

Nobody in that room had seen the form before that morning, and about four people in that room have worked out since that nine people looked at a folded piece of paper at about the same moment, and that about nine people in four hundred miles who had never seen it at all had been sent to a room by a sheet of paper with nothing at the top of it, and that the whole of the arrangement turns on a thing none of them can check.

The form went flat on the table. It had been folded twice with a bone and the creases had been pressed hard, and the heading was across the top in a hand nobody in this city writes, and under the heading there were four lines ruled the same distance apart and nothing in any of them.

"Everybody in this room has been asked to come by a sheet of paper with nothing at the top of it. It is read out in the local language, in daylight, with the day on the door, in the order it is printed on, and I am going to stop at each of them long enough for the people at the back to hear it, and I am not going to explain any of them while I am standing up."

Nobody said anything. About four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who has spent a fortnight explaining things in rooms has decided that a form is not one of them, and that the deciding to stop explaining a thing in front of the people it is going to be explained to is either very good judgement or the end of a very good week, and that nobody in that room could tell which and that they were not going to ask.

---

Nobody in that room was working. That is the thing about a reading, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a room in this city has had about nine people in it and not one of them doing anything since the seventh hour, and that this is the second morning this month that has happened and that on the first one a man at the back stood up and refused a rule the room had made for itself, and that the rule is still on the wall in the same words, and that the man who refused it is not in this room and has not been asked to come.

The man of about thirty-nine was against the wall by the step at the back where the light does not reach, with his own slate on his knee, and his right hand lay flat over the face of it the whole time, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who trades nineteen rates on a board in four markets has not carried a slate anywhere in four hundred miles, and that whatever is on the face of it is the only thing in this city he has taken off a surface himself, and that he has not shown it to anybody and is not going to.

The man of about forty-one in a very good coat sat down at the near end of the bench and took a document out of the inside of the coat and did not open it for about a quarter of an hour.

"I do not have the local language and I am not going to ask anybody to slow down for me."

"**Nobody is going to slow down. You are the fourth person this month to be in this room without the local language and the first who has said so.**"

He read his document for about an hour after that, and nobody in that room told him where anything had been decided, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man in a very good coat came up that stair without a reason he was going to say out loud, and that he has now said it, and that the saying of it cost him nothing he can be charged for.

---

She read the heading first and then she read the four places, and she read them in the order they are on the page and not in the order anybody in that room had been thinking about them since Monday.

"A district."

She stopped there for about as long as it takes to fill a pail.

"**A district is where it is. That place has no start in it, and that is not a fault in the form, and six gates on a stretch of road were walked in this flood and gave six answers and two of the six were about a turn and not one of them was a name.**"

"A population."

"**A population is a number. Somebody has to hold it and whoever holds it is given it, and there is one man in this city who is given one of those and keeps a second one, and he is not in this room and I am not going to say what the second one is.**"

Nobody said the number. About four people in that room have worked out since that about four hundred and forty people on eleven miles of flats are the only population in this flood that anybody in this city knows by heart, and that the woman keeping this register has just told a room she is not going to say it out loud, and that a figure which is said every morning on a set of boards by somebody else is a different kind of page from one written in ink.

"A rota."

"**A rota is a set of days against something. I am not going to fill this one in, and I said in this room on the thirty-first day of that month that I would walk out of a room rather than put a person in the third column of my own book.**"

About four people in that room have worked out since that the third place is the one a room stops at, and that it stopped for about as long as it takes to fill a pail twice over, and that the man of about thirty-nine's hand did not move on his slate while it stopped, and that the man of about forty-one in a very good coat turned a page.

The room did not make a sound. About four people in that room have worked out since that a person who says a thing twice in a room is doing it for the second hearing and not for the people in front of her, and that the second hearing is for whoever reads about it later, and that there is nobody in this city to read about it later except a register, and that the register is hers.

Adrian Vale was at the shelf behind the table and he had the second slate in his hands and he read it from the top down, which took about as long as it takes to fill a pail, and then he read the third column of her open book, which was empty, and he put a finger about a finger's width from the edge of it and did not touch it, and nobody in that room asked him why and nobody in that room is going to.

---

Two hours had gone by when she came to the last of them, and about four people in that room have worked out since that she had put it at the end on purpose and that a person reading four things out loud to nine people can decide the order and that a woman who has decided the order has told a room what she thinks before she has said a word of it.

"And a share."

She said that one last and she said it flatly and she did not stop afterwards, and about four people in that room have worked out since that she had put it at the end deliberately, and that a thing you put at the end of four things is the thing the other three were for, and that about nine people in a room with a date on the door had now heard a form say out loud in the local language that the fourth of the four places is about somebody's share, and that not one of them had known it was there at the seventh hour.

"A share is a thing a household has. Not a figure, not a place, not a thing you can divide. Somebody in this city is going to have to say whose, and I have been keeping a public register for nine years and there is no column in any book in this city for a household's share, and I am not inventing one this morning in front of nine people."

Nobody asked her what the fourth place was for. About four people in that room have worked out since that about nine people have just been told what the fourth of four places is, in a room in daylight, in the local language, and that not one of them has a household it could go to, and that the one place in this city where a household of about nine keeps a share has not been written to and is not going to be, because nobody has written to it.

---

Then she read the heading again, all of it, out loud, and about four people in that room have worked out since that she read it the second time slower than the first, and that a woman who reads a heading twice is a woman who has just read four places underneath it and is checking that the heading is still the same heading.

"**I am not going to put a person in the third place. I said so in this room on the thirty-first day of that month. I am aware of what I am doing with a shelf behind me, and that shelf has a date on a slate and nothing under it, and I am not going to pretend I have not noticed that.**"

Nobody thanked her and nobody improved on it. About four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who reads a form out loud in the order it is printed on is a woman who is not going to improve on it, and that a room which has just heard the fourth place named and has not answered it is a room that has been told something about itself, and that the thing it has been told is that the form it has been filling in for nine years is about a household and not about a street.

The form was still on the table at the hour the light went, in the same orientation as it had been at the seventh hour, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who read four places out loud and filled in none of them has done the only thing she can do with a document that is not going to be defeated, and that a document which is not going to be defeated is a document that is going to go out, and that the going out is on somebody else's calendar and is not in this room.

It was not filled in and was not sent and did not go back and did not come back, and about four people in four hundred miles have worked out since that a document that goes back is the ordinary end of a document and not a defeat, and that this one is not going back yet, and that the room has four days to find out what it is going to do about a share.

---

The light went off the bench nobody was sitting on at about the fourth hour and the man of about thirty-nine said two things, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who had his hand flat over his own slate for the whole of a morning in which four places were read out in the local language chose the last hour and not the first, and that he has not been asked anything and has not offered anything.

"**That is the whole of it.**"

"That is the whole of what."

"**A form, a heading, four places, and the fourth of them belongs to somebody who is not in this room, and every one of those four is correct, and this is the first room in about a fortnight where I was told what the last of the four was, out loud, before anybody asked me for it, and I am not going to be asked about it.**"

Nobody asked him about it. About four people in that room have worked out since that the man of about thirty-nine has spent a month being the only person in this city who has done the part of a thing that cannot be taken back, and that this morning he said two speeches in the last hour of a room in which about nine people had been told the fourth place out loud for the first time, and that the two of those facts are about the same building and about nothing else in the world, and that nobody in that room was going to say so to him and nobody did.
