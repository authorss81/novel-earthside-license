# Chapter 0327 — Nine Places, And Not Her

Nine folded sheets went out of a room over a market on the twelfth day of that month, a Saturday, at about the seventh hour, in a bag with a piece of chalk and a cloth in the top of it, and the wind came up the street and went straight through the room because the window on that side has never shut properly, and the cold was out of the glass and in the bag handle and in the palm of a girl of seventeen who had been holding a plain slate for about an hour, and what was under about four pairs of hands that morning was a plain slate, a lump of chalk, a cloth, and nine sheets of paper with nothing at the top of any of them.

---

She had her name at the top of the slate before she had anything else on it, which is what she does, and she wrote it standing up at about the eighth hour with the window open and the wind going through the room, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a girl of seventeen who puts her name on a thing before she puts anything else on it is the only person in this city who has ever been made to, and that nobody in this room has ever asked her to.

She had copied the second of the four onto the slate, both sides, the way she has copied every asking anybody has written in this flood, and she has signed none of them and she was not going to sign this one, and about four people in that room have worked out since that this is the fifth of them and the fourth with a place on it and no name on it, and that a girl who copies is the only person in four hundred miles who can hold the whole of what a city has said to itself in a fortnight and has never once been asked to hold any of it.

"Where does this one go."

"**To nine places. And you are not one of the nine, and I am going to say that out loud in this room rather than have it found out on Wednesday, because you have asked me a question and I do not know the answer to the second half of it.**"

Nobody in that room improved on that, and about four people in that room have worked out since that the woman who has a sheet of road with nine places on it did not put the ninth place in the nine this morning and nobody saw her leave it out and everybody in the room understood it inside about four minutes.

---

The man of about twenty-five had been in that room since before the light, and his left boot was mended at the front with a length of cord that is not new, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man of twenty-five who has mended his own boot with a piece of cord he had in his pocket has been in a city since before the water and has not been given anything by anybody, and that nobody in that room has ever asked him for anything either, and that the two of those are not the same thing and he has never said so.

He said the thing he had come to say and nobody had asked him for it, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who was in this room on the fifty-fourth day of the Short Month and asked out loud what the day after the sixtieth would be, and got no answer, has come back to this room and has not asked a single question, and that the not-asking is a decision and it is the first one he has made in this flood that anybody has seen.

"**I wrote something. I am not going to say what it is and I am not going to be asked, and the reason I am not going to say it is that it says one thing about me and about nine words, and a thing that says one thing about me is mine, and if I say it here then it is in here, and it will be in here on Wednesday when I am not able to look at it.**"

He put the folded sheet on the table with the ninth line of it uppermost and did not push it towards anybody, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man of twenty-five came into a room over a market and put a sheet on a table and did not let go of it, and that a person who is not going to be asked does not have to refuse anything, and that refusing is expensive and this was free.

---

The bag went out at about the seventh hour with the man of about thirty-one who carries things for a living, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who carried one sheet out on the Friday carried nine out on the Saturday and did not tell the room he was going to and would not have taken nine yesterday for anything, and that a man who has said out loud that a hand is not a page has just put one hand into a bag nine times over and gone up a hill with them.

Nobody in that room said the reason for the nine out loud, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a second notice copied nine times is a safer notice than a first one copied once, and that the reason is that there is nobody in four hundred miles with standing to argue with a notice, because the only person who could say what one means is the person whose hand it is and that person is not going to be asked and has not been asked, and that a person who wants to be arguing with a sheet has to put a name at the top of it, and that a name at the top of a sheet is the only thing on it that would change what it is, and that a notice with a name at the top of it is not a notice and is a piece of paper somebody wants a thing done with, and that nine houses holding the same hand is nine houses that can each see down the row that the one next door did not write a name on theirs.

The girl of seventeen went out of the room about a quarter of an hour after him because there is nowhere in that building for a person of seventeen to be that is not that room, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a girl who is on no roll of anything is in a room over a market every morning of this flood because it is the only place she is not asked to be anywhere else, and that nobody has ever noticed that and she has never once mentioned it.

She did not go up the hill. She went the other way, down to the low ground and along it, and the wind was behind her going the other way and about four people who have watched her go have worked out since that a girl who walks into the wind is a girl who has somewhere to be, and that she had not said where, and that about four of the nine people in that room wanted to ask her and about four of them did not, and that the not-asking has been the ordinary condition of that room for a fortnight and is now the whole of it.

---

Nine places in about six hours and the light going off the low ground about a quarter of an hour before the tops, and the first one took a sheet and put it on a sill and the second one took a sheet and asked whether it had to be answered, and the third one took a sheet and said nothing at all, and about four people on that road have worked out since that eight of the nine people who took a sheet did not ask what it was for, and that the one who asked has had a sheet in her hand since about the fourth hour of the afternoon and has not opened it, and that opening it is not a duty and closing the door is not a refusal and nobody in four hundred miles has ever said which of the two things a person in a house with a sheet in her hand is doing.

The second place was a house with a sill and about nine of them said the same three words through about half of it, and about four people on that road have worked out since that three words through a half-open door is the ordinary courtesy of a road in this flood and that a man with a bag of nine answers it the way he would answer anybody.

**"Do I have to send anything back."**

"**No. And if you do send something back then you will have signed it, and if you have not signed it then it is a piece of paper with nothing at the top of it, and the second one is the one you want.**"

The woman of about twenty-eight who had said the three words shut about half of the door, and about four people on that road have worked out since that a person who shuts half a door has not refused anything and has not accepted anything and that this is the ordinary condition of nine houses in a row in this flood, and that it is the only condition anybody in four hundred miles can actually work in.

The ninth of the nine was eight houses in a row with a gap for a cart between two of them, and about four people on that road have worked out since that a woman walked nine places in three days a week ago and got no answer at that one and wrote nothing on the ninth line, and that a man with a bag put a sheet on the post in the gap for the cart because the post was the only thing there that was not a door.

The gap for the cart had a post in the middle of it and the post had a nail in it about four feet up, and the nail was not put there for a sheet, and about four people in four hundred miles have worked out since that a nail in a post in a gap for a cart is the only thing in this city that has ever been put up for an announcement and never once been used for one until now, and that nobody knows who put it in and that everybody on that road has known about it since they could walk.

Nine sheets out of a bag of nine and the bag folded and in the coat.

The walk back down the hill took the same six hours in the dark, because the road is a road and not a river, and about four people in four hundred miles have worked out since that the second of four notices went out on the twelfth of that month at about the seventh hour for a room on the sixteenth, and that that is four days, and that it went to nine places and reached nine places.

There is one person in this city for whom there is no place at all, and she was in a room over a market at the seventh hour this morning, and she is on no list and she was never on one.

Being unfindable is not a qualification, and about four people in four hundred miles have worked out since that a person who cannot be sent for cannot be sent for, and that the four days are the only instrument this city has, and that about four people in a room over a market worked out on the twelfth of that month that there is one person it does not reach, and have not written it down and are not going to.
