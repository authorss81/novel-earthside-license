# Chapter 0328 — A Window, And A Man Who Was Not Asked

A room over a market had the thirteenth day of that month in chalk on its door, a Sunday, and about nine people in it, and the wind came up the street and went straight through it, and the cold was out of the glass and in the rebate of a window that had not shut properly since before the water, and what was under about four pairs of hands that morning was a folding knife with a stiff hinge, about nine lines of pencil on a single sheet of paper with nothing at the top of it, a register with nine years in it, and a length of new rope over a chair back.

---

The man of about forty-eight came in at about the seventh hour and sat down, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has been in a city since before the water and has not been asked anything has learned to sit down in a room the moment he comes into it, because standing about is what a person does when they are expecting to be asked, and he has not been asked anything in a long time and it has not occurred to him this morning to expect it.

He had the knife on the table. It is a folding knife with a hinge that has gone stiff and does not spring, and he opens it and shuts it twice with the thumb of the other hand to make the joint take, and then it will hold about nine minutes, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has to do that before he can use a tool has been doing it for years and has never mentioned it and is not going to mention it now.

Nobody asked him anything about it, and about four people in that room have worked out since that the man who keeps a public register has watched him do it four times this month and has written nothing down about it, and that a woman who has kept a register for nine years knows exactly what she is not writing down.

---

He put a sheet on the table about the eighth hour, face down, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who puts a thing face down on a table in a room has decided in advance what the room is going to get out of it, and that another man in this city has done the same thing at this same table twice in about a fortnight, and that about four people in that room worked out on the twelfth day of that month that the two men were doing the same thing and nobody said so and nobody is going to say so now.

"There is a place on that and there is a day on it, and it goes out this morning, and the room is on the seventeenth, and that is four days, and I am not going to be asked whether it is going to work."

Nobody asked him, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man of forty-eight came into a room and said the words *I am not going to be asked* out loud, in daylight, with a date on the door, and that he was not asked, and that the saying of it cost him nothing, and that a man who has not been asked anything for a long time does not get the credit for a refusal anybody has made for him.

---

Then he got up and did the window.

Nobody asked him to. The catch had been lifting at the top left corner for nine years and the wind came in under it at about a hand's width and everybody in that room had stopped noticing it the way a person stops noticing a step, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a room in this city has spent a fortnight arguing about a boundary nine hundred paces off a shut gate and has had a draught in it the whole time, and that neither of those two things is in anybody's book.

He got the knife open, which took him the two thumb moves, and he took the catch off with about four turns of a screw he did not have to find because he could feel where it was, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who can find a screw in the dark in a window frame he has never worked on before is a man who has done it a great many times, and that he did it in about four minutes with a knife and his thumbnail and did not once ask for a tool.

The draught stopped. The wind did not stop. About four people in that room have worked out since that a window which has been letting a hand's width of weather in for nine years is not the same thing as a window that has been letting a hand's width of a person in, and that the man who closed it is not the woman of about fifty at a shut gate on another road and has never met her and is not going to.

---

The man of about fifty-seven was at the step at the back with the rope over his arm and had not said anything for about an hour, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has watched nine households give their days back and then watched a room decide that nobody is going anywhere is the only person in that room with any use for a window, and that he has not said so and is not going to.

"You are not going to be paid for that."

"**No. And I am going to tell you why in a room with a date on the door, and then I am going to stop, and the reason is not a good one and it is the only one I have.**"

The man of about fifty-seven put the rope over his other arm, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has paid money for half a holding and has watched a room decide that nobody goes has just said *you are not going to be paid for that* to a man with a knife, and that it is the first thing he has said to another person in that room in three mornings, and that he said it about a window.

---

"I did it because I was sitting at it."

"That is not a reason anybody has ever accepted in a room."

"**Then it is a reason nobody has ever accepted, and I have been in about nine of these rooms since before the water and every one of them has had a draught in it, and I am the only person in this city who goes up a ladder for a living when he can get the work, and there is not enough of it, and that is a thing I would rather say in here than say at a door.**"

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register wrote nothing on the second slate and about four people in that room have worked out since that she has declined four things out loud in this flood and named the cost of each one before she said it, and that she did not decline this one, and that the not-declining is not agreement and is not a decision and is a woman who has decided that a man fixing a window in a room she keeps a book in is not a thing that requires a decision.

He sat down again. The knife went on the table in about the place it had been when he came in, which is a thing about four people in that room noticed and about four of them did not say anything about.

"Will you tell me what you are for."

"I have just told the room what I am for and it took me about four sentences and a window."

"**I know what you told the room. I am asking a different question and it is the first one anybody has asked you in this city and I would like it noticed that I have asked it and that I am not going to ask you a second one.**"

He turned the knife over on the table and did not open it, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who keeps a public register has asked a man one question in a fortnight and that the question was not about the boundary and not about the sheet and not about the four days, and that it cost her something to ask it in front of about nine people and that it cost him more to answer it.

"Climbing. Ladders, mostly, and a bit of shuttering. I have done about nine days of it since the water and about nine of them have been for people who could pay and about four of them have not, and I am not going to tell this room which is which and neither am I going to tell them at a door."

---

The sheet went out at about the ninth hour with the man of about thirty-one, and the third of the four went out on the thirteenth of that month in the morning for a room on the seventeenth, and about four people in four hundred miles have worked out since that a man who puts a sheet down face up, gets up, and mends a window in four minutes with a knife and his thumbnail has spent a morning in a way nobody in that room will be able to put on a page, and that the room has a catch on its window that did not shut for nine years and that about four people will notice that tomorrow and about four of them will know why and nobody else will.

Nobody asked him whether it would go, and the sheet had nothing at the top of it, and his hand was on it about nine seconds before he let it go, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a hand is not a page and that a hand stays in the hand that made it until the person that made it is dead, and that a man with a stiff knife and a window in his morning is going to be in a room on the seventeenth and nobody in this room knows whether he will be and he is not going to tell them.
