# Chapter 0340 — Four Seconds, And A Name Off His Own Board

The room at the back of the Open Hand has no key and no person owns it, and the right of refusal that cannot be bought out is in about nine rooms in this city every day, and it means that about nine people can sit in it for four hours and nobody in four hundred miles can turn them out and nobody can be angry with them for being in it, and on the twenty-fifth day of that month, a Friday, about nine people were in it, and the light came in at the back and lay along a bench nobody was sitting on, and the cold was out of the floor and in the grain of a board that had nineteen rates on it, and what was under about four pairs of hands that morning was a board, a handcart nobody had brought in past the step, a length of rope, and a man's own hand.

---

He had the board under his arm and he had it face up, and a man who trades on a board has never once carried a board with the writing on the underside where the rain can get at it, and a man who puts nineteen rates on a board in four markets for four years has a habit about the underside of it, and that the habit is the only thing about a board anybody in this city has ever respected.

"There is a place at the top of that road," he said, before anybody had asked anybody anything, and a man who has been asked nothing in about four hundred rooms in this city has come into one of the nine of them and has started talking about a place at the top of a road, and about four people in that room have been waiting a fortnight for him to say something.

Then the wall said one thing, and it said it once, and it is entered that nobody in that room can be told where it came from, and the woman against the wall did not go the colour of the wall and nothing else in the room moved at all.

> A word may be put to a thing in a room in daylight with a date on it,
> and a word may be taken back the same way,
> and a person may not be taken back at all.

He was on his feet before the last of it had finished coming up and the board was on the bench face up and his hand was flat on it, and a man who has traded on a board for about four years has refused a thing before a sentence was finished four times in four years and once every one of those times a woman who is on nothing was the one who did the refusing, and this is the fifth and that the fifth time it is his own board that is in the room and not a woman's refusal.

"**No. And I am not going to explain the no, and I am not going to be asked, and I want it said that I said it before the room had finished reading it.**"

Nobody argued, and a man who has said no four times in four years before a sentence was finished each time has said it a fifth time in a room that cannot turn him out and has not been a woman doing it, and a room in this city has spent a fortnight learning not to argue and has got it right in about nine days, and that about four people in that room got it right about this man in about four years.

---

And then the refusal is what did the rest of it, and it took him about nine minutes to get from the no to the other thing and the no is the reason he said the other thing, and that a rule about a word going on and coming off a page cannot be refused by a man who has put a person on a page and taken them off it, and that the rule is the reason the room got the other thing and not the man's character.

"That is about words."

"**That is about words and it says a word can go on a thing in a room and come off it again in a room, and it says a person cannot come off at all. I have put a person on a board and taken them off it, and I did it in my own hand, and nobody in four hundred miles knows that, and I have had it since about the ninth year of this flood.**"

The room did not make a sound, and a man who has traded on nineteen rates in four markets for four years and has never been refused by his own board has just said out loud in a room in daylight that he took a name off that board, and the name is not on it and has not been on it since about the ninth year of this flood and that nobody in four hundred miles has ever asked.

---

He told it in the order he had it in his head, and a man telling a story like this in a room tells it in that order because he has been rehearsing it since the ninth year of this flood and has never had anywhere to put it, and the order is the one he rehearsed and the order is the cruelty of it.

"**A man asked me to put his own name on that board, in about the ninth year of this flood, and I wrote it on in my own hand, and it was on there about nine days.**"

He did not lift his hand off it.

"**And a woman who is on nothing and is named on nothing asked to be added by name at the back of a market about a hundred days into this flood, and I refused her before she had finished the sentence, because that board has nineteen rates on it and no names and has had no names since before the water.**"

Nobody in that room said a word, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who trades on a board refused a woman who is on nothing in nineteen words at the back of a market and has never said so to anybody, and that he is saying it in a room where nobody can be angry with him for it, and that he has not said her name and about four of them have noticed that he has not said her name.

"**And the man whose name was on the board came to her afterwards and told her the board had his name on it and hers was not going to go on. He was not a fool about it. He worked out it in about as long as it takes to read a line that a board that has refused a person can be shown to have accepted somebody, and he was right, and I do not think he knew what he was doing.**"

He stopped there, and a man who has said a thing like that in a room and stopped has left the room to do the rest of it himself, and about four people in that room wanted to ask him a question and about four of them did not, and that the four who did not were right.

"**I took it off the next morning with a cloth. In my own hand, and with my own cloth, and there is a place on that board where it was and everybody in four markets can see the place and has seen it since the ninth year of this flood and not one of them has ever asked me what was there.**"

---

And the cost is not the board and it is not the cloth and it is not the nine days. The cost is that he has said it out loud, in daylight, in a room that cannot be turned out, to about nine people, on a day with a date on the door, and that a person was put on a page in four hundred miles because of what he did in that market in about the ninth year of this flood and cannot be put back, and that about four people in that room have worked out since that the rule on the wall is not about a word at all and never was, and that the sentence on the wall is the cheapest description of what a name does in this world that anybody in four hundred miles has ever been given, and that the man who refused it is the only person in this city who has done the part of it that cannot be taken back, and that he did it in about the ninth year of this flood, and that he is the only person in four hundred miles who knows both halves of that sentence.

"Then the rule is no use to you."

"**The rule is the only thing anybody has said out loud in this flood that I have not got a way round. I have been getting a way round words for nine years and that is the whole of my trade. That one has a person in it.**"

He put both hands on the board and did not pick it up and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has refused a thing out loud before a sentence was finished and then said the true thing about himself nine minutes later has not had a day to think about either of them and did both in one morning, and that about four people in that room have worked out since that this is the fifth time and that the five are not the same thing five times, and that the fifth one is about himself and the first four were about a rule.

---

The man of about sixty with a handcart and a length of rope was at the back of the room with the rope over his shoulder and had a rope that had been used and was not new, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man of about sixty told a woman at a gate that he would come back about nine days from a morning and it has been a good deal more than nine days, and that he came back, and that he came into a room rather than to a gate, and that nobody in this room has asked him what for and nobody is going to.

He asked one question and it was at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and about four people in that room have worked out since that it was the first thing anybody in four hundred miles has asked about the sentence on that wall and it was asked by a man with a handcart, and that it is the question the sentence does not answer and that about nine people in this city have been arguing for a month without asking.

"Can a person be put back."

Nobody in that room answered him, and the wall said a thing about a word going on a thing and coming off it again and said a person cannot come off at all, and a man has been sitting at the back of a room for four hours with a rope over his shoulder and has asked whether the other half of that can be undone, and that about four people in that room have worked out that nobody in four hundred miles can answer him and that the man with the board has already answered it about himself, and that the two answers are in the same room and they are not the same answer, and that a room at the back of the Open Hand has a right of refusal that cannot be bought out and has no answer in it at all, and about four people who have been in about nine of these rooms in this city have worked out since that this is the first time anybody in this city can call to mind that a refusal has been the whole of what a room had to say, and that nobody has been angry.

The board went out of the room at about the fifth hour of the afternoon with the nineteen rates on it and a place on it where something had been for about nine days once, and about four people in that room have worked out since that the wall said one thing this morning and it is said once and it is not going to be said again in this flood, and that a man who has carried a board in four markets for four years and who has taken a name off it in his own hand with his own cloth is carrying it out with the place still showing, and that about four hundred people in this city can see that place and that not one of them will ever be told, and that the people who were in that room this morning now know it, and that knowing it does not put anybody back, and that there is no panel and no page and no mouth in four hundred miles that is going to say so out loud twice.
