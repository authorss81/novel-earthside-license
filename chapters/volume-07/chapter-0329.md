# Chapter 0329 — She Says It Herself, And Nobody Had Asked Her

A room over a market had the fourteenth day of that month in chalk on its door, a Monday, and about nine people in it, and the light came in early and the window on that side shut for the first time in nine years, and about four people in that room have worked out since that the weather in a room is not weather and that a man with a knife and a stiff hinge is the reason nobody in that room has said anything about the cold for two days, and the cold was in the glass anyway and in the grain of a hazel stick that a woman of about sixty-one was carrying in her hand and not leaning on, and what was under about four pairs of hands that morning was a piece of chalk, a register with nine years in it lying open at a page nobody was looking at, a length of new rope over a chair back, and a stick.

---

The woman of about sixty-one came in at about the eighth hour and put a sheet on the table face up, which about four people in that room noticed, and then she did not sit down, which about four people in that room also noticed, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman of sixty-one who stands in a room holding a stick she does not lean on is a woman who has come to say a thing and has not decided what order to say it in.

The sheet was the fourth of the four. It had a place on it and a day on it and nothing at the top of it, and it went out that morning for a room on the eighteenth of that month, and that is four days.

Nobody in that room asked her anything about it. About four people in that room have worked out since that a man of forty-eight came into this room on Sunday and said out loud that he was not going to be asked whether it was going to work, and that nobody asked him, and that nobody has asked anybody anything about any of the four sheets in this room, and that this is a rule the room arrived at by itself in about nine days and has not written down and is not going to.

---

"There are four of us."

Nobody in that room improved on ita woman has said the number out loud in a room with a date on the door and has not gone on to say what the four of them are, and that there is no word in this city for what the four of them are, and that about four people in that room would have used one a month ago, and that about four of them have worked out in the last nine days that there is not one, and that they have all arrived at it separately and none of them has said so to anybody.

"You are not going to be asked anything about that sheet."

"**Nobody has asked me anything about that sheet, and nobody has asked me anything at all, and I am sixty-one and I have been in this city since before the water and there is not one person in it who has ever asked me a question, and I would like that said out loud in this room with a date on the door, and then I am going to say the other thing, and the other thing is mine and I am not going to be asked for it either.**"

---

She said the other thing.

"**I am not going.**"

The room did not make a sound, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman of sixty-one said four words in a room with a date on the door and that nobody had asked her, and that a person who has not been asked anything and who says the answer out loud before anybody has asked the question has done the only thing in this flood that costs her and cannot be taken back, and that she did it in about four seconds.

Nobody argued with it and about four people in that room have worked out since that this is the seventh morning running that somebody has said a thing in here that the room did not argue with, and that six of the seven were said by people who could have been argued with, and that this one cannot be argued with at all, and that nobody in this room is going to be told she is wrong and nobody in this room is going to be told she is right.

---

The man of about fifty-seven was at the step at the back with the rope over his arm and said the only thing he said that morning, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has lost a season and still has a certainty has just watched a woman of sixty-one lose the standing of the fourth person in a thing nobody can name, and that he is the only man in that room who knows exactly what it costs and that he is not going to say so in front of her.

"Then the room on the eighteenth has three people in it."

"**The room on the eighteenth has whoever comes, and I am not one of them, and three is a number and four is a number and neither of them is what we are, and there is no word in this city for what we are and I have looked for one for a long time and there is not one and that is not an injustice, that is just the language.**"

Nobody wrote it down, and about four people in that room have worked out since that the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register heard a woman of sixty-one say in a room with a date on the door that there is no word in this city for what the four of them are, and did not write it down, and that a woman who has put a name at the top of nothing for four years did not put a name at the top of that, and that she would not have put one on it in any case.

---

The carrier took the fourth sheet out at about the ninth hour and about four people in that room have worked out since that a man who has carried three of them went out with the fourth in a coat and did not ask the woman anything about it and did not look at her while he did it, and that the not-looking was careful and about four people in that room noticed that it was careful.

Then the room had nothing to do, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a room which has four days of notice in it and three days of it spent has about four minutes of work in it and that four minutes is what a room in this city has on an ordinary day, and that nothing has to be invented to fill them and nobody in that room is going to invent anything.

The woman of about sixty-one picked her stick up off the table where she had put it down and stood in the middle of the floor with it in her hand and not leaning on it, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a stick a person carries and does not lean on is not a stick for walking, and that nobody in that room has ever seen her use it, and that a room which is going to be wrong about a thing in a fortnight is the ordinary condition of a room and is not a fault in it.

"Before you go. **Nobody has ever told us what we are for, and I am not asking, and I want it said that I am not asking. I will tell you what we were told once and it is all we have, and we have never once opened anything, and that was the whole of the instruction and it was given in about four words by a man who is not in this room.**"

The room did not ask her a single question, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman has put the whole of what four people have for a long time on a table in a room with a date on the door and that nobody in that room has taken any of it, and that about four of the nine people in that room will go out of that room this afternoon knowing that there are four people in this city who have never opened anything and that nobody knows why they were told not to and that all four of them are going to be asked to go and not one of them will be asked whether it will go.

The fourth notice went out on the fourteenth of that month at about the ninth hour for a room on the eighteenth, and that is four days, and a woman of about sixty-one said four words in a room with a date on the door before anybody had asked her for them, and about four people in four hundred miles have worked out since that a refusal that nobody asked for is the cheapest kind there is and is the only kind in this flood that nobody can take back, and that a woman who carries a stick and does not lean on it has just put the last thing this city had on a page and has walked out of the room with it still in her hand.
