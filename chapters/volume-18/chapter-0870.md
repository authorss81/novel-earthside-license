# Chapter 0870 — Two Of Them Said The Same Words

The hand of the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register went to the near corner of the open page and lifted it off the fold and let it down again, and nothing had been entered on the page that morning and the page had not been shut since the middle of the week.

It was Wednesday, the five hundred and fifty-fifth day of the Bare Month, and the light came straight up the market row and lay along the bench and up the open stairwell without a break in it. The chalk on the outside of the door below carried a day and a bare board beneath it stood off the wood at its near end. On the top step above, the bar lay along the stone with nothing under it and its two sockets empty.

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board stood at the far end of the bench with his board propped against the leg of it. At the near end of the same bench the man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall two stalls along sat with the board flat across his knees and his thumb along the foot of it, where a rate had wanted a day against it since the middle of the week and had not got one. The keeper sat at the side of the bench with her hand on the page. The woman of about fifty-two with her hands at the sides of her dress stood at the end of it where the light came off the sill. The man of about thirty-one who carries things for a living was by the window with the satchel under his arm and the flap of it down. The man with the one wet sleeve was against the far wall on the side of the table with his coat over the chair at the far side of the room.

---

**"Nothing in this row wants saying twice."**

He said it into the middle of the floor at about the eighth hour with his hand flat on the face of his own board, and nobody answered it and nobody took it up. The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall two stalls along went on with the rate at the foot of his own board and put his thumb back where it had been and waited for a day which did not come. The keeper turned a leaf and squared it against the fold. The man of about thirty-one who carries things for a living went down the stair and came up again with the flap of the satchel down, and nobody asked him anything.

At about the ninth hour the man of about twenty-seven came up with his bucket and the rag over the rim of it and set it by the leg of the table, and the water in it stood flat, and nobody said anything about the sentence which had gone into the middle of the floor an hour before or about the one which was going into it after.

**"You will get a day against that rate or you will not,"** said the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register, **"and the page will be open or shut."**

**"It will be open,"** said the man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall two stalls along, and put his thumb where it had been.

---

Near the end of the morning the man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall two stalls along lifted his thumb off the rate at the foot of his board and said the same sentence, in the same words, into the middle of the floor and to nobody in particular.

**"Nothing in this row wants saying twice."**

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board was at the far end of the bench with his own hand on the face of his own board and he did not turn round and he said nothing, and the keeper's hand stayed on the page where it had been and she wrote nothing down, and the man with the one wet sleeve against the far wall did not move, and nobody in that room said that they had heard it before and nobody said it was the second time and nobody said anything else about it at any hour of the rest of the day.

---

The box of chalk stood on the stone at the top of the market step with its lid up at the angle it always stands at, and there was a thumb's width of chalk dust on the stone beside it where there had not been any standing on it, and the dust had gone hard on the side of it away from the wall and was still soft where the lid of the box stands over it, and the light came along the step and lay on the dust and showed the two textures of it, and nobody in the room had said one word about either.
