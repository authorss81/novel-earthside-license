# Chapter 0127 — The Sixth Room

On the thirty-seventh day of the month after this one, which was the hundred and twenty-seventh day of the flood and a Sunday, about nine people sat in a room at the back of the Open Hand for about four hours and failed for the sixth time in a hundred and twenty-seven days to find out how a page that has already been used once came to end a document, and the failure was entered with the date on it, and at about the fourth hour and nine minutes of the afternoon a pale field came up in that room and offered to make one person the account of it, and a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf said no in about four seconds, and the count of refusals in a book kept at home and never shown to anybody went from twenty-three to twenty-four, and the count at Aurel is thirteen, and the difference is eleven.

**The licence is seventy-seven days old. The undertaking is void and no office has been told. The field was not paid on the first of March, which was the eighty-fifth day of this flood, and that is forty-two days ago. The fee of eleven ounces of silver is entered as owing with no date for the sixty-eighth time. The black line in his right palm is one hundred and twenty-six days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch.**

---

**And the room at the back of the Open Hand has no key, and no person owns it, and the right of refusal that cannot be bought out is in about nine rooms in this city every day, and it means that about nine people can sit in it for four hours and nobody in four hundred miles can turn them out and nobody can be angry at them for being in it. That is the reason it is the sixth room and also the reason it failed like the other five, and about four people worked that out and nobody said it until a girl of seventeen said it at about the third hour of the afternoon.**

The page is in a lockable room off the salt store with the door shut. It is a heading and seven lines. **It was used once, on the second day of the month after this one, on a document whose own condition had not been met, and it cannot be used again, and there is no second copy, and the inside was folded twice and nobody read it out, and nobody in this city can say why it worked and nobody is going to write it down.**

---

**And the six things they tried, and the six of them, and the count entered with the date on it, and nobody improved on any of them, and a notary of fifty read all six out loud in about nine minutes and did not say one word that was not in the book.**

The first five are the five from the twelfth day of the month after this one, which was the hundred and second day of the flood: what the page says, what it was laid on, who was in the room, the document, which holds, because a thing that argues for itself is a thing you can check, and why, which is nothing.

The sixth took about nine minutes to find and about nine minutes to fail and it is the finding of the thirty-seventh day of the month after this one, and it is one line, and a girl of seventeen said it and it is hers.

"**Who wanted it to work.**"

Nobody said anything for about nineteen seconds.

"That is not a thing about the page," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall.

"**It is the sixth thing about the page and it is the only one of the six that has an answer, and the answer is that nobody in this city has ever wanted it to work, and about four of us have wanted to know how it worked, and that is a different wanting, and a man of thirty-two has spent a hundred and twenty-seven days learning the difference between a thing said by a person and a thing a page said about them and this is the first time he has had to learn the difference between wanting a thing to work and wanting to know how a thing worked.**"

"I do not want it to have worked," said a man of thirty-two, who is on no roster of anything.

"**I know you do not,**" said a girl of seventeen. "**That is why you are the one it is hardest for and I am not going to say anything else about it. I am going to say that the five rooms before this one failed because of what they were, and this room failed because of where it is. A thing nobody can be angry at is the reason nine of us were allowed four hours, and it is the reason we got nothing, and the same shape is in about nine pages in this city and in a board with no names and a chair on a landing and a stone nine miles up a road, and it has not once produced an explanation of anything.**"

It is entered.

---

**And a man of thirty-two said one thing in that room, and it is the whole of what he said in four hours, and a girl of seventeen wrote it out and put the date on it and did not put a name on the page, and a man of thirty-two asked her to put a name on it and she said no in under a second.**

"**A description of a thing is made by people who want the thing to be different from what it is. That is the whole of what a description is for. I have been trying to describe a page for a hundred and twenty-seven days and not one of us has ever once wanted it to have not worked, and that is why five rooms and then a sixth have not got anywhere. It is not because the page is hard. It is because we are the wrong shape for the job, and we have been the wrong shape since the second day of the month after this one.**"

"**Then who is the right shape,**" said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall.

"**I do not know,**" said a man of thirty-two, "**and I have said that in about four rooms in a hundred and twenty-seven days and this is the first time I have said it about myself. And I am not going to look for one. A person who wants a thing to have not worked is a person who knows what it does, and there is nobody in this room who knows what it does, and if there were one he would be a man this city has spent a hundred and twenty-seven days refusing to build, and he is not going to come and sit in a room with no key in it and be asked a question by a docker who cannot read.**"

Nobody said anything for about nineteen seconds.

**And the sentence that came closest was not written down, and the reason is on the page from the second day of the month after this one and has been on it since: a sentence about a page that works is a mechanism, and a mechanism is a diagram, and a diagram of the most dangerous object in this city is a worse thing to have in a hundred and twenty-seven days than nine minutes of nothing.**

---

**And at about the fourth hour and nine minutes of the afternoon a pale field came up in the air in front of about nine people in a room with no key and stood there for about nineteen seconds, and it is the thirty-first thing the wall has said in this city, and a notary of fifty read it out in the local language in about nine seconds, and it was answered in about four seconds, out loud, in the local language, by a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf, and it was not accepted.**

> **A thing that has failed in five rooms may be described by one person, once.**
>
> **The person who describes it is not described by it. The person is not on any roll.**
>
> **The cost is that one person is then the account of it, and every later account of it will be checked against that one person, and that person will not be asked whether they would have it.**
>
> **The cost is entered. There is no further cost.**

"No."

It is entered that she said it in about four seconds, that nobody had asked her anything, and that the count of refusals in the second book a clerk of the Registry keeps at home and has never shown to anybody is **twenty-four**, and that it was twenty-three at the end of the last ten days and twenty-two for a long time before that, and that this is the second time it has moved. **The count at Aurel is thirteen. The difference is eleven, and it was ten for about a hundred days, and it was nine before the first registered refusal, and it has never been made smaller.**

"And what it cost," said a woman of twenty-four, who is on no roll of anything.

"**That I have said it out loud in a room, in the local language, in front of about nine people, and that saying it out loud is a thing that cannot be put back in a drawer, and that what I have said out loud is that I know what a page that ends a document is, and I am a person who knows that, and a person who knows that is a column, and I have spent a hundred and twenty-seven days refusing to put a name on a board and I have just made myself a person who knows something in a room with no key in it.**"

"**And the cost is that in about five weeks the next case goes out of this city and the account of a page that ends a document is going to be whatever an office nine hundred miles away says it is,**" said a man of about thirty-two. "**And what an office says about a page is a page about a district, and a page about a district is a removal with a column for where the person is, and the district is this one, and this time it is the wrong way round, because this time there is nothing in the column at all.**"

"**Yes,**" said a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf. "**That is the cost and I know it and I said no anyway and I would like it entered that I knew it before I said it, because a woman of twenty-four will otherwise say that I did not.**"

It is entered. **And a woman of twenty-four said that she had been going to say that, and that she is glad she was wrong, and that being glad she was wrong is the fourth time in a hundred and twenty-seven days that she has been glad she was wrong, and that a notary of fifty entered that she noticed, and entered that she noticed, and said that it was not modesty.**

---

**And the failure was entered with the date on it, and the count of rooms on the page is six, and a seventh room was wanted at about the sixth hour of the evening and was not held, and the reason was given in about nine seconds.**

"Six," said a girl of seventeen, and wrote it, and put the date on it.

**Six rooms. Six attempts. The page stays shut. Nobody produced a mechanism, nobody showed the page working, nobody reproduced it, and nobody in this city can say why it did what it did, and the one sentence that came closest was not written down, and it will not be written down, and the count of rooms is entered because a city that has failed at a thing five times and has never written the five down will fail a sixth time in the same place, and one that has written them down has still failed a sixth time in the same place, and that is the finding and it is not an improvement.**

---

**And nothing else happened on the thirty-seventh day of the month after this one, and a hearing is in twenty-seven days and there is one hearing and one clock, and about nine people have said out loud that they will stand in it and nobody has been asked whether they will, and nothing has been written down about any of the nine. And a box came up this river on the twenty-sixth day of the month after this one and it is in a room with a door on the second floor of a building over a market and it is not opened and the strap on it is still untied, and a notary of fifty has been asked about it five times in five days and has answered none of the five and has not been asked a sixth time. And a case went down this river on the eighth day of the month after this one with a question in its eighth column and no name in it and it is still on the water. And nine households of a village refused a fourth time on the twenty-second day of the month after this one and the water has not been received for fifty-eight days counted from the sixty-ninth day of this flood and the quarter is gone. And a page in a stone nine miles up a road has a line in it that is now wrong and the household that keeps that stone has never been asked what it thinks. And the measurement column has twenty-four readings and one ruled line left and the twenty-fifth reading has not been taken for forty-two days. And the main breach is closed, dressed, measured and silent and has never said a word, and the Narrow Mark has been opened twice in a hundred and twenty-seven days, and the boards under the chapel have not been lifted since the fortieth day of this flood, and the stone in the arch has said nothing in a hundred and twenty-seven days. And there have been thirty-one things the wall has said in this city and two of them have been refused, and this is the second time the refusals in that book have moved and it moved by one, and the count at Aurel is thirteen, and the wall said one thing today and a person refused it in about four seconds, and a day the wall speaks on is not a silence however hard the day was, and the pattern is thirty-four for thirty-four and stands where it stood this morning.**

**And the last thing of the thirty-seventh day of the month after this one is a page in a book in this city with a date on it and a number on it, and the number is six, and the sixth of the six is a line that says who wanted it to work, and the answer is that nobody did, and a page that ends a document is in a lockable room off the salt store with the door shut, and about nine people have gone out of a room with no key into a Sunday evening, and one of them is a person who knows something and is on nothing, and a hearing is in twenty-seven days.**
