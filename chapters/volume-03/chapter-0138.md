# Chapter 0138 — A Correct Number About a District

On the forty-eighth day of the month after this one, which was the hundred and thirty-eighth day of the flood and a Saturday, two things came up the Reach road in the same cart, and one of them was the shape of an answer and one of them was an answer to nothing, and the finding of the forty-eighth day of the month after this one is that the second one is the thing a hundred and thirty-eight days of this city have been asking about and the first one is only a receipt, and that a correct number about a district arrived six days before a hearing and cannot be stopped by anybody in this city.

**The licence is eighty-eight days old. The undertaking is void and no office has been told. The field was not paid on the first of March, which was the eighty-fifth day of this flood, and that is fifty-three days ago. The fee of eleven ounces of silver is entered as owing with no date for the seventy-ninth time. The black line in his right palm is one hundred and thirty-seven days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch.**

---

**A case went down this river on the eighth day of the month after this one with a question in its eighth column and no name in it, and it is still on the water, and a clerk of that office may not ask a question and may not refuse one, and a person named in a question is not told the question exists, and the question may not be answered here and may not be intercepted and may not be taken back.**

It is forty days on the water. A case that carries a document is eighteen days each way and has been twice. **A case that carries a question is not a case that can be closed, and a case that cannot be closed goes up a desk, and a desk that has a question on it cannot answer it and cannot send it back, and a quarter is about thirteen weeks, and a girl of seventeen wrote that arithmetic out in a column four lines high in a woman's words and not in her own and it is on the page.**

What came up the Reach road in a cart on the forty-eighth day of the month after this one was a receipt for the case of the eighth day. It is four lines. It is not a Crown instrument, it is a carrier's acknowledgement with a seal on it that is not the seal of that office, and it is the ordinary paper of this city.

> **Received at the fourth desk. Carried. Not closed. Return by the quarter of the year next following. No answer is due before then. A case with an eighth column is not a case that can be closed.**

"It says *not a case that can be closed*," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall. "**In a hand. About a question.**"

"**It is correct,**" said a notary of fifty. "**That is what is on the page and I am not going to be the one who says it is not.**"

---

**And the second thing in the cart was a return, and it is a new shape and it is dated, and it is the twelfth, and a girl of seventeen wrote it out in a column four lines high and read it back before anybody had asked her to.**

> **THE TWELFTH SHAPE, and it is dated the forty-fourth day of the month after this one and it came up the Reach road in a cart on the forty-eighth. A return of a district after a flood, made by a district waterboard in the ordinary course, of the number of households, the water, the channels, and the assenting. Local in its making and Crown in its form. It is not the Registry's notice of the twenty-eighth day of this flood and it is not the third instrument of the seventeenth day of the new month and it is not the second answer of the twenty-second and it is not the schedule of crossings of the thirty-first and it is not the fifth thing that office put in a case on the fifty-second day of the new month. It is not the undertaking of the Lantern Concord, which is ours and void. It is not the design of the Quiet Choir, which is not the Crown's. It is not the eighth shape, which came up a river sealed. It is not the ninth shape, a boundary page of the village of Nine Elms, except that it is the same form. It is not the tenth shape, a table in a coat. It is not the eleventh shape, a compact, which is not written down. It is a new shape and it is correct and nobody asked for it.**

---

**And then a man of thirty-two read it out in the local language in a room over a market, in daylight, because nobody else could, and he said before he started that he was going to be the column again and that he was going to do it anyway, and it took about nine minutes and nobody improved on it.**

There were about nine people in the room and the door was shut. It has no names in it anywhere. **It is a return about a number and a number about a number and about two lines of words, and the whole of it is four lines and a heading, and there is not one person's name on it, and that is not a mercy and it is the finding.**

"**It is headed with the district. It gives the district by its name and by a number, and the number is old, and the waterboard has used that number since before any of us were born. The households are nine. The water is not received. The fourth channel is shut and has been since a date that is the twenty-second day of the month after this one. And there is a line that says the district assented, and the word in the assenting line is the word they use when a number of men have been taken.**"

Nobody said anything for about nineteen seconds.

"**The count is nine and the assent is nine and they are the same place,**" he said. "**There is one place for the count and one place for the assent and they are the same place, and a number went in it, and where a number goes in that office the number is the assent. I read that out loud in this room on the twenty-ninth day of the month after this one off a page a hundred and forty years old that a woman of seventy carried in a cart, and it is the same form, and it has been the same form for a hundred and forty years, and somebody at a waterboard filled it in on the forty-fourth day of the month after this one without doing anything wrong.**"

---

**And then he said the rest, and it is on the page, and it is the whole of what he said on the forty-eighth day of the month after this one.**

"**I am the column again. I said on the twenty-ninth day of the month after this one that I am not going to be able to stop, and this is the day I stop pretending that is a thing I might be talked out of. There are no names in this and I read it out anyway, and in about five weeks this page is going to be compared against whatever an office nine hundred miles away says this district is, and there is going to be one man in that comparison and he is standing in this room with his hand flat on a table.**"

"Then do not read it," said a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf.

"**There is a number on that page about nine households, and the fourth channel of a junction is shut, and the only person in four hundred miles who can say what a form is saying instead of what a form is, is me, and about four of you have worked out in a hundred and thirty-eight days that a person who carries a cost is a person who can be asked. So I am going to be asked, and I am going to answer, and that is not the same as being rescued and I would like it entered that I noticed the difference and that it took me nine minutes to notice it.**"

It was entered.

---

**And a notary of fifty said the part that nobody in that room was able to say, and she had it in her hand and she read it out herself, and it took about four minutes, and it is the finding of the forty-eighth day of the month after this one.**

"**A correct number about a district is a district that is described correctly.**"

"**It is a return. It is a form filled in by a waterboard in the ordinary course about a flood.**"

"**It is correct, and it is a return, and both of those are the problem and not the answer.**" She put it down flat. "**Nothing in that page is a lie. Nine households, the water not received, the fourth channel shut, the district assented. Every line of it can be checked by anybody who walks to that junction. And a page that is correct is a page nobody can argue with, and a page nobody can argue with is a page nobody can stop, and in about four months that page is the account of that district and the account of that district is going to say that nine households assented, because that is what is written on it and it is correct.**"

"**It is a count,**" said a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf, in about four seconds, "**and I said it was a count in a room on the thirty-ninth day of the month after this one and I am not going to be the one who says it is not.**"

"**It is a count,**" said the notary, "**and a count of households is a count. And the eight at the foot of a boundary page a hundred and forty years old are not households and were never households, and there is no line in that return for a man who was taken, and there has not been one for a hundred and forty years, and that is not what has happened to them. That is where they are.**"

---

Nobody said anything for about nineteen seconds, and the cart was still outside and the two of them were on the step in the sun.

"**Where they are,**" said a man of thirty-two.

"**In the form,**" said a notary of fifty. "**In a place where a number goes in and the number is the answer. That is a form nine hundred years old and it has not changed and it is not going to, and I have been a notary for twenty-six years and I have never before said a thing like that out loud, and I am entering that I noticed and I am entering that it is not modesty.**"

Both were entered.

---

**And nobody answered the case of the eighth day, and nobody intercepted it and nobody took it back, and a woman of twenty-four wrote the four lines of the receipt into a book that cannot travel with the date on it, and the return itself is on a wall in a converted salt store under oiled cloth beside the roll of public business, and a wall in a room is read by about forty people and is not a column, and a column goes in a case, and a girl of seventeen said that sentence out loud on the thirty-ninth day of the month after this one and a woman who keeps a register said she had been able to say it in about four seconds on the second day of this flood.**

---

**And nothing else happened on the forty-eighth day of the month after this one that anybody has written down, and a hearing is in sixteen days and there is one hearing and one clock, and a correct number about a district arrived today in a cart and cannot be stopped by anybody in this city and is six days from being in front of anybody. And a box is in a room with a door on the second floor of a building over a market and has been there twenty-two days and is not opened and the strap on it is still untied, and a notary of fifty has been asked five times and has answered none of the five. And nine households of a village refused a fourth time on the twenty-second day of the month after this one, which is twenty-six days ago, and the water has not been received for sixty-nine days counted from the sixty-ninth day of this flood, and the date in the chalk on nine doors is not washed. And thirty-nine of forty men turn up under the near gate and have not been paid since the first of March, which is fifty-three days, and one of the forty has not come for twenty-four days and nobody has been told. And the measurement column has twenty-four readings and one ruled line left and the twenty-fifth reading has not been taken for fifty-three days, and six disagreements stand. And the main breach is closed, dressed, measured and silent and has never said a word, and the Narrow Mark has been opened twice and not a third time, and the boards under the chapel have not been lifted since the fortieth day of this flood, and the stone in the arch has said nothing in a hundred and thirty-eight days. And there have been thirty-one things the wall has said in this city and two of them have been refused, and the refusals in the second book a clerk of the Registry keeps at home are twenty-four, and the count at Aurel is thirteen, and the difference is eleven, and it has never been made smaller. And a Saturday on which a correct number about a district came up a road in a cart and nobody asked for it is the forty-fifth silence and the pattern is forty-five for forty-five.**

**And the last thing of the forty-eighth day of the month after this one is a wall in a converted salt store with a correct number on it, and a woman of seventy of that village who has refused four times and has never been asked, and a hearing in sixteen days.**
