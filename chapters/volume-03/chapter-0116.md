# Chapter 0116 — A Case Comes Up a River

On the twenty-sixth day of the month after this one, which was the hundred and sixteenth day of the flood and a Friday, a wooden box with a strap and a wax and a second strap came up this river for the first time in a hundred and forty years, and about nine people stood in front of it in a room with a door and did not open it, and the finding of the twenty-sixth day of the month after this one is not what is in it, and is that a city which has spent a hundred and sixteen days building things nobody can check has just been handed a thing it cannot check and cannot open, and that the protection this city has relied on for a hundred and forty years has arrived on its own account.

**The licence is sixty-six days old. The undertaking is void and no office has been told. The field was not paid on the first of March. The fee of eleven ounces of silver is entered as owing with no date for the fifty-seventh time. The black line in his right palm is one hundred and fifteen days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch.**

---

**And it came up in the middle of the water with the case that went down on the eighth day of the month after this one, and about nine people in this city have said that neither of the two overtook the other, and nobody in this city can prove that, and the proof is not going to arrive, and the whole of the twenty-sixth day of the month after this one is that this city has just been given its first thing in a hundred and forty years that it cannot check, and it got it on purpose, and it did not send for it.**

There were four people on the quay at about the fifth hour of the afternoon, and a woman of fifty-eight who cannot read was one of them, and a woman of thirty-eight who is on no roll at all was one of them, and a man of sixty-eight who has carried nine cases in his life was one of them, and the fourth was a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall.

"**The one going down had our seal on it and the one coming up has theirs,**" said the man of about forty-four. "**And they were abreast in the middle of the channel for the length of about four hundred yards and neither one got in front of the other, and I have watched boats come up this river for eleven years and I have never seen two cases at once, and there is one case a quarter, and there has never been one going up.**"

"How long," said a woman of twenty-four.

"Nobody knows. Nobody in this world knows. That is what a case is."

It is entered that the four gave the account separately over about nine minutes and that the four accounts agree, and that a notary of fifty entered that four agreeing accounts are not a record, and that this is the first time in a hundred and sixteen days that this city has had a thing it could not make a record about.

---

**And the box is a foot and a half long and it is not new, and the strap is a strap and the wax is a wax, and there is a second strap, and the second strap is not tied, and it is a strap that was put on by somebody who expected the box to be opened and reclosed before it was carried in.**

"Same box," said a man of sixty-eight. "**Not the same box as ours. The same kind. I have carried nine of these in my life and there are about nine of them in a hundred and forty years that anybody in this city can remember, and I have never carried one that came up.**"

"The wax is not the wax on ours," said a woman of fifty-eight who cannot read, who had gone down on her hands and had her face four inches off it and had come back up. "**Ours was red and pressed with a thing like a gate. That is a flat one with a line of writing round the edge of it and I could not read the writing because I cannot read.**"

"**Then nobody in this room can read the writing on the seal,**" said a man of thirty-two, who is on no roster of anything, "**except me, and I am not going to. The thing I can do is read. The last time I read a thing in this city in a language nobody else in the room had, nine hundred miles away somebody used it against a village that did not know it existed. The wax stays shut.**"

---

**And it is a new shape, and it is dated, and it goes on the list beside the other seven and does not go into any of them, and a girl of seventeen wrote it out in a book in a column four lines high and read it back before anybody had asked her to, and a man of sixty-eight read the face of the box out loud to her because she cannot read the local hand, and a man of thirty-two read the four lines under the seal because a form is not a face, and it is entered that nobody read the writing round the edge of the wax.**

"The face of it is not the inside of it," said the man of sixty-eight. "**I have carried nine of these and I have never once opened one I was not told to open. There is a district on this one and there is a date, and the date is the nineteenth day of the new month, which was the forty-fourth day of this flood, and there is a flat seal with a line of writing round the edge of it, and under the seal there are four lines in a form that is not a form anybody in this city has seen, and the four lines say that the district shall name one person to be heard at the hearing, and that where no person is named the district shall be described by a number.**"

"Then that is eight," said the girl of seventeen, and wrote it, and stopped with the chalk up.

"That is the eighth shape out of that office into this city, and it is not the notice of the twenty-eighth day of this flood, and it is not the third instrument of the seventeenth day of the new month, and it is not the second answer of the twenty-second, and it is not the schedule of crossings of the thirty-first with a fee of eleven ounces of silver on it, and it is not the fifth thing that office put in a case on the fifty-second day of the new month, which is a fourth shape with one more column and is a different count. The undertaking of the Lantern Concord is a seventh shape and it is ours and it is void and it is not withdrawn. **The design of the Quiet Choir is a sixth shape and it is not the Crown's and it is under a different heading and it is not this.**"

"And the direction of the nineteenth day of the new month is the eighth," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall, "**and it is the only one of the eight that came up.**"

"It is the only one of the eight that came up," said a girl of seventeen, "**and it is the first thing in a hundred and forty years that has come up, and I want the date on it and I want the wax described and I do not want the inside of it, and I am not going to be the person who finds out what is in it, and if I am asked to be I will say no in under a second and it will be in the book.**"

"**I did the face,**" said a man of sixty-eight. "**A face is a thing a carrier reads and a carrier reads it out to the room. The four lines under the seal are in a form and a form is not a face and I could not have read them in a hundred years of carrying them, and the man of thirty-two read them because they are in a form. There is writing round the edge of the seal and nobody in that room read it, and I have carried nine of these and I have never once read one of those, and I would like that entered with the date on it.**"

It was entered. **And a man of thirty-two said four seconds later that the reason he had not read the wax was that the wax is not addressed to anybody, it is a mark somebody pressed, and that he is not going to be the man in this city who reads a mark somebody pressed, and that he had said the same thing once already that afternoon and was not going to say it a third time, and that a woman of twenty-four asked him whether it had been twice and that he said it had.**

---

**And the not-opening was settled in about four minutes, and it was the easiest thing anybody in this city has done in a hundred and sixteen days, and about four people noticed how easy it was and did not like it.**

"There is no procedure," said a notary of fifty, in the flat voice. "**That office has never in a hundred and forty years sent this city a thing it meant to be opened on arrival, and there is no instrument in four hundred miles that says what a receiving district does with a case it was not sent a form for. If I open this today I have read it, and if I have read it somebody nine hundred miles away can ask me what is in it, and then this box is a document and not a room. And a document is a thing an office can act on. A case that is opened early is a case that has been read.**"

"Can it be kept," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall.

"**In a room with a door. That is the whole of what I know how to do with it, and I have done it for twenty-six years with about nine hundred other things, and about nine hundred of those nine hundred are in a building nine hundred miles away and I am not the notary of any of them.**"

---

**And at about the fourth hour and nine minutes of the afternoon a pale field came up in the air in front of about nine people and stood there for about nineteen seconds, and it is the thirtieth thing the wall has said in this city, and it was answered in about four seconds, out loud, in the local language, by a notary of fifty, and it was not refused, and the count of refusals in the second book a clerk of the Registry keeps at home and has never shown to anybody is twenty-three and did not move.**

> **A thing that has come up a river may be described by any person who has seen it. The person who describes it is not described by it. The person is not on any roll.**
>
> **The cost is that the description is the only account of the thing that will exist. The cost is entered. There is no further cost.**

"Accepted," said Sera Vail, in about four seconds. "**And I want it entered that I accepted a thing in about four seconds for the third time in a hundred and sixteen days and that the first two moved nothing and that I did not think about this one at all, and that I have entered that I did not think about it, and that a woman of twenty-four says that not thinking about it is the finding and not the excuse.**"

"**It moved nothing here either,**" said a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf. "**Nothing is opened and nothing is read and any number inside that box is the number it was at noon. I have been on this wharf eleven years and I did not believe this was going to happen in my working time, and I was wrong about that.**"

---

**And the finding of the twenty-sixth day of the month after this one was made at about the seventh hour of the evening by a man of thirty-two who is on no roster of anything, and about four people in that room had got there first and let him say it.**

"**For a hundred and forty years the protection of this city has been that nothing comes back,**" he said. "**A case went down four times a year and a count came back, and the count was the emptiest thing anybody could put in a case. And in a hundred and sixteen days about nine of us have built about nine things that cannot be checked. A page nobody can check. A stone nine miles up a road. A board with no names. A form whose fifth thing is a question and not a duty. A house with a date on it that nobody made keep the door open. They are all the same shape, and the shape is a thing nobody can be angry at, and a thing nobody can be angry at is also a thing nobody can be stopped by, and we found that out about four days ago and we wrote it down.**"

"And this one cannot be checked either," said a woman of twenty-four.

"**This one cannot be checked and it cannot be opened, and that is not the same as the other nine, and the difference is that the other nine are ours.**"

Nobody said anything for about nineteen seconds.

"**The other nine we made, in rooms, in daylight, in front of about two hundred and forty people, and every one of them has a person in it who can be angry, and that is why they work and why they do not travel. This came up a river. Somebody nine hundred miles away has a hand on it and that hand has never seen this city, and the box is on this side of the water and the wax is on the outside and the inside is nine hundred miles away in a form we have never seen. We cannot check it. We cannot open it. We cannot refuse it, because a refusal is an answer and an answer is a description and a description is a removal with a column for where the person is, and a district that is removed is exactly the thing that office would like.**"

"So we stand in front of it," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall.

"**We stand in front of it for thirty-eight days, and then in about eight weeks nine of us stand in a room and say what it says and what it does not say, and that is the whole of the instrument. And I want it entered that the protection this city has relied on since before anybody alive in it was born has just come up a river on its own account, in a sealed box, in a form we cannot read, and that it is on our side of the water, and that nobody in this city sent for it.**"

It was entered. **And a notary of fifty entered, on the twenty-sixth day of the month after this one, that she would like to be asked before it is opened, and entered the reason, and the reason is that she has been the notary of about nine hundred things and that about nine hundred of them were somebody else's sentence and that she would like to be asked in a room, in daylight, with a date on it, by a person, before this one.**

---

**And a hearing is in thirty-eight days and there is one hearing and one clock, and the number nine is on a page with a date on it and nobody at the top of it, and it is not a roll. And the box is in a room with a door and is not opened, and nobody has read the writing on the wax and one person could have and did not. And a case that went down this river on the eighth day of the month after this one with a question in its eighth column is still on the water and may not be intercepted, answered or taken back, and what comes back from it will be a correct number about a district with eight people not in it. And the fee of eleven ounces of silver is owing with no date for the fifty-seventh time, and the licence is sixty-six days old, and the seat of holder of the pull is vacant and the instrument says nobody. And the boards under the chapel have not been lifted since the fortieth day of this flood and the ground is a scar, and the stone in the arch said nothing today. And the wall said one thing today and it was answered in four seconds by a person and not refused, and the count of refusals is twenty-three, and a day the wall speaks on is not a silence, and the pattern is twenty-four for twenty-four and stands where it stood this morning.**

**And the last thing of the twenty-sixth day of the month after this one is a wooden box with a strap and a wax and a second strap that is not tied, standing on a floor in a room with a door, and nobody in this city knows what is in it, and nobody in this city has opened it, and for a hundred and forty years the reason was that nothing came back, and the reason tonight is that something did.**
