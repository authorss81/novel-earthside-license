# Chapter 0800 — A Hand On The Flap

The man of about thirty-one stood at the foot of that stair with his satchel under his arm and his right hand resting on the flap. Rain had got into the stairwell and the bottom step shone.

It was the fourth hour on the four hundred and eighty-fifth day of the Bare Month, a Wednesday. The market had not filled. The door at his back showed its chalk day outward, with the bar home in its sockets within when it stood shut.

He climbed three steps and stopped, with his hand still on the flap. Light from the door fell across the leather and the stitching along its edge. He stood there a while without going up and without going back, his thumb pressed to the corner where the flap meets the strap.

People went past on the paving behind him without looking. The keeper came down the flags with the book under her arm and passed him on her way up, and he stepped aside to let her by with his hand never leaving the leather. The stair stayed quiet above.

He lifted his thumb and set it down again in the same place. The flap stayed down as it had stayed down on every morning of this flood. There was no way of telling from the leather, from his hand, or from the light whether it had been done before.
