# Chapter 0440 — The Sheet Comes Back On A Sunday, And One Of The Four Is Not Where It Went Out

The hundred and twenty-fifth day of the Bare Month was a Sunday, and the light came off the water in a market at the bottom of this city and lay along the flags and did not move, and at about the eighth hour a man of about thirty-one who carries things for a living came up nine steps into a room over that market with a satchel with a flap and put it on the table and did not open it, and about nine people were in that room and had all of them been in it before. The hundred and twenty-fifth day of the Bare Month is day 440, and a sheet with a heading on it and four places ruled under the heading came up that road in that satchel, and a barrow came down the same road the same morning with a hurdle of hazel rods in the bottom of it that a man made and a bar across the top of it that he replaced, and neither of those two things had anything to do with the other.

---

She opened the satchel herself and turned the sheet out on the table in the light, and the four marks were in the second of the four places, one above the other, and three of them were where they had been and one of them was not. The first of the four places was empty and the fourth of the four places was empty and the third of the four places had a crease pressed down the length of it with a bone and nothing in it at all, and the fold down the third was where it had been pressed before it ever came up this road.

Nobody in that room said one word about it for about as long as it takes to fill a pail.

---

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board was at the wall end of the bench and he looked at the four marks in the second of the four places for a while and then he said one thing, and what he said was about the paper and not about the four.

"**That is the same sheet. That is the same four marks and the same crease and the same heading, and one of the four is not in the place it went out in, and I am not able to tell you which of the four it is and neither is anybody else in this room, including the four of them who put them there, because one of those four read his out loud in this room and did not say it was his.**"

Nobody in that room asked him how he knew it was not the same one of the four. The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register said one thing from the other side of the table and she said it in the same voice she reads four places out loud in.

"Then we are not going to know which one it is, and there is no page in this city I can put that on, and I am not going to have this room spend a morning on it, so you can stop looking at it."

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board took his slate off his knee and put it down on the table beside the sheet and left it there. Nobody in that room asked anybody anything. Nobody in that room said one word about who, and nobody in that room went down nine steps to say one word to anybody about it, and about four people in that room have worked out since that there is no page anywhere in four hundred miles on which a thing like that can be entered, and that a sheet that comes back different is the ordinary end of a sheet and is not a thing anybody in this city is going to be thanked or blamed for.

---

Then nothing happened for about an hour, and that is the whole of what happened in that room for about an hour, and a man who carries things for a living sat down on the bench by the door and did not say where he had had it or how long he had had it or who had handed it to him, and nobody asked him.

A woman near the step said one thing into the middle of the room, and she said it to nobody.

"**There is one person in this city who would have been able to tell this room something about that page, and she is not in this room and she has not been in it since the morning she was asked where her line was, and none of us is going to go and ask her, and that was settled in here without a word being said on it.**"

Nobody in that room agreed with her out loud and nobody in that room disagreed with her out loud, and nobody in that room has been asked anything since, and the one person in this city who would have been able to say something about that page is not going to be asked about it by anybody, and the reason is not that she would not answer.

---

The sheet lay flat on the table in that room through the rest of that Sunday with four lines in the second of the four places and nothing in the third of them, and nobody wrote anything on it and nobody entered a word about it in the book that is shut on the shelf behind that table, whose third column has been empty on purpose and is empty on purpose now, and the second slate was where it lies with a date on it and nothing under the date and was not picked up and was not turned over and was not written on. The day on the door was the hundred and twenty-fifth day of the Bare Month and nothing was said about that day by anybody, and the one thing that happened on it is that a page came back and one of the four lines on it was not where it had gone out.
