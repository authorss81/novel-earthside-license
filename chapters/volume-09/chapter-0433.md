# Chapter 0433 — Nine Steps Down Into A Market, And Two People With No Date On Anything

The hundred and eighteenth day of the Bare Month was a Sunday and the light came off the water along the flags of a market at the bottom of this city and lay there and did not move all morning, and about nine people went up the nine steps above that market and about four of them came down again before the middle of the day. A stall board stood on its string at the market end with the ordinary line on it in chalk, a barrow stood at the end of that stall with nothing in it, and a woman who had not been in that market since the morning the page came up the road came down the nine steps of the building above it at about the ninth hour with her hands empty.

---

She had come down nine steps and into a market and there was nothing in that market about any of the things she says out loud in a room, and about four people who came through that market before the tenth hour worked out since that the woman who keeps the register in the room over that market had come down into the middle of a market on a Sunday morning and had bought nothing.

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall had the string board up already and the cord coiled beside it and his hands in his sleeves, and he did not say one word about her being there, and neither did she.

---

"You have not been down here in a long while."

"**I have not been down here since the page came up the road, and I have not been in that room on a morning that was not a working morning either, and today is a Sunday, and that is the whole of my reason and I am not going to give you a better one.**"

He put the string board straight on its string. Nobody in that market thanked either of them for saying anything, and a woman who came through it and bought about two feet of the cord in that quarter of an hour did not know that either of those two people had a room over her head with a date in chalk on its door, and the cord went in a bag and the bag went out of that market and that was the whole of what that purchase was.

---

"Do you want to be up there on Friday."

"**I will be up there on Friday because I have never once not been up there, and I have not been asked whether I want to be, and I would like it said that I have noticed that nobody asks me whether I want to be anything.**"

That is the whole of what was said at that stall front in about a quarter of an hour. The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board came through that market at about the eleventh hour the way he comes through it every morning, on his own way to somewhere, and he did not stop at the end of that stall, and he had not stopped at the end of that stall on any morning of this flood, and about four people in that market have counted that he has not, and the counting is the whole of what that market has done about the room over it.

---

Then she went back up the nine steps and stood in the doorway for about as long as it takes to count nine, and the door behind her had the hundred and eighteenth day of the Bare Month on it in chalk and nothing above it, and she did not go in for the rest of that morning, and the market went on under her the whole time. The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall did not watch her go up and about four people in that market have worked out since that he did not watch her go up, and that is the whole of what either of them got out of a Sunday, and that neither of them would want it called that.
