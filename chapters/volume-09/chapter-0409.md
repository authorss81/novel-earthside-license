# Chapter 0409 — There Is No Form In Four Hundred Miles For Answering One

A room over a market had the ninety-fourth day of the Bare Month in chalk on its door at about the seventh hour, a Thursday, and the light came in over the step at the back with no name on it and lay along a bench and did not reach the table, and what was under about four pairs of hands that day was a sheet with four places ruled under a heading and a crease pressed down the third of them, a book with three columns in it, a lump of chalk, and about nine people who had all been in that room in the last four days and none of whom was sent.

---

She had told them on Sunday that she was not going to say what the third place was for until she had found out, and it was Thursday, and she had not found out, and this was the fifth morning of it counting the one she said it on, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman who says a thing out loud in a room in this city has put a date on it by saying it, and a room can get used to a thing it is not going to be told.

The sheet was on the table where she had left it on Sunday. The crease pressed down the third of the four places had not been undone, and it is the only thing on that page anybody has ever touched, and a crease pressed with a bone in a building nine hundred miles off is not an entry and is not going to become one.

Nobody went near the second slate on the shelf behind the table. It was not picked up and it was not turned over and it was not written on, and the date on it is the only thing on it and it has been the only thing on it for fifty days.

---

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall was at the back with his hand flat on his own inside breast pocket, and about four people in that room have watched a woman not ask him for a piece of chalk and have worked out since that a man with a piece of chalk in his pocket is a man everybody in the room is looking at, and that on Thursday the whole of the room looked at him and the third place on the sheet did not move.

"Two of us have been in this room every day this week."

"**Two of us have been in this room every day this week because there is nothing else to be in, and I have not been the reason anybody started, and I am not going to be, and I have a piece of chalk in this pocket and I am not going to take it out in a room that is waiting for a man to take it out.**"

Nobody in that room said anything about the chalk. He has asked in advance, in a lane, not to be thanked, and a man who has asked that is not thanked, and a man who has a piece of chalk and will not use it in a room that wants a heading is doing the only thing in that room that is not being done by accident.

---

Then she said the thing, and she said the cost first, out loud, standing, in front of about nine people, with her hands on the edge of the table on either side of a page that has a place for a person in it.

"The cost of saying this in this room is that from today nobody in here can be surprised by it, and about four of you are going to be angry with me for saying it, and I would rather be angry with than surprised."

Nobody in that room thanked her and nobody argued with her, and she put her hands back on the table flat on either side of the sheet.

"I am not going to be the person who decides what the third place on that sheet is. Somebody is going to have to, and it is not going to be me, and I am saying so in this room on a day this month so that nobody in it can say afterwards that they did not know it was coming."

She did not name the day. The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall spoke from the back of the room without getting up and without turning round, and about four people in that room worked out what the woman had done, because a woman who says a day this month in a room that has a date in chalk on the door every morning is describing a room on one particular morning, and she had not said which one, and she was not going to say which one, and a decision that is announced in a room before it is taken is the only instrument this world has that is older than a page.

"**Then somebody in this room is going to decide it and it is not going to be you, and about nine of us have been in this room every day this week and not one of us knew that was coming until this morning.**"

"You were not going to take it out."

"**I am not going to take it out, and I have not taken it out since the forty-third day of the Short Month, and whoever it is can have it off me, and I would like it said out loud in this room that a man who has said a thing twice is not going to say it a third time.**"

Nobody in that room thanked him for either, and nobody is going to. The woman who reads forms out loud and the man who writes a line on a board for a living have said the same thing out loud on the same morning, which is that they are not the one, and that neither of them is going to say what the other one is for either.

---

The third of the four places is the only one of the four that cannot be filled in by anybody except the person it is about, and that is not an accident of the printing.

A page gets answered two ways in four hundred miles and there are only two. It gets sent back. Or it gets filled in. And both of those need a hand that is not the hand of the person the page is about, and a page with a place for a person in it cannot be sent back and cannot be filled in at all, because the only person who can put themselves there is the person who would then be there, and that is the whole of what a page like that is, and it is not a defect in the printing and it is not a trick and it is not a thing anybody in four hundred miles did on purpose to anybody.

Nobody in that room said that. About four people in that room had worked out that it was true since Sunday morning and not one of them had said it out loud, and a woman who keeps a public register is not a woman who takes a thing four other people have worked out and says it for them, and the third column of that book has been empty on purpose for fifty days by her own decision made out loud in that room, and she has never been asked about it and is not going to be.

---

She shut the book at about the ninth hour and it went back on the shelf it lives on, and the third column was empty and the second column was empty and the first column has a day in it and nothing under the day, and that is the whole of what is in that book and it is the whole of what has been entered in it.

The sheet went down onto the table flat with the crease pressed down its third column and nothing in it, and it did not go anywhere and it did not get sent and it is going to have to go somewhere on a date, and about four people in that room have worked out since that there is no rule in four hundred miles about where a sheet with a person in it goes, and that the only thing left in the world to carry one is somebody's own two hands and a road.
