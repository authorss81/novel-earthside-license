# Chapter 0418 — Two Men At The Two Ends Of One Bench

A room over a market had the hundred and third day of the Bare Month in chalk on its door at about the seventh hour, a Saturday, and the market underneath had got going early and the sound of it came up through the boards of the floor the whole morning, and what was under about four pairs of hands that morning was a length of new rope over the back of a chair, a piece of chalk in an inside breast pocket, a sheet of paper on a table with four places ruled under a heading on it, and about nine people who had all been in that room before and none of whom was sent.

---

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall came up the nine steps at about the seventh hour and sat down at the back where he has sat down at the back every morning since before that sheet came up the road, and the man of about fifty-seven was at the end of the bench with the length of new rope over the back of his chair, and the two of them had been in that room on about the same number of mornings and had never once had one sentence between them.

Nobody asked the man of about fifty-seven anything. He was not asked on the day a woman at the other end of the same bench said one of the four lines on a page was going to be hers, and he was not asked on the day a man worked out that the page came out of a fold on the third of the four places, and he was not asked on the day a woman counted three of the four places out loud and said the fourth could not be done by anybody in the room, and about four people in that room have worked out since that this is the third week and it is not an accident and it is not anybody's kindness.

He said it himself, at about the ninth hour, to the end of the bench the man of about thirty-four was sitting at, and it was four sentences and none of them was about a fence.

"**I have not been asked one question in this flood and I am not going to be, and there is a first thing I could say to anybody who asked me and I am not going to say it, and I have said out loud that I am not going to say it so that everybody in this room knows there is one and nobody can say afterwards that I led anybody up to it.**"

Nobody in that room said anything. The register-keeper had her hand flat on the table on the side of the sheet that is away from the crease and did not look up, and a man who tells a room there is a thing he is not going to say has just made it the only thing in that room anybody could ask him about, and nobody is going to ask him about it, and the not-asking is now the whole of what that man has.

---

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall answered it, and he did not turn round, and he was not thanked for answering it and did not ask to be.

"**Then that is the arrangement, and I have said it back to you because you are the only man in this room who has been asked nothing as often as I have, and I am not going to be the one who asks, and I have a piece of chalk in this pocket that has not been out since the forty-third day of the Short Month, which is day two hundred and ninety-eight of this flood, and I am not taking it out this morning or any other morning.**"

Nobody in that room argued with that either, and nobody thanked him, and the man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall has asked in advance, in a lane, not to be thanked, and a man who has asked that is not thanked.

The length of new rope stayed over the back of the chair it was on and it was not used and it is not going to be used. Nobody in that room asked where it came from or what it was for or how long it was, and about four people in that room have worked out since that there are two lengths of rope in this flood that people talk about and they are not the same rope and nobody in that room has ever joined them and nobody is going to.

---

The road that goes out of this city towards the four hundred miles is the only road anybody in this city can be shown a person on, and nobody in this city has ever been shown one person on it. Three went down it on the three hundred and ninety-first day of this flood, a Sunday, one each with a barrow, and a party of four is four, and the fourth of the four is not on that road and has not been on it since and is not going to be, and about four people in that room have worked out since that the reason this city cannot be shown one person on that road is that a road four hundred miles long with three people on it is the only thing in this flood that a return cannot be filled in from, and that this room is a room of about nine people and every one of them has been told what is on that page and none of them is on that road.

The sheet stayed on the table with a crease pressed down the third of the four places under its heading and nothing in any of the four, and the two men at the two ends of the bench had one sentence each and neither of them was about the thing either of them has, and the fence along the top of that shut road out of this city was still sixteen posts and eleven withies and no seventeenth and no twelfth. The length of new rope was over the back of the chair it had been on all morning, and about nine people went down nine steps by the fourth hour with nothing written on anything and nothing asked.
