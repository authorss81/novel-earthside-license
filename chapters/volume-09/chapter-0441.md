# Chapter 0441 — Nobody In This City Knows When The Cart Comes Round Again

The hundred and twenty-sixth day of the Bare Month was on the door of a room over a market in chalk before the light was off the low ground. About nine people were in it by the time the stair had been up four times and down four times. The sheet lay flat on the table where it had lain since the Sunday it came back, and the first thing that happened in that room that morning was a hand put down flat on the wood beside it and left there.

---

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board came in at about the tenth hour with his own board under his arm. He put his slate flat on his knee under his right hand and asked the question that was in the room. It was a fair one, and nobody in that room had asked it.

"**It went out on a Friday and it came back on a Sunday and both of those were on a round, and the next one comes round on its own day and not on anybody's, and there is nobody in this city that I know of who knows what day that is.**"

Nobody answered him straight away. A barrow went past on the flags under the step at the back. The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall had his own hand shut flat over the inside breast pocket of his coat and did not turn round. The register was on the shelf where it lives, and a second slate was on the shelf behind it with a date on it and nothing under the date, and nobody went near the shelf.

---

Then a woman near the step said the thing that everybody in that room had been carrying since the Sunday, and she said it to the middle of the floor.

"**There is not one page in four hundred miles that tells a city when its own cart is going out, and I have been up and down this market on two feet since the water came asking four people who sell things, and not one of them knows and not one of them is going to, and I am not going to go back down those steps and ask any of them a second time.**"

Nobody thanked her for that. She has spent the week walking round a market asking strangers about a cart, and that is the only shape asking anybody anything has been able to take in this flood. She is the only person in this city doing it. She has stopped doing it.

---

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board said the other half of it, and said it flat, and to the table.

"I do not need the day. I have been in this room every working morning since that page came up the road, and there has not been one morning in it where anybody could have put anything on that table. I would not know what to do with the day if it were written on a slate and handed to me at the door."

"Then the day is not the thing."

"**The day was never the thing. What a page does is that it is on a table on a morning and it is not on a table on a morning, and everything anybody in this city has done about that page in this flood has been about the second of those two things and not about the first.**"

Nobody in that room improved on what he said, and nobody in that room thanked him for it. A man who writes nineteen rates on a board of his own for four markets has been thanked in this room for none of the things he has said out loud in it.

---

Adrian Vale was in that room from the seventh hour with his hand on the sheet. He read the first of the four places and the second of them twice and the fourth of them. He did not read the third of them, and he said nothing at all that morning. Nobody asked him anything, and nobody thanked him for being in it. A man who reads pages has no standing whatever in a room that is waiting for a cart, and about four people in that room have worked that out since.

---

The sheet went back into the satchel it came up the road in, and the satchel went behind the table where it had been put on the Sunday. The room went on the way a room goes on. The day on the door was the hundred and twenty-sixth day of the Bare Month, and about nine people went down nine steps by the fourth hour. A page that has been up a road and come back is going to have to go up it again, and there is nothing in four hundred miles that says when, and nobody was sent for.
