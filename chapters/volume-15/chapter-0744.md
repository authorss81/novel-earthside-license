# Chapter 0744 — A Length Of New Rope

The man of about fifty-seven was standing in his own doorway with his own two hands flat on his own knees. Behind him in his room there is a length of new rope over the back of a chair at the far end of a bench. He has not taken it up at any hour of this flood and nobody in this city has ever asked him what it is for.

It was about the fourth hour on the four hundred and twenty-ninth day of the Bare Month, a Wednesday, out past the last named house, where the road has been shut for the whole of this flood, and nobody in this city can be shown one person standing on it.

That rope is new and it has lain over the back of that chair every morning of this flood, and no hand of his and no hand of anybody else's has gone near it. It has not been cut and it has not been taken up and it has not been lifted off the chair, and it was not going to be at any hour of this day either.

Along the top of that shut road there is a fence, and it is sixteen willow posts with eleven withies bound onto them with the same cord, and that is the whole of what is in it. Nothing has been added to it and nothing taken from it, no post in it has moved, and nobody in this city has gone along that fence with a thing to measure it and nobody in this city is going to.

---

At about the sixth hour the man of about sixty came up the shut road with his own handcart behind him, the hazel mallet with its head bound in cord lying along the side of that cart under a twist of sacking and a coil of rope on the other side of it. That coil has been used and it is not the length of new rope behind that doorway. He went on up that road past the doorway and did not stop at it and did not say one word to the man standing in it.

At about the seventh hour the woman of about fifty-two came up the shut road on foot, because there is no other way at that end of that row of houses to get to the far end of it, and she stopped in front of the doorway and put her hands down at the sides of her dress and looked past his shoulder at the back of the chair.

"**the rope behind you is new. Nobody in this city has ever asked you what it is for and I am not going to ask you what it is for either, and I am only saying that it is new, and I do not know what that makes it and you do not have to tell me.**"

Nobody on that road agreed with her about that, nobody argued with her about it, and nobody thanked her for saying it out loud. The man of about fifty-seven looked at her and then looked down at his hands where they rested on his knees, and he did not say that it was new and he did not say that it was not new, and nobody asked him where he had come from.

---

At about the ninth hour the man of about sixty came back down the shut road with his own two hands on the shafts of that handcart and passed the front of the doorway and said nothing to the man in it, and the man in it said nothing back.

At about the tenth hour the woman of about fifty-two turned round at the near end of the road and went back down it on foot, and from the top of the shut road she could still see the front of the doorway and the two hands of the man standing in it on his own knees, because the doorway faces the road and he had not gone in.
