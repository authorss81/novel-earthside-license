# Chapter 0732 — A Man Of About Thirty-Four Alone In His Own Row Of Trestles And Boards At The Front Of His Own Stall From The Fourth Hour To The Tenth, A Piece Of Chalk In An Inside Breast Pocket Against His Own Chest That Does Not Come Out At Any Hour Of That Day, And The Fact That Nobody In That Market Row Has Asked Him What He Is Going To Do And That He Has Told Nobody

The man of about thirty-four had his own right hand flat on the edge of his own trestle and his own left hand down at the side of his own coat with the flat of it against the cloth over that pocket, and he kept it there most of that morning without taking anything out of it or putting anything into it.

It was about the fourth hour on the four hundred and seventeenth day of the Bare Month, a Friday, at that end of that market, where the thing a person walks along is the line of trestles and boards, and where the ground at the foot of the stair at the back of that building is a different thing altogether and gets no part of that morning, and one word stands for both of them in this city and everybody standing on either of them knows which one he is standing on.

His own board stands face in against his own trestle with the bare back of it turned out into that row of trestles and boards behind his own shoulder, and anybody coming along that row could put an arm out and touch it. There is one piece of chalk in an inside breast pocket against his own chest and it is not out of it. It came out of that pocket once in this flood and went back into it unused and it has not been out of it at any hour since, and there is a second piece of chalk in this city at the far end of that row in a box on a step whose lid does not shut, and the two of them are not the same chalk and have never been in one hand at any hour of this flood and are not in one hand at any hour of that day.

Nobody in this city has ever asked that man what he decided in a room over that market on a morning that is not one of the fifty days anybody has kept, and nobody has asked him what he has said he is going to do about it, and nobody has asked him since. He said out loud in that room that he was going to do something about it, and he named what that would cost him in his own mouth, and he did not tell that room which one person in it he was going to ask and he has not told anybody since and he is not going to.

---

He stood at his own front from the fourth hour to the tenth hour and about four people came along that row of trestles and boards in the ordinary course of it, and not one of them stopped at his front and not one of them said one word to him, and there was nobody standing near enough to that front to have taken all of anything he might have said there.

The man of about twenty-seven came along that row of trestles and boards at about the sixth hour with his own bucket in his own hand and went past his front without slowing down and without turning his own head, and Adrian Vale was not in that market row at any hour of that day and was not in that market at all, and no hand in that market row was on that pocket at any hour of it.

---

At about the ninth hour he took his own left hand off the cloth over that pocket and put both his own hands flat on the edge of his own trestle on either side of his own goods, and he counted out of his own hand what was lying in it onto the edge of his own board and pushed it back off again with the flat of his own thumb, and he did that twice, and there was no figure on that board in this city at any hour of that day and none has been put on it and the board is stale and it is not blank.

The piece of chalk was in that pocket at every hour of that day and it came out of it at no hour of that day, and that man took his own two hands off the edge of that trestle and put them back into his own sleeves and stood at his own front with his own arms across them until the tenth hour and told nobody one word about what he is going to do.
