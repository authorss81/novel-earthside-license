# Chapter 0720 — A Question Going Round That Market Row For About An Hour, Put Four Times And Answered Four Times By Somebody Saying They Do Not Know, And Not Put To The Woman Who Keeps The Book Because She Was Standing At A Bench In A Room Over That Market And Not In That Market Row At Any Hour Of That Morning

The man of about thirty-four had his own right hand on the top of his own trestle and he pushed the board standing face in against that trestle a finger's breadth further back with the heel of that hand, and the bare back of it came a finger's breadth further out into that market row, and the piece of chalk in his own inside breast pocket did not come out of it at any hour of that day.

It was about the fourth hour on the four hundred and fifth day of the Bare Month, a Sunday, at that end of that market, where the thing a person walks along is the line of trestles and boards and not a piece of ground, and the ground at the foot of the stair at the back of that building is somewhere else altogether, and the one word that stands for both of them in this city is never given a sense by anybody using it.

There was a place at that end of that market where two trestles used to stand side by side and where the ground has been empty and wet since before the water went down, and somebody in that row wanted to know what was going to be done with it, and wanted to know it out loud, and that question went along that market row for about an hour.

Whichever of them it was that first asked it got up off his own seat and put his own two hands flat on the top of his own board and stood there while it was being put to him. Nobody in that row put a hand on that man while he was standing there. The man of about thirty-four kept the flat of his own right hand on his own boards and did not take it off them.

---

It was put the first time at about the fifth hour to a man with a board under his own arm, and he got up off his own seat and put his own two hands on the top of that board and stood there while he asked it, and the man of about thirty-four put the flat of his own right hand on his own boards and did not take it off them while the whole of it was being put and answered.

"**I do not know what is going to happen to that ground and I have never been told, and I have nineteen rates on this board and not one of them is about that ground, and I have got a stall of my own to open.**"

It was put the second time at about the sixth hour at a trestle along, and the woman at that trestle put her own two hands flat on her own boards and looked along that market row for a while before she answered it.

"**I do not know, and I have been at this trestle every day of this flood, and nobody has come and stood where you are standing and told me one thing about it.**"

It was put the third time at about the seventh hour at the front of the stall two stalls along, and the man of about thirty-four took his own hand off the top of his own trestle.

"**I do not know what is going to be done with it, and I have stood at the front of my own stall with about four people a day going past the back of that building and not one of them has ever told me one thing about anything, and I am not going to be asked about that ground by anybody and I would not answer it if I were.**"

It was put the fourth time at about the eighth hour at a trestle two along the other way, and the man there said that he did not know and said that he had a barrow to get back to, and about four people a day went past that end of that market in the ordinary course while all of that was going on and about four of them caught the whole of it and about four of them did not catch one word, and nobody stopped.

---

It was not put to the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register, and the reason is that she was not standing in that market row at any hour of that morning. She was in that room over that market at the near end of that bench the whole of that morning with the register open in front of her, and the second slate stood on the shelf behind that register with a day cut across the head of it and nothing whatever under that day, and the middle column of that register held five figures with a day entered against each of them and none of the five struck, and the column on the right of that page carried one word near the head of it in a hand that is not hers, and no hand but her own went near that shelf at any hour of that day.

Nobody in that row asked anybody why that question was not put to her and nobody in that room said one word to her about it, and the question was asked four times in that market row and answered four times by somebody saying they did not know, and it was asked and answered and it was not put to the one person in this city who keeps a book.

---

That question was still going round that market row at the tenth hour, and nobody in that market had stopped it and nobody in that market had answered it, and it had not been put to the woman who keeps that book, and that was not because anybody in that row had decided it and not because anybody in that row was thanked for it.
