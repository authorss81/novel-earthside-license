# Chapter 0730 — A Shelf Along The Wall At The Far Side Of A Room Over A Market At The Height Of A Person's Shoulder, And A Sheet On It With A Heading And One Place Ruled Under That Heading And A Figure In That One Place, And Four Sheets Of That One Shape That Have Come Through This City And One Of Them Still In It, And Seven Documents In All And Not Going To Be An Eighth

The woman of about twenty-nine had the flat of her own two hands on the wood of that bench at the near end of it and then she got up off that bench and went to the shelf along the wall at the far side of that room and put the flat of her own right hand on the front edge of that shelf at the height of a person's shoulder, and she did not take anything off it.

It was about the fourth hour on the four hundred and fifteenth day of the Bare Month, a Wednesday, in that room over that market, and about nine people were in it, and the wall along the far side of that room said nothing at any hour of that day, and there is nothing on that wall, and nothing has ever been put on it by anybody at any hour.

That shelf stands at the height of a person's shoulder and that sheet is the only thing on it. It is a sheet with a heading and one place ruled under that heading, and the one place holds a figure, and the figure is not printed here. It has been standing on that shelf since a day that is not one of this flood. It has never been returned and it has never been withdrawn and it has not been written in at any hour of this flood and it has not been read out at any hour of this flood, and nothing in that room has ever been laid beside it and nothing has ever been put in one hand with it and nothing has ever been compared out loud with it in a mouth.

Four sheets of that one shape have come through this city and only one of them is in it now, and the four of them went out of this city on four different days and the second and the third and the fourth of them are not in this city and are not coming back and not one of those three is on any surface in that room. The third of them went back up that road with a line of words under its figure. Not one of those three is beside the sheet on that shelf and not one of those three is in that room and not one of them has been read out in that room or compared out loud with anything at any hour of this flood. Seven documents in all, three of them with four places in them and four of them with one place in them, and this city is not going to get a fifth of that one shape and is not going to get an eighth of anything.

---

At about the seventh hour the man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall came to that shelf and stood at the height of his own shoulder in front of it and put the flat of his own two hands flat on the wood of that shelf on either side of that sheet, one on each side of it, and he did not put a finger on that sheet.

"**I have never seen that page filled in and nobody in this city has ever been shown it filled in, and it is the only thing any of us has ever had for putting a figure on a person, and it is standing on that shelf at the height of my own shoulder where I could take it down with one hand, and nobody in this room has ever asked anybody what it is for.**"

Nobody thanked him for that and nobody improved on one word of it and nobody asked the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register what that sheet was for or who had put the figure in it or where it had come from, and she took her own hands off the front edge of that shelf and put them flat on her own apron and said nothing at all about it, and there is nobody in this city that anybody could be shown who knows.

Nobody in that room read one word out of that sheet at any hour of that day. The figure in the one place on it is the only thing on the whole of that page, and it is this city's own, and it has been there since a day that is not one of this flood, and it was put there in a hand, and no hand in that room at any hour of that day went near it with anything that writes.

About nine people in that room stood at the height of a person's shoulder in front of that shelf over the course of that morning, one after another, and about four of them got a thing off the thing they were standing next to and about four of them did not, and not one of them asked what that sheet was for.

---

That sheet was on that shelf at the height of a person's shoulder at the tenth hour with the figure in its one place and nothing written in it and nothing read out of it, and the man of about thirty-four left the flats of his own two hands on the wood of that shelf on either side of it and did not put a finger on it, and that is where he left them.
