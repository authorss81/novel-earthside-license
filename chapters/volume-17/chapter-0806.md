# Chapter 0806 — A Chalk In A Breast Pocket

The man of about thirty-four put his left hand on the bare back of his own board where it stood face in against his trestle, and turned it a hand's width so that the bare back lay flatter against the row, and his right hand stayed in his coat where it had been since he came in.

It was the fourth hour on the four hundred and ninety-first day of the Bare Month, a Tuesday. The market row was filling on both sides of that walk. The board on the outside wall at the far end of that market stood on two nails above a step with three sets of figures on it and no line of words under any of them.

The keeper turned a leaf. The man of about thirty-nine came up that stair with his board under his arm and stood the face of it against the near leg. The man of about twenty-seven set his bucket by the window. The woman of about fifty-two waited at the side of the bench.

At the near end the keeper told the man of about thirty-nine that a rate on that board of his had been copied twice out of the same hand and once out of a different one, and he said that was the way it had always been copied and he would not be changing it at this hour.

---

There was a rate on that board that wanted a day put against it, and the chalk was in the man of about thirty-four's inside breast pocket, and his hand did not go into the pocket at any hour of that morning, and the place where the day should have gone stayed as it had been left.

The man of about thirty-nine came along the row about the sixth hour and stood at the back of that trestle and looked at the face of the board and said nothing about it, and went on up the paving. The man of about thirty-four did not take his hand out of his coat while the other man was standing there, and he did not take it out after he had gone either.

---

The bare back of that board stood turned out into the row all morning and nothing was written on it and nothing was rubbed off it, and the two marks that were already on the wood from the winter were still in the same places at the tenth hour.
