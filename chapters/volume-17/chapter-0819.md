# Chapter 0819 — Light Coming Up The Stair

The man of about thirty-one came down that stair with his satchel under his arm and one hand on the strap, and stopped two steps above the bottom, where the door at the foot of it stood open into the room above.

It was the fourth hour on the five hundred and fourth day of the Bare Month, a Monday. The light came in off the flags through the open door and up the stair past him, and lay across the steps all the way to the top. The bar lay on the top step above the light, out of it.

He went on down past the open door and out onto the paving, and came back up at the fifth hour with nothing in his hands but the satchel. The keeper turned a leaf at the near end. The woman of about fifty-two waited at the side of the bench with her hands at the sides of her dress.

The light lay on every step from the bottom to the top, and the dust in it, and the worn hollows of the wood where the years of feet had gone up and down.

---

The door stood open through the morning and nobody shut it. People came up and went down past the light on the steps, and the room went on round them about rates and a trestle and the weather, and no mouth in that room said whether the door wanted shutting or wanted leaving.

**"The light is on the stair this morning,"** the man of about thirty-one said, at the far end, to nobody in particular.

**"It will go off it by evening,"** the keeper said, without looking up off her page.

---

The steps dried from the top down, and the bottom one was still dark at the tenth hour.
