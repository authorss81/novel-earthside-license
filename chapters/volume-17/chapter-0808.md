# Chapter 0808 — Not Coming Back Up The Stair

The man of about thirty-eight set his hand flat on the top board of a trestle at the store's end of that market and pushed it a hand's width along its own length to square it, and it went, and stopped where a wheel had been standing.

It was the fourth hour on the four hundred and ninety-third day of the Bare Month, a Thursday. The row was filling on both sides of that walk and the water in the trough at the top of the step was flat and had not been drawn off. The bar lay on the top step of a stair at the back of that market and no one in that row knew it.

The man of about thirty-four came in from the far end of that market with his board face in and his hands in his coat. The carrier went past both of them with nothing in his hands.

---

At about the fifth hour two men at the near end of that row said the thing between them, and it was said twice in two different shapes, and neither of them looked up while they said it.

**"They will shut that door or they will not, and either way it will be settled in that room and not out here in a market."**

A third man at the board on the outside wall put his own version on it before the second of them had finished.

**"The man that keeps the register had it off him that keeps the awning. It is his business and not theirs."**

The first man said that was not who it came from, and the third man said it was, and by the end of it neither of the three of them could say which of them had heard it first, and the row went on filling behind them.

---

The board on the outside wall at the far end of that market had been wiped round three sets of figures with a rag by the tenth hour, and there was still no line of words under any of them, and nobody at the top of that stair had heard one word of any of it.
