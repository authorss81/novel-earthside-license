# Chapter 0230 — What Is Left In It

A man of about fifty-two who digs went down to the bottom of a cut on the Reach at about the seventh hour of the morning on the seventy-fifth day of the Long Month with his hands in his pockets and left a rod lying flat in a crack about eleven feet in, where he had put it on the sixty-ninth day of that month. The bottom of that cut was pale and had been dry for thirty-four days and it is the twenty-eighth day since he said four weeks in a market on a causeway on the forty-seventh day of the Long Month, and about nine people were standing at the top of it and about four of them had come eleven miles to be standing there on a day he did not choose.

---

**And he said nothing at all about what he expected to find, because he said at the top of that cut on the sixty-ninth day of that month that he was going to be at the bottom of it on this day in daylight and was not going to say a single thing about what he expected, and he has not improved on it in six days, and about four people at the top of that cut have been counting since the seventh hour and about nine of them have not.**

"Six days you had to get here."

"**Six days. And about four people in this city worked out on the sixty-ninth day of the Long Month which day I had named, and about nine of them have not told me which day they worked out, and I am not going to ask, and I have come down this bank at the seventh hour of the morning on a Sunday to stand in a cut where there is nothing and say nothing about it, because a man who said a direction in a market on the forty-seventh day of the Long Month is not a man who gets to improve on it on the day it stops being a direction.**"

"Does it stop."

"**It stops today. Twenty-eight days after the forty-seventh day of the Long Month is the seventy-fifth day of the Long Month, and that is today, and it is not stopping with water coming back, and I want that said first and in that order, because about four people in this city are going to say afterwards that a man said four weeks and the water came back on the day he said it would, and there has been no water in this cut since the forty-first day of the Long Month and there is none now.**"

He put his hands in his pockets and went down the side the water used to come in on. About nine people at the top of that cut watched a man walk into a dry channel on a Sunday morning and about four of them followed him down. The channel bed is hard all the way across and it rings under a boot the way a road does, and about four people in that cut heard it and about nine of them had not.

---

**And at the bottom of that cut he turned round and said the thing he has not said in two hundred and thirty days, and it was not what about four people at the top of that cut had come eleven miles for, and about nine of them had not come for anything.**

"What was the four weeks about."

"**I do not know. I said it twice, in a market on the forty-seventh day of the Long Month, and I have never once said what it was a direction about, and I have been in this flood two hundred and thirty days and I have put a rod in a hole about nine times and dated a bank and I cannot say whether I said four weeks about the water or about the bottom or about my own yard, and about four people in this city have assumed they know and about nine of them have asked me and about four of those nine got an answer that was a shrug.**"

"You told us it was the water."

"**I did not. I said the water stopped six days before that and then I said four weeks, and about four people have joined those two in their own heads and about nine people have not, and I have let them join them for twenty-eight days because it was easier than saying which of the two I meant, and it stops today because today is the day and I have nothing to be easier with.**"

He said it at the bottom of a dry channel with his hands in his pockets and did not say anything else for about as long as it takes to walk back up a bank, and about four people in that cut watched him say it and about nine of them were above the bank and did not hear it at all.

---

**And a man who has carried racks up and down that cut since the second month of this flood was at the top of it and had brought a rack with him, and he had said on the seventy-second day of that month that the rest of it was worth a day of walking and that he was not going to say it in a cut with about nine people in it, and he came down the bank with it and put it down at the edge of the bottom and did not say the rest of it.**

"That is no use down there."

"**I know it is no use down there. I have known it since the forty-first day of the Long Month and I have known it exactly and I have been standing on this bank for six days waiting for a man to come down it on a day, and I have not come down it on any other day and I am not going to start today because there is a man in the cut.**"

"Put it in anyway."

"**I am not putting it in. A rack is four lengths of wood and a rope and four people, and the wood came out of frames, and I have a frame that four people can work and I have had it since the second month of this flood, and if I put this one in a dry channel on the seventy-fifth day of the Long Month then there are two of them and about nine people in this city will ask which one is the real one, and there is no page in four hundred miles that says, and I would rather have one rack and a hole than two racks and a question.**"

He left it at the edge of the bottom with the rope coiled on the top length, and about four people at the top of that cut watched a rack not go into a cut and about nine of them had not known until that moment that a rack could be carried to a place and not used there.

---

**And Adrian Vale came up that road at about the ninth hour of the morning with eleven miles on his boots and went down the side of that cut on his own without being asked by anybody, and about nine people at the top of it had not known he was in the city and about four of them had, and nobody in that cut asked him anything about a gate and nobody offered him anything and he asked for nothing.**

"You came eleven miles to stand in a dry channel."

"**I came eleven miles because a man said four weeks in a market on the forty-seventh day of the Long Month and about four people in this city turned it into a day and he did not know it. I have never in two hundred and thirty days in this city met anybody who had a day they did not choose and knew it, and I wanted to see whether the day was any different from the day I chose, which was the first one I was asked for. It is not. I have been in about nine rooms in this city where a date on a door was the whole of a thing and I have been in none of them where a date nobody chose did that.**"

"And what was it."

"**It was a man in a dry channel with his hands in his pockets who said nothing for about four minutes, and I have said that in a yard in this city before and about four people thought it was a sentence, and it was not a sentence, it was a channel.**"

He stood at the edge of the bottom of that cut for about as long as it takes to say a thing twice and then he went back up the bank and did not stay, and about four people at the top of that cut had been waiting for him to say something about a gate since the fifty-fourth day of the Long Month and about nine of them had not.

---

**And a woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road and a woman of about thirty-nine who cannot get a rack above her shoulder and a woman of about fifty who counts water were all three at the top of that cut at about the tenth hour of the morning, and the three of them have been at the top of it twice each since the fifty-ninth day of that month, and about four people in this city have worked out that they come up here on a day that is not a Tuesday and about nine of them have not worked out why.**

"Twenty-eight days."

"**Twenty-eight days since you said it, and thirty-four since the water stopped, and about nine people in this city have said both of those numbers out loud this morning and about four of them have said them in the same breath as the date, and I am the one who counted and I am not going to be the one who joins them.**"

"You do not have to join them."

"**I do not have to join anything. That is what I have been for nine years. I have kept a list of persons for nine years and every name on it is alive and I have never once in nine years put two of my own figures next to each other, and about four people in this city know that and about nine of them think it is a way of being careful, and it is not, it is a way of not being the one who is wrong about both.**"

The woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road said nothing at all on that Sunday and about four people in this city had been waiting since the fifty-ninth day of the Long Month for her to choose a day and about nine of them have stopped saying that she is going to.

---

**And a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall came up that bank at about the tenth hour of the morning and stood at the top of it and did not come down, and about four people at the top of that cut had been waiting for him to say whether the ninth line on a wall nine miles up the road works, and about nine of them had not been waiting for anything, and he said two sentences and neither of them was about a line.**

"**I have been asked that in a market and I have not answered it, and I came up here this morning to stand on a bank and I have not answered it, and about four people in this city have worked out that a man who will not answer a question on a Tuesday will not answer it on a Sunday either, and I am not going to be a different one because somebody came up eleven miles to ask me in a better light.**"

"You could have asked me."

"**You could have asked me and I gave you the same thing on the sixtieth day of the Long Month in a market and I am giving you the same thing now, and the same thing is that a posting is a page about labour and a page about labour is not a wage, and the only line on it that anybody can check is a line somebody put there on purpose, and I put it there on purpose, and whether it works is a question about about nine people standing in a line in front of a door and I am not going to answer it about a man in a dry channel.**"

He said it at the top of a bank and about four people in that cut heard both of the sentences and about nine of them were on the other side of it and heard one of them.

---

**And a man of about thirty-two came up that bank at about the eleventh hour and stood at the top of it and did not come down, which is the fourth day in a row he has stood on that bank and none of the four of them has been a Monday, and the five Mondays he stood on it were the five Mondays before the seventy-first day of that month and the nearest of the five was six days ago, and about four people in this city have worked out what that is and about nine of them have not, and he said four sentences and none of them was about the four weeks.**

"**I did not come down here this morning. I have been on this bank five Mondays in a row and I said in a market on the forty-eighth day of the Long Month that I am not going to be the man who walks up nine feet and asks a woman whether a thing she said on a date is still the thing she says on the date, and I have not, and I am not going to start on a Sunday with about nine people on a bank above me.**"

"You are on the bank."

"**I am on the bank, and a bank is not a room and nobody has asked me anything, and about four people in this city have said since the forty-ninth day of the Long Month that I stand on banks, and about nine of them have said that I should come down, and neither of those is a question and I have been asked about nine of them and I have answered one.**"

---

**And a rod was lying flat in the cracked bottom of a cut on the Reach where a man had left it six days before, and there was no water in it.**
