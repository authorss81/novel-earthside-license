# Chapter 0233 — The Hole That Filled In

A rod in a hole nine feet from the one everybody measures had come up about four inches in its standing since the fifty-ninth day of the Long Month, and the hole it was in had come up to meet it, and on the seventy-eighth day of that month the fine stuff off eleven miles of flats had been blowing along the bottom of that cut since the seventy-fourth day of that month and there was a skin of it in the bottom of the second hole about the depth of a thumb. The channel had been dry since the forty-first day of that month and it is the thirty-seventh day of that, and the bottom of the cut is hard all the way across and it rings under a boot the way a road does. A rack is standing at the edge of that bottom with the rope coiled on the top length, and it has been standing there since the seventy-fifth day of that month.

---

And a woman of about fifty who counts water on the fingers of her left hand came down the bank at about the eighth hour with a board under her arm and stood over the second hole and did not go down into the cut, and about four people at the top of that cut have been watching her count since the seventh week of this flood and about nine of them had stopped.

"**That is a rod in a hole.**"

"**That is a rod in a hole that has stopped being a hole, and it is not the hole four people measure and it never was, and I have been at the top of this cut about nine times since the fifty-ninth day of that month and I have not been able to say a thing about that one and this morning I can say a thing about it, and what I can say is that it is a rod in a heap.**"

"You can count a heap."

"**I can count a heap and I have counted water since I was about eleven and there is not one figure in me that will come out of a heap of dust, and I am not going to take one off this, and about four people in this city have worked out that the one person who would have been able to read that rod is the one person who was never going to be in this cut at the eighth hour, and about nine of them have worked out that a woman who counts water is not a woman who counts things.**"

She put her hand flat on the rim of that hole and it gave about an inch under her palm and came back, and about four people at the top of that cut watched her hand and about nine of them watched the rack at the edge.

---

And a woman of about thirty-nine who cannot get a rack above her shoulder came up that bank at about the ninth hour and stood at the top of the cut and looked at the second hole and at the dust in it, and she has been at the top of that cut three times since the fifty-ninth day of that month and she has a refusal with a date on it that she has not shown anybody. About four people in that cut have known that since the fifty-ninth day of that month, and about nine of them have only known that she asked.

"You could clear it out with your hand."

"**I could clear it out with my hand in about four minutes and about four people in this cut have watched me not do it since the eighth hour and about nine of them have not been here since the eighth hour. A hole that nobody has measured filling in with the wind is a thing that has happened, and if I clear it out this morning then it is a hole a man put his hand in, and about four people in this city would call that a measurement and about nine of them would call it the beginning of one, and it would be the beginning of one by a woman who cannot get a rack above her shoulder, which is a sentence I have not got a use for.**"

"Does it matter who clears it."

"**It matters that it is not done this morning by anybody, and I have got a reason and the reason is that I came up here to stand at the top of a cut and not to be useful, and about four people in this cut have worked out that and about nine of them think I came up here to be asked for something, and I have not been asked for anything since the fifty-ninth day of that month.**"

---

And the man of about fifty-two who digs came up that bank at about the tenth hour with a rod under his arm and went down the side the water used to come in on and stood over the second hole for a while and did not go down into it, and about four people at the top of that cut had been counting the days since he said four weeks in a market on the forty-seventh day of that month and about nine of them had not.

"That hole is done."

"**That hole is finished and it finished itself and I did not do it, and I put a rod in it on the fifty-ninth day of that month to find out one thing, and I have never told anybody what the one thing was except that it had to do with a sack that went onto a beam in a yard nine miles up the road on the fifty-eighth day of that month, and about four people in this city have worked out that a hole nobody has measured is the only hole in this flood that has not been turned into a column, and about nine of them have worked out that it is not a hole any more, and those two are the same sentence and I have not got a third one.**"

"You could put a rod in another one."

"**I could put a rod in another one about nine feet from this one, and the second hole is nine feet from the one four people measure, and if I put a rod in a third one then there are three holes in about eighteen feet of the bottom of this cut and about four people in this city will have a number about this cut inside a season, and I have been measuring this cut since the forty-first day of that month and I stopped once and started again once and I am not going to start a third time for a man who wants a figure.**"

He put his hand flat on the rim of the second hole the way the woman of about fifty had put hers on it and it gave about an inch and came back, and about four people at the top of that cut watched two hands do the same thing and about nine of them had gone back up the bank.

---

And the woman of about thirty-nine who cannot get a rack above her shoulder was still at the top of that cut at about the eleventh hour when the man of about fifty-two who digs looked at the rack standing at the edge of the bottom with the rope coiled on the top length, and about four people at that cut have watched that rack not go in since the seventy-fifth day of that month and about nine of them had stopped counting the days.

"You could ask her to get a hand under that."

"**I am not going to ask her to get a hand under that, and I have not asked her to do anything at any time, and the reason is that I have said no about wood three times in this cut since the seventy-second day of that month and about four people in this city have worked out that a man who has said no three times about a thing has to be careful about who he asks a fourth time, and about nine of them have worked out that he is a man who would ask anybody, and I would rather be the second of those than the first.**"

"You have not said no to her."

"**I have not said no to her and that is the whole of it. There is a rack at the bottom of this cut with a rope on it and a woman at the top of this cut who cannot get a rack above her shoulder, and about four people in this city have worked out what those two facts are when they are put next to each other and about nine of them have worked out that they are two facts, and the second one of those is what I have got and I am not going to give it up on a Wednesday morning with about four people in this cut.**"

He did not say anything else about the rack and about four people at the top of that cut noticed that a man had decided not to say something and about nine of them had not noticed it at all.

---

And the rod that is lying flat in the cracked bottom of that cut about eleven feet further in had been lying flat in that crack for nine days, and a man of about fifty-two put it down flat instead of in a hole on the sixty-ninth day of that month because a woman of about thirty-four had said a number out loud at the top of that cut and he had found out there was one, and about four people in this city have worked out that a rod lying flat in a crack is a man admitting a thing, and about nine of them have worked out that it is a rod.

"**Nobody has picked that up in nine days.**"

"**Nobody has picked that up in nine days and I put it down flat on purpose on the sixty-ninth day of that month and about four people in this city have worked out that a rod in a hole is a thing a man measures and a rod flat in a crack is a thing a man put down, and about nine of them have worked out that I put it there because a woman said the number of a thing I had said out loud in a market and I did not know there was one, and both of those are true and I have not picked it up because picking it up would be putting it back in a hole and a hole is a column and I do not have another one.**"

"Where does it go if you pick it up."

"**It goes in my hand and then it goes up that bank and then it is a length of iron in a yard in this city and about four people in four hundred miles will ask me what a rod is for, and I have not got an answer for that and I have had nine days to get one and I have not, and this hole filling in with the wind has not helped and has not made it worse.**"

---

And a woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road was at the top of that cut at about the eleventh hour with nothing in her hands, and she has come up that road on days that are not Tuesdays and has not chosen a day to ask in since the fifty-sixth day of that month. About four people in that cut had been waiting since the eighth hour for her to say a number and about nine of them had not been waiting for anything.

"You have a rod and a hole and a sack and a yard nine miles up the road."

"**I have a rod that is not in a hole, a sack that was one piece on the sixty-sixth day of that month, and a beam with one pan on it in a yard, and about four people in this city have worked out that those three things are one argument and about nine of them have worked out that they are three things that happen to have happened in the same flood, and I have not got a number and I have not been asked for one and I am not going to say one in a cut.**"

"Then what are you doing up here."

"**I have walked eleven miles to stand at the top of a cut and look at a hole that has filled in with the wind, and about four people in this cut think I have come for something and about nine of them have worked out that I come up here on days that are not Tuesdays, and the reason I come up here on days that are not Tuesdays is that a Tuesday is a day about four hundred and forty people are somewhere else on, and I would like that written down and I would like it not written down, in that order, and I have not decided which.**"

---

And a rod standing in a thumb's depth of the flats at the bottom of a hole that has stopped being a hole, and nobody has cleared it out.
