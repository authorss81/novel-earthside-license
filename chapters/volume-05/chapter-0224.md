# Chapter 0224 — The Number He Did Not Say

A rod came up out of a hole at the bottom of a cut on the Reach at about the seventh hour of the morning on the sixty-ninth day of the Long Month and came up dry, and the man of about fifty-two who digs put his thumb and forefinger on it and rolled the two of them together and looked at what came off them. The channel had been dry for twenty-eight days. The crack in the bottom of the cut had gone the width of a hand since the fifty-ninth day of that month, and about nine people were standing at the top of it and about four of them had come eleven miles to be there.

---

And it is his own hole, the one everybody measures, and he has put the rod in it on and off since the forty-first day of the Long Month, and the second hole is nine feet from it with a rod in it as well, and neither of them has ever given anybody a figure. He said the number of days out loud before anybody asked him for it, which is a thing he has done about nine times in this flood.

"Twenty-eight."

"**Twenty-eight. And that is the day the water stopped coming down this channel and not the day I said the thing, and I am going to say the other number now and I want about four people at the top of this cut to hear it properly because I have never said it in one go.**"

Somebody at the top of that cut asked him not to.

"**You are going to want to hear it. A woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road has come up this road and told me the number this morning and I have had it since the eighth hour and I have not been able to put it down anywhere, and I have been putting rods in holes for four days to see whether a number will go in a hole, and it will not.**"

---

And the woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road said it herself, at about the eighth hour, standing at the top of that cut with nothing in her hands, and about nine people heard it and about four of them had it before she said it.

"The seventy-fifth day of the Long Month."

"**I said four weeks. I said it in a market on the forty-seventh day of the Long Month, on a causeway, in the middle of four miles of it, and I said there was nothing at the bottom of this cut in about four weeks that anybody could put a rack into, and I said that four weeks is a direction and it is not a figure, and I said it a second time, and about nine people in this city have written the number down instead.**"

"Who wrote it down."

"**I do not know who wrote it down. I know that about four people in this city have said the seventy-fifth day of that month out loud since the forty-seventh and I have found out about two of them in twenty-two days and I did not find out about either of them from the person who said it.**"

He put the rod down flat on the cracked bottom of the cut instead of putting it in a hole, which is a thing he has not done since the forty-first day of that month, and about four people at the top of that cut said afterwards that they had not known what to do with their hands while he did it.

---

And a woman of about fifty who counts water stood at the top of that cut at about the eighth hour with a board in her left hand and she counts on the fingers of that hand and she had got to a day and stopped, and about four people in that cut have been watching her count since the seventh week of this flood and about nine of them have stopped.

"How many days."

"**Twenty-eight. And that is a count of days and it is not a count of water, and I have been keeping the two of them in the same hand since the forty-first day of that month and on the fifty-first I wrote a line on a board nine feet up that said there was no water in this channel and I have not taken it down since, and I am not going to take it down on the strength of a rod coming up dry.**"

"You are going to be wrong."

"**I am going to be right about a day and I have been right about that day since the forty-first and I have been wrong about about nine other things in this flood and every one of them was on a line on a board, and about four people here have heard me say that and about nine of them have not.**"

She put her hand flat on the board and did not write anything on it, and about four people in that cut watched her not write and about nine of them watched the rod on the bottom.

---

And a man who has carried racks up and down this cut since the second month of this flood said the thing that everybody at the top of that cut had been standing in for four days, and about nine people heard it and about four of them had worked it out and had not said it, and he said it once and did not say it again.

"**A rack goes in the water. That is the whole of a rack. Four lengths of wood and a rope and four people, and the water is what holds it up, and there has been no water in this cut since the forty-first day of that month, and a rack that is not in the water is a frame, and a frame goes in the mud, and I have been standing on the bank on about nine days telling people to be patient and there is nothing to be patient about and I have not said it.**"

"Then what was the four weeks."

"**The four weeks was never about the water coming back. I have not been able to work out what the four weeks was about, and he has put a rod down flat instead of in a hole, and I have been going to say the rest of it and about four of you are not going to want to hear it from a man who carries racks and not from him.**"

"Then say it."

"**I am not saying it. I have said one thing and I have said it in the right order and I am going to go and carry a rack up this bank and put it on my own ground and I am not going to say the rest of it in a cut with about nine people in it, and about four people in this city have said since the forty-fourth day of the Long Month that the rest of it is worth a day of walking and about nine of them have not.**"

He went up the bank with a rack on his shoulder and it went up four lengths at a time and about nine people at the top of that cut watched a rack leave and about four of them had worked out where it was going.

---

And a man of about thirty-two stood on the bank above that cut and did not come down to it, which is the fifth Monday in a row that he has stood on that bank, and about four people at the top of that cut had counted them and about nine of them had not, and he has no mark and no workway and no title and no seat and he is on no roster of anything, and he said one sentence and nobody in that cut improved on it.

"You have a date you did not choose."

"**I have a date I did not choose, and I said a direction, and it turns out a direction is a figure with the day left off it, and I have known that since the eighth hour of this morning and I have not been able to be angry about it, and I have about three hundred days of being angry at myself and I am not going to spend one of them on a thing I said about nine days before you were born in this argument.**"

"What will you do on it."

"**Nothing. I am going to be at the bottom of this cut on the seventy-fifth day of the Long Month in daylight and I am not going to say a single thing about what I expect to find, and about four people at the top of this cut have just said they will be standing here and about nine of them have just said they will not, and I am not going to be a man who arrives at a day he did not choose and improves on a direction he gave in good faith.**"

---

And a woman of about thirty-nine who cannot get a rack above her shoulder came up that bank at about the ninth hour and stood at the top of the cut and looked at the rod lying flat on the cracked bottom of it, and about four people in that cut have known since the fifty-ninth day of that month that she has a refusal with a date on it and about nine of them have only known that she asked.

"You have taken the rod out."

"**I have not taken the rod out. I have laid it down. There is a difference and about nine people in this city would not see one and about four of you would.**"

"There is a difference."

"**There is a difference and it is that a rod in a hole is a figure and a rod on the ground is a thing you can see from the top of a bank, and I have had a figure I could not get out of a hole for twenty-eight days and I have got a thing I can look at for about a minute, and that is all I have got and I am not going to dress it up as a decision about anything.**"

---

And about four people in that cut had been counting days to a date they worked out on their own on the sixty-ninth day of that month, and about nine of them had been, and the first one to say it out loud was a woman of about thirty-nine who cannot get a rack above her shoulder, and she said it to him and not to the bank.

"I have been counting."

"**I know you have. I have been in this flood since the seventh week of it and I have watched about four people do arithmetic in their heads for two hundred and twenty-four days, and I have never once been one of them about a thing I said. It turns out I have been, since the forty-seventh day of the Long Month, and about nine people in this city worked out the number before I knew there was one. I am not going to ask which four, because a man who asks which four people worked out his own number is a man who has begun to think of himself as a figure, and I have not got six days left to stop.**"

"You should ask."

"**No. And about four people in this cut have just heard me refuse a thing that would have cost me nothing and about nine of them did not hear it, and that is the last I am going to say about it, and I am going to go and put my hand into a crack in a pan that I have not told anybody about.**"

He went up the bank about nine feet and stopped and did not go on, and about four people in that cut watched him stop and about nine of them watched the man of about thirty-two up on the bank above, who had not moved.

---

And the woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road did not ask anybody anything for the rest of that morning, and about four people in that cut were waiting for her to, and about nine of them have said since that a woman who came up a bank eleven miles to say a number somebody else said and then said nothing at all for about four hours has done harder work than the man who did not come down off the bank.

"The day I chose."

"You are not going to choose another one."

"**I am not going to choose another one in a cut, and I am not going to choose one in a yard, and I have not chosen one since the fifty-sixth day of that month, and I would like somebody to write down that I had a number in my mouth this morning about a man who did not choose his and I did not spend it on anything, and about four people here know exactly what I am talking about and about nine of them do not.**"

"You could spend it on a room."

"**I could spend it on a room and there is no room with a date on a door that I have been asked to walk into, and that is not the same as there not being one, and I have been careful with that distinction for twenty days and I am not going to lose it at the top of a cut because a man has finally given me a number I did not ask him for.**"

She said no more than that and about four people in that cut understood all of it and about nine of them understood about half.

---

And about the tenth hour the sun came over the bank of that cut about an hour later than it had on the fifty-ninth day of the Long Month and lit the bottom of it, and the rod lying flat in the crack had a shadow along the whole of its length, and about nine people at the top of that cut went down the bank on the side the water used to come in on and about four of them went up.

---

And a rod lying flat on the cracked bottom of a cut on the Reach at about the ninth hour of the morning, and the water twenty-eight days from coming back, and a day neither of them chose.
