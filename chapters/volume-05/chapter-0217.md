# Chapter 0217 — A Figure With No Owner

A man of about thirty-nine who trades on the Open Hand board counted on the fingers of his right hand in a market in this city on the sixty-second day of the Long Month at about the tenth hour of the morning and got to a number out loud in front of about nine people. He counted with the hand he uses for everything else, standing at his own board with it, and the market went on behind him. About four people had heard him start and about nine of them heard him finish, and it is entered that he did not know he was going to say it until he had said it.

---

**And what he said was a number of days, and he gave the day he counted it off before he gave the number, and he said it once in a mouth in daylight in that market with his hand still up.**

"**Seventy-two days. Counted off the fifty-fifth day of the month after this one, which is the day four people and a question were named in a room over a market in this city, and not off a week, because there is no week. I am not going to say that number again and I am not going to add anything to it and I am not going to be the one who corrects it either, and I would like it entered that I said it out loud on a Monday morning in a market because counting is my trade and I have been the man in this city who does not put a column on a person for about nine years, and I have just put a column on four people in front of nine people and I heard myself do it.**"

Nobody in that market said anything. His hand stayed up for a moment and then came down onto the edge of his own board, and about four people in the market were angry and about nine of them were not, and about nine of the nine have said since that the number is the number anybody would have got.

"You know what that does."

"**I know exactly what that does. I have spent about nine years describing things in a way that a person cannot be found in, and I have said the reason out loud about four times and the reason is that a description is a removal with a column for where the person is. Seventy-two days is a column. It is a delay and you can count a delay and you cannot count four people, and as of a minute ago there is a figure in this market about four people who have not been asked whether they will go, and nobody in this market asked them, and about four people in this market have noticed that the figure arrived before anybody did anything wrong.**"

---

**And a man of about twenty-nine from the settlement's workroom was in that market for about nine minutes before the eleventh hour and said the thing about a figure with no owner that nobody else in there had thought of. It is in a book in that workroom that goes nowhere with a date on it. About four people in that market have said since that it is the worst true sentence anybody has said about a number in this city, and that it is about a number nobody in that market has said.**

"A figure with no owner is a figure anybody can add to."

"**That is the whole of it and I have known it since the thirty-eighth day of the Long Month and I have not said it out loud about nine times, and I have not said it because the one I know it about is me. I have got a book in that workroom with entries in it that go nowhere and nobody reads and no figure at the end of any of them, and that book is a figure with no owner and about four people in that workroom have added to it about nine times since the thirty-eighth day of the Long Month without knowing that they were adding to it.**"

"Nobody can add to a book in a workroom."

"**Nobody can add to a book in a workroom. About nine people in this city can add to a number that was said out loud in a market, and the difference between my book and this man's seventy-two days is that mine has never left a room and this one has, and I have been arguing with myself about that since the fifty-seventh day of the Long Month and I have lost.**"

"And the entries."

"**I am not going to recite them and I have never recited them. A cost with no figure on the end of it is a cost and a price with a figure on the end of it is a promise, and a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall said that in a yard on the forty-sixth day of the Long Month and it is the truest thing anybody has said about a number since the forty-sixth day of the Long Month, and I have put it in my own book in my own hand and I would like it known that I put another man's sentence in a book of mine and did not tell him.**"

---

**And a woman of about twenty-four who is on no roll of anything was in that market with a slate under her arm and she wrote the seventy-two days down, and then she rubbed it out with the flat of her sleeve in one movement, and the sleeve took the chalk off it as well. About four people in that market watched her do it and about nine of them did not know that she had written anything at all.**

"You wrote it."

"**I wrote it and I have rubbed it out and the slate is in my hand and it is blank in the place where the number was and I would like somebody to write down that I did that in one movement and not in a temper, because I have been the person who writes things down in this city since the second day of this flood and I have never rubbed one out and I did it today and I want a date on the decision.**"

"Why."

"**Because I do not own that figure. A man said it and it is his and he has said out loud that he is not going to say it again, and if I write it down then it is in a hand and in a book and it is about four people, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register tore a page out of her own book in a yard on the fifty-fifth day of the Long Month because a list of four people who have not been asked is a roll, and about four people in this market would call that an argument and it is not one. It is a woman with a pocket and a man with a figure and neither of them is the four.**"

"Nobody has asked them."

"**Nobody has asked them and about nine people in this city have said so in about nine rooms and it is in a book and it is the correct outcome, and the figure that went round this market this morning does not say they were asked, and it does not say they were not asked, and that is exactly why it is a column. A figure about four people does not need anybody to have done anything.**"

---

**And a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall was in that market and said one thing. He has not put a sentence on a page since the fiftieth day of the Long Month and has not said out loud that he is going to stop. About four people in that market have noticed that this is the first thing he has said in a market since the forty-fifth day of the Long Month, and that on that day he was in a room over that market and not in the market, and that it was about four people he does not know.**

"You could have said the number in a room."

"**I could have and I did not, and I want it entered that I did not, and the reason is not a good one. In a yard a number is a thing four people remember. In a market a number is a thing nine people repeat. In a room in daylight with a date on the door a number is a thing a person can be held to, and the one who said it this morning did not choose a room and about four of us were standing in the worst place available to hear it.**"

"That is a reason and not a good one."

"**That is a reason and it is the only instrument I have got and I have said that in about nine rooms since the thirty-seventh day of the Long Month, and I am not going to get a better one, and about four people in this market are going to go home and think that a man who only says things in rooms is a man who has a room and about nine of them are going to be wrong, and I would like it entered that I know that and did it anyway.**"

---

**And a woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register was not in that market and was not in that city on the sixty-second day of the Long Month, and about four people in that market knew that and about nine of them did not, and nobody in that market asked anybody about her and about four people in that market wanted to and did not.**

"She should be told."

"**No.**"

"That is the whole of it."

"**That is the whole of it and I will not be the one. She said the thing she has to say three times and I have been in a room for two of them and in a yard for one and I am not going to be a person who carries it to a fourth place and calls that being useful. About four people in this city have asked me in about nine rooms whether I would go and get a person into a room, and I have said no every time and the reason has never once been a better one than the last, and I am not going to start on a Monday.**"

"And if somebody else says the number tomorrow."

"**Then it is a figure in this city with about four hands on it and no owner, and the only person who could take it back is the one who wrote the first page and she has it in her own pocket with a date on the back of it, and about nine people in this city have been working that out since the thirty-ninth day of the Long Month, and about nine of them have worked out the other half of it, which is that she will not carry it a fourth time.**"

---

**And about the fourth hour of the afternoon a girl of seventeen who is on no roll of anything came into that market and stood about nine feet from the man of about thirty-nine who trades on the Open Hand board, and she stood on the same paving he was standing on and looked at the boards of his stall rather than at him. About four people in that market saw her do it, and she did not say anything, and about nine of them have said since that they had expected her to, and about four of them have said since that they had not.**

"You said the number."

"**I said the number.**"

"You are the only person in this city who says a cost out loud in four sentences and does not put a figure on it."

"**That is a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall and he is four streets from here. I say a number when I am buying and selling and I have said one out loud in a market this morning because counting is what my hands do and they do it whether I am talking or not, and I have had four minutes of being a column today and I would like to be a man who buys salt this afternoon.**"

She did not say anything else, and she looked at the ground for the rest of it, and the ground was the only part of that exchange she was ever going to look at. It is entered that about four people in that market have said since that a girl of seventeen stood about nine feet from a man who had just made a figure about four people and said nothing, and that about nine of them have said that that is the only thing that happened in that market after the eleventh hour. About four of them have said that they wanted her to say the other half of what she said in a room on the fifty-second day of the Long Month, and that she looked at the ground when they said so, and that nobody in that market has said since whether that was an answer or not.

---

**And at about the fourth hour the man of about thirty-nine who trades on the Open Hand board bought a quarter of a sack of salt at a stall in that market and paid for it without hesitating, and he counted it out of his own hand into the other one's, and about four people in that market watched him do it. It is entered that about nine of them have said since that a man who made a column about four people at the tenth hour of the morning paid a figure for a quarter of a sack at the fourth hour of the afternoon as though it were the only number in the building, and that about four of them have said that it probably was.**

**And a market in this city on a Monday with nothing in it about four people, and a number in about four hands with no owner, and a page in a pocket eleven miles away with a date on the back of it.**
