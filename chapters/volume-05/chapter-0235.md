# Chapter 0235 — The Corner Of It

The wet stripe on the boards of the yard of the house at the bottom of the Redroot cut was not there at about the seventh hour of the morning on the eightieth day of the Long Month, and nobody had scrubbed it out. The boards of that yard had been the colour of the boards under them since the seventy-fourth day of that month and by this morning the stripe was the same colour as the rest of them, and about four inches of one end of it was still four inches darker than the boards two inches either side of it, which is where a corner of a sack had sat on the sixty-first day of that month. Nobody came up that bank with a sack on a strap. About four people in that yard had looked at the boards before the sun came over the step and about nine of them had not come out.

---

**And a woman of about fifty-nine who keeps that house came out onto the step and stood on it and looked down at four boards and did not go down into the yard, and a boy of eight came out behind her and stood on the step and looked down at them too, and there is one step in that yard with any sun on it and the sun had not come on to it yet.**

"**It has gone.**"

"**The stripe has gone. The boards have been the colour of the boards under them since the seventy-fourth day of that month and the stripe went with the boards, and I did not scrub it and nobody in this yard scrubbed it, and about four people in this city have worked out that a wet stripe on four boards is a mark a sack leaves and about nine of them have worked out that a mark the wind takes is not a mark anybody made.**"

"Then nothing is on them."

"**There are four inches at one end that are darker than the boards either side of them and about four people in this yard can see them and about nine of them cannot, and that is not a stripe and it is not about a sack, and I am not going to say what it is about, and I have had a stripe on those boards since the sixty-first day of that month that I could see every morning and this morning I have got four inches.**"

She sat down on the step after that and about four people in that yard saw her do it and about nine of them had never seen her sit on that step, and the boy of eight sat down on the boards at the bottom of it about four minutes later and did not say anything about it.

---

**And a woman of about thirty-four in that house came out onto the step at about the eighth hour, and she is the youngest person in that house and she has been on no rota since the seventh week of this flood, and she went down into the yard and looked at the four inches and did not put anything near them.**

"**I am not going to scrub that out.**"

"**I know you are not and I have known you were not since the seventy-fourth day of that month when you said so in this yard, and about four people in this city have worked out that a woman who says on a day that she is not going to do a thing and then does not do it on a later day is a woman who has been right, and about nine of them have worked out that she said it about a stripe and there is not a stripe.**"

"**It is the same four inches.**"

"**It is four inches of a corner and it is not a stripe and it is not going to turn into a stripe, and I am not going to be a woman who scrubs out a corner to make a floor a floor, and I am not going to be a woman who leaves four inches on a floor either, and about four people in this yard have worked out that those are the same decision and about nine of them have worked out that one of them is a decision and the other one is a habit, and the habit is mine and I have had it since the seventh week of this flood.**"

She put her own foot down on the boards beside the four inches and about four people in that yard watched a woman put a foot down next to a mark and about nine of them looked at the step.

---

**And a woman of about thirty-nine who cannot get a rack above her shoulder was in that yard at about the ninth hour and had come down the bank from the top of the cut, and she has been at the top of that cut about four times since the fifty-ninth day of that month and she has a refusal with a date on it that she has not shown anybody. About four people in that cut have known that since the fifty-ninth day of that month, and about nine of them have only known that she asked.**

"You came down for this."

"**I came down the bank because I have been at the top of it since the eighth hour and I have not gone down it since the fifty-ninth day of that month, and about four people in this city have worked out that I have not come down that bank in twenty-one days and about nine of them have worked out that I have not come down that bank because of what is at the top of it, and both of those are about the same thing and I have not got a different reason.**"

"You could have looked at it from the top."

"**I could have looked at it from the top and I have not been able to see four inches from the top of that bank, and I have been standing about nine feet above four inches of a floor for about an hour and I have come down it, and about four people in this yard are going to say that is the first thing I have come down that bank for and about nine of them are going to say that I came down it to be in a yard with a woman on a step, and the second of those is not true and I would like it not written down.**"

She looked at the four inches for a while and then she looked at the boards on either side of them and about four people in that yard watched a woman look twice and about nine of them watched her once.

---

**And the boy of eight went out into that yard at about the tenth hour and untied a length of rope off a wall and tied it again and came back in, and it is about nine years old and it is not tied to anything and about nine people in this city know that and about four of them have said it out loud and nobody has said it in this yard, and he has untied it and tied it again every morning since the seventy-fourth day of that month and he did it with his teeth in the end of it this morning and his hands had gone white at the rope about halfway.**

"That rope."

"That rope is nine feet from my step and it has been nine feet from my step since the seventh week of this flood and I have never once said one word about it in this yard, and about nine people in this city have been waiting since then for somebody to say a word about it and about four of them have said it to about four other people. Nobody has said it where the boy can hear it, and I have decided that is the right arrangement and I am not going to change it this morning."

"You let him do it."

"**I have let him do it every morning since the seventh week of this flood and about four people in this yard have worked out that a woman who has let a boy of eight do a thing every morning since the seventh week of this flood has decided something about it, and about nine of them have worked out that I have not decided anything and I have gone back inside, and both of those are true and I have a step and a rope and the distance between them and that is the whole of what I have got to say about either.**"

The boy came back in and the boards under his feet were the colour of the boards under them and about four people in that yard watched a boy come in and about nine of them had not looked up.

---

**And the woman of about thirty-nine who cannot get a rack above her shoulder asked the woman of about thirty-four in that house one question at about the eleventh hour, and it is the first question anybody has asked in that yard since the seventy-fourth day of that month, and it was about four inches of a corner and it was not about a sack.**

"Is it going to go."

"**I do not know. It has been nineteen days and the stripe went with the boards in about four days and the corner is deeper than the stripe was, because a corner is where all the weight of a thing goes, and about four people in this city have worked out that a mark somebody put a foot on is a mark that goes slower and about nine of them have worked out that it does not go at all, and I have not got a figure and I have not got a reason and if it is there in about nine years then it is there and I am not going to be a woman who keeps a corner.**"

"You will not scrub it."

"**I have said in this yard on the seventy-fourth day of that month that I am not going to scrub out a stripe and I have said this morning that I am not going to scrub out a corner, and those are two sentences and about four people in this city have worked out that a woman who says no twice about a mark has decided something and about nine of them have worked out that she has run out of words, and I have run out of words and I have not run out of anything else, and that is the difference and it is about four inches long.**"

Nobody in that yard said anything after that and about four people in it were still standing and about nine of them had gone inside, and the step was empty and the rope was on the wall.

---

**And a woman of about fifty-nine who keeps that house said four sentences at about the tenth hour and about nine people at the bottom of that bank heard all four of them, and the fourth one was about a corner of a sack and the first three were about a bank, and nobody in that yard asked her a question about any of it.**

"**A man carried four of these up this bank and told nobody where they came from, and I have not asked him where they came from once in nineteen days, and the days went back on the forty-first day of the Long Month, unasked, on a flat, in daylight, and the sack did not stop. I have had four sacks come up that bank inside nineteen days and I have had six days of a sack not coming up that bank, and about four people in this city have worked out that I have a number and about nine of them have worked out that the number is the only thing in this yard anybody has, and it is not, because there are four inches of a corner on my boards and the number is not on them.**"

"Do you want the number written down."

"**I want four inches of a corner on my boards for about nine years and I do not want anything else, and if somebody writes a number down about this yard then the day the first sack came up this bank is a first and the day the last one did not is a last, and about four people in about nine years are going to read a book about how many days in a row it was, and I have told the woman of about thirty-four in this house that I am not going to be in a book about that, and she told me the same thing in this yard on the seventy-fourth day of that month and we have not improved on it in six days.**"

Nobody came up that bank for the rest of that day and about four people in that yard noticed that and about nine of them did not, and nobody in that yard said a word about it to anybody.

---

**And four inches of a corner on four boards at the bottom of a bank, in a yard where the boards have gone the colour of the boards under them.**
