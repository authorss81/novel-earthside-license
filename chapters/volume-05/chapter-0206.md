# Chapter 0206 — A Refusal With No Reason

A man of about thirty-nine who trades on the Open Hand board said no to a line on the posting on the fifty-first day of the Long Month at about the seventh hour of the morning, in a yard nine miles up the road past this city, in daylight, in front of about nine people, and he did not give a reason, and nobody asked him for one, and about four of them spent the rest of the morning working out whether they had been allowed to.

It is a Thursday. The queue's day is a Tuesday and there is no queue today. The posting has been up five days on a wall in that yard with a date at the top of it and a price under every line, and about four hundred and thirty-one people on eleven miles of flats are not on any of them, and the causeway has had nothing on it for nine days.

---

**And the shortest sentence on that wall is the one that was read into a page in this city seven days ago in a room with a date in chalk on the door, and about four people in that yard had found it already, and it says that nobody may be bought out of a refusal, and that a refusal does not require a reason and is not asked for twice.**

"**No.**"

"**Say it against a line.**"

"**I have said it against a line. I have not said which one and I am not going to say which one. I have been in about nine rooms in three weeks saying a cost out loud in words because a number can be wrong in a way that nobody will notice, and I have spent since Friday making a page that nobody can lie to, and I have now said no on it, and I cannot tell anybody which line, and if anybody in this yard writes down which line they have invented a fact.**"

---

**And a woman of about twenty-four who has written down every decision this city has made since the second day of this flood took a piece of chalk off the ledge under that wall, put it in her hand, and put it back down again, and about nine people in that yard watched her do it, and about four of them have said since that she is the only person in this city who would have been within her rights to.**

"**You did not write it.**"

"**I did not write it and I am not going to, and the reason is on the wall above my hand. A chalk mark on that page is a record. A record is a thing that gets administered. If I put a mark beside that line, then from today a refusal on that page is a thing with a mark on it, and a thing with a mark on it is a thing somebody can count, and about four people in four hundred miles will be able to say who has refused and how often, and that is a column, and I have looked for a column twice and there is not one and I have stopped looking.**"

"**Then how does anybody know.**"

"**About nine people know because they were standing here. That is the whole of the instrument. It is the same instrument this city has had since the seventh week of this flood, and it has been called a cost and a memory and nine people remembering a thing, and I have not got a better one this morning and I have not got a worse one.**"

---

**And a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall said the thing that the fifty-first day of the Long Month turned on, and it is four sentences, and nobody has improved on it, and about four people in that yard wrote it down in their own hands and about four of them have improved on it and the improvements are wrong.**

"**A refusal that cannot be administered cannot be recorded. A refusal that cannot be recorded cannot be counted. That page has no line for a number of refusals and it is not going to get one, and I do not think that is a fault in the page, and I want about four people in this yard to hear the rest of it before anybody in this city decides anything about Friday.**"

"**Say the rest of it.**"

"**The rest of it is that a posted day pays a sack and a place at a fire and a day of the queue, and a person who says no does not get them. That means the right of refusal that cannot be bought out is a right of refusal that cannot be afforded either, and about nine people in this city can afford it and about four hundred and thirty-one cannot, and nobody in that room on Friday thought of it, and I did not think of it, and I have been the man who has argued against that room since the seventh week of this flood.**"

"**Then it is a hole in the fourth line.**"

"**It is a hole in the fourth line, and it is not the same hole as the one in the rule of the Reach, and I am not going to walk four hundred miles to tell anybody they are the same hole. I would like it entered that I found this one on the day I used the right and not before, and that I found it by saying no, and that about four people in this yard have been waiting five days for somebody to use that right so that they could stand here and watch what it does.**"

---

**And the man of about thirty-nine said the rest of it, and it took about four minutes, and he has been refused by name four times in four years and has never once been in a room where a thing he wrote went into a page that did not have his name on it, and that stopped on Friday.**

"**Five days ago I said in a yard that a page with a mistake in it is the first thing this city has built since the seventh week of this flood that anybody can be angry at, and I said that I would rather have that than a wall of good sentences, and about four people in that yard wrote it down and about nine of them agreed with me and one of them is standing in front of me now.**"

"**So I have made one. I have made a page with a mistake in it and I cannot tell anybody what the mistake was, and the mistake is mine, and I have had to say so in front of nine people to get it into the world, and that is the first time in four years that saying no has been the whole of what I had to do.**"

"**And I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to be argued with either, and I would like the nine of you to notice what has not happened this morning. Nobody asked me why. Not one of you. And I have been in about nine rooms in three weeks talking continuously, and it is the first time since the thirty-seventh day of the Long Month that I have said less than nine sentences in a room and got away with it, and I am not going to be the one who breaks it by explaining.**"

Nobody explained. It is entered that about four people in that yard wanted to ask and did not, and that about nine of them have said since that the not asking was the correct thing and that about four of them have also said that they would not have been able to hold it.

---

**And then about four people in that yard noticed the other thing, and it took them about four minutes, and it is the second finding of the day and nobody has written it down in any book yet.**

"**The page does not say whose labour it is.**"

"**It does not.**"

"**The labour is shared and posted, and the posting is bound to the compact, and the compact says the two bodies that made it are the Redroot Commons and the Reed Commons, and there is not one name on that wall. There is not one name on it anywhere. It is bound to a document with two bodies on it and it does not say which of the two bodies a line belongs to, and a man has just said no to a line and I do not think he knows which body's line he said no to either, and neither do I, and I have been in that room.**"

"**That is five days old.**"

"**That is five days old and about four hundred and forty people have read that page, and about nine people worked it out this morning, and about nine people worked it out on Friday and have not said it out loud, and I would like to know which of us is right about the number and I am not going to be the one who counts.**"

Nobody counted. A woman of about twenty-four said that was correct and entered that she had been asked and had not, and about four people in that yard have said since that it is the second time in five days that she has been asked to do the only thing she will not do.

---

**And a woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road came up the road in the middle of the morning and was told about the refusal by about four people before she got to the wall, and she asked the only question she has ever said she can answer, which is which side of a channel is whose, and about nine people in that yard did not know she had been asked that question before.**

"**Then I know the answer to your question and you are not going to like it.**"

"**Say it.**"

"**About four hundred and forty people have worked salt on those flats for nine weeks with no name on anything anywhere, and not one of them has a household that answers to either of the two bodies on that document, and I said that on the causeway four days ago and about four people heard me. The only difference between that and this morning is that this morning it is written down. That is the whole difference and it is not nothing, and it is not a wall with names on it either, and about four hundred and thirty-one people are going to read that page in about four days and work out exactly what I have just worked out, and I could tell you today which four of them and I am not going to because I have been naming people in markets since the seventh week of this flood and I am tired of it.**"

"**Then what do you want.**"

"**I want about four people in this yard to say the sentence out loud. Not to me. To each other, in daylight, on a day one of them chose, with a date on a door. That is the only instrument I have left and it was in this city before either of us.**"

---

**And a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall said one last thing at about the ninth hour of the morning with his hand flat on the wall under the ninth line, and it is the line that says the store and its queue are on this posting and take their place on it and not above it, and he did not turn the page over or touch it.**

"**I wrote that line on Thursday of last week and I have read it four times this week and I put my hand to it because it is the only line in nine sentences anybody in that room loses something by, and on Monday morning I found out what it costs and it cost me nine sentences in a yard, and this morning I found out the second half of what it costs and it is that a man can say no on that page and nobody can tell whether he is refusing my line or the channel or the road.**"

"**I am not taking it off the wall. I want that entered by somebody. I have been the man who says a thing out loud in about nine rooms since the thirty-seventh day of the Long Month and every single one of those nine sentences is on that page, and I put the store and its queue at the bottom of it on purpose, and about nine people in this city now know that the only functioning way out of this world is ninth, and I did that, in a room, in daylight, with a date on the door, and nobody stopped me and I would have gone on if they had.**"

---

**And a line on a wall in a yard with no mark beside it, and a piece of chalk on the ledge underneath it.**
