# Chapter 0237 — Face Down Against Her Leg

A man came eleven miles up the road at the bottom of the Redroot cut on the eighty-second day of the Long Month with nothing in his hands and went to the top of that cut at about the seventh hour of the morning and stopped about nine feet from a woman who had a board in her left hand, and neither of them said anything to the other. The wind came up that cut off eleven miles of flats and it had been doing that since the seventy-fourth day of that month, and the cut has had no water in it since the forty-first day of that month, and that is the forty-first day of it. About four people at the top of that cut had seen a man come up it and about nine of them had not known he was in this city.

---

And the keeper of the Redroot gate was at the top of that cut from first light and had not put the board down once, and it is not on a list of anything, and it carries two dates in two hands and a set of knife marks, and the twenty-fourth is not on it, and nobody at the top of that cut has asked her about any of that in fifty-eight days and nobody is going to today.

"You have come up the bank."

"**I have come up the bank. I have been in this city for two hundred and thirty-seven days and I have come up this bank four times and I have not been asked anything on any of them and I have not asked anything on any of them, and about four people in this city have worked out that that is a use of a morning and about nine of them have worked out that it is nothing to do with anybody, and I have got to the top of this cut at the seventh hour of a Sunday and I am not going to improve on either of those in the first hour.**"

He sat down on the bank above her and about four people at the top of that cut watched a man sit down about nine feet from a woman and about nine of them had already gone down the bank.

---

And a woman of about fifty who counts water on the fingers of her left hand was at the top of that cut at about the eighth hour and looked down into a channel that has been dry for forty-one days and did not go down it, and about four people at the top of that cut have been watching her count since the seventh week of this flood and about nine of them had stopped and had started again since the seventy-eighth day of that month.

"**There is nothing in it and there has been nothing in it since the forty-first day of that month and about four people in this city have worked out that a channel with nothing in it is not a channel that has gone and about nine of them have worked out that it is a channel that is waiting, and I have been standing at the top of this cut four times since the fifty-ninth day of that month and I have not taken one figure off it, and a figure is a thing I take and I have not taken one, and I would like about four people in this cut to notice that I have not taken one this morning without saying anything about it.**"

"You could take one now."

"**I could take one now and about four people in this city would have it by the second hour of the afternoon and about nine of them would have it by the seventh hour of the evening in a town nine hundred miles from here, and there is no figure I could take off that cut this morning that would not be a figure about a hole, and a hole that four people measure is a column about a hole and I have said that out loud twice and I am not going to say it a third time on a Sunday.**"

She put her hand flat on the rail of the bank and about four people at the top of that cut watched a woman hold a rail and about nine of them watched the wind.

---

And at about the ninth hour the keeper of the Redroot gate took her left hand off the board and turned the board over and put it face down against her leg and put her hand back on the outside of it, and she did not stop working the channel. About four people at the top of that cut saw her do it, and about nine of them did not, and nobody said one word to her about it, and she has not said one word to anybody about the twenty-fourth, about the year, or about the fifty-eight days of it.

"What is on the other side."

"**Two dates in two hands and a set of knife marks, and the twenty-fourth is not one of the two, and it is not on a list of anything, and I have not had it out of my hand since the forty-eighth day of the Long Month and I have not put it down once in fifty-eight days.**"

"Then you have turned it round so nobody can read it."

"**I have turned it round so that it is against my leg, and about four people in this city have seen a page go face down since the sixty-fourth day of that month and about nine of them have not, and about four of the four have been waiting since the sixty-fourth day of that month to see whether a second one came, and there is not going to be a second one from me, and I have not said one word to anybody about why I have done it and I am not going to, and a year is a year and it has not run out.**"

She said the last three sentences to the boards of the bank and not to anybody, and about four people at the top of that cut heard both of them and about nine of them heard nothing at all.

---

And a woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road was at the top of that cut at about the tenth hour with nothing in her hands and she saw the board go face down against a leg and about four people in that cut had seen her see it and about nine of them had not, and she has not chosen a day to ask in since the fifty-sixth day of that month and has not chosen one today.

"You saw that."

"**I saw it and I am not going to say a word about it and I would like it noticed that I did not say a word about it, and about four people in this city have worked out that I have come up this bank four times since the fifty-ninth day of that month and asked for nothing every time and about nine of them have worked out that I came up here this morning to see that, and I did come up here this morning to see something and it was not that, and I am not going to tell this cut what it was.**"

"You could tell me."

"**I could tell you and I have a number in my mouth since the forty-seventh day of that month and I have not spent it in a yard and I am not going to spend it on a bank, and there is a woman nine feet from me with a page turned face down against her leg and about four people in this city would say that is the moment to say a number and about nine of them would say it is the worst possible moment, and both of them are right and I am not going to do it in front of either.**"

---

And a man of about fifty-two who digs was at the top of that cut at about the tenth hour of the morning and had come up that bank to look at a channel and had looked at it and had not gone down, and he has not put a rod in a hole since the fifty-ninth day of that month and the hole nine feet from the one everybody measures has filled in with the wind since the seventy-eighth day of that month, and about four people at the top of that cut have known both of those things and about nine of them have only known one.

"Is that hole still there."

"**That hole is not a hole and it has not been a hole since the seventy-eighth day of that month, and I put a rod in it on the fifty-ninth day of that month to find out one thing about a sack, and I have not told anybody what the one thing was, and about four people in this cut have worked out that I have not said what it was because I have not found it out and about nine of them have worked out that I have not said it because of what it is, and I have not said it to anybody on either count.**"

"You will put another rod in."

"**I will not. I said on the seventy-eighth day of that month that I am not going to start a third time for a man who wants a figure, and about four people in this city have worked out that a man who stops measuring and says so out loud is a man who has found the end of the thing he was measuring and about nine of them have worked out that he has not found the end of it and has run out of reasons, and both of those were said in that cut and neither of them was to me.**"

He said the last two sentences to the woman of about thirty-four and not to anybody else, and about four people at the top of that cut heard both of them and about nine of them heard one.

---

And Adrian Vale was still sitting on that bank at about the eleventh hour when the light came off the flats and went up the far side, and about four people at the top of that cut had been waiting since the fifty-fourth day of the Long Month for somebody to say a name and about nine of them said nothing, and nobody asked him about a gate and nobody offered him anything and he asked for nothing.

"You have been sitting there four hours."

"**I have been sitting there four hours and I have said about nine sentences to about four people and two of them were to you and neither of them was a question, and about four people in this city have worked out that a man who can be fetched has sat on a bank for four hours and been fetched by nobody, and about nine of them have worked out that he was not there for a page and he was there for a hole, and I was on the seventy-eighth day of that month at a cut eleven miles from here and a hole nine feet from the one everybody measures has filled in with the wind and a rod in it has come up about four inches, and I have not told anybody that either.**"

"You could have told me that."

"**I could have told you that and I have told you two sentences about it now instead, and the two sentences are the same thing and one of them is longer, and about four people in this city have worked out that is how I talk and about nine of them have worked out that I do not always know it, and I have been on a bank for four hours and I have not worked out which of those is true.**"

"You have not asked her either."

"**I have not asked her and I have not said one word to her, and about four people in this cut have been waiting since the fifty-fourth day of the Long Month for somebody to say a name in front of her and about nine of them have been waiting for a man to say one to her, and the two of those are not the same and I have not said a name to her and I am not going to, and a woman with a board in her hand at the top of a cut is not a room and I have been in about nine rooms in this city where a date on a door was the whole of a thing and I have not been in one of them on a Sunday.**"

He got up off the bank and went down it and about four people at the top of that cut watched a man go down a bank he had been sitting on since the seventh hour and about nine of them had gone inside before he got to the bottom.

---

And a board turned face down against a leg at the top of a cut, with a hand flat on the outside of it.
