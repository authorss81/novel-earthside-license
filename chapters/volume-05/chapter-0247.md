# Chapter 0247 — A Broom

A woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road came up the bank of the cut on the Reed Reach on the ninety-second day of the Long Month at about the ninth hour of the morning with a broom under her arm, and she put it down on the boards of the bank at the top of that cut about nine feet from the keeper of the Redroot gate, and she did not say what it was for. The channel has had no water in it since the forty-first day of that month, and there is a thumb's depth of the dust off eleven miles of flats in the bottom of it, and the wind has been putting more of that dust down that cut since the seventy-fourth day of that month, and about four people at the top of that cut had come up that bank in the four days before and the rest had not come up it at all.

---

And the keeper of the Redroot gate was at the top of that cut from first light with the board in her left hand, where it has been since the forty-eighth day of that month, and she did not stop working the channel, and she did not look at the broom for about the length of a breath and then she looked at it once and did not look at it again.

"That is yours."

"**It is mine and it has been mine, and I have brought it up here and I have not said what it is for and I am not going to, and I would like it noticed that I have not said, because I have come up here to be at the top of a cut on a day that is not a Tuesday and to put a thing down.**"

"You have brought a broom to a gate."

"**I have brought a broom to the top of a cut and I have put it down about nine feet from where you are working and I have not given it to you, and there is a difference between those two things that I have thought about on the road for about nine days, and I am not going to explain the difference to a person who works here.**"

---

And the dust in the bottom of that channel had a shape on it where the wind had come down the cut and turned at the corner, and the shape was the width of a board and about four feet long, and the keeper of that gate had been working that channel for two hours and had not swept it and had not been asked to by anybody.

"Does that want sweeping out."

"**It does, and a person who sweeps a channel for a keeper is a person who has taken a thing over, and a person who sweeps a channel for a keeper is a person who was asked to by nobody and did it anyway. I have got both of those and I have not decided which of the two I would be, and I have not got a broom, and I am not going to use that word again this morning.**"

"Where does it come from."

"**Off the flats. Eleven miles of them, and about four hundred and forty racks on them, and a wind that has not stopped since the seventy-fourth day of that month, and a cut that has had no water in it since the forty-first day of that month, and about four people in this city have worked out that a dry channel is a channel that is waiting and nobody has worked out that it is a channel that is filling with the wrong thing.**"

"Will the wrong thing go out."

"**It will go out when there is water to carry it, and I have not got a day for that and I am not going to invent one, and about nine people in this city have asked me in a year and a bit when the water comes back and not one of them has asked me in a way that wanted an answer more than a date.**"

---

And the broom lay on the boards of that bank where she had put it, and the handle was about four feet long and the bristles were worn flat on one side, and the wind came down that cut at about the tenth hour and moved the bristles about an inch and left them.

"You will come back."

"**I have been up this bank four times since the fifty-ninth day of that month and asked for nothing on any of them, and this morning I have brought a thing up it and left it, and those are two different uses of a morning, and I have not got a reason for either of them that I would put in front of a person in daylight with a date on it.**"

"Then what will you say if somebody asks."

"**I will say a broom is on the boards at the top of this cut, and if somebody wants a day with that on it they can have the ninety-second day of the Long Month, and nobody has worked out that I have not got a name on it, and that is the part I would want read.**"

---

And the keeper of the Redroot gate worked that channel from the first hour to the eleventh hour on a morning when there was nothing in the channel to work, and about four people who came up that bank after the tenth hour stepped over the broom and the rest did not see it at all.

"**Off the flats, and there is nobody on those flats but about four hundred and forty people, and not one of them is in this cut and not one of them can be, and I have known that since the seventy-fourth day of that month and I have said it out loud twice and both times to somebody who had not asked me.**"

The woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road said that to the boards at the top of that bank and not to the keeper, and the keeper of the Redroot gate picked the stick up again and went on with the invert and did not look up and did not say one word about four hundred and forty people.

"And I have worked that channel from the first hour today and there is a thumb of the flats in the bottom of it, and about four people in this cut have worked out that a keeper is a person who keeps a thing, and nobody has worked out that there is no water in it to keep and I have kept the grit off a stone instead."

"You are not going to move it."

"**No. I put it down at the ninth hour and I have not touched it since and it is not going to be a thing I come back for, because the moment a person has to come back for a thing they put down, that thing is a rope and about nine people in this cut have worked out that a rope is a tool and the rest of them have not worked out anything.**"

---

And the woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road had come up that bank four times before the ninety-second day of that month and had asked for nothing on any of them, and about four people in this city have worked that out and the rest have not, and none of the four times was on a day that a queue stood in a yard nine miles up the road.

"You have never come up here with a thing before."

"**I have not, and nobody has worked out that I have come up here four times because a bank with a person on it is the only place in this flood where nobody has ever asked me for anything, and the day I bring a thing up it stops being that, and I did the arithmetic on the road and I came anyway.**"

"You are sorry."

"**I am not sorry and I have not got the time for it. I have had a number in my mouth since the forty-seventh day of that month and I have not spent it in a yard and I am not going to spend it on a bank, and I have not spent it in a market either, and nobody has worked out that I am not saving it. I am keeping it off a page.**"

"Where is it now."

"**It is in my mouth and it has been in my mouth for forty-five days and it is not on a page and it is not on a wall and it is not on a board, and nobody has worked out that the only thing I am holding it for is the day somebody asks me for it.**"

---

And the light came off the flats at about the eleventh hour and went up the far side of that cut, and the broom was on the boards of the top of it, and the woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road went down that bank at about the eleventh hour and picked up nothing.

She got up off the boards where she had been sitting and her knees were white with the dust off the top of that bank, and she beat them once each against the side of her leg and the dust came off in two pale smears and stayed on the cloth of her skirt, and she went down about nine feet of that bank and stopped at a flat stone about four steps from the bottom of it and stood on that instead of the boards. The wind was still coming down that cut and the broom was still on the boards and neither of them moved about an inch in the time it took her to go down, and the keeper of the Redroot gate did not watch her go and did not say one word about the two smears on her skirt.

---

And the broom lay on the boards at the top of that cut for the rest of the ninety-second day of that month, and about four people came up that bank after the eleventh hour and stepped over it, and nobody at the top of that cut picked it up and nobody took it away, and the keeper of the Redroot gate walked past it about nine feet once more before the light went off the flats.

---

And the keeper of the Redroot gate had a knife in her right hand at about the tenth hour of that morning and she was not cutting anything, and the board was under her left arm with the face of it against her sleeve, and about four people at the top of that cut have known for about four years that she keeps that knife for the knife marks and not for anything else.

"You have not looked at it."

"**I have not looked at it and I am not going to, and the knife is for the marks and the marks are for a thing I keep a count of without a page, and nobody has worked out that one of the two is on a board nobody can read and the other one is on her.**"

"How many marks."

"**That is not a question and I am not going to treat it as one, and if you want to know what is on that board you can come and look at it, and the face of it is against my sleeve and it is going to stay against my sleeve, and nobody has worked out that a person who has said come and look and has not turned the board round has decided something about herself and not about you.**"

She went back to the channel and the knife went back into her belt and the board came off her sleeve and went into her left hand again the way it had been since the forty-eighth day of that month, and the broom lay on the boards about nine feet off and neither of them was touched.

---

And the keeper of the Redroot gate was working a channel that has had no water in it since the forty-first day of that month, and about four people at the top of that cut had worked out in about four years what her work is on a day like this and nobody had ever said so out loud, and she said it herself at about the tenth hour to a woman who had not asked.

"You are cleaning a dry channel."

"**I am clearing the invert, and the invert is the bottom of a channel and it is the only part of a channel that nobody else can do, and about four people in this city have worked out that a person who clears the bottom of a thing that is not working is a person who is preparing for a thing to work, and nobody has worked out that I am not preparing for anything, I am taking the grit off a stone with a stick because that is what the stick is for and there is about a thumb's depth of the flats on it.**"

"Nobody would know."

"**Nobody would know and about nine people in this city have worked out that a thing nobody knows about is a thing that cannot be counted, and nobody has worked out that I keep a count of it on a board that is on no list and I have done since the sixth week of this flood, and that is the whole of what I do up here when there is no water, and you did not ask me for it and that is the only reason you have got it.**"

---

And a broom lying along the boards at the top of a cut, with the bristles toward the water and the handle out.
