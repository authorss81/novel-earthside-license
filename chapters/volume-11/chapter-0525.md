# Chapter 0525 — The Cost Said First And A Column With Room In It And No Vote

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register was standing with both hands flat on the working slate on the table under the one window at about the ninth hour on the two hundred and tenth day of the Bare Month, a Monday, and about nine people were in that room and none of them had been sent for anything. The register stood on its shelf behind the bench with its spine to the wall and the cover shut and it did not come off that shelf that morning, and the second slate stood on its edge behind it with a day across the head of it and nothing whatever under that day, and neither of them was picked up and neither of them was turned over and neither of them was written on, and no hand in that room but hers went near that shelf and hers did not go near it either. The four figures of the same kind of thing were standing on the face of that working slate one under another in the order they were made in, and the day beside each of them, and the register's middle column was ruled into five lines and one figure was standing at the foot of it with a day of its own written beside it, and the four lines above that one were empty, and the column on the right was empty from the top of the page to the bottom.

She said it first, before anybody in that room had said anything, and she said it in her own mouth and she said it once.

"**Only a count made on foot can be shown a person.**"

"**I am going to choose one of the four of them this morning, and choosing one of them does not mend the other three, and a person four hundred miles off is going to be handed one of them and is not going to be told that there were four, and that is the bill and it is mine.**"

---

A man at the far end of the bench said the thing that is said, to the slate.

"You have not said which one of the four."

"**The one off the most recent morning. I am not going to say why that one goes and there is nobody in this room who can say why that one goes, and the reason is not that it is the best of the four and it is not that it is the nearest to the one on the wall.**"

A man at the end of that bench nearest the window said the thing that is said, to the table.

"That is four figures in a column and one of them going out of this city."

"**All four of them going into the column that holds figures, one under another, in the order they were made in, and the top one of the four is the one that goes, and the column on the right stays exactly as it is and nobody is ever going to be told why by me.**"

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board was at the near end of that bench with his own board under his arm and the face of it out into the room, and there are nineteen rates on it and a space about two fingers wide along the bottom of the middle column of it with nothing in that space, and he looked at the four figures on the working slate and then at the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register and said the four seconds' worth, once.

"**You are going to send the one that is on the top and the one on the top is the newest, and the newest is the one anybody would write down last, and there is not one reason for that in this room and there is not going to be one.**"

Nobody in that room said that he was right. Nobody improved on what he said and nobody thanked him for saying it and nobody asked him where a column of a book is read from, and the nineteen rates were still on the face of his board and the space in it was still not filled when he sat down.

---

There was no vote. Nobody in that room put a question to the room and nobody in that room put a hand up and nobody in that room said that the thing had been agreed to, and the four days that would have been the cooling are not available in this city and there has been no eighth notice in this flood and there is not going to be one. A run of fifty days has two days in the middle of it, and this is the nearer of the two of them, and neither of them is the middle of anything else. The oldest of the four figures was made fourteen days ago, measured from the first morning of the count on foot, and the newest of them was made four days ago, measured from the fourth morning, and there is no fifth morning of that count in this city and there is not going to be one.

Nobody filled a place on anything that morning. Nobody produced a document and nobody named a place and nobody in that room was asked to do anything, and the register stayed on its shelf with its spine to the wall and its cover shut, and about four people in that room had worked out that the real reason the top one goes is that it is the one anybody would write down last and not one of the nine could say why that is a rule.

She paid for it in that room and not one of the nine people in it said one word to her about it, and the four figures of the same kind of thing are still standing on the face of a working slate under the one window with the day beside each of them, and the middle column of this city's only public book is shut with one figure standing at the foot of it and nothing whatever in the four lines above that one, and the column on the right is empty from the top of the page to the bottom, and the nearer of the two middle days of this range is gone and it was not the middle of anything.
