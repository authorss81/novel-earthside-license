# Chapter 0509 — A Man On The Floor And Nine Words About A Shape

The hundred and ninety-fourth day of the Bare Month was in chalk on the door at the back of the room over a market, a Saturday, and the light was low and coming along the flags. About nine people were in that room by about the eighth hour and none of them had been sent for anything. Behind the bench there is a shelf on two brackets, and the shelf has been on those brackets since before the water came, and the brackets are not the same height at the front of them as at the back. The register stands on that shelf with its spine to the wall and the cover shut. Behind the register a second slate stands on its edge on the same shelf, leaning back, with its face in to the wall, and there is a day scratched across the head of it and nothing whatever under that day.

---

A man at the window said the thing that is said to the shelf and not to anybody.

"That thing has moved in the night again."

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall said the thing that is said, to the window and not to the shelf.

"**It moves about the width of a finger every time somebody puts a hand on that shelf, and it has been doing it every morning since I started coming in here, and there is nothing in this room that will hold a thing still except the two brackets it is standing on.**"

Nobody asked him anything else about it. The register-keeper was in the room and she had not gone near that shelf and the second slate had not been picked up and had not been turned over and had not been written on, and no hand in that room but hers goes near that shelf, and hers did not go near it that morning.

---

Adrian Vale got down onto the floor of that room with his back against the bench and his two hands under the front edge of the shelf. His left hand went flat on the ledge under that second slate and the fingers of it came over the front edge of the shelf itself, and that was all of it, and he had nothing else in his two hands. On the ledge under the slate there was a straight break in the dust about the width of a finger, and the break had a soft edge on one side of it and a hard edge on the other, and the soft edge was the way it had come.

The second slate was walking on that shelf. It had been walking on that shelf since the day it was put there, and it walked about the width of a finger every morning and about the width of a finger every time a hand went near it, and it walked towards the back wall because the back bracket of that shelf is the higher one and a thing on a shelf that is not level goes downhill on it.

He wanted something flat to go under the far edge of that slate. He had looked for it from where he was lying and there was nothing flat within reach of the ledge and he did not go and get anything and he did not send anybody to get anything, and he did not put anything under it. He wanted it to stop walking. It did not stop.

---

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall came out from behind the bench with the chair in his two hands and put the chair down about a foot out from under that shelf and then turned it round and backed it to the wall, and the chair is where it was before. He put his left knee on the seat of the bench and got his weight on it and put his hands on the top rail of the shelf, and the shelf came forward on its two brackets about the width of a finger.

It did not go on coming forward. Adrian Vale had both hands on the front edge of that shelf and the ledge under that slate and he held it where it was, and the shelf stopped about the width of a finger out and no further, and the second slate on it stopped with it.

The man of about thirty-four got his eye to the gap that had opened between the back of the register and the wall, and looked down through it for about as long as it takes a man to count nine, and came down off the bench, took the chair up off the flags and put it back where it had been standing, and stood in the middle of that room and said the thing out loud, and said it once.

"**That one place is the shape of the second column in her book.**"

Nobody thanked him for saying it. Nobody in that room said whether it was so and nobody said whether it was not, and about four people in that room had been looking at the one empty place on the sheet on the table since the fourth hour, and about four of them had not, and the man who had said it did not say it again and did not say one word about how anybody in that room ought to use either of the two things he had named.

---

A man at the far end of the bench said the thing that is said.

"He did not even open it."

"**He did not have to open it. He has watched her open it in this room and he has watched her close it, and a man who has watched a book shut knows what shape its columns are, and that is the whole of how he knows it.**"

Nobody improved on that. The man of about thirty-four had a piece of chalk of his own in his own inside breast pocket, and the day that piece of chalk went into that pocket is the two hundred and ninety-eighth day of this flood, which is the forty-third day of the Short Month, and it had not been out of that pocket since. His own board was standing face in against his own trestle out in the row and the bare back of it was out in that row, and he had come up that stair without it and had said nothing about it on the way.

---

He came off the floor of that room backwards with his hands off the front edge of the shelf and off the ledge and onto the flags, and he got the grit that was on that ledge into the two lines of his palms doing it, and the two lines of his palms were grey to the first joint when he stood up. The register stood on that shelf with its spine to the wall and the cover shut, and the second slate stood on its edge behind it with its face in to the wall and a day across the head of it and nothing whatever under that day, and neither of them had been picked up and neither of them had been turned over and neither of them had been written on. The slate is a hand's width above where that man's hands were, and it is a hand's width further back on that shelf than it was an hour before, and there is nothing under it.
