# Chapter 0541 — A Bill Named By Somebody Who Did Not Pay It

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register put the flat of her right hand down on the table under the one window, palm on the wood, beside the bone, and left it there. The two hundred and twenty-sixth day of the Bare Month was in chalk on the door at the foot of the stair at the back of that room and it was a Wednesday, and no name has ever been written under a date on that door. About nine people were in that room by the ninth hour and not one of them had been sent for anything. The sheet that is shut on that table with two folds in it was where it was put down the day before with the bone beside it, and nobody in that room had opened it since it was folded.

---

The light off the one window came across that table and stopped about a hand's width short of the far edge of it, and where it stopped it made a straight line along the wood that did not move for about as long as a man stands at a gate. The stair at the back of that room creaked once under somebody going down it and once under somebody coming up it, and neither of them was a person anybody in that room knew.

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board was at the near end of that bench with his own board under his arm and the face of it turned out into the room. The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall was at the window with his hand flat on the sill. The man of about thirty-one who carries things for a living was at the far end of that bench with his hands on his knees and the satchel with the flap on the seat beside him and nothing in it.

---

A woman near the step said the thing that is said, to the table.

"Nobody has said what that decision cost."

A woman near the far end of that bench said it to the table, and she said it once, and she is not the woman who paid it.

"**I am not the one who paid it and I am going to say what it cost anyway, because it was paid in this room in front of about nine people and every one of us heard it being paid and not one of us has said one word about what it was, and I will say it once and I will not say it again.**"

Nobody in that room improved on that. Nobody in that room thanked her for saying it, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register did not take her hand off that table and did not say one word back to her, and she is the one who paid it and she said nothing whatever about it.

---

A man at the near end of that bench said the thing that is said, to the window and not to the bench.

"You are speaking for her."

"**I am not speaking for her and she is sitting at that table with her hand on it and she can speak for herself and she has not chosen to this morning, and what I am putting in the air is not her bill and it is mine, and I have had to put it in the air because there is no place in this room where a bill gets written down.**"

Nobody said that she was right. Nobody improved on her and nobody thanked her, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register turned her head once towards the two of them and then put her hand flat on the wood again where it had been, and about four people in that room had worked out before the woman at the far end opened her mouth that she was the one who paid it and about four had not.

---

What she put in the air was this, and she said it once, and it was not a figure and it was not a document and it was in the local language. That a thing counted by a person on foot across eleven miles with that person's own time is the only kind of figure of this city that anybody can be shown a person for, and that when a room chooses one of four of them and sends one of them out of this city, the three that stay do not become wrong and are not corrected, and the three that stay are not a correction of the one that goes and the one that goes is not a correction of the three, and a person four hundred miles off from this city is going to be handed one of the four and is not going to be told there were four.

Nobody in that room counted anything out loud. Nobody in that room asked her where the four came from, and nobody in that room asked her who walked the eleven miles, and nobody in that room said the name of the person who made the figure that is in that sheet, because nobody in that room can say it. About four of the nine people in that room could have said the eleven miles out loud and about four could not.

---

Nobody wrote any of it down. There is nothing in that room to write it on and nobody went to the shelf behind that bench for anything at any hour of that morning, and the register was not opened and nothing was entered anywhere in that room on that morning, and the column that has been empty since it was decided empty was still empty.

The bone lay on that table where it was put down the day before and nobody picked it up and did not move it, and the folded sheet lay beside it with its two folds in it and its one place against the wood, and the window light stopped a hand's width short of the far edge of that table and the man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall kept his hand on that sill and did not turn round.

The bill is in the air in a room where nothing has been written down, and about four people in that room know which of them paid it and about four do not, and the woman near the far end of that bench has not said it again and is not going to.
