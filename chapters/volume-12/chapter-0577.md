# Chapter 0577 — The Fold That Is The Fold

Adrian Vale put the flat of his own left hand on the near corner of that sheet where it lay on the table under the one window and put his own right hand flat on the bone that was lying beside it at about the ninth hour on the two hundred and sixty-second day of the Bare Month, a Thursday, and the one window of that room had the shutter at the back of it stuck since the light came good and had not been opened at any hour since. The morning's date was on that door in chalk at the foot of that stair and a name has never gone under a date on it. There were about nine people in that room at that hour and every one of them was in that room for something of their own.

The sheet that came up that road in a satchel with a flap has a heading at the top of it and one place ruled under that heading, and the one place is not empty, and the figure in that one place is right and it is written out on no page in four hundred miles and it is a figure of this city that this city did not make. There is one place on that sheet and there has never been a second one and there is nothing under that figure and nothing beside it.

---

The fold on that sheet was twice and a half with a bone, and that is the fold the first sheet of that shape was folded on in this flood, and about nine people were in that room while it was being done and not one of them said one word about what it means that it is the same fold, and about four of them were looking at the near corner of the sheet while it was being done and about four were not.

The man of about thirty-one who carries things for a living was at the near end of that bench and he had had that bone in his own right hand and he watched it go across that table and he said the thing that is said, to the sheet, and it took him about four seconds to say it.

"**A sheet that goes back up that road is folded to be opened by somebody who is not going to know what is in it, and this is the fold the last one of them went out with, and I did not open that one on the road either and I am not opening this one in this room.**"

Nobody in that room said out loud that he had it right. Nobody in that room improved on him and nobody in that room thanked him for it and nobody in that room asked him a second question about it, and the one sheet of that shape that went out of this city did so in a satchel with a flap and is not in this city and is not coming back and nothing in this flood has an instrument for bringing a figure back.

---

He put the bone back down on that table where it had been and it had salt on it that had not been on it before, and the shutter at the back of that room was not opened with it at any hour of that morning, and the room was at the back of that market with the light off one window until about the half hour after ten.

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall said the thing that is said, to the table, and it was short.

"**Nobody asked him to do that and nobody in this room is going to say a word to him about it now, and there is a man in this city who has been doing a thing nobody asked him to do at the fourth hour every morning for the whole of this flood and he is at the other end of that market and he is not thanked either.**"

Nobody in that room improved on that and nobody in that room thanked him and nobody in that room said that he was right, and the man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall put his own hand flat over his own inside breast pocket and did not say another word at any hour of that morning.

Nobody in that room said a word to Adrian Vale about any of it. He was at the near corner of that table with his own hands on the near corner of that sheet and his own coat open at the front, and he was not asked about the fold and he was not thanked and not one of them said that he was right.

---

Through that one window the store's board can be seen on the outside wall at the end of that market, up on its two nails, carrying three sets of figures of which none has been rubbed out and none has anything under it, and about four people at that end of that market stop at the end of that row on their way past, read one set of them, and go on their own way. The lid of the box on the step below that window stands open and the store's own chalk is in it, and it was not used that day, and the piece of chalk in the breast pocket of the man at the far end of that bench was not used either. That sheet came up four hundred miles of open road and nobody in this city has ever stood at the far end of it.

Four mornings, four figures and no fifth, and the mornings were the five hundred and eleventh day of this flood, the five hundred and fifteenth, the five hundred and seventeenth and the five hundred and twenty-first, and every one of them was got by a person with a slate under one arm and a tin in two hands, and the four of them do not agree, and nobody in that room counted anything and nothing in that room was a count.

At the near end of eleven miles of flats, a long way from the door of that room, the man of about thirty-eight at that salt wharf turned his own tally-board over on the top of his own stack at about the tenth hour with one hand on the near edge of it, and it is face up now and it is not going to be put back, and nobody in that yard said one word about it and nobody in that room thanked him for it and nobody in that room thanked Adrian Vale for anything.

Behind that bench the register stands on its shelf with its spine to the wall and five figures in the column of it that holds figures, and on that shelf beside it a second slate stands edge up and face to the wall with a day cut across the head of it and nothing whatever under that day, and it was not picked up and it was not turned over and it was not written on, and the keeper's was the only hand that came near that shelf that day.

That bone is lying on that table where it lay before it was used on that sheet, and there is salt on the flat of it that was not on it this morning, and nobody in that room wiped it and nobody turned it over, and it is the only thing on that table that the fold changed.
