# Chapter 0585 — Eleven Miles And No Figure Coming Out Of It

She started at the near gate about the sixth hour in the morning on the two hundred and seventieth day of the Bare Month, a Friday, with a slate under one arm turned in flat against her own ribs and a tin in her two hands, and she went out along the channel on the low side of those flats with the water still standing in the channel from the night before. The light was behind her going out and in front of her coming back and the wind came off the low side in long pushes and did not settle anywhere between the two ends of that ground. About four people walked that ground in the ordinary course between the sixth hour and the middle of the afternoon and not one of them was walking it with a slate and a tin, and not one of them came into the same two hundred yards that she was in.

The face of that slate was turned in to her own body the whole way. The lid of that tin was down and her own fingers were round it the whole way, and it was not opened at the near end of those flats and it was not opened at the far end of them and it was not opened anywhere between the two, and about four people walked past a woman carrying a shut tin on that ground and not one of them looked down at it.

---

A salt wharf stands at the near end of those flats and a yard is in front of it, and the man of about thirty-eight was at the open end of that yard with a knife in his own right hand and the handle of it turned away from him. He said the thing that is said, to the man beside him, and it was short.

"**She has been out there since before the light and she is coming back the same way and there is nothing on that ground this morning that anybody has to count.**"

That man beside him said the thing that is said, to the yard.

"Somebody ought to go and ask her what she is doing."

"**Nobody is going to go and ask her anything, and I have not asked her anything in this flood and I am not going to start this morning, and you can go and ask her if you want to and you will not.**"

Nobody in that yard thanked him for it and nobody in that yard improved on it and nobody in that yard said that he was right, and about four people at that end of that yard heard the two of them say it and about four did not, and the man of about thirty-eight at that wharf did not say another word that morning, and nobody in this city has been shown a person who can say where a figure of this city comes from.

---

The four mornings that anything has ever been counted on that ground are named here and not on any other surface in this city. They are the five hundred and eleventh day of this flood, the five hundred and fifteenth day of the same flood, the five hundred and seventeenth day of it and the five hundred and twenty-first day of it, and there was one kind of thing being counted on each of those four mornings and it was counted on foot across eleven miles of flats by a person with a slate under one arm and a tin in her two hands, and there were four figures at the end of the four of them and all four of those figures were entered in a book in a room over a market in the order they were made in. The four of them do not agree with one another and they are not going to be made to agree in this city and nobody in this city has ever been asked to make them agree.

There has not been a fifth morning. There is nobody in this city who is going to walk out over that ground again for the purpose of counting a thing, and this morning was not one and nothing came out of it, and no figure of this city was made on the two hundred and seventieth day of that month by anybody at any hour of it, and the person who made the other four was the person who walked this one, and nobody on that ground knew that and nobody in this city is going to be told.

---

She came back onto the stone at that near gate about the middle of the afternoon and the lid of the tin was still down and her own two hands were still round it and the face of the slate was still turned in against her own ribs, and about four people were on that ground at that hour and not one of them looked at her, and the channel on the low side of those flats was lower by the width of a hand than it had been at the sixth hour in the morning, and no figure came off that ground on that day and there is nowhere in this city to put a fifth one, and nobody in this city has asked her for one.
