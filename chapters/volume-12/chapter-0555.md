# Chapter 0555 — The Form Read Out With A Date In Chalk On The Door

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register stood at the head of the table under the one window at about the ninth hour on the two hundred and fortieth day of the Bare Month, a Wednesday, and the two hundred and fortieth day of the Bare Month was in chalk on the door at the foot of the stair at the back of that room with no name under it, and about nine people were in that room already and had been in it for the better part of an hour, and nobody in this city sent a notice for anything and there is no eighth one. Nobody in that room had been told in advance what the morning was for, and that is what the four days before a thing is for, and there have not been four days before anything in this city for five volumes running.

She put her own two hands flat on the wood on either side of that sheet, the one that came up that road in a satchel with a flap, and she said what it was for before anybody asked her.

"**This is a form with a heading on it and one place ruled under that heading, and there is a figure of this city in that one place and this city did not put it there, and the form is to be answered with a figure of this city and to go back up that road, and the figure that is already in that place is not going to be rubbed out and nobody in this room is going to be told what it is a figure of.**"

---

She read it out in the local language and she read out the heading and then she read out the one place and then she stopped, and she did not read out any figure, and the number in that one place is not on a page of this book in any form. She said what it was for twice and she said it the same way both times, and about four people in that room had understood the whole of it by the time she finished the first time and about four had understood about half of it, and not one of the nine said so.

The room heard that there is one place and that the place is already filled and that a figure is going into it. The room did not hear what the figure is going to be a figure of. There is no place on that sheet for the person who made it and there never was one, and nobody in that room asked who made the figure that is already in that place, and nobody in that room asked the man of about thirty-one who carries things for a living what was in the satchel that carried it up that road, and he was at the far end of that bench with his hands on his knees and did not say a word.

---

A man at the near end of that bench said the thing that is said, to the table.

"So the form goes back and nobody has put a date on it for going back."

"**It goes back on the quarterly round with whoever is going up that road that week, and that is the ordinary end of a form of that shape, and there is no place on that sheet for a day, and the day it went is on that room's own door in chalk and it will not be on the form.**"

Nobody in that room improved on her and nobody thanked her and nobody in that room said that she was right, and the man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board was at that window with his own board under his arm and the face of it turned out into the room and he looked at that sheet once and then out of that window at the board on the wall of the market below it with its three sets of figures on it and said one thing in about four seconds, and nobody in that room asked him what it was and nobody in that room said that he was right.

---

She put her own two hands under the near edge of that sheet and lifted it off the wood and held it up off that table with the two of them at the height of her own chest so that the row at the back of that room could see the one place in it, and about four people at the back of that room stood up off the bench they were on to look at it and about four did not, and she held it there for about as long as it takes a man to walk from that window to the far wall of that room and back, and then she set it down on the same part of the wood it had been on, with the same fold in it and the same way round, and the bone was still where it had been put down and nobody in that room picked it up at any hour of that morning.

---

There are six documents in this flood of that road and three of them have four places ruled under a heading and three of them have a single place ruled under a heading with a figure in it, and not one of the six was laid beside another at any hour of that morning and not one of the six was in two hands at once and not one of the six was compared out loud in a mouth in that room. The first of the three of one place is standing on the shelf behind that bench and it was not brought down off that shelf and not looked at. The second of the three of one place went out of this city in a satchel with a flap and is not in this city and is not coming back. The third of them is on the table under the one window with a figure of this city in its one place that this city did not put there.

That sheet stayed on that table all morning and nothing was written anywhere in that room and no figure went into that one place. The bone was picked up at the eleventh hour and put into the inside pocket of a coat, which is where it goes, and the room went out one at a time, and the last man out pulled that door to behind him, and the two hundred and fortieth day of the Bare Month stayed in chalk on the outside of it where anybody coming up that stair can read it.
