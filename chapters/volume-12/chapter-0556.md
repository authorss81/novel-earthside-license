# Chapter 0556 — A Wall And A Book And About Four People Who Knew It

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board was at the near end of that market at about the seventh hour on the two hundred and forty-first day of the Bare Month, a Thursday, with his own board under his arm and the face of it turned out into the row, and he stopped where a man stops when he has stopped, and the row went round him. The store's board was on its two nails above the trough against that wall with three sets of figures on it, and the trough was on its own spot and the rag was on the stone lip of the step and there is not a line of words under any of the three of them. About four people at that end of that market were in that row in the ordinary course and about four were not, and about nine people were in the room over that market at the same hour.

---

A woman with a stall in that row said the thing that is said, to the wall.

"There is a number on that wall and there is a number in that room upstairs and they are the same kind of number."

"**A wall of figures is read across and a book of figures is read down, and one of those is made in an office nine hundred miles off and came down here on a form with one place under a heading, and the other two were put on in chalk in this city on two mornings, and they are of one kind of thing and they are not the same thing, and nothing in four hundred miles is going to turn one of them round and stand it beside the other one.**"

Nobody in that row said that he was right. Nobody in that row improved on him and nobody in that row argued with him and nobody in that row asked him which of the three of them a person is meant to be reading, and about four people in that row had worked out that the number that came down that road and the numbers on that wall are of one kind of thing and about four had not, and not one of the two halves said one word to the other half about it.

---

A man near the gate said the thing that is said, to the flags.

"Then write the same number on the wall and it is finished."

"**Nobody in this city is going to put a figure on that wall and there is a piece of chalk in a box on that step that is the store's and it is not anybody's, and if two of these three are of one kind of thing that is not a reason for them to be of the same value, and the two that are in chalk on that wall do not agree with each other now and nobody has rubbed either of them out.**"

Nobody improved on that and nobody thanked him, and the man of about twenty-seven who washes that board was at his own trough at that hour with his own hands in the water and his back to the row and was not asked anything by anybody in that row, and the box of chalk stayed on that step with its lid standing open and about a thumb's length showing.

---

He set his own board down against the wall of that store on its long edge with the face of it out into the row at about the eighth hour, and he put his own heel on the bottom rail of it to hold it, and about four people in that row read that board at that hour and about four walked past it without stopping at it, and after a while he took his own heel off it and picked it up and put it back under his own arm with the face of it turned in, and nobody in that row said a word to him about doing either of those things.

---

Seven sheets have gone out of this city in this flood and they went out in the two volumes before this one and there has not been an eighth one, and nobody in that row wrote anything and nobody in that row said that a sheet was going out of this city that week, and the room over that market is not going to have a sheet put up on its door telling anybody anything. About four people in that room had worked that out and about four had not.

Nineteen rates are on that board in three columns with the rows going across. Along the foot of its middle column there is about two fingers of bare board with nothing whatever on it, and there was a name in that space in his own hand and he took it off himself about nine years ago and has not said so to anybody since. About four people at that end of that market have noticed that there is a space on that board at all and about four have not, and nobody in that row has ever asked him what was in it.

---

The figure in the one place of that form came up that road four days ago, measured from the day it came up it, and it is lying on a table under one window two floors and one stair above that wall, and the man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board put his own thumb along the top rail of his own board on the way out of that row and left a mark about the length of a thumb on the paint of it, and about four people in that row watched him do that and about four did not, and the mark was still on the rail of that board at the ninth hour.
