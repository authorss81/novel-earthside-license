# Chapter 0560 — Three Sheets Counted And Two Numbers About A Room

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register took three sheets off two places and set them on the table under the one window at about the ninth hour on the two hundred and forty-fifth day of the Bare Month, a Monday, and set them down one at a time in three different parts of that table with a length of the wood between each of them and the next. The stair at the back of that room has a date in chalk on the door at the foot of it and there is no name under that date and there never has been one. About nine people were in that room at the ninth hour and nobody in this city sent anybody a sheet to tell them to come.

---

One of the three came off the shelf behind that bench, where it has stood at the height of a person's shoulder since it was read out in this room a long time ago, and it has never been returned and has never been withdrawn. One of the three was already lying on that table and has a figure of this city in its one place that this city did not put there. One of the three came off a nail by the door of that room and has four places ruled under a heading on it and nothing whatever in any of the four.

The three of them were not laid beside one another at any hour of that morning. None of the three was ever in one hand with another of the three, and none of the three was compared out loud in a mouth in that room. There are six documents in this flood and three of them have four places ruled under a heading and three of them have a single place ruled under a heading with a figure in it, and one of the three of one place went out of this city fifteen days ago, measured from the day it went out, in a satchel with a flap, and it is not in this city and it is not coming back and nothing in this flood has an instrument for bringing a figure back.

---

A man at the far end of that bench said the thing that is said, to the table.

"That is three and there is a book on that shelf as well and you have counted three out of four things in this room that are paper."

The man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board was at that window with his own board under his arm and he said it to the table and it took him about four seconds to say it.

"**You have counted three and she has counted three and the two of you have counted the same three, and a register is a book and a book is not a form and it does not go up that road, and there are six of them in this flood and four of them have come down that road and two of them have gone up it, and nobody in this room is going to say one word about where any of the six came from.**"

Nobody in that room said that he was right. Nobody in that room improved on him and nobody in that room thanked him and nobody in that room asked him a second question about the six, and about four people in that room had worked out that there are six documents in this flood and about four had not, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register put her own hand flat on the wood and did not say a word back to any of them.

---

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall was at that window with his own hand flat over his own inside breast pocket. The piece of chalk in that pocket has been in it since the two hundred and ninety-eighth day of this flood, which is the forty-third day of the Short Month, and it was not out of that pocket at any hour of that morning and it is not going to come out of it in this city. The chalk in the box on the step below that window is the store's chalk and its lid stands open and about a thumb's length of it is showing, and that chalk is not his, and neither of those two chalks was used at any hour of that morning. That sheet of four places came down that road four hundred miles and it is a measure of a distance and not a place anybody in this room has stood at the end of.

Over the back of the chair at the far end of that bench there is a length of new rope and it has not been used and no hand in that room lifted it at any hour of that morning and nobody in that room looked at it once.

---

The three sheets went back to the two places they came off in the order they came off in, and the register went back on its shelf behind that bench with its spine to the wall, and behind that register the second slate stood on its edge with its face in to the wall with a day cut across the head of it and nothing whatever under that day, and it did not move and it was not turned over and it was not written on, and no hand in that room but the keeper's went near that shelf all morning.

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register counted that room's own papers in her own head while she was shutting that book and got a number, and did not say it, and the man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board had said a different one out loud, and the two of them are two numbers about a room and not about this city.
