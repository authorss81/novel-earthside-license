# Chapter 0179 — A Question About a Term

A wage came out of a store on the morning of the twenty-eighth day of the Long Month and went into nine hands on a flat eleven miles away, and about four people in this city have said that the two things that happened at about the ninth hour of that same morning, three hundred yards apart, had nothing to do with each other, and that they have had nothing to do with each other since the first day of this flood, and that a man of about thirty-two took four days to get to the other half of it and has not said it yet.

**It is a Tuesday. The queue at the store up the road past this city works on a Tuesday and the wage went out on one, in the shape a man of about thirty-four in this city invented out of a stall, with the payer's name on the line and the paid man's off it. And on the same morning a woman of about thirty-six who is a public official and is on nobody's side was asked a question she is allowed to look at and not to answer, and she looked at it for about four minutes.**

---

The wage is a day and a share and a ration, and the ration is a sack and not a figure and about four people on that flat have argued about the size of the sack for about thirteen days, and it arrived at about the ninth hour in nine sacks and the ninth sack was the size of the other eight and that was the whole of the finding on that part of the day.

"**It is the same,**" said a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf, with a sack on her shoulder. "**It is the same sack and I have been wrong about it for about thirteen days and I am not going to say so to about nine people, so I am saying it here, and I am not going to say it anywhere else.**"

"You are not wrong about it," said a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall. "**You were right about it on the fourteenth day and you have been arguing with about nine people about a thing you were right about, and I have watched you do it, and I did not stop you because I wanted to see whether you would stop yourself.**"

"That is a horrible thing to have watched."

"**It is. I would not do it again and I did it.**"

---

**And at about the ninth hour of the same morning, three hundred yards away, in a gateway with a door that shuts, a man of about thirty-two asked a question properly for the second time in a hundred and eighty-three days, and the instrument took four days to build and it was used on a Hearthguard of forty-four once, and it worked, and it worked again, and what it got him was nothing.**

The instrument is this and it is in a book in this city: the question in the person's own words, given in writing to everybody who will be in the room four days beforehand, anybody who cannot be given it not going to be in the room, a date on a slate, and nobody asking the person whether the day suited them.

He gave it four days. He wrote it out himself. He gave it to about nine people on the Friday and the person he was asking it to on the Saturday, and about four of the nine came back and changed a word in it and he left their word in, and one of them took a word out and he put it back.

And the question is this.

"**Is a workway offered by a person who is party to an instrument, in a room, in daylight, with the whole of its cost read out before the accepting, a breach of the attached page of that instrument?**"

And the attached page of the trial licence says, in the words of a page that has been on a table in four hundred miles for a hundred and eighty-three days: *no workway of any kind, offered to anybody, to anybody else, now or at any time, by that instrument or by anybody using it.*

**And it is a real question and it is a correct question and nobody in this world can answer it, and the reason nobody can answer it is not a secret and it is not anybody's fault and it is on the page in a gateway with a door that shuts.**

---

The woman of about thirty-six read it twice and then said the part that is the chapter.

"That is a real question and it is correctly framed and there is a column for it."

"**Is there.**"

"**There is a column for it and the column is the seventh and it has been there since the instrument was drawn and about four clerks in nine hundred miles have used it in nine hundred years, and the answer to a question in that column goes back down a road that does not exist on any map in this building, and I have looked.**"

"How long."

"**Six days.**"

"Then it is not a question you cannot answer. It is a question you cannot answer for six days."

"**It is both, and the second one is the one people get wrong, and here is why, and I am going to say it in about four sentences and then I am not going to say it again.**"

She put the page down on the table and squared it, which is a thing a clerk of the fourth desk does and which about nine people who have watched her do it have found is the whole of what she is.

"**A question in the seventh column goes to a desk. A desk is a room. In about four months somebody in that room will read a yes or a no, and if it is a no, a person will be told that a person who is party to an instrument may not offer a workway, and if it is a yes, a person will be told that they may. And on the day that answer arrives, what happens to a family that is being paid out of a store for nine days of a week?**"

Nobody answered her and about four people in that gateway have said since that nobody answered her because the answer is that nothing happens, and that the woman of about thirty-six said it herself in the next sentence, and that is why she is on the page.

"**Nothing. That is the answer and it is a correct one and I am a public official and I am on nobody's side and I have said it out loud. The answer will be correct and it will be about four months from now and every wage in it will have been paid and every day of it will have been let through, and a correct answer about a term of an instrument is worth nothing to a family and it was worth something to a man standing in a gateway, and I would like that entered and I would like whoever entered it to enter that I am a clerk and I am not on his side.**"

---

**And a man of about thirty-two said the next thing himself, and he said it in about four minutes, and it is on the page in his own hand and a woman of twenty-four was not in that room and wrote it down afterwards and did not improve on it, and it is the whole of what happened in that gateway on the twenty-eighth day of the Long Month.**

"**Then I have two questions and I have had them for about a week and I have not put them next to each other, and I am putting them next to each other at about the tenth hour of the morning on the twenty-eighth day of the Long Month, in a gateway, in daylight.**

**The first is: is a workway offered in a room by a party to that instrument a breach of the attached page. And the second is: a man of thirty-two on no roster of anything at his own request, in his own hand, with the date on it, holding no mark and no workway and no title and no seat and no wage and no land right, asked a clerk of the fourth desk of an office nine hundred miles away, in a gateway, about a term of an instrument, and the clerk has read it and has said the column exists.**

**The first question is real and the second question is the reason the first one is worth nothing, and I have known the second one since the sixty-fourth day of the month after this one and I have not let the two of them touch in twenty-nine days, and I have let them touch this morning because a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf said in a yard on Monday that she has been angry at the wrong thing since the first day of this flood, and she was not talking to me and I heard her.**"

"The man holding nothing cannot be a party to an instrument."

"**He cannot. Not because anybody decided it. Because the instrument bars anybody whose standing comes from him, and I put that in it, and I wrote the longest page in this city and there is not one thing in it they may do, and I said on the sixty-fourth day of the month after this one, in about two hundred and forty people, that a man with no mark and on no roster is the most findable person in this world and that this is not a safety, and I have not said it since and I am not going to say it again this month.**"

"Then who asks it."

"**Somebody with a standing. And there are about nine of those in four hundred miles and every one of them is party to something, and one of them is a man of forty-two who has no office and claims none and stands in no roll and has a hundred and forty people who work for him on a day.**"

"And will he ask it."

"**He will offer to pay for it. That is what he will do, and he will do it out of a store, and if he does that then the answer to a question about a term of an instrument belongs to a man who has an interest in the answer, and I have about six days to work out how to stop that, and I am not going to work it out in this gateway, and I am going to write it in a book that does not travel and I am going to date it.**"

---

**And then a man of about thirty-nine who trades on the Open Hand board walked about four hundred yards up a road and told the man of about thirty-two one thing about the channel at Nine Elms, and it is on the page because it is the third thing on that water in nine days that nobody can act on, and it is the oldest.**

"**The fourth channel of that junction has been shut seventy-one days. It was shut by a fourth line of a term sheet that is in force and was never given up. The quarter that was to pay for it is gone. The undertaking that was to pay for it is void and is on a nail on a river and no office has been told. And that junction has not taken water in a hundred and fourteen days, and it is the only junction in four hundred miles where a commons keeps a record of who may use a water gate, and the record has been kept for nine years and every name on it is alive, and there is a channel on that junction that is shut and there is nobody in four hundred miles who can open it, and I have walked past it nine times this week and I have not asked anybody anything about it because a description is a removal with a column for where the person is, and I am telling you because you are the only person I know who is not going to write it down.**"

"**Then I will not write it down.**"

"**You are going to write it down. You have just written it in a gateway. But you have not written it in a book, and that is the difference between a man with a page and a man with a memory, and I have watched you for a hundred and eighty-three days and that is the only difference I have found.**"

---

**And the last thing on the page is a page, in a hand that is not a clerk's, with a question in it in the second person, and a date on it, and a square of chalk dust on a table where a clerk of the fourth desk squared it, and six days.**

**And on the twenty-eighth day of the Long Month the twenty-fifth day of a delay Adrian entered on day one hundred and sixty-four expires in six days, and he has not broken it, and it has cost a household nine days of a week, and on the last of the six a thing that was going to happen on a day is going to happen or it is not going to happen.**
