# Chapter 0089 — A Document That Cannot Be Angry At

On the sixty-fourth day of the new month, which was the eighty-ninth day of the flood and a Saturday, a notary of fifty said a sentence in a room over a market for the third time in ninety days, having said it on the forty-sixth day of the new month about nine copies and having not answered a version of it on the fifty-ninth and it was aimed at a document instead of at nine copies, and a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf and whose name is not on anything said nine sentences that are the reason that document cannot be checked and cannot be refused, and both of those are true at once, and a form was drafted in about eleven minutes, and nobody could say who it was about, and a case went down to a wharf at the seventh hour of the evening with a page in it that nobody had signed and nobody had refused.

**The licence is thirty-nine days old. The undertaking is on a nail in a chapel arch, is three days old, has been read aloud about nine times, and goes to a building nine hundred miles away this evening.**

---

**Sera Vail had said it on the forty-sixth day of the new month, about nine copies made by hand in a city of eleven hundred people, and she had said it in eleven words, and she had entered that she could not un-ring it, and the un-ringing is the part she minds.**

> **A document nobody may copy is a document nobody may check.**

"That was about nine copies," she said, at about the third hour of the afternoon, "**and it is the third time I have said it, and I have said it in a room both of the other two times, and I have been sitting here since the second hour asking myself which of the three is the real one and I have decided that it is not mine to decide. I am saying it now because it is also about a document nobody may check, and because a document that nobody may copy is only a copy of itself, and this one is a document that everybody may copy, and it is the first time in eighty-nine days that the eleven words have pointed the other way.**"

**Nobody in that room had noticed the repetition and about nine people in that room noticed it in the next four seconds, and the two of those are not the same number of people, and the difference is the whole of what happened in that room on the sixty-fourth day of the new month.**

"A sentence said twice is a mechanism," said a woman of twenty-four. "**I said that in a yard behind a chandlery on the forty-eighth day of the new month and I have not stopped saying it, and I have been waiting since the forty-eighth day for a notary to say a thing twice about the same subject, and I would like to say now that I did not know I was waiting and that it has happened and that the room has to sit with what it means about sentences.**"

"It means we are both repeating ourselves," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall.

"**It means a room that has heard a sentence has not answered it,**" said the girl of seventeen, who had heard a notary of fifty say a version of it on the fiftieth day of the new month in about nine seconds and had written it down then and had not been asked to use it and was using it now, "**and it means that if a sentence is said a second time and it is still true then it is not a finding, it is a standing, and a standing cannot be refused, and I have just described the document on the nail using a sentence about sentences and I would like somebody to tell me whether that is a joke.**"

Nobody told her whether it was a joke.

---

**And then a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf stood up at the back, and about nine people in that room had not known she was in it, and about four of them had not known her name and it is not on anything including the four names on the levy of the first Wayhouse, because she sells no goods on the road and pays the levy on what the others pull.**

She had been in nine rooms in four days. Nobody had asked her anything in any of them, and she was entered on no roll, and she had no work that anybody expected her at, and about nine people in this city have a reason about that and none of the reasons is a good one.

"I am going to say nine sentences," she said, "**and I am not going to say them twice, and I am not a notary and I am not on a roll and I am not one of the people that paper costs, and that is the only reason I have got anything to say, and I would like it entered that I know that is the reason and that I am using it.**"

It is entered that all nine went in in her own words, and they are in the book, and about nine people in that room heard all nine and about two hundred and thirty people have not and will be told a version of four of them with a shape in it and none of the shape will be in this.

"**One. I have been in nine rooms in four days and nobody in any of them asked me what I think of it, and I would like that on the page in front of my name, or in front of no name, because there is no name.**"

"**Two. I am not in that guild and I would not be in it, and I am not one of the people it costs either, and that is the whole of my qualification and I have never had a better one.**"

"**Three. I have read it four times and I cannot find a lie in it, and I have been looking for one since Monday morning.**"

"**Four. That is not because it is true. It is because every sentence in it is a sentence somebody in this city said out loud, and a sentence somebody said out loud cannot be a lie, and that is the entire way it was built and it took a man with a book and nineteen years and no opinion whatever.**"

"**Five. A page made out of true sentences is harder to argue with than a page that is not, and every person in this room has spent eighty-nine days learning that, and about nine of us learned it too late and that is my fault as much as anybody's.**"

"**Six. You cannot check it. Checking a document means finding a sentence in it that is not true, and there is not one, and that is not a thing anybody in this room has ever been able to say about anything.**"

"**Seven. You cannot refuse it. Refusing a document means being angry at something, and there is nothing in it to be angry at, and I have been trying for four days and I have a stall and I am not going to be one of the people who says she tried.**"

"**Eight. Those two things are usually the same thing, and they are not the same thing here, and nobody in this city has ever been in a place where they came apart, and I have been in about nine rooms in four days and not one of them had it in it.**"

"**Nine. I am telling you this because it costs me nothing to say, and I would like one thing in this city to cost nothing to say, and I have not had one in four days.**"

Nobody argued with any of it. **It is entered that nobody argued, and that about nine people in that room understood that the reason the document cannot be checked and the reason it cannot be refused are the same reason, and that the same reason is that it was built entirely out of things this city had already agreed to, and that a city cannot argue with itself in public without arguing with about two hundred and thirty people who did not hear the argument.**

---

**Then a pale field came up in the air in front of about nine people and stood there for about nineteen seconds, and it was the twenty-fifth thing the wall had ever said in this city, and nobody in that room answered it, and it went away.**

> **A form about a person may be drawn by any person in this district. The person the form is about need not be told that the form exists. The cost is entered. There is no further cost.**

**Nobody refused it. Nobody accepted it.** It is entered that the room was drafting a form about a person at about the fifth hour of the afternoon and that the field arrived in the middle of that, and that the form being drafted was about nobody, and that there was nobody in the room for the field to be refused by, and that a girl of seventeen asked which book it goes in and that nobody could tell her, which is the fourth time in eighty-nine days that she has been in that position. **The count in the second book kept at home, which has never been shown to anybody, is twenty-two and did not go up, and a field that nobody answers goes in neither book, and the running number of things the wall has said in this city is twenty-five, and it has never once registered a refusal of one of them.**

---

**After that somebody drafted a form in about eleven minutes, and it is four lines high, and it is the shortest form anybody in this city has written since the nineteen lines, and it was meant to make a document refusable.**

> **This undertaking is offered to me by name.**
>
> **I may refuse it. My refusal is entered. My refusal does not stop the undertaking. The undertaking proceeds without me. I am not on any roll by having been asked.**
>
> **Name. Date. The person asking. My answer.**

"That is four lines and it is perfect and it would work on a person," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall, who has been the second name on four things this month and is not going to be the second name on a fifth. "**And it does not work, and everybody in this room can see that it does not work, and I would like the notary to say why in one sentence so that it stops being in my head.**"

Sera Vail read it twice. Then she said one sentence.

"**Who is it about.**"

And nobody in that room could answer, and it is entered that nobody could answer, and that the question took about nine seconds and that about four people in that room actually tried, and that the reason none of them could answer is that the undertaking on the nail is offered to a hundred and forty people by a hundred and forty people and there is no person in it, and a form about a person cannot be used on a document that has no person in it.

"Then it is a form about nobody," said a man of about thirty-one with a hand that will not close.

"**Then it is a form about nobody, and it is entered anyway, as an unfinished thing, and unfinished is allowed in this city and it is the only word I have got,**" said Sera Vail, "**and I am entering that a form about a person was drafted on the sixty-fourth day of the new month in about eleven minutes and that four people in a room could not say who it was about, and that it is on the page unfinished, and that nobody is to be asked to sign it and nobody is to be asked to fill it in and that whoever completes it completes it in daylight in a room with a date on it or does not complete it at all.**"

**It is entered that about nine people in this city have now built a thing they cannot name, and that it is the first time since a docker of about sixty stood up in a hall on the fortieth day of this flood and said that a record is not a fence, and this is the other half of that sentence, and nobody has put the two halves next to each other, and about nine people have noticed that they belong together.**

---

**Then a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall read the four lines out in a yard in daylight in front of about two hundred and forty people, and it took him about a minute, and it was the reading that showed what a document is.**

He read it in the local language first and then in English. He read it at the ordinary volume, standing up, with nobody behind him holding it flat, the way a keeper reads a binding in a yard, and about nine people at the front of that yard heard every word of it.

And then a girl of seventeen, who was standing at the back, said one sentence out loud, and it is in the book in her own words, and it is twenty-eight words, and it is the reason this city is going to have trouble in about four months and nobody saw it coming.

"**Nobody at the back of this yard can hear him, and that is what a document is: the four lines are for the nine people at the front.**"

---

**And at about the seventh hour of the evening a wooden case went down to a wharf with a strap and a wax and a second strap, and Peth Lammay carried it, and the undertaking went in it unsigned and unrefused with the fifty-ninth day of the new month on it as the date it was written and the sixty-fourth day of the new month on it as the day it left, and a case that carries a review is a case an office will read before it reads anything else, and this case carried a review and a second page and a schedule of crossings and an undertaking that nobody had signed and nobody had refused, and it did not carry the form about nobody, and that is entered, and the reason it is entered is that about nine people in that room wanted it in the case and about nine people in that room did not and there was one tie and the tie was the girl of seventeen and she has said since that she did not know how she voted.**

"A case with something in it that nobody wanted is the best kind there is," said Peth Lammay. "**I get paid the same either way and the office has to read all of it.**"

On the quay, a clerk of the Registry at Aurel who has sat at a table in this city since the fourth day of the flood and has never once been asked to stop stood and watched the case go into a boat, and the girl of seventeen asked him whether the page was going in a case or going into a roll, and he said that was not a thing anybody knew, and that he kept a book at Aurel and a second book at home and that the two had never once agreed with each other and that he had entered that they never had.

**It is entered that he was not asked about either book by anybody in that crowd except a girl of seventeen, and that he answered her, and that a man of forty-one in a very good coat was standing four yards away and did not come over, and that this city has not convicted him of anything and is not going to.**

---

**Nothing was gained. A notary said eleven words for the third time and a woman of twenty-four pointed out that a sentence said twice is a mechanism and a girl of seventeen observed that a room which has heard a sentence has not answered it. **A woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf and whose name is not on anything stood up in about forty people and said nine sentences in about four minutes, and they are the reason a document cannot be checked and the reason it cannot be refused, and those are the same reason, and about nine people in that room understood it.** A pale field came up in the middle of a room drafting a form about a person and offered that a form about a person may be drawn without telling the person, and nobody refused it and nobody accepted it, and the refusal count in the second book kept at home is twenty-two and did not go up, and a field nobody answers goes in neither book. **A form of four lines was drafted in about eleven minutes that would have made the document refusable, and it is a form about a person, and a notary of fifty asked who it was about, and nobody in that room could answer, and the form is entered as an unfinished thing.** A document went to Aurel in a wooden case unsigned and unrefused with a date on it, and a hundred and forty people will read a case that carries a review before they read anything else in it. Adrian Vale is Stage 1, Witness, Unbound, on no roster of the licence and on no roster of the instrument, holds no mark of his own, no workway, no title, no land right, no class privilege and no seat, and is party to a trial licence thirty-nine days old and is given nothing by it. He performed no working today. The black line in his right palm is eighty-eight days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch. The main breach is closed, dressed, measured and silent and has never said a word, and one ruled line is left in the measurement column and it is not filled and the twenty-fifth reading has not been taken. The Narrow Mark was not opened. The chapel stone is silent and is not repaired. The ground under the chapel is a scar, is not repaired, and the boards have not been lifted since the fortieth day of this flood. The form on the nail has four items with the price of the salt written under them and a fifth item that is a question and not a duty, and none of the five can close anything or say where a residue goes or say who is in a room. A room with no window and no door anybody has found is three knocks and four knocks into being knocked on, both entered and neither deleted, and nothing was knocked this week and nothing was used as a reason to open anything. The fee of eleven ounces of silver is entered as owing with no date for the thirtieth time and the salt was not sent. Nine Elms' water is not received, the fourth line of a term sheet is in force, and the whole of the Reed Reach's standing charge is on a man of thirty-two. The field was not paid on the first of March and is not going to be paid this month.**
