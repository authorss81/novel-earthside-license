# Chapter 0078 — A Work With No Office

On the fifty-third day of the new month, which was the seventy-eighth day of the flood and a Tuesday, a woman of forty-one did a work that there is no office for, on a resonant place with a boundary cut into it, in the wrong weather, in the wrong company, and nobody had asked her to, and nobody was going to ask her, and the residue of it went into her arm, and about nine people were close enough to see it happen and four feet back of it there was a man from another world who was not barred from anything and could not do a single thing about it.

**The licence is twenty-eight days old. The door is shut. March is seven days off. The answer to the eleventh question of the review is four words long, it is on a page, and yesterday this city added a line saying that the person an instrument costs may be asked once, on a named day, in daylight, in a room, and the answer may be no, and the named day is five days from now.**

**She had said yes twice in about four seconds each time and nobody had asked her whether she meant it, and as of yesterday nobody in this city is in a position to ask her, and she said that out loud in a room over a chandlery on the Monday night before she went, and about nine people heard her say it and about two hundred and thirty had not been in the room.**

Perry Lask, forty-one, of the second list, came out of the chapel floor on the twenty-fifth day of this flood with ten other people. She said *this is a closed beta* in a yard on the ninth day and had it entered that she said it first. On the thirty-second day of the new month she said yes in about four seconds to the nineteenth field, and said one sentence first: **that she had been waiting since the ninth day of this flood for the wall to say something she recognises, and that she was not going to pretend to a room that she checked it first.** On the fortieth day of the new month she said yes in about four seconds to the twentieth field, and said nothing at all first, and the not saying is the finding.

The twentieth field said: **a holder's work may be described, in any document, in her own words, without anybody asking her first; nobody may be asked to describe it; the holder does not owe the standing charge; the cost is entered; there is no further cost.**

"A rule that only covers the thing you can see is a rule about a thing and not about the world," she said on the thirty-second day, in a room over a chandlery, in about nine people, and it is her sentence and it is the volume's.

**And on Monday night she said the other thing, and it is in the book, and she said it in eleven words and she is forty-one and she is the only person in this world who holds a workway:**

> **Nobody can ask me anything until the day you named yesterday.**

"Then I will not ask you," said Adrian Vale.

"You are the only person in this city I would have answered," said Perry Lask. "**And you put a line on a page yesterday that says a person the instrument costs may be asked once, on a named day, and the answer may be no, and the day is a Sunday five days from now, and that line is between me and every question anybody has got, and it is there because you fought two hours and ten minutes for it, and I want somebody to write down that I know what it is for.**

"**What is it for.**

"**It is for making sure that nobody can be asked about what they are doing. I have been doing that for seventy-eight days and I have never once asked you what you wanted and now I never can, and I would like that in the book in my own words and I would like it on the page with the line.**"

It is in the book. It is on the same page as the line. The girl of seventeen wrote it in a column four lines high and the width of that column is entered as a fact for the seventeenth time in this year, and the whole of the entry is that.

---

**She went out at about the fourth hour of the afternoon on the Tuesday, and she told nobody, and she was gone about four hours, and there is no procedure for any of what she did, and there is no office, and there was nobody with her because nobody knew she had gone.**

The Lock of Salt is a resonant site because three jurisdictions repeat the same maintenance oath there. The boundary stone is nine miles up the upper road and four miles off the Reach road, at a place where the road forks and the ground drops away on the north side. It is a stone about the height of a man's chest with a cut in the flat of it, and the cut is a line, and the line is about a hundred and forty years old, and the line is a decision.

Perry Lask walked nine miles in the wrong weather with a bar and a folding rule and no paper, and she did not tell anybody she was going, and it is entered that she did not tell anybody and that the reason she gave afterwards is fifty-three words and that the reason is hers and she has not decided whether to give it.

"She went at about the fourth hour," said the girl of seventeen. "**I know because I was on the wall with the slate and the slate says the last time anybody wrote anything was the seventh hour of the morning and the next thing is now.**"

**What she did is entered, and it is entered because Mina Okoye entered it, and Mina Okoye is the only person in this book entitled to say the sentence that is the load-bearing rule about it, and she said it in a room in about nine people and about nine seconds and it is in the book in her own words and hers alone:**

Nobody in the room claimed it. It has been in this book since the thirty-ninth day of this flood, in four settled pages about a man who is under a board behind an ordinary wall, in her own words and credited to her, and **no character may claim to have coined it, and nobody in that room did, and it is entered that nobody did, and that a sentence with a person in it standing in it is worth four hundred sentences with no person in them.**

**And what Perry Lask did was this, and it is entered as what a person said and not as a finding, and it took her about four minutes and there was no witness and no cost entered beforehand.**

**She answered a promise.** The river made a promise about where the boundary is. It is not a promise anybody made to her and it is not a promise anybody heard, and it is a hundred and forty years old, and it was made by people who are not in this book, and it is cut into a stone at the top of a road with a knife, in one hand, on one morning.

She put her hand on the cut and she let the workway do the only thing it is a permission for, which is to understand one direct promise at a time, and the promise came up out of the stone the way a voice comes out of a black line in a floor, and it was not in a language, and it was a promise about water, and it is a hundred and forty years old and nobody alive has been told it and nobody alive has agreed to it.

**And the residue went into her.**

**Adrian Vale was four feet back, on the north side of the fork, in the wrong weather, with about nine people, and there was no instrument to bar him from anything and no bar and no page, and that is the whole of the problem and it took him seventy-eight days to arrive at.**
