# Chapter 0094 — The Fourth Day of the Month After This One

On the fourth day of the month after this one, which was the ninety-fourth day of the flood and a Thursday, a woman of forty-one of the second list was given a day she had been given in a hurry thirty-seven days before, in a room over a chandlery on the upper road, in daylight, in front of about nine people, and she used it, and about nine people in that room understood that a bad procedure had just been made worse on purpose by the person it was about, and a man of thirty-two offered himself a seat with a name on it and was told by an instrument that there is nobody, and said eleven words, and did not say anything else about himself for the rest of the volume of days.

**The licence is forty-four days old. The undertaking is void. The field was not paid on the first of March. The black line in his right palm is ninety-three days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch.**

---

**The date was put on the page in about a day by nine people on the thirty-second day of the new month, which was the fifty-seventh day of the flood, because about four of them said that was the soonest it could be reviewed, and it is the fourth day of the month after this one, and the page says the review is a day on which the holder may say one thing.**

Nobody in that room asked her anything else. It is entered that nobody did, and that this was not kindness and not tact, and that the line which stops the asking was fought for in about two hours and ten minutes on the fortieth day of the new month, which was the sixty-fifth day of the flood, and stands, and that the person it belongs to is not going to be asked anything on this date or on any other.

What the room did instead was read out four things, in the order they were decided in, and the order was put there on purpose, and the reason is in the book in the words of a notary of fifty: **the three refusals first and the question last, so that a refusal of the question takes the refusals down with it.**

"One," said the girl of seventeen, reading. "**I refuse.**"

"Two. **I refuse.**"

"Three. **I refuse.**"

"And the fourth," said the notary, "**is a question, and the question was asked once, on the fifty-eighth day of the new month, which was the eighty-third day of the flood, by a man of thirty-five who asked it because a man of twenty-four was not allowed to, and the answer was nine words and it is in the book in her own words, and it is hers, and it may be taken back today, and nobody is going to ask her whether she wants to take it back, and that is the point of saying so out loud.**"

Nobody in that room asked her whether she wanted to take it back.

---

**And she did not take it back, and she did not say it again, and at about the second hour of the afternoon she did a third thing, and the procedure of nine clauses and nine weaknesses had no line for it, and she said so before she did it, which nobody in ninety-four days has ever done in a room.**

"I am going to say one thing," she said. "**And I am going to say it before anybody asks me anything, because if anybody asks me anything first then what I say is an answer, and it is not an answer. Write it down in my words. And do not make it a form. And if anybody in this room makes it a form then it is not what I said and you should strike it.**"

Nobody struck anything, because nothing had been written yet.

"**I am not asked again, and I want it written that I said no to the second asking and not to the work, and those are two different things, and this document cannot tell them apart, and that is the ninth weakness of it, and I have just found it, and I have fixed it, and I have fixed it worse for the document than it was.**"

And then she said the rest of it, in her own words, in daylight, in a room, and it is on the page exactly as she said it and nobody improved on it and nobody has asked her about it since.

"**The second asking is in clause four and clause four is written as though I am a thing that gets asked, and I am not a thing that gets asked, I am a person who said yes once in about four seconds to a word in a field I did not check, and I have been sorry about those four seconds every day for thirty-seven days without saying so to anybody, and I am saying so now to nine people and not to a page, and the difference is the whole of what I wanted and I did not get it. So clause four stands and I stand on top of it, and the thing I am putting on this page is not an answer and it is not a refusal of the work. It is that a holder may say no to the asking, once, without a reason, on a day that is on the page before the day, and the asking is not repeated, and the person is not on any roll by having been asked, and if that line is ever read as a power to ask me something else, I did not write it and I would like my name off it.**"

It is entered that nobody in that room asked her a question about any part of it, and that a woman of twenty-four had two questions and did not ask either, and that she entered that she had them and did not ask them, and that entering that is the whole of what she did.

---

**And then a notary of fifty said the thing that had to be said, and it took about nine seconds, and about nine people in that room had already worked it out and about two hundred and thirty people in this city have not.**

"I drafted the procedure of nine clauses and nine weaknesses on the thirty-second day of the new month, and I drafted the amendment of one line this afternoon, and I have not told anybody either of those things until now, and I am telling them now, and I am entering that **I may not grade my own paper.** I have been the notary of every instrument in this city for twenty-six years and I have never once been the person who decides whether my own page is any good, and I am not going to start on the fourth day of the month after this one, and if nobody in this room is able to say whether the amendment is any good then the amendment stands ungraded and that is the correct outcome and I would like it entered that I think the correct outcome is the worst one available."

"It is the worst one available," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall. "**And the amendment is worse for the instrument and better for the person, and both of those are true at once, and I have been sitting here for about nine minutes trying to work out whether a page is allowed to be both, and I have decided that it is, and that nobody in this city has ever had to decide that before.**"

"You have never had to because no page in ninety-four days has ever had a person in it that wanted something," said a man of fifty-eight who cannot read, "**and that is the whole of the finding and I am not going to improve on it and I am not going to be thanked for it.**"

---

**And then a man of thirty-two offered a seat, and the offer was the fifth, and the four before it had all come in the five weeks before the seventy-fifth day of the flood, which was the fiftieth day of the new month, and the instrument has said nobody four times, and it said nobody this time in about four seconds, and this time the seat was not offered by nine people and it was not a Crown advocate and it was not a stipend and it was not a quarterly sitting.**

It was a seat with a name on it and a review date and a standing in this city, in this city's own book, in this city's own words, and the person who put it there was the man it was for, and he wrote it out in his own hand on the back of a copying list and put it on the table face up, and about nine people in that room looked at the hand before they looked at the words.

"**It is not a Crown instrument, it is not this city's, and it is not one of the four that came before it,**" he said, "**and I am offering it to myself, in daylight, in a room, and I want the refusal entered, and I want the offer entered, and I want it entered that I made it because the review of a bad procedure is the day a person looks round the room for somebody to be angry at and there is nobody, and I have been that person four times in ninety-four days and I have never once been on this side of the table.**"

"**Nobody,**" said Sera Vail, and it is the line of the instrument of the twenty-second day of the new month and it has said it since the twenty-fifth day of the new month, and the licence says that nobody answers for the next test.

"I cannot hold a seat," said Adrian Vale. "**I have nothing to hold with.**"

And it is entered that nobody improved on that sentence, and that it is eleven words, and that it is not the sentence he said the first time or the second time or the third or the fourth, and that on the fourth he had said that he had refused it three times and was not going to make a fourth refusal into a performance, and that this time there was no performance in it at all, and that about nine people in that room understood that the reason was the finding of the day before, which is that he has nothing, and that a thing a person is angry at has to be a thing, and that he is not a thing.

"You cannot hold a seat," said a man of forty-four, "**because there is nobody to hold it with. A seat is a place to be angry at, and a place to be angry at is a person in a room, and the room has to be able to reach you, and there is no road in this world that reaches you and there has not been since the fourth day of this flood, and you said that out loud in a yard in about nine minutes on the fifty-fourth day of the new month and nobody has forgotten it.**"

"Then it is done," said Adrian Vale.

**And it is entered that he said nothing else about himself that day, or on any day after it, and that he noticed he had done it, and said so, and that a woman of twenty-four asked him whether he was all right and he said a thing that was not about himself, and that nobody thanked him for the seat and nobody congratulated him on the refusal, and that a man of sixty-one said later that the fifth refusal is the first one anybody in this city has been able to check, because a man who offers a seat to himself has put his own name at the top of it, and because a name at the top of a thing is what makes it a thing that can be angry at, and because nobody has done that in ninety-four days.**

---

**Nothing was gained. A woman of forty-one used a day she was given in a hurry, and refused the second asking and not the work, and said so before anybody asked her anything, and put one line on a page in her own words and would not let anybody make it a form.** A notary of fifty entered that she drafted the procedure and drafted the amendment and may not grade her own paper, and entered that she believes the correct outcome is the worst one available. A man of thirty-two offered himself a seat with a name on it in his own hand and the instrument said nobody and he said eleven words and did not say anything else about himself for the rest of these ten days.

The undertaking is void and is on a nail and on a river and in a wooden case in Aurel. Nine Elms' water is not received, the quarter is gone, the fourth line of a term sheet is in force, and the whole of the Reed Reach's standing charge is on a man of thirty-two. A field of ninety-one acres out of a hundred and forty is not paid, and the cost column has eleven entries in one month, no figure on the end of any of them, and no twelfth. A fee of eleven ounces of silver is entered as owing with no date for the thirty-fifth time. The licence is forty-four days old, in force and weaker, and the door is shut and has been opened twice in ninety-four days. The measurement column has twenty-four readings, both of the last two eight and seven-eighths, one ruled line left, and the twenty-fifth reading has not been taken. The chapel stone is silent and is not repaired, the ground under the chapel is a scar, and the boards have not been lifted since the fortieth day of this flood. The form on the nail has four items, the price of the salt under them, and a fifth that is a question, and none of the five can close anything or say where a residue goes or say who is in a room. The arch carries five things and about nine people have not said what happens to the arch.

Adrian Vale is Stage 2, Passage holder, in the doing, and holds no mark of his own, no workway, no title, no land right, no class privilege and no seat, and is on no roster of the licence and on no roster of the instrument of the twenty-second day of the new month. He performed no working today. The black line in his right palm is ninety-three days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch. **And there are twenty-six things the wall has said in this city and it has never once registered a refusal of one of them, and a city that reviewed a bad procedure and made it worse on purpose is a tenth silence, and the pattern is ten for ten.**
