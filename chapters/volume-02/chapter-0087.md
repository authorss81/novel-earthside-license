# Chapter 0087 — A Guild That Does Not Go Home

On the sixty-second day of the new month, which was the eighty-seventh day of the flood and a Thursday, about nine people went down to a boat-yard at Long Water to find the sentence that made a document safe, and a man of forty-two who has been right in eight sentences out of nine came down with them and was right about this one too, and a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall found the clause in about nine minutes, and a woman of seventy from a village of nine households said twenty-four words that are the whole of the day's finding, and nobody in that room said the word legal.

**The licence is thirty-seven days old. The undertaking is neither refused nor accepted and it has been on a nail in an arch for one day, and a page on a nail is a page people read.**

---

**A man of forty-two came down to a boat-yard at Long Water at about the second hour of the afternoon and he had walked nine miles, and nobody asked him to, and he has been building a settlement on the upper road since the seventh week of the flood and it is called the White Ledger and about nine people in this city have stopped saying the name out loud.**

Jonah Pike is forty-two and he was a warehouse supervisor in Leeds and he has been right about nine parts in ten of almost everything since the sixth day of the flood, and the tenth part is always the same part, and the tenth part is always a local woman's word for a social fact, and on the thirty-fourth day of the new month he said the fifth and the sixth of them in a yard and the sixth of them was that a guild is not a person, and a guild can be refused, and a person can be refused, and the difference is that a guild does not go home and get old and want a different winter — which is a different sentence from a docker's, and he arrived at it from the other end, and neither of them knew about the other.

That was twenty-eight days ago. **It has not been on a page once in twenty-eight days, and the document on the nail is the seventh sentence of a man who has been selling that answer since the third week and selling it badly and on purpose, and everybody in that boat-yard knew it, and he knew they knew it, and he came anyway.**

"I am not here to sell it," he said. "**I am here because it is the best thing anybody has put in front of this city in eighty-seven days and I have been trying to sell something worse for two months, and I would like to be in the room where the better one is being looked at, and I am not going to say anything until somebody asks me.**"

"Say that again in a month," he had said in a yard on the twentieth day of the new month, in about six words, to a man from another world.

"I will not," the man from another world had said.

"You still will not."

"**I still will not,**" said Adrian Vale, "**and I have thought about it for forty-two days and I am not going to and here is the reason and it is not an argument: the reason I would not say it again is that the only person it would persuade is me, and I have been wrong once in public this month and I am not going to be the man who goes looking for the argument that makes him sound careful.**"

**It is entered that about nine people in that boat-yard heard a man refuse an easy thing twice in forty-two days and that about four of them understood the reason and about five of them heard a man be careful.**

---

**And then the question was asked of him, and he answered it, and he was right, and the tenth part of it was a local woman's word, and it landed.**

"What would you do with it," said a woman of twenty-four. "**Not what is wrong with it. What would you do with it, and I am asking because you are the only man in this room who has spent two months trying to sell a worse version of it, and a person who has tried to sell something is the only person here who knows what the buyer wants.**"

"I would hold it," said Jonah Pike, "**because it is the only durable thing anybody has produced in two months, and a durable thing is a thing that is still there when the men who made it are tired, and I have watched four men in this city build the best document ever written about a door and I watched the licence get reviewed by a room of two hundred and forty people in daylight and come out of it weaker than it went in.**"

"And the hundred and forty people it is held by," said a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall. "**They are the ones it costs.**"

"They are the ones it costs nothing," said Jonah Pike. "**That is the entire architecture of the thing and it is why it is durable. It asks nothing of anybody. I have been trying to build a structure for two months and every version of it asks one person for one thing, and that one person is always the person who is hardest to replace, and I have never once found a version that does not come round to a single man. This one does not come round to anybody. It comes round to a hundred and forty, and a hundred and forty people are not a person, and a person is the only thing in this world you can lose.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds, and then a woman of twenty-four read out nine words in the local language, and they were in the book, and they were said in a yard on the tenth day of the new month by a docker of fifty-eight who cannot read, and she has not been asked anything since Sunday, and a man of forty-two has been building the same wall for twenty-eight days without knowing that she got there first:

> **That is a lock that a guild can close.**

"He is right," said Adrian Vale.

"**Say that again,**" said Jonah Pike.

"**You are right, and I am not going to pretend you are not, and I have been trying to get round you for forty-two days and I have not got round you and I am not going to get round you today, and I have entered that I tried and failed and that the failure is the finding.**"

And then a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall, who is owed a season by the Brass Measure and who has been the second name on four things this month and is not going to be the second name on a fifth, said the thing that changed the shape of the day, and it took him about nine minutes and he did it with a pencil on a board because the girl of seventeen had a slate and he would not take a slate off a girl.

---

**The clause is in the first sentence, and it is not a clause, and it is the whole of the document, and nobody had seen it, including the man of forty-one who read the dates out on Monday.**

> **The Concord is a body of about a hundred and forty persons. It holds the lock, it holds the roll, it holds the notary and it holds the gauge. It is not a person. It is not anybody's servant.**

"Read the first sentence again," said the man of forty-four, "**and listen to what it is doing. It is not describing the Concord. It is telling you that the Concord is not a person. And a thing that is not a person cannot be asked separately about anything, and cannot be refused by anybody, and cannot be made to say why it did what it did on a Tuesday, and cannot be got at.**"

"It is honest," said a woman of twenty-four. "**It says out loud that it is not a person. A thing that says out loud that it is not a person is being careful.**"

"It is careful," said the man of forty-four. "**And here is the part nobody has read. An undertaking of this kind is not in force until a person stands at the top of it and answers for it alone. That is what an undertaking is. I have signed four of them. You stand at the top and you are the one who is wrong if it is wrong, and that is the entire difference between a person and a body, and the entire difference between a thing a person can be angry at and a thing a person cannot.**"

"Who wrote that rule," said a woman of twenty-nine of the second list.

"**Nobody in this room, and I have not got it from a book, and I am a man who keeps a stall and I have signed four undertakings and been wrong about one of them and paid for it,**" said the man of forty-four, "**and that is the whole of my authority and I am aware it is the wrong authority and I am using it anyway because it is the only thing in this room that is not a page.**"

"**Then the document has no person at the top of it,**" said the girl of seventeen.

"**The document has no person at the top of it, and no person at the bottom of it either, because the bottom of it says a hand that is not named on it,**" said the man of forty-four. "**And that is either the safety or the hole, and I have been sitting here for about nine minutes trying to work out which, and I am going to say both and I am not going to choose, because I do not know.**"

"The safety," said a woman of seventy, from the step, and then she did not finish it, and nobody finished it for her.

---

**And a woman of seventy was on the step and nobody had walked her down there and she had come on the cart that brings the salt, and she is Ilda Marsh and she is of the fourth house at Nine Elms and she has refused three times in public and forced a fourth line of a term sheet through a meeting-ground with nine men and eleven women in it, and she is the only person in this room whose own house is named in the document on the nail, and it is named in the third clause, and the second clause is the one she is about to read out loud.**

Nobody in that boat-yard had expected her. **It is entered that about nine people in this city are not in the rooms, and that one of them is seventy years old and stands on a step, and that about two hundred and thirty people in a converted salt store yesterday did not know she had come.**

"Second clause," she said. "**The standing charge is received by the Concord and held by it and it does not return and the water of Nine Elms is received and the quarter is restored. I have read that nine times this morning with the help of a girl of twenty-nine and I am going to say what it means in this village and it is one sentence and it is not a thank you.**"

Nobody said anything.

"It means that from the day that page was dated we get our water back, and our quarter back, and the drain in the fourth channel gets cleared by whoever is nearest, and that a man of thirty-two does not have to pay our quarter out of his own body. **I have waited four years for somebody to take that off us and I did not have to do a single thing and I did not have to refuse a single thing, and I want to be careful here, because a favour you cannot refuse is not a favour, and I said that in my own house to a Crown officer on the forty-fourth day of the new month and I have not stopped saying it. And we have put the twenty-second day of the month after this one on our own door in chalk, the way this city does everything, because a village believes a date on its own door and not a date on a page nine miles away, and I have come down here to find out whether that date is anything at all.**"

"And the fourth line," said a woman of thirty-one of Nine Elms, who had walked eleven miles to use it against a man once and had never been thanked for that either.

"**The fourth line is ours and it stays ours and I did not give it up and nobody has taken it,**" said Ilda Marsh, "**and this document does not use it and does not need it and does not repeal it, and in four months when a man in a building nine hundred miles away reads this page he is going to ask a question that none of you have thought of, and the question is: if the charge never returns, what is the fourth line for.**"

**And then she said the twenty-four words, and they are the finding of the day, and they are in the book in her own words, and about nine people in that boat-yard understood all of them and two hundred and thirty people are not going to hear any of it.**

> **A guild that can be asked has a standing, and a standing cannot be refused, and I know it because we have refused three.**

Nobody in that room said the word *legal*.

**It is entered that nobody said it, and that about nine of them had it in their heads, and that a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall said afterwards that this city keeps arriving at the same word by four different roads and has never once been on it.**

---

**And a girl of seventeen asked for the whole of it to be entered, and it was, and the entry is four lines high and it is the widest thing she has ever written down.**

> **A document becomes a thing a person can be angry at when there is a person who can be angry at it.**
>
> **On the sixty-second day of the new month, at a boat-yard at Long Water, about nine people in a room understood that undertaking.**
>
> **Nobody in that room could be angry at a guild.**
>
> **A woman of seventy said a guild that can be asked has a standing. A standing cannot be refused. Both of those are entered as hers.**

**And it is entered that a man of forty-one in a very good coat asked, on the way up the road, whether anybody was going to say the number of days, and that a notary of fifty told him what the number of days was, and that he said he had not asked.**

"What number of days," said a man of about thirty-five.

"**The Crown channel was offered to this city on the fortieth day of this flood,**" said Sera Vail, "**and it is forty-seven days, counted from that day and from nothing else, and he has not mentioned it once, and nobody in this city has asked him about it once, and I have just told him the number to my own hand and I would like that entered, because I am tired of being the only person in this room who keeps a count.**"

"It is entered," said the girl of seventeen, in a column four lines high.

---

**Nothing was gained, and a sentence was found. A man of forty-two came down nine miles to a boat-yard and was right about the document and said so, and a man from another world entered that he had tried to get round him for forty-two days and had failed and that the failure is the finding. **A man of about forty-four who keeps a stall, on his own authority and not out of a book, established in about nine minutes that the undertaking is not in force until a person stands at the top of it and answers for it alone, and that the document has no person at the top of it, and that that is either the safety or the hole and that he is not going to choose.** A woman of seventy stood on a step and said twenty-four words that are the day's finding, and the twenty-four words are hers, and the fourth line of a term sheet is in force and this document does not use it. A body of about a hundred and forty people that holds the lock, the roll, the notary and the gauge was entered as a thing that can be asked, and about nine people in that boat-yard understood that a guild that can be asked is a guild that has a standing, and a standing cannot be refused. **Nobody said the word legal and about nine of them had it in their heads.** A notary of fifty entered a count of forty-seven days on her own initiative and said she was tired of being the only person who keeps one. Adrian Vale is Stage 1, Witness, Unbound, on no roster of the licence and on no roster of the instrument, holds no mark of his own, no workway, no title, no land right, no class privilege and no seat, and is party to a trial licence thirty-seven days old and is given nothing by it. He performed no working today. The black line in his right palm is eighty-six days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch. The main breach is closed, dressed, measured and silent and has never said a word, and one ruled line is left in the measurement column and it is not filled and the twenty-fifth reading has not been taken. The Narrow Mark was not opened. The chapel stone is silent and is not repaired. The ground under the chapel is a scar, is not repaired, and the boards have not been lifted since the fortieth day of this flood. The form on the nail has four items with the price of the salt written under them and a fifth item that is a question and not a duty, and none of the five can close anything or say where a residue goes or say who is in a room. A room with no window and no door anybody has found is three knocks and four knocks into being knocked on, both entered and neither deleted, and nothing was knocked this week and nothing was used as a reason to open anything. The fee of eleven ounces of silver is entered as owing with no date for the twenty-eighth time and the salt was not sent. Nine Elms' water is not received, the fourth line stands, and the whole of the Reed Reach's standing charge is on a man of thirty-two.**
