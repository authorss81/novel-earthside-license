# Chapter 0694 — A Room With About Nine People In It Going Through What Is Left That Can Be Done To A Page And Finding That There Is Nothing Left

The flat of the man of about thirty-four's own two hands was on the bare back of his own board where it stood leaning against the side of that bench with the face of it turned in to the wood, and he took the hands off it and put them at his own sides and left the board where it was. It was about the fourth hour on the three hundred and seventy-ninth day of the Bare Month, a Tuesday, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register was at the near end of that bench with her two hands flat on the wood on either side of an open book, and about nine people were in that room.

Seven notices have gone out of this city in this flood. All seven of them went in Volumes 07 and 08, there is not an eighth, and no notice has been written in this city since, and there is no notice to be had out of anybody in that room at any hour of any day.

---

They went through it in the ordinary way a room goes through a morning. A man at the back of that room said out loud that there was a thing that could be done to a page and that a person was to decide what it was and get on with it, and about four people said they did not know what he meant, and about four people said nothing, and nobody in that room managed to get those two things into one order on the table in front of them.

Seven documents have come out of this city in this flood and there is no eighth of any kind. Three of the seven are sheets with four places ruled on them. Four of the seven are sheets with one place ruled on them, and the first of those four is on a shelf at the height of a person's shoulder against the wall at the end of this room with a figure in its one place and it has never been written in.

The second of those four went out of this city and has not been in it since. The third of those four went back up the road with a line of words standing under the figure in its one place, and the line of words is not coming back either. The fourth of that shape is not in this city and nothing in this flood brings it back, and no person in this room has ever had any two of the four of them in one hand at once, and no person has ever laid one of them down beside another one, and no person has ever said out loud what any two of them are.

"**There is a sheet on a shelf at the end of this room about nine feet from where all of us are standing with one place ruled on it and a figure in that place, and it has been on that shelf since before any of us came up this stair, and not one person in this city has ever asked anybody what that one place is a place of.**"

A woman at the front of that room said out loud that she did not know. A man beside her said the same thing in other words. The rest of that room said nothing at all, and the woman who keeps the book said nothing and was not asked anything, and nobody thanked him for having said it and nobody improved on it and nobody said he was right.

---

Nobody wrote in that sheet. Nobody read it out. Nobody opened it and nobody stood in front of that shelf and looked at it, and it went on standing on that shelf at the height of a person's shoulder with a figure in its one place, and it is the only page in this city that has one place in it and nothing in that place has been entered on it in this flood.

There is no notice to be had that would let a person in this city put a thing in front of the people it is about before the morning it is about, and there are seven notices and no eighth, and the four days that would have been the cooling are not available to anybody, and that room found out on the morning of that day what it was being asked, which is what that room has been doing on the morning of every day of this flood.

At about the seventh hour a man at that bench said out loud that the page could be turned round to the back and something put on the back of it, and the woman who keeps the book said out loud that the back of that page is not a place anybody has ever shown her, and the man said nothing else about it, and about four people in that room said they did not know and about four said nothing, and nobody wrote anything on the back of anything that day.

At about the ninth hour a man at the back of that room said out loud that there was a figure on a board at one end of that market and three sets of them, and that a person could put a line of words under one of those the way a line of words went under a figure on a sheet that is not in this city, and nobody in that room said he was right and nobody said he was wrong and nobody in that room went and looked at that board.

The man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall stood at the far end of that bench with his own two hands at his own sides and said nothing at all after his one turn, and the chalk was in his own inside breast pocket where it had been, and it did not come out of it that day, and nothing in that room asked him about it.

---

A woman at the back of that room stood up off her own chair at about the tenth hour, and took it by the back of it, and carried it out of that room and down that stair and set it down in the row under the eaves at the side of that door and stood it against the wall of that row, and she came back up that stair with her own two hands empty and sat down on the floor of that room with her back against that wall and her own knees up, and nobody in that room said one word about where her chair had gone and nobody moved it for her.
