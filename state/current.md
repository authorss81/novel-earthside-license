equals day on all ten and weekday is correct on all ten. Pressures as the plan's own column: character, recovery,

**WHAT IS NEW IN THE WORLD AS A RESULT OF THESE TEN DAYS, AND IT IS FOUR THINGS. NONE OF THE FOUR IS UNDONE BY ANY LATER
CHAPTER.** **1. THE STOOL STANDS A FOOT FURTHER OUT** from the wall it stands against, moved by the woman it belongs to
with her own two hands on 967, and there is a clean square on the flags about the size of a hand where that leg had been
standing. **2. THE GAP AT THE HEAD OF THE KEEPER'S LEAF IS CLOSED**, the two pages gone back into one another over about
three days of damp and drying, and the dry cloth has no gap left to go into. **3. THE EMPTY SECOND HOLE IN THE OUTSIDE
WALL TOOK A DAY OF RAIN AND WENT SOFT ABOUT THE WIDTH OF A NAIL ALL ROUND ITS LIP**, and no second nail went into that
wall. **4. A GATE AT THE FAR END OF THAT SHUT ROAD WAS PUSHED ONCE ABOUT THE WIDTH OF A HAND ON ITS HINGE AND CAME BACK**,
and a clean arc about the width of a hand is on that iron where the paint and the rust broke, and the gate stood open at
the going of the light exactly as it stood at the coming of it.

**AND THE THREE THINGS THAT WENT OUT OF THAT ROOM AND CANNOT BE GOT BACK.** On 961 the man of about thirty-eight at that
salt wharf put one thing to the man of about twenty-seven in the yard at that wharf about the trough at the top of that

at her own wall before anything else that morning and put one thing to the man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board

written down.** And on 969 she said she was asked something on the five hundred and ninety-eighth day of that flood and has
been carrying the answer to it and did not know until that morning that she had it, **she did not say what the answer is,
nobody can check it, and no chapter of days 970 to 1000 prints it — and day 970 is on disk and does not print it.**

## THE THREE THINGS THE FIX PASS ON THESE TWO BATCHES FOUND, AND THEY NOW HOLD

1. **THAT STAIR HAS ELEVEN STEPS IN IT AND IS NEVER COUNTED.** The ninth step down from the top is neither the bottom step
   nor the top step, and there are two steps under it before the flags. The bar and the trough are nine risers above the
   ninth. **A writer must not put a number on that stair at all, and must not put a person on a step that does not exist.
   Batch 0001 called it nine steps in eighteen places and two of its chapters were unreadable because of it, and the
   surviving `nine steps` in `chapter-0958.md` is `a room nine steps down`, which is a distance to a different room and is
   the plan's own phrase at §6.4 and stays.**
2. **A SCENE'S STATE IS PAST TENSE AND A GENERAL TRUTH IS NOT, AND THE LINE IS PUBLISHED SO IT CAN BE HELD.** A verb
   describing the state of a specific located object on that morning goes to the past — the sockets are empty, the bar lay
   where it has lain every morning of this flood. **A verb describing how a material behaves, how a boot goes on a wet step,
   what a hole does after a nail has been in it a long time, or how that tin came to have its shape, stays in the present.**
   The gnomic present is not a fault in this manuscript; 22 conversions were made without touching it once.
3. **AN ATTRIBUTION DOES NOT RESTATE A PERSON'S WHOLE HANDLE EVERY TIME THAT PERSON SPEAKS.** §17.5 binds this. The second
   handles this volume has and may use are **the keeper, the tradesman, the stallholder, the carrier, and the man of about
   thirty-eight**, and She and He where a person has been established in the chapter. Each second handle sits beside the age
   and the trade on first appearance in a chapter and never instead of it. Measured full-descriptor restatements on the ten
   days of 961 to 970: **0.**

**AND TWO HOUSE FIGURES THAT ARE NOT DEFECTS AND THAT NO REVIEW SHOULD RE-RAISE AS NEW.** True contractions stand at zero
in this batch and at zero in sampled chapters of Volumes 15, 18 and 19, so the absence is the register of the book and not
a fault of these days; and *his own* runs at about one in 141 words here against about one in 106 in Volume 19, so that
phrase is thinning and is not to be thinned further by cutting it everywhere. **Measure and publish the multi-volume
comparison and change nothing.**

## THE TWO DEBTS THIS FILE CARRIES FORWARD, BOTH OPEN ON PURPOSE

**THE LENGTH SHORTFALL.** `PHASE_SYSTEM.md` asks for about 2,200 to 3,200 words an ordinary chapter and the ten days behind
measure a mean of **1,215.7**, which is 984 a chapter under the floor. It is inherited, not new: Volume 19's last twenty
measure 1,160.2 and batch 0001 of this volume measured 1,303.6. **Nothing has been padded and no finished scene has been
split to reach a number, and a writer who closes this debt by padding has closed it wrong.** The disposition written into
`workspace/volume-20/batch-0003/PROMPT.md` is to let a scene that has room take it.

**THE PROSE GOING HOLLOW, WHICH THE FIX PASS MEASURED AND DID NOT REWRITE.** Across the same three sets, negation tokens
being *nothing, nobody, no, not, never, cannot, neither* run **458 in 23,464 words on Volume 19's last twenty, 287 in 13,036
on batch 0001, 295 in 12,157 on batch 0002** — one in 51.2, then one in 45.4, then one in 41.2. Bare *that* runs **2.41 per
cent of words, then 3.31, then 3.94.** Both are drifting the wrong way across three batches. **A held string binds what a
chapter may print; it does not license a paragraph spent reporting that it did not print it.** Fixing this in the ten days
already written would be rewriting the batch, so it is carried forward as a measured number in the next prompt instead.
