## VOLUME 13 AFTER DAY 630 — CHAPTERS 0601 TO 0630 ON DISK, DAYS 601 TO 630, AND TWENTY DAYS OF THIS VOLUME STILL TO WRITE

**THE STORY, IN ONE PARAGRAPH, AND IT COMES FIRST BECAUSE THE COUNTS COME SECOND.** A yard at the
near end of eleven miles of flats, a barrow across an open end, a thumb putting one corner of a
line of grey grit back down over a figure nobody has ever been asked about. A room asking one
question in ordinary conversation for an hour and working out that nobody in this city can be
shown one person who was ever told. A room finding out on the morning it is asked what it is being
asked, four of them at the ninth hour and four at the eleventh. A woman alone with a register and
two slates, refusing a man who wanted any line at all put in that book, looking along the
underside of a shelf from the floor, and reading a date in chalk on a door with nothing under it.
On the nearer of this volume's two middle days a man named what a decision in that room would cost
before he made it, and decided the order two words go in, and a man who wanted the opposite went
down that stair and did not come back up it. A man with an empty satchel going up and down a stair
four times with the one thing nobody had asked him for, which is an opinion about that order he is
not going to give. A hand into a pocket and out again with nothing in it. A woman asking one man
one question about him and not about the book, on the third Saturday, and his one answer said once
and going nowhere. A yard, a trough that would not be moved six feet, and a stack two boards
higher at the ninth hour than it was at the fifth. And a room reading out an order it is going to
keep and writing neither word down, and leaving the heading of a sheet the wrong way up for
whoever came in after that.
