## Canon after Chapter 0060 — the end of Volume 02 Batch 0001

**The door is not a road home, and that is now on the page in nine rooms and in a form.** A controlled opening requires a receiving community that understands the purpose; **there is none on the far side of the Narrow Mark and no instrument in this world can make one from this side.** The far side is a room with no window and no door anybody has found, about eleven people have stood in it, and Eli Mercer is the twelfth person anybody in this city knows of. **The first request for an opening is entered as a request**, with a name, a date, a person who asked, an answer that is a reason and not a refusal of the person, and a fifth line that is not part of the form. **Eight of the eleven of the second list want the road that goes where they were born. Three do not, and each of the three is named.**

**A room with no door is being knocked on.** Three knocks on the night of the twenty-ninth day of the new month and four on the night of the thirty-fifth, reported by a man in the lower gallery of the Lock of Salt, **both entered and neither deleted**, on the standing rule that a man who cannot be right about a number hears a number and a join that is dressed does not knock, and that neither fact is a reason not to write the next one down. **Nobody opened anything and nothing in this city was done at either hour.** The number going up is not a pattern and is also not nothing.

**The review is in fifteen days.** The flood-season review is on **the fiftieth day of the new month** — day 75 — **on a slate on a nail in the lock-chapel arch with a date on it and no questions on it**, and a woman of thirty-eight of the second list named what that shape is and her second sentence is ten words: *a review with no questions on it is a queue.* **A person who wants to change the date or the questions has to do it in a room, in daylight, with a date on the page, and nobody in this city has written a question yet.**

**Winter is here and it is material.** The price on the nail under the chapel arch is **salt**, and salt went up a third on the twenty-seventh day. Ottavia Reek offered thirty tons on the retail price against the crew's shift list and it was refused in eleven words; the salt was bought with labour out of the crew's shift. **The rail on the two hundred steps is half-rotten, went on ice, condemned, and costs timber four weeks away at a price the Measure has tied to the crew's list.** A docker's apprentice of twenty went down on it and could not rise for about nine minutes, and **the four who had to choose between the man and the seam had no clause, and the choice is entered as what a person chose and not as what was right.** The cost column is entered for the eighth time in one month and **still has no figure.**

**The four who hold the door are the four who need the door.** The four who change the salt twice a day and take the four chalk depths are the four who sign the fresh inspection before every opening, and **the nine named persons who may stop the working may stop neither the form nor the inspection**, and a woman of fifty-eight who cannot read said the sentence in nineteen words, and a woman of seventy who has never been asked anything in fifty-one years said *I would rather be asked.*

**Two workways have been offered and one has been taken.** The eighteenth field, on the twenty-eighth day of the new month, to a man of thirty-one from Leeds whose right hand will not close, and the nineteenth field, on the thirty-second, to a woman of forty-one of the second list, **and both put the whole of the cost on a named person and called that fair, and neither contains a lie, and the lie is the position.** The first was refused in under a second by the wrong man. **The second was accepted in about four seconds.** **There is no office in Saltwake for a hand that holds things**, and a workway in this city is a claim on a job that does not exist, and the cost of it is not the work: the cost is that a man with a standing is a man who has been given a reason to be angry, and a seat is only ever got rid of by the people who are angry at it. **They wrote a procedure in about a day, with nine clauses and nine weaknesses, and both are entered, and the work itself is not written down anywhere and the document says so in twenty-eight words on its own face.**

**A bar is a bar and this one was a bar.** The page of the twenty-sixth day of this flood forbids a workway offered to any of the seventeen by the instrument or by anybody using it. It is the longest page in the licence and it is perfect. **It does not reach a pale field in a room, and nobody used anything, and there was no hand on any lever.** Adrian Vale said it out loud in thirty-one words in front of nine people: *I wrote a rule about a thing and I did not write a rule about the world, and I have spent fifty-seven days telling a city that the difference is pedantry.*

**A description is a removal with a column for where the person is.** Hettis Roke was asked, in daylight, in about two hundred and forty people, about eight graves with no stone on them, for the first time in nine years, because the fifth item of the form is a question and not a duty. **He said no to the form and yes to a yard.** The answer: the names were written down once, in his grandfather's hand, in the year the ground was made, because a Hearthguard who held a gate was required to give a roll of the dead of that gate to the Crown twice a year; the stones went up the same month and four of the eight got a name cut on a neighbour's stone because there was not enough money for eight; the book went to Aurel in a wooden case four times a year for nineteen years; **nobody came; the count was eight every year and stopped being written down in the year the gate came off the roll.** **The roll of strangers goes in the same kind of case, four times a year, on a river boat, and a notary of fifty said the whole of it in eighteen words: the column is for the office, the office is nine hundred miles away, it has never sent anybody. And nobody in Saltwake has ever seen a name come out of one of those cases.** **And one of the eight is cut on the flat top of the eleventh stone of the upper road, nine miles away, in a hand his grandfather taught him to read, and the names were not lost. They were moved.**

**The word *player* is Adrian's.** He wrote it on a market board on the ninth day of this flood in his own hand in a language almost nobody in this city reads, told six men it was their error, and let it stand for forty-five days. He said so out loud in about two hundred and forty people on the twenty-ninth day of the new month. A woman of forty-one of Silt Row entered the **fourth statement of error in the history of the book**, four sentences, all of them correct, and withdrew her belief and not her vote. A docker of fifty-eight said *then take it down*, and, when he said he could not, **said four more words: then say over it.** A woman of twenty-four got up on a trestle and wrote **fifty-nine words over it in chalk, in the market, in two lines of ten and forty-nine, with the shorter word left underneath**, and anybody standing at that wall reads the longer one first.
