## 3. THE STANDING QUESTION, ANSWERED IN THIS RECORD'S OWN WORDS, WHICH IS NOT A FINDING AND NOT A FIGURE

**The volume's own claim, at `outline/volume-15.md` §12, is that a strip of ground that about four people a day walk on was the only instrument this city had for telling afterwards that anybody had ever been in a room; that the instrument stops working the moment a room says out loud that the people on it are not to be asked; that it stops working without a mark; and that what is lost is the fact that they were. The claim has three parts and the fifty support the first two and carry the third as a cost and not as a proof, and it is worth being exact about which page carries which part, because a claim of that kind is worth nothing if the chapter that carries it is the chapter that is only saying it.**

**THE FIRST PART IS CARRIED BY A SCENE, AND THE SCENE IS DAY 725.** A man stands in a room with about nine people in it and decides that the people on that strip are not to be asked, and says what the decision is in about nine words in his own mouth before he takes it, and the room finds out what it is being asked on the morning it is asked because there is no eighth notice, and nothing is written down and nobody outside that room is told. **What makes it the finding is not a mouth and it is not the wall: it is that the ground is left exactly as it was and the instrument is gone anyway, and the only record of the decision is that nine people were in that room and heard it.**

**THE SECOND PART IS CARRIED BY CHAPTERS THAT SAY IT IN NARRATION AFTERWARDS.** That strip goes on carrying about four people a day in the ordinary course, and about four of them get a thing off the thing they are walking past and about four do not, and nobody counts them and nobody asks them. **And on day 750 the last image says that strip has about four people a day's feet on it and nothing on it and nothing said about it, and that there is no person in this city that anybody could be shown who learned anything from that ground.**

**THE THIRD PART — THAT WHAT IS LOST IS THE FACT THAT THEY WERE — IS CARRIED AS A COST AND NOT AS A SCENE.** No mouth in the fifty says the cost falls on the class, and no narration says it either, except that the plan's day entries name it and the resolution enacts it: on day 745 one of the about four is her, and nobody in that room is told that. **The finding is asserted by the narrator across the fifty and spoken by nobody, and it is never once joined to the decision by anybody, and the two are not joined here.** A reader who wants the fifty to prove the third part will find that they enact it without proving it, and that is a fact about the shape of a volume whose subject is a class of persons rather than a fault, and nothing was reworded to make the shape better.

**AND THE ONE PLACE THE FIFTY GO FURTHER THAN THE CLAIM, WHICH IS A FINDING AND NOT A RESTATEMENT.** On day 740 a wall said one thing and nobody agreed with it, and on the same morning the one person who had not been asked in three volumes answered one question about herself. **The volume therefore has two findings and they are of opposite kinds: a decision puts a class of persons out of reach without a mark, and a page said out loud in a room of about nine people can be agreed with by nobody. Neither of them is joined to the other by anybody in the fifty, including the narrator, and this close joins them to neither.**
