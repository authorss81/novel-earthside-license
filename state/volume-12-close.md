## 3. THE STANDING QUESTION, ANSWERED IN THIS RECORD'S OWN WORDS, WHICH IS NOT A FINDING AND NOT A FIGURE

**The volume's own claim was threefold: that a line of words put under a figure is not a record, that the figure's bareness does not travel with it, and that a column kept empty is a place where a refusal is kept and not a record of one. The fifty pages support all three of those, and it is worth being exact about which page carries which, because a claim of that kind is worth nothing if the chapter that carries it is the chapter that is only saying it.**

**THE AGREEMENT AND NOT A RECORD is carried by a page and not by a mouth, and the page is Chapter 0590.** The wall says it once, in its own block, giving a rule and not an answer, and it is refused out loud in about four seconds, and the man who refuses it agrees with it, and the cost of that agreement is the whole of what he has. **What is on the page is not the finding; what is on the page is the rule. The finding is on day 589, where the man whose figure it is under reads the line out once and then says in about nine words that nobody can check one word of what he has just said, and on day 595, where the sheet goes up the road with the words under it and nobody opens it and nobody is going to.** Those two pages make the finding by what happens to a thing, and they would make it just as well if the wall had said nothing at all on the one day it says something. **The wall's rule is the cheapest thing the volume has and it is not what the volume found.**

**THE FIGURE'S BARENESS DOES NOT TRAVEL WITH IT is carried by five days and by nothing anybody says.** Day 570, five days before the decision, in narration, that not one surface in this city carries a line of words under a figure and that every figure on every board and in every column and in every place ruled under a heading is a number with nothing under it. Day 579, the day after the board is turned face up, in the same shape. Day 580, the day the room has agreed what goes in the one place and has not written it. Day 588, the day the one figure gets its words and the book behind the bench is not written in at all and still holds five. Day 0595, the day the sheet leaves. **A person four hundred miles off holding a figure with a line of words under it will read one without as a figure that is missing something, and no character in this book ever says that, and the volume asserts it five times in narration and never once in a mouth, and that is the correct place for it.**

**THE COLUMN IS A PLACE WHERE A REFUSAL IS KEPT is carried by day 599, in a mouth, and it is the one of the three the volume states out loud rather than in narration, and it is the one that costs somebody something.** A man says in about nine words that a column like that is where a word goes, and the room does not do it, and the reason is given in narration on the next page and it is not a reason anybody had before: nobody in that room has ever been told what the column is for, and a person who does not know what a column is for should not write in it. **On day 574 the woman who keeps the book looked at that column four times and said nothing about it four times, and on day 599 she says out loud that she is leaving it empty, and the difference between the two days is the whole of what five volumes did to that column.**

**AND THE THING THE FIFTY PAGES DO NOT SUPPORT, WHICH IS PUBLISHED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED.** The volume is a volume about a line of words under a figure, and the line of words is on one page, in one hand, in one place, on one day, and it is read out once, and it leaves the city on the fifth day after it is written, and it is not here after that. **Thirty-eight sentences across the fifty files carry the form *a line of words* and every one of them is a sentence about the line, and not one of them prints a word of it, and not one of them says what is in it, and that is the rule and it is held. But the fifty days are about a sheet, a column, a board, a book, a slate, a fence, a road and a trough, and the words themselves get eleven days, and on the other thirty-nine days the line is named and never said, and a reader who is waiting for the words gets a sentence about the words on every one of those days.** That is a fact about the shape of the volume and not a fault, and it is the price of a rule this volume set for itself and kept, and it is worth saying out loud here because a close that only reported what it liked would report a volume about a sheet and a sheet is not what it is about. **None of it was repaired and nothing was reworded to make the shape better.**
