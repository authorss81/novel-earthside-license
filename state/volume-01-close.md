# Volume 01 Close Record — *The First Door*, Chapters 1–50

Day 1 was the Tuesday Adrian Vale came out of the wall. Day 50 is the Tuesday the city signed a licence and the field is still unpaid. One page of the notebook is a sheet with somebody else's name on the cover; one notebook is in a notary's keeping and he has never asked for it back; the black line in his right palm is forty-nine days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch.

This is the one page the next phase has to read before it plans anything.

---

## 1. What Volume 01 answered

**The main breach is closed and dressed, and the closing of it was performed by named locals and a tide and forty men, and the man who read the number was four feet back on the sill of the third bay and said out loud that he was not in it, and a docker made him say it in the local language to twenty men.** That is the volume's central promise discharged.

**The scar is visible, monitored, and not closed.** A black mark in a place under the lock chapel, with a five-item form on a slate on a nail, watched twice a day by four people with no list and no name at the top of it, with a price on it, and entered with what it **cannot** do: it cannot close anything and it cannot say where a residue will go. **The boards have not been lifted since the fortieth day of the flood.** The residue of the working went under that floor on the forty-ninth day of the flood and came back warm and opened nothing, and **that was luck and not skill**, and the entry says so.

**The trial licence exists.** A standing route, one named living person per opening, two witnesses not of the crew, a fresh inspection before every opening, a separately costed return mark that belongs to the person who goes, a review date on the page before the day, a right of refusal, a right to be asked, a cost record, a quorum, and a page about the day the crew does not turn up. **It is built out of the ordinary work of a lock, a roll with no name at the top, a gauge, four inspectors and a notary's chain.** It is the first thing in the series allowed to be a standing route and it carries no land rights, no class privileges and no workway to anybody in it.

**The reduction from four witnesses to two per opening is the volume's argument.** Four authorised the route on the fourth day of the flood and four still do, and cannot be changed but by themselves, in a room, in daylight, with a date on it. **Two carry one person through the door. A working can be made cheaper without being made private.** The proof is that a boy of nineteen was asked for nothing and came anyway, and that **a boy's own name was struck off the top of the second page of the roll of forty names in daylight, for a true reason, and the date he put there stayed.**

**A dead man's record is a record and not a finding, and it is four pages long.** Nobody may write *he died because*. When the Concord's assessor of strangers tried to compress it into a Crown column of four lines, the correction came from the woman who wrote the clause, and the four lines that replaced it were true and fit the column and said nothing about cause. Owen Park is under a board in the ordinary ground behind the ordinary wall in the ground where a Hearthguard's grandfather is, in a ground that now holds twelve graves of which eight have no name stone, and the name is cut twice in two languages in two hands and the second line is upside down.

**The seventeen are named, and the longer list is what they do not have**, and it is a term and not a favour. **Adrian Vale is Unbound to the last line of the volume** and the page says in the notary's hand that he is party to the licence and is given nothing by it. **A stipend is refused in a yard, and the reason is a roll: a wage is owed to a person, a person is described on a roll, and the roll goes to Aurel four times a year in a wooden case.** The price of being visible in this city is being described.

**Two posts came off two surfaces in nine days.** The keeper's rule, a rule about a man, came off a board in daylight in front of a hundred and forty people and nobody asked. The post, readmitted for a final term on a date, ended at noon on the forty-ninth because a term is a term and a date is a date, and its terms went with it, and what was inside it went into a form as a **question** on a nail where it can be refused. **A question is not a post.**

**A boy of nineteen asked for a rule and there was none, and the difference between an answer and a rule is that the page is what is left when the person is asleep or hurt.** That is the sentence the method of this book is built out of.

**The volume does not end on that sentence. It ends on a stair above the Lock of Salt, with a boy of nine, on *but you were the first one*, and a man from another world saying: *I was not; I was the first one anybody could see.***

## 2. What Volume 01 deliberately did not answer

None of these is a gap. Each is a live question and each was protected in prose.

- **Whether there is a person on the other side of the chapel floor, and what is in the room at the far end of the Narrow Mark.** Petra Oake of Nine Elms asked twice and was answered once in the worst possible way. Eli Mercer walked in and came out and said **four words** — *the same room, taller* — and **nobody wrote down what it means.**
- **What the light Owen Park saw was.** It is in the fourth page as what a person said, corroborated by nothing, and a woman of fifty-eight has said since that the fourth reading of a mystery is available to anybody. **A man is a scar in a person is never to become a lesson, a consolation or a rule about anybody else.**
- **What is on the nineteen stones.** The work is now on a wall above a market, dated, in two hands, with **Eight** at the bottom of the cell, and **the meaning is unwritten.**
- **Hettis Roke's eight graves with no name stone.** Nine years, one sentence in a yard, no question asked and no refusal given. **A person who has never been asked is not a person who has refused.**
- **The voice.** The black line in the chapel stone said nothing on the twenty-third, forty-ninth or fiftieth days of this flood and has said nothing since the twenty-fifth. It is not repaired. The twelve words it said in English on the twenty-fifth — *One. A place people already agree on, used already, for another reason* — are a sentence that describes the Lock of Salt, and one man knows it, and he has told a notary once and no one else, and he did not say it in a yard, and the reason is entered.
- **The shape a man in a good coat has been getting at since the fourteenth week.** It is on the page as a shape. **The name Single Witness and the name First Grammar are not used in prose anywhere in Volume 01 and must not be used in Volume 02 without a room, in daylight, with a date on it.**
- **Two letters.** Nora Vale in Camden and Hattie Park in Dunedin. **There is no route in this world that a letter can travel.** A Crown channel was offered on the fortieth day and has been neither refused nor accepted in ten days and has not been mentioned since.
- **Marrow.** Publicly useful, privately dangerous, unaccused, and **not convicted in any scene and not made useless in any scene.** He is the most useful man in every room he walks into and the most frightening version of himself, and the volume ends with him in the room.
- **March.** The bottom field, the woman in Corrow, the year of certainty, and the four of the forty who will be hurt before March, one at a time, on an ordinary morning, in no working, and no instrument in this world can stop it.

## 3. What is live into Volume 02

1. **March.** The cost column, entered seven times, with no figure on the end of it. The day it is not paid, the shift stops and the join stops being worked. **The volume does not end with the field saved and nothing in Volume 02 may retroactively save it by saying the work was cheaper than it was.**
2. **The Registry's quarterly sitting**, at about a hundred and ten days from the second Crown letter, and three Crown instruments in this city's book that have not been answered in the form required. **Keep the Crown's Registry demand, the Crown's single-author containment, and the Quiet Choir's design as three distinct instruments forever.**
3. **Ottoline Vask's unanswered objection.** The forty words are the toll and the pattern, and a city cannot spend a thing and be its own foundation at the same time. It survives into Volume 02 and is not answered.
4. **The flood-season review**, whose date is on the page before the day, and **the four named persons who must turn up in the wrong weather with a gauge before every opening**, and a gauge with one mark cut on it by a man who did not say why.
5. **The scar and the form**, and the fact that nobody can say where a residue goes. The form is a form, not a promise, and it must never be made to do either of the two things it cannot do.
6. **The nine names who may stop the working**, one of whom is named by herself and one of whom may take her name back.
7. **The trial licence in its first months**, two witnesses at a time, one person at a time, with a review that a city can lose.
8. **The seventeen**, living in a city that describes them four times a year in a wooden case.
9. **The roll of strangers** and Adrian's Unbound entry with two dates on it, and the third refusal behind it, and the twenty-second day of the new month when he said *yes, and here is what it will cost, and I am the one who will have to say so every time* and also said out loud that he believes it will fail.
10. **A man who has said nine things out loud and got a room to change its mind four times, and a woman of fifty-eight who cannot read, and a woman of twenty-four who writes the bar, and a woman of seventy who keeps a floor and washed a rule off it.** The volume's institutions are people and the next volume must not turn them into offices.
11. **The seventeen System offers**, none of which was ever registered as refused, and the fact that a man dying, a city committing, a city closing its gate and a city signing a licence each produced nothing at all.

## 4. What must not be re-litigated

- **Owen Park is dead.** Not softened, not revisited as a mistake, not explained away, not turned into a lesson or an instruction. The four pages stand, the shoulder is in a grave, and **nobody may write *he died because*.**
- **The keeper's rule came off the board on the sixteenth day of the new month in daylight, and so did Adrian's own sentence, with the same cloth, and the reason is entered: washing a man's own writing off a board is a decision and not a keeping.** The board is washed every Sunday morning. The four words and the fifth go back on. **Vask's four items are on the other side of the door and are not washed and must not be.**
- **The post is gone and ended by its own date.** It is not renewed by anybody without a room, in daylight, with a date on it.
- **Adrian is Unbound.** Three refusals of the workway, the fourth answer in a book: *I refused it because it worked.* No workway, no return mark of his own, no land rights, no class privileges, no title, no seat, and a page that says so.
- **The measurement column is not continuous.** Twenty-four readings, eight and seven-eighths, two steps, both entered in an unnamed mason's own hand before the number was read, **one ruled line left**. A later writer may not give it a figure that is not in `state/continuity.md` and may not move a step.
- **One date form set, two forms only:** *the Nth day of this flood* up to the fortieth, *the Nth day of the new month* after it. **Never *of this month*.**
- **The five lock sites stay apart**, and two of them did nothing in this volume.
- **No crowd ever crosses.** One named person, one opening, a fresh inspection, and a receiving community that may suspend. **There is no working route in this world.** The trial licence is the only standing route and it was built out of a lock, a roll, a gauge, four inspectors and a notary's chain.
- **A rescue is a working, a pattern is a toll, and a cost is a promise.** Keep those three apart in every scene.
- **The three Crown-side instruments never collapse into each other**, and the third one — the shape a man in a good coat has been getting at — never gets a name in a room without a date.
- **The name *Single Witness* and the name *First Grammar* are not in the prose of Volume 01 and must not be introduced at the close.**

## 5. The one thing the next phase should know about the shape of the book

Nobody in this volume got stronger. The join closed because a city did ordinary work on a named tide with a cost entered before the tide turned, and the man who read the number was four feet back and said so when he was asked. A woman of twenty-nine found the pattern on the twentieth day of the new month, on the walk-round, and it is what the working was built out of four days later. A docker of fifty-eight who cannot read found the mechanism. A woman of twenty-four wrote the clause that stopped a promise. A woman of seventy wrote a rule about a man and then washed it off a door in daylight. A boy of nineteen asked for a rule twice and was given the answer instead, and put the chalk down on a ledge rather than write the same word twice.

**The gains are a page, a slate, a form, a roll, a gauge, a licence and a gravel bed with twelve graves in it. None of them is a power and all of them are heavier than a power.**
