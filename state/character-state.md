On the fifty-third she said out loud in a yard, at about nine feet from a woman who had been on a page for eight days, that the rule of the Reach has a hole in it shaped exactly like a household, that she has not gone near it and nobody in that yard is going to, and asked for somebody to write down that a hole and a wharf were in the same yard and that nobody joined them up.

## A woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register

**Four people and a question have not left for the ground people call the First Landing, nobody has been asked whether they will go, and the only instruction it has is *to open nothing*, and that is her sentence.** She said it in a room on the fifty-second day of the Long Month for the second time in a room and on the fifty-fifth in a yard, and said she is not going to say it a fourth time because about nine people in this city can now say it. On the fifty-fifth she said she had started to write down who the four of them are, got two names in, stopped, tore the page out, and it is in her own pocket and not in the book, and the reason is that a list of four people who have not been asked is a roll.

## Everyone else, in one line each

**A man of about twenty-nine from the settlement's workroom** said a page with a date on it and a price under every line and no figure under any price is a list of intentions, and a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall told him it was the price, and neither of them has improved on the other. **A man of about forty-four who keeps a stall** copied the front of the compact onto the back of a slate on a market table on the afternoon of the forty-fifth because he thought a page ought to be in two places, put it on the boards of a stall by a wharf on the fifty-third, said he would rather be laughed at than be a man who has carried a piece of paper about for nine days, and said a cost with no figure on the end of it is a cost and a price with a figure is a promise. **A woman of about thirty-four from a fourth household on the road** gave two numbers about the flats in that order and unjoined, asked how she gets on the posting and was asked whose household she is, and said the only question anybody has asked her she can answer is which side of a channel is whose. **A man of about fifty-two who digs** said six days and then fourteen days, and a rod that goes in further every time and not much, and that he has stopped measuring it because about nine people who are not him have started, and that he can date a bank and cannot date a number. **A woman of about thirty-four in the store on the upper road, who is on no list in it** hung a slate with the fifty-sixth day of the Long Month on the chest on the forty-sixth so that nobody would have to ask, and said the date out loud four times to about four different people on the fifty-fifth. **A woman of about fifty who counts water on the fingers of her left hand** read a whole page with her fingers on it and then said the man who wrote it had put the settlement's own sentence on a wall, and asked that it be entered that she was asked a question in a yard and did not answer it. **A man of about thirty-one with a hand that will not close** read the posting aloud with his finger and got to the ninth line, and said two things have happened on this cut in six days and about nine people in this city have only been able to talk about the one that does not cost anybody anything this week, and on the fifty-fourth he said he did not know whether a name said out loud in a room is good or bad. **A woman of about fifty-six who keeps a shop four streets down from a market** said that about four people in this city have already worked out which road the seventh line is about and have told each other, and that if a person wants to know they can stand at the end of it on a Friday morning in daylight and say so.

## The woman of forty-eight in a fourth house at Nine Elms

**Not asked, not approached, not named, and nobody stood outside the fourth house. She is not in these ten chapters at all.** The hole in the Reach's own rule that is shaped exactly like her was said out loud in a yard on the fifty-third day of the Long Month by a woman of about twenty-four who said she has not gone near it and that nobody in that yard is going to, and the hole is not filled.
