# Character State

Compact live state for the next batch. Updated after Batch 0001 (Chapters 1–10). Private want, current pressure, physical state, and what the last ten chapters cost them. The full character bible is `bible/characters.md`; the long arc is `bible/premise.md`.

## Adrian Vale — 32, protagonist

- **Stage 1, Witness.** Perceives the nearest join-line and understands one direct promise at a time. Cannot create a working, open a threshold, or hold a place. **Unbound — no workway, by choice.**
- **Mark:** spent into the maintenance seam in Chapter 10. A thin pale trace remains in the heel of his palm. Under it, separate from it, is a **thin black line two inches long** that opened in Chapter 8 and has not moved, faded, or gone into the stone — an overload warning, invisible to everyone else, not a return mark. No return mark. No way home.
- **Seat:** named holder of the pull in the Chapter 10 ledger entry — a recorded duty on a **pending, unusable** instrument, not a License holding.
- **Debt:** a season of the bottom field, owed to eleven villages and the Reed margin, enforceable, reviewable on the last day of the salt month.
- **Body:** two days without real sleep. Cut hand from the Chapter 2 iron spike that has not been mentioned since and should not have healed. Left ear still ringing from the Chapter 9 failure. Ninety seconds of blindness after trying to take a working off five people.
- **What he wants now:** a route home for six people who are not his; a door that works; and a chair he has decided he must not take. He wrote the chair down so he could not pretend later that he had not wanted it.
- **What he has learned about himself:** he got used to being the only one who could see, and that is how he broke. He liked being needed, and he gave authority away on purpose. He is keeping a paper notebook because Veyra values witnessed physical records.
- **Voice:** flat, technical, deflects with design language. Says "in the flattest voice he had" as a tic. Crosses out his own conclusions in the notebook rather than erasing them.

## Tamsin Quill — 29, anchorwright

- **State:** professional ally, not lover. She checks Adrian and told him she will go on checking; he agreed to be checkable in public and it is in the record.
- **Pressure:** her name is on the Concord's own table of failed attempts to move the river boundary, and she is carrying a public debt alongside him. She runs the public bridge register and is the only interpreter any of them have.
- **What she wants:** the Lock and the Hearth Marches not claimed by a Crown, a guild, or a player settlement. Nine Elms' refusal is safe only while somebody with a ledger keeps reading it out.
- **Trust ledger so far:** he returned the copper token to the numbered board; he refused the workway; he accepted her rule about asking first; he gave the mark into the stone. He also made a rule for people without asking them, and she told him what that builds.
- **Voice:** short sentences, level voice that gets worse when she is least angry. Says a thing once and does not repeat it.

## Mina Okoye — 29, first-cohort medic

- **Injuries:** a four-inch thigh graze from the wall, dressed with a torn shirt and boiling water. Working.
- **Pressure:** she cannot save everyone and she has started writing numbers down as *what we said*, never *what it was*, because she has watched a room remember itself the way it wanted to go. SURVIVABLE is in her book in capitals.
- **Established:** the first person to say a route shown is not permission to use it. She holds authority over the medical list, including Adrian's fourth rule.
- **Want:** to get back to her family and to prove practical knowledge beats a spectacular class. Fear: being unable to tell a patient she can save from one she has already failed.

## Dante Kuo — 35, Hearthguard captain in everything but name

- **Injuries:** dislocated forearm, right. Not reduced by anyone with authority; held against his chest.
- **Pressure:** he puts himself between crowds and doors by default. He has not yet disagreed with Adrian about a rescue. That fight is owed.
- **Want:** keep the seven alive and prove a disciplined plan beats panic. Fear: being remembered as the man who ordered others into a disaster.

## Priya Shah — 24, first public record

- **Injuries:** forearm gash, four stitches with a sewing needle and boiling water.
- **Pressure:** her phone has no bars and its clock stopped at 11:52. She narrates into it anyway and logs refusals — Jonah's gatekeeper offer, Vail's terms, Adrian's refusal of the false prompt, and Owen's offer on the relic question. She also wrote the six names and six fears Adrian asked for in Chapter 5, and that sheet went up on the loft wall in Chapter 6 in public. Fear: that the whole thing was a bit and she made people laugh and now somebody is dead.
- **The record has left the room.** Nothing was *published* in the sense she means, but the names are on a wall above a market where any passer-by can read them, and she says so out loud before anyone else does. Do not write her as unaware of this.

## Jonah Pike — 42, the colonial mirror

- **Injuries:** none. He arrived dry.
- **Pressure:** he made the gatekeeper proposal in Chapter 6 and withdrew it inside the hour, and then asked what the alternative is. He warned that the brass man will be buying Earthside tools in about three weeks. He is at the Chapter 10 hearing working the room, six feet from the Concord's table.
- **Want:** a safe, recognizable settlement where the seven can live without depending on an indifferent Earth. Fear: another failed attempt at making a home for people who have already lost one.
- **Note for later batches:** he is not secretly anything. He is the argument Adrian lost.

## Noor Haddad — 27, quartermaster

- **Injuries:** dust inhalation, aspirating, sick twice.
- **Pressure:** her mother's house is the one fear she named, and she is the only one who ever left. She built the group's ledger — what we have, what it is for, who it belongs to, who may use it, who is owed — and Adrian's cash and phone are on it.
- **Telling:** her gift is noticing who has been left out of a distribution. She is the reason the trial license will have a public cost record.

## Owen Park — 36, security contractor

- **Injuries:** cut scalp from the wall, from the moment he came through it. He is the **first** of the six out, and the room corrects him on it in Chapter 10 rather than letting a false statement into the ledger. By Chapter 10 the scalp **is not healing** and Mina is angry about it. He has no other lasting injury; his shoulder is reserved for Chapter 25.
- **Private light:** he alone perceives a relic-shaped light through the damaged wall, coin- or seal-shaped, standing on its edge. It walked away from the wall after the arrival. He has said the word *story* in front of this group and it is in the record. His statement is in the public ledger marked **unverified** and stays there until somebody else sees it.
- **Chapter 6 is his worst chapter and he knows it.** He insists the light can be retrieved before it goes, is called a crank by the room, puts his coat on and walks nearly out of the yard, and only stays because Adrian refuses to tell him the light is not there and instead makes the retrieval **a question the wall's owners get asked in a room with witnesses**. Sesh Brack stops him with the cost of each direction rather than a threat, and sends him to a notary. He put his name on the question, not on the crew, and his offer is in Priya's record. He treats locals as obstacles through accent and phrasing rather than contempt — in Chapter 6 he asks the Hearthguard woman on the top step where the second bay is and it comes out sounding like an instruction.
- **Pressure:** his instinct is to secure the room and control access, and the same instinct is what put him in the wrong place at the wrong end of the outer tear in Chapter 9. He has never been wrong in front of a crowd before and is handling it badly.
- **Planned death, Chapter 38, permanent.** It must carry three causes: Adrian's underestimated crossing cost, the Chapter 25 shoulder no healer could set, and Owen's own refusal to follow instructions. He is alive at the end of this batch, with a scalp that will not close.

## Ivenn Marrow — 41, Quiet Choir's First Reader

- **Public face:** Crown Law scholar from Aurel, six weeks in the city, the man who arrived before the Crown's clerks. Charming, patient, useful, and the only person in Saltwake not frightened by the wall.
- **What he has actually done:** quoted Adrian's unpublished design note back at him and said he has only ever heard it through a wall; made a partial offer with a workway and a route and no cost in it; written down Adrian's refusal; said the man in the lock join is a person; recorded Adrian's own prediction that he would try to become permanent; and told Adrian the narrow mark in the seam is only the first one to remember his name.
- **What nobody suspects:** that the prompts are his. The workway offer is still open, cheap, and pointed at Aurel.
