On day 709 a trough and a bucket he did not put there stand in the middle of the way to that man's own front and he has to go round them, and he says out loud that he is not going to stand there and say whose they are, and nobody in that market row answered him. On day 710 he draws a chair out from under a shelf he knows nothing about, says out loud in his own mouth that he did not know what was on that shelf and does not know now, puts the chair back, and nobody thanks him. **HIS PIECE OF CHALK IS IN THAT INSIDE BREAST POCKET AT EVERY HOUR OF ALL TEN DAYS AND DOES NOT COME OUT ON ANY OF THEM. HIS STANDING DECISION ABOUT THE PIECE OF BOARD UNDER THAT DATE IS NOT MADE AGAIN. AND THE STANDING DISAGREEMENT ABOUT WHETHER THIS PERSON IS A MAN OR A WOMAN IS CARRIED AND NOT SETTLED BY ANY OF THE TEN.**

**THE WOMAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-NINE WHO KEEPS A PUBLIC REGISTER. SHE IS IN SEVEN OF THE TEN, IS ASKED NOTHING ON ANY OF THEM, AND SAYS ONE COST OUT LOUD ON ONE OF THEM.** On day 701 she names, in her own mouth and in front of about nine people, that she has been at that bench every morning of this flood and that not one person in this city has ever asked her one question about anything, and nobody thanks her for it. On day 704 she comes in at the front door of that building at the fourth hour, as she has every morning of this flood, crosses the floor and goes up the stair at the back, and at about the fifth hour the man of about thirty-four comes in at that same door and has to stop in front of her, and neither of them says one word at that moment; he says one thing about why he comes in at that door, and she is not asked anything and does not ask him anything. On day 710 no hand but hers goes near that shelf at any hour. **ON ALL TEN OF THESE DAYS SHE IS ASKED NOTHING, AND NO CHAPTER OF THESE TEN SAYS OR IMPLIES THAT SHE KNEW WHAT THE ROW WAS FOR, OR THAT SHE KNEW IT BECAUSE SHE KEPT A BOOK, OR THAT ANYBODY KNEW.**

**THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-NINE WHO TRADES ON A BOARD. HE IS IN FIVE OF THE TEN AND IS ASKED NOTHING IN ANY OF THEM.** On day 701 he says out loud that there is a rate on that board he has had wrong and has not put right, and says he is telling that room before anybody in it finds it out off the board; it is still wrong at the tenth hour and nobody thanks him and nobody says he was right. On day 705 he says what the seven notices were and that this room is not writing an eighth. On day 708 his board is the subject of the chapter: nineteen rates in three columns, a bare space about two fingers wide at the foot of the middle of them, and **NOBODY IN THAT MARKET ROW HAS EVER ASKED HIM ABOUT IT AND NOBODY IN THESE TEN ASKS HIM ABOUT IT AND THE NAME IS NOT ON THAT BOARD AND IS NOT IN ANY MOUTH IN ANY OF THE TEN; THE MAN TWO STALLS ALONG LOOKS AT THAT SPACE AND DOES NOT ASK AND NEITHER OF THEM SAYS A WORD.** On day 710 he is at the far end of that bench and does not look at that shelf and is not asked to. **HIS SPACES, HIS RATES AND HIS SILENCE ARE NOT JOINED TO THE ROW IN ANY OF THE TEN AND WERE NOT BY THE NARRATOR.**

**THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT AT THE SALT WHARF. HE IS IN TWO OF THE TEN, HE IS THE ONE ADRIAN VALE'S HANDS DO SOMETHING TO IN BOTH, AND NOBODY IN EITHER ASKS HIM ANYTHING AND NOBODY IN EITHER THINKS HIM.** On day 703 he comes down that row of flats with his own barrow empty and his own knife in his belt, finds a near gate lying against a rail that had stood open the whole flood, goes round by the low side for nothing, and says out loud that he is not going to ask anybody whose two hands shut it. On day 706 he comes down that second row of flats, goes over the top of a plank instead of round by the low side, his own boot goes through under the far end once, he comes back over it and does not go over it a second time, and says out loud that he did not know whose two hands laid it there. **THE FIGURE ON HIS TALLY-BOARD AND THE FIGURE ON THE OTHER SIDE OF IT ARE PRINTED IN NEITHER CHAPTER AND ARE NOT EQUATED BY ANY MOUTH IN EITHER, AND ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE A DAY WALK PAST THAT YARD IS NOT COUNTED IN EITHER AND IS NOT EQUATED WITH THE CENSUS AT THE FOOT OF THAT STAIR IN EITHER.**

**THE MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-SEVEN WHO WASHES THAT BOARD. HE IS IN THREE OF THE TEN AND IS ASKED NOTHING IN ANY OF THEM, AND HE SAYS THE VOLUME'S FIRST DISCOVERY OUT LOUD ON ONE OF THEM.** On day 705 the man of about thirty-four comes out at that front door and says one thing out loud to him at his own front and he says he will hear it and that there is no paper of it in this city. On day 707 he carries a bucket up that stair and says out loud, in that room and in front of about nine people, that he has walked past that door every morning of this flood and has never once seen the same four go past twice, and that nobody is keeping that number, and that a number kept would come out different every morning and would be a number of people and not a number of that room; about four people in that room say it is obvious and about four say nothing; and he names what it cost him to say it and nobody thanks him for naming it. On day 709 he comes along those flags with his own empty bucket and finds the joints dry, and does not fill the bucket, does not pick the rag up, and does not put a hand on any part of that end of that market. **THE WALL ABOVE THE TOP EDGE OF THAT BOARD IS WASHED BY NOBODY AND BY NO SECOND PERSON ON ANY OF THESE TEN DAYS, AND THAT BOARD'S FACE IS NOT WASHED ON ANY OF THEM EITHER.**

**THE WOMAN AT THE NEXT TRESTLE ALONG IN THAT MARKET ROW. SHE IS IN ONE OF THE TEN, SHE HAS NO AGE AND NO DESCRIPTOR BEYOND THE ONE ALREADY ON A PAGE, SHE ASKS HIM TO HOLD THE EDGE OF A BARROW AND HE DOES IT, SHE SAYS TWO THINGS OUT LOAD, AND SHE IS NOT ON ANY OTHER PAGE OF THIS BATCH.**

**THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-NINE AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT ARE THREE PEOPLE AND THE TWO OF ABOUT THIRTY-FOUR AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-FIVE ARE NOT AMONG THEM. NOTHING IN THESE TEN MERGES ANY TWO OF THE THREE MEN AND NOTHING SETTLES ANY TWO OF THEM IN NARRATION, AND THE MAN WHO KEEPS THE STALL AND THE MAN WHO TRADES ON A BOARD ARE IN THREE CHAPTERS BETWEEN THEM AND IN NONE OF THEM ASK EACH OTHER ANYTHING.**

**ADRIAN VALE. STAGE 2 ON ALL TEN DAYS, IN EXACTLY THREE OF THEM — 0703, 0706 AND 0709, WHICH ARE THE THREE THE DAY MAP'S COLUMN NAMES — NO WORKING PERFORMED, NO THRESHOLD OPENED, THE OTHER WORLD NOT NAMED ONCE, NO WORKWAY OFFERED AND NONE ACCEPTED, AGED NOWHERE, AND **IN ALL THREE HE IS THE CAUSE OF AT LEAST ONE THING THAT HAPPENS TO SOMEBODY ELSE AND NOBODY THANKS HIM AND NOBODY TELLS HIM HE WAS RIGHT AND NOBODY IN ANY OF THE THREE IS WAITING FOR HIM TO BE USEFUL.** On day 703 a gate came across of its own accord because he turned a loose peg with his own thumb, and a man with a barrow went round by the low side for nothing. On day 706 a man went over the top of a plank and came back and did not go over it a second time. On day 709 a man of about twenty-seven did not use a trough, a bucket and a rag that had been put where they were not, and a man of about thirty-four had to go round them. **THE THREE WANTS AND THE THREE OUTCOMES ARE THE PLAN'S AND WERE NOT SUPPLIED BY A CARD: NOT OBTAINED, OBTAINED, OBTAINED, IN THAT ORDER, THE FIRST A WANT THAT FAILS, THE SECOND A WANT THAT HOLDS, AND THE THIRD A WANT THAT HOLDS FOR A MAN WHO WAS ON THAT ROW BEFORE HE WAS. NOBODY THANKS HIM FOR THE TWO HE GOT.**

**AND THE FOUR PEOPLE WHO ARE HANDLES AND NOT PEOPLE, WHICH NO CHAPTER OF THESE TEN GAVE ANYTHING TO. THE WOMAN WHO WALKED A MARKET IS ON NO PAGE OF ANY OF THESE TEN AND WAS GIVEN NO AGE, NO NUMBER, NO NAME AND NO DESCRIPTOR. THE ONE OF ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE A DAY WHO STOPPED AT THAT DOOR ON A DAY THAT IS NOT ONE OF THIS VOLUME'S FIFTY WAS GIVEN NO WORD, NO FACE, NO LINE, NO AGE, NO NUMBER, NO NAME, NO DESCRIPTOR AND NO SECOND APPEARANCE, WAS JOINED TO NOBODY, AND DID NOT APPEAR. ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE A DAY IS A CENSUS AND NOT A PERSON AND WAS GIVEN NO DESCRIPTOR AND WAS NOT COUNTED IN ANY OF THESE TEN. AND THE PERSON WHO GOES PAST THAT DOOR IN A CHAPTER OF THIS VOLUME IS A DIFFERENT PERSON AND IS ALSO A HANDLE; NOBODY STOPS AT THAT DOOR ON ANY OF THESE TEN DAYS, SO NO CHAPTER OF THESE TEN COULD MAKE THE TWO THE SAME PERSON, AND NONE DID.**
