**Nyla Ferrow** dictated a five-item form for watching a hole in a floor that cannot mend it, with a price on it, in her own words, with the word *form* in the book next to her name, and **asked the question that had been unasked for nine years**.

**Wenna Cray** refused a man a place on the list of nine and was right, said *then somebody had better put him in the form* and was right, and asked *fact or fault* in a wet gallery for the second time in nine days.

**Renna Ord** changed *refused* to *not owing*, named the cheapness of a stipend and why, answered Vask in nine words, and after fifty days was asked what she decided and gave the smallest answer in the book.

**Ovin Sarr** had his own name struck off the top of the roll's second page in daylight for a true reason, and wrote that word once and then put the chalk down rather than write it every time.

**Ferris**, a gate-hand of about fifty, who said in a yard that he would rather say no for a week and be asked again than say yes for good, **did not come back for the second shift on the day the working was done**, because he has not been paid, and there is no entry and no fault and nobody went to his door, and **the next evening, at about the seventh hour, before the tide, he wrote *works* in the box against the fiftieth day in a hand of about fifty years old, and nobody asked him what he wrote.**

**Eli Mercer** said yes once, to a page, and said four words when he came back, and was not asked a second question.

**Sabra Vint** put nine years of paper on a wall in two hands that are not hers and said the number nineteen to a boy of nine and told him that not asking for nine years is a different thing from not knowing.

**Ivenn Marrow** did not put his hand up, entered that he was going to and does not know why he did not, and **Adrian Vale cannot tell whether it was a choice or a technique and has entered that he cannot and that it is not a thing he can use.**

**The girl of seventeen** took down the fourth page, the fifth item of the form, the two witnesses, the opening, and the whole of the licence, and the width of her column is entered as a fact for the third time in the same year.
