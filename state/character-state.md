# Character State

Compact live state for the next batch. Updated after Batch 0004 (Chapters 31–40). Private want, current pressure, physical state, and what the last ten chapters cost them. **Owen Park is dead as of the thirty-eighth day and his entry below is a record, not a plan.** The full character bible is `bible/characters.md`; the long arc is `bible/premise.md`.

## Adrian Vale — 32, protagonist

- **Stage 1, Witness.** Perceives the nearest join-line and understands one direct Veyran promise at a time. Cannot create a working, open a threshold, or hold a place. **Unbound — no workway, refused three times, the second in a room and the third out loud in a hall of three hundred and ten on the thirtieth.** On the seventh day of the new month he put a **worse and truer reason on a keeper's board in his own hand**: *I refused it because it worked. I liked saying no, in a room, in front of people, in the rain.* A mason of fifty put his own name under it.
- **Mark:** the pale fragment-mark is in the maintenance seam. Under it, separate, two inches of hair-thin black opened on the second night, now **thirty-nine days old and unmoved by one sixteenth of an inch.** He did not mark anything on the thirty-eighth, because there was no join in that yard, only a hole.
- **Seat:** named holder of the pull, readmitted on the thirtieth for a further term. On the thirty-eighth he had the standing to stop a crossing and was in a room when a man was found alive and **did not say the word**, and a notary entered the fact herself, in her own hand, because he is barred from entering a statement against his own seat.
- **Debt:** a season of the bottom field, entered day 3, continued and enlarged on day 30, **thirty-seven days old at the end of day 40 with no final figure** — and now the whole of the Reed Reach's standing charge as well, returned to him by Nine Elms under the fourth line on the thirty-sixth. A season of that field has been entered in the book **five times**; the notary has entered that a cost entered five times is a notation.
- **Post:** for as long as the ground is open, the ground does not open without him. He has said out loud, in a room, that he wanted so much to be a person who is answerable in a place that he would rather have put a man into a hole than find out whether he was one.
- **What he signed unread:** the eleventh page of the book, on the thirty-sixth, in four seconds, without reading it, and did not notice himself not reading it. It is a reed cutter's account of a light at the bottom of a reed channel, entered under a heading about eleven people from another world, and it is now the first corroboration of Owen Park's statement in a public record.
- **Body:** the cut hand healing. Headache behind the left eye. About four hours a night for most of the batch. Thirty-nine days in the world and he has not slept properly since the flood began and is not going to.
- **What he wants now:** to be asked the question by somebody who is not in a room; to know whether a room with a man in it is a room somebody has been in twice; and to know what a man in a good coat meant in a yard on the fortieth. **He has not asked for page forty-one back and it is in a notary's keeping.**
- **What he has learned about himself this batch:** that a reason which works becomes a rule, and that a rule in his own hand on a keeper's wall is a thing he will still be standing behind in nine months; that *a description is not a place*, and that he had the place available and wrote a true sentence instead; that a true sentence does not stop a person who has understood it; that a silence in a yard is a third thing and not a refusal; that a record is not a fence; and that **the reason he could not say one word in a room for nine days was that he did not want to be the man who killed it.**
- **Voice:** shorter, worse at pretending. Slips: *chair* (corrected in public by Tamsin Quill in a hall on the thirty-fifth). Never says *quest*. Says *I do not know* in a yard when there is nothing better, and has said it twice in a month.

## Owen Park, 36, security contractor — DEAD, day 38, permanently

- **Died at about half past four in the afternoon of the thirty-eighth day of the flood, in the low room by the salt wharf, of a head wound that opened on the first day of this flood in another world, on a shoulder that had been out of its socket since the twenty-fifth and was never set, in a body that took a working's residue on a flagstone in front of three hundred and ten people.** Conscious and lucid from the eighth hour of the morning to the end, and he used the time he had. He is from **Dunedin, New Zealand**, and worked for a security contractor's firm, and had a sister called **Hattie Park** who does not know.
- **No respawn, no return, and the mark does not transfer.** There are two marks in the world and one of them is his, and it is **the first black scar carried in a person's own return mark** — the first scar in a person in this world, and the first one that ends a life. Eli Mercer's is not one; about a third of Eli's went. A scar in a place can be mended with work and money; a mark in a person cannot, because nobody steps over a hand.
- **His three causes are all entered in the four-page record and all three are on the page:** Adrian's underestimated crossing cost (a cost column that named seasons and salt and never once named the body of the man who goes), the shoulder that was never set, and **his own refusal to follow instructions** — which was not disobedience. He was asked, in a yard, in front of two hundred and forty people, to be sent. He asked. Nobody said the word. He went, and he knew the man had been found.
- **What he said with the time he had, and all three are in the book:** the light is not a relic, it is the same shape and the same proportion as the mark on Adrian's palm, and he has been looking at Adrian for eleven days; the road and the person to send are the same thing, he built the thing that sent him, and it worked, and the road went the other way and took the man who had not asked; and **Marrow asked for *the* true reason and Adrian wrote *his own*, and a reason in a good coat's hand is an argument and a reason in a keeper's hand is a record, and a record is the only thing in this world that has ever survived anybody.** He also said that the reason Adrian did not ask him a question for eleven days must be entered, and the not-why with it.
- **He is to be buried under the name Owen Park, in both languages, on the Sunday afternoon.** His four-page account of his own death, which he dictated and which is the first self-account of a death this book has, is read at the graveside by **Hesper Saye** in full and is not summarised. **The record notes that the injury which killed him is the one that was never set, and any shorter account of the thirty-eighth that omits the shoulder is entered as an error and read back.** He asked to be buried under a real name, and when Adrian asked him for the reason he gave it, at about the eleventh hour of the morning of the thirty-eighth, in a room, five and a half hours before he died, and it is in the book in his own words because Adrian asked for the reason and not the fact and did not know which of the two he was going to get: *I did not have a false one and I am not going to be given a good one.*
- **Unresolved and owed:** a letter to Hattie Park in Dunedin that cannot be sent, and a man in a good coat who offered a Crown channel out of Aurel and was neither refused nor accepted. A twelve-month lease with two conditions he never met, one of which was *go up the street and ask Sesh whether the roof over her head is the roof being sold*; Delya Crane has entered that the conditions stand. A boy of nine who followed him at six feet. A statement on a loft wall with an **empty chain of three names**, which is the thing that was standing in his head and which is the mechanism of his death.

## Ivenn Marrow — 41, Quiet Choir's First Reader

- **Public face:** Crown Law scholar from Aurel, in the city eight weeks, the man who arrived before the Crown's clerks. Charming, patient, useful, and the only person in Saltwake not frightened by the floor.
- **What he actually did this batch, four things, all of them in public:**
  - He was the **most useful man in the yard on day 25** — tarpaulins, a stretcher, eleven frightened people sat down in a ring with his back to the weather, and a bonesetter he had gone and found *before* the tear, on the Thursday, because he has a list of every person in this city who could be useful in a room. He put Dante Kuo on paper work for five days and never told him, which is the single most useful thing he has done and which protected a shoulder from a cause nobody had seen coming.
  - He said aloud, in a yard, the true thing about the tear: a good working's residue arriving at a place it was not sent. **Adrian underlined it twice and it is the reason the volume's midpoint is not a villain.**
  - He **translated a frightened group's account of the other side for two hours, word for word, kept the four disagreements in, and added one sentence saying he had not removed them** — and then said, unprompted, that a man in a good coat doing the thing he is best at, in a room of frightened people, is genuinely useful, **and that this is the dangerous part.** That is the most frightening version of him yet: he is reading their account as a professional and not as a recruiter, and he is therefore genuinely useful to the people he wants to use.
  - He **took his own expired chalk off the keeper's board in daylight, in front of thirty people, and withdrew the offer in his own hand** — refusing to let it stand as a refusal, because the refusals are in the roll and the withdraw is his. He wrote that the holder's answer is better than the one he would have given, asked for the true reason to be written under it in Adrian's own hand, and added that Adrian will do it.
- **What he did in Batch 0004, five things, all of them in public and none of them convictable:** he asked, in a room rather than a yard, for the **true** reason for Adrian's refusal, in Adrian's own hand, on a keeper's board, and got a worse and better answer than he wanted; he went to a grieving kitchen at Long Water **twice** and told a woman of thirty-four that there is a place on the other side of that floor, and told a boy of nineteen in the same room that it is a room eleven people stood in and there was nobody else in it, and said to the boy that going back to a house a second time is what he does and that nobody has ever made him say what it is; he refused, in a yard of about a hundred and forty people, to let Adrian's refusal of the fortieth night's field be a private matter, and said **I did not put it there**, which was not believed and was not accused; he **offered to carry a dead man's letter to Dunedin through a Crown channel out of Aurel**, in about thirty seconds and entirely reasonably, and was neither refused nor accepted; and he said the sentence about one hand.
- **The sentence about one hand, the fortieth day, in a yard of about two hundred and ten people, in about a hundred and thirty words:** that the holder's last sentence is correct; that this city is at the end of everything it can do about a thing it has carried for thirty-nine days; that the main breach is open and is stepped by the ordinary maintenance of a lock; that the only question left in Saltwake is whether the join in that gate is going to be closed; that a working is a pattern and a hand and a willing witness and a cost; that the pattern for that join is the forty words under the near gate; **and that the hand would have to be the same hand on every occasion for as long as this city stands**, which he offered as a fact about how a working works and not as an opinion. **Adrian does not know yet what the sentence is. Vail has entered underneath it that it is not the notice of the Registrar of Crossings and Tides, is not the same instrument, and that the two documents which say the same thing do not agree with each other.**
- **What nobody suspects:** that the prompts are his. He is the only man in Saltwake with both reason and access. **His complicity must stay plausible and unproven to the end of the volume and he must stay publicly useful. No public scene may convict him.** He has also just written, in chalk, that he expects to be argued with, and that he has nowhere else to be — which is a man building a dependency in public, in a good hand, in daylight.
