## 3. THE STANDING QUESTION, ANSWERED IN THIS RECORD'S OWN WORDS, WHICH IS NOT A FINDING AND NOT A FIGURE

**The volume's own claim, at `outline/volume-14.md` §12, is that a bare piece of board was the only instrument this city had for telling afterwards that a person had chosen to leave a place alone; that the instrument stops working the moment a room says out loud that the place is being left alone on purpose; that it stops working without a mark; and that the person who knew what it was for is the person who is not asked. The claim has three parts and the fifty support the first two and do not support the third, and it is worth being exact about which page carries which part, because a claim of that kind is worth nothing if the chapter that carries it is the chapter that is only saying it.**

**THE FIRST PART IS CARRIED BY A SCENE, AND THE SCENE IS DAY 675.** A man stands at the far end of a bench in a room with about nine people in it and decides that the space under a date is not to be written in, and says what the decision is in about eleven words in his own mouth before he takes it, and the room finds out what it is being asked on the morning it is asked because there is no eighth notice, and nothing is written down and nobody outside that room is told. **What makes it the finding is not a mouth and it is not the wall: it is that the place is left exactly as it was and the instrument is gone anyway, and the only record of the decision is that nine people were in that room and heard it, and afterwards there is nobody in this city who could be pointed at and asked where it was said.** The panel on day 690 is the rule; the rule is the cheapest thing the volume has and it is not what the volume found.

**THE SECOND PART IS CARRIED BY A CHAPTER THAT SAYS IT IN NARRATION THE DAY AFTER.** Day 676, a Friday, and the chapter opens by saying that a range of fifty days has two days in the middle of it and that neither of them is the middle, and then says in plain narration that the decision made in that room the day before was not written on anything and had gone out of the room with nobody, and that the piece of board was carrying what it had been carrying every morning of that flood, which was a date and a hand's width of nothing. **And on day 700 the last image says that there is nothing on that door and nowhere else in this city that says a room over a market ever decided anything at all about that wood, and that there is no person in this city that anybody could be shown who learned anything in that room.** Three days, and the first of them is a scene and the second and third are narration, and the narration is not restating the scene, it is the opposite of it: the scene is a room full of people and the narration is that there is nobody to be shown anything.

**THE THIRD PART — THAT THE PERSON WHO KNEW WHAT IT WAS FOR IS THE PERSON WHO IS NOT ASKED — IS NOT SUPPORTED, AND IT IS PUBLISHED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED.** Twenty-three of the fifty files carry a statement that she was not asked, and Chapter 0691 is a whole chapter in which she says in her own mouth that nobody in that room has ever asked her what that column on the right of the page is for, and Chapter 0699 is a whole chapter in which she has the room to herself and nobody comes up the stair and the chapter says in narration that nobody came and does not say why. **What the fifty do not do is connect the not-asking to the decision. Not one of the fifty comes within a sentence of a reason. No mouth in the fifty says that the cost of the decision falls on the person who is not asked, and no narration in the fifty says it either, and the plan's own day entries for the three costs do not say it. The finding is asserted by the narrator on a handful of days and spoken by her on two, and it is never once joined to the thing that destroyed the instrument, and the two are not joined here.** A reader who wants the fifty to prove the third part will find that they do not, and that is a fact about the shape of a volume whose subject is a place with nothing on it rather than a fault, and nothing was reworded to make the shape better.

**AND THE ONE PLACE THE FIFTY GO FURTHER THAN THE CLAIM, WHICH IS A FINDING AND NOT A RESTATEMENT.** On day 690 a page was said out loud in that same room and nobody in it agreed with it, and the man who has been right about a page in about four seconds five volumes running was asked and said nothing. **The volume therefore has two findings and they are of opposite kinds: a decision destroys an instrument without a mark, and a page said out loud in a room of about nine people can be agreed with by nobody.
