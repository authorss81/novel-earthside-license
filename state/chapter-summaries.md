**AND THE FOUR THINGS NO CHAPTER OF ANY LATER DAY MAY UNDO. 1. THERE IS A SECOND ENTRY ON THAT SHEET**, written on 997 below
the three lines of 981, a hand's width in from the same right edge, and the space below the third of those three lines is bare
and is still bare at the going of the light on 1000. **2. THE DRY CLOTH IS IN THE CRACK IN THE COLUMN OF THAT SECOND BOOK'S
LEAF**, where it went down between about the fifth hour of 995 and the going of the light. **3. THE WOOD AT THE FAR END OF
THAT BENCH CARRIES THE PRINT OF A HAND IN ITS DUST**, and the paper below the six lines on that sheet carries the same print in
a film of dust. **4. THERE ARE TWO LINES IN THE GRIT AT THE STORE'S END OF THAT ROW, AND LINES GOING UP FROM THAT END OF IT JOINT AFTER JOINT,
AND MORE OF THEM THAN ANYBODY HAS EVER COUNTED**, and nobody has asked what any of them are for. **The row is not enumerated and no
chapter of this volume may state how many there are: the figure of four at four joints was the plan's count of day 920 and the
manuscript had already contradicted it at 952, 978, 992 and 1000.** **AND THE STATE THAT MOVED BEFORE ALL OF THOSE STILL STANDS: the woman of about
fifty-two is at the far end of that bench and not against her own far wall, and she has a clear strip of floor about the width
of a hand the whole length of that wall with grit in the joints of it that nothing has walked on, and she carries that stool
up that stair every morning and never sits on it.**

**AND FOUR MORE THINGS NO CHAPTER OF ANY DAY MAY UNDO.** Pale dust in the gap behind the bottom edge of the board on the
outside wall, and the wood around the lip of that empty second hole soft a whole finger's width from the hole all the way
round, and still no second nail in that wall. A near corner of the walking space at that stallholder's trestle standing up
on its edge about the depth of two fingers, with the foot of that trestle still in the flag it went down into and shaved on
two sides on 991 and on 998, and the heap of those shavings gone off the walking space by 993. Six strokes in a column with a
day against each and a seventh place bare of everything. The bar along the top step out of its two sockets, on its thousandth
morning, not put back on any of the fifty.
