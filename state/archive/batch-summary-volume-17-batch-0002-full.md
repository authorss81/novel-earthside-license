## THE TEN CARDS, DAYS 811 TO 820

### Card 0811 — day 811, Sunday, the four hundred and ninety-sixth day of the Bare Month, political. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the strip at the foot of that stair. Somebody with both hands in a coat, and about four
  people a day going past on the paving.
- **Goal:** to stand a quarter of an hour at the foot of that stair and not take it.
- **Business:** a quarter of an hour at the foot of that stair and no stair taken. People go past on the paving
  and nobody stops. The room above goes on with its morning.
- **Resistance:** rain off and on, and feet on the paving to stand clear of.
- **Change:** a quarter of an hour is spent at the foot of that stair and nothing comes up it that would not
  have come up it.
- **Closing beat:** rain taking footprints off the flags before the tenth hour — a fact new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the paving and the weather, and the room above going on.
- **First paragraph:** somebody's hands are in it, in a coat. **No chapter of the ten says whose feet those
  are, and no mouth in this chapter asks.**

### Card 0812 — day 812, Monday, the four hundred and ninety-seventh, discovery. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the room over the market. The keeper at the near end; the man of about thirty-four; the
  man of about thirty-nine; the woman of about fifty-two at the side of the bench.
- **Goal:** to notice, correctly, that a person has not gone up, and to put no morning to it.
- **Business:** a person noticed not to have gone up and the notice correct and undatable. The keeper says he
  has not come up. Another mouth says he may have gone past below. Nobody in that room can put a morning to it.
- **Resistance:** talk on the bench covers the fifth hour and the morning wants to go on.
- **Change:** an absence is said out loud in that room and stays without a morning on it.
- **Closing beat:** the keeper turning a leaf back and going on with the page — a fact new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the bench and its talk, and the paving below.
- **The chapter does not date the absence and does not supply a figure.**

### Card 0813 — day 813, Tuesday, the four hundred and ninety-eighth, cost. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the room over the market. The woman of about fifty-two; the keeper; the man of about
  thirty-four; the man of about thirty-nine; the man of about thirty-one.
- **Goal:** for the woman of about fifty-two to say out loud, in her own words and before she says anything
  else, what it has cost her to stand at the foot of that stair and see whether the bar was in the door.
- **Business:** **THE FIRST COST OF THIS VOLUME, named by the person it is paid to, in her own mouth, first.**
  She says it before anything else she says that morning. Nobody thanks her. Nobody improves on one word of
  it. Nothing in that room is done about it at any hour of that day.
- **Resistance:** none that stops her; the morning is ordinary and the room is full.
- **Change:** a cost is said out loud in that room before any decision, and the room goes on as it went on.
- **Closing beat:** the hollow standing open-side-up on the far-end board till the tenth hour — new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the bench and the morning's ordinary work.
- **No mouth asks her what she does at the foot of that stair, and the chapter does not say why.**

### Card 0814 — day 814, Wednesday, the four hundred and ninety-ninth, character. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the room over the market. The keeper with the register; the man of about thirty-four;
  the man of about thirty-nine; the woman of about fifty-two.
- **Goal:** to carry the register the length of the room, set it on the bench, open it, and have no mouth ask
  what it is for.
- **Business:** the register on the bench and the book open and no mouth asking what it is for. People glance
  at the page and go on with the morning.
- **Resistance:** the ordinary work of the bench, which wants hands and eyes.
- **Change:** the book spends the morning open on the bench where it usually spends it shut on the shelf.
- **Closing beat:** the light going off the bench wood before the tenth hour — new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the bench work and the light through the one window.

### Card 0815 — day 815, Thursday, the five hundredth, discovery. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the room over the market, at the bench. The keeper; the man of about thirty-nine at the
  near end; the woman of about fifty-two.
- **Goal:** to look at one open page and see what is on it and what is not.
- **Business:** a column in that book with a day against each of five strokes and no sixth entered. The page
  is looked at and nobody says a number out loud and nobody writes one.
- **Resistance:** the morning goes on; the page wants nothing and asks nothing.
- **Change:** what is on the page is seen by people who had not looked at it together before.
- **Closing beat:** chalk dust on the keeper's cuff at the tenth hour where there was none at the fourth —
  new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the bench and the page itself.
- **The figure about a page is the one printed and no other; no elapsed figure is printed.**

### Card 0816 — day 816, Friday, the five hundred and first, political. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the market row. Two men of the row; the man of about thirty-four at his trestle.
- **Goal:** for one man to be told by another where the bar is lying, in about four words, and for the row to
  go on as it went on.
- **Business:** a man told by another man where the bar is lying, in about four words. The answer is four
  words and nothing else is added to it.
- **Resistance:** the row filling, and trestles that want squaring.
- **Change:** a thing everybody in the row could have seen is said out loud once, and the row does not take it up.
- **Closing beat:** the awning dripping at one corner all afternoon with nothing set under it — new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the trestles and the filling row.

### Card 0817 — day 817, Saturday, the five hundred and second, physical. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the salt wharf at the near end of eleven miles of flats, in weather. The man of about
  thirty-eight with his barrow; the man of about thirty-one; hands to carry.
- **Goal:** to get a stack of boards in under an awning out of the weather before the light goes.
- **Business:** eleven miles of flats in weather and a stack carried in under an awning. Board by board, hands
  on wood, the stack goes in and the weather stays out.
- **Resistance:** wind off the flats and wet wood, and a barrow that wants steadying.
- **Change:** a stack that stood in the weather stands under an awning by the tenth hour.
- **Closing beat:** the road out to the flats shining to the bend with nobody going down it again — new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the weather and the boards to be carried.
- **The board lying face up under two lines of grit is not wiped, not turned and not gone near.**

### Card 0818 — day 818, Sunday, the five hundred and third, character. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** one end of one market. The man of about thirty-four at his trestle; the man of about
  thirty-nine with his board under his arm.
- **Goal:** for two men to stand at one end of one market through a morning and neither to ask the other.
- **Business:** two men at one end of one market and neither asking the other. They talk of rates and weather
  and neither puts the question that is nearest.
- **Resistance:** a morning side by side, and the nearness of the thing neither says.
- **Change:** a morning is got through side by side with nothing asked and nothing told, and both men go on.
- **Closing beat:** one trestle standing empty at the end of the row till the light went — new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the trestles and the morning trade.

### Card 0819 — day 819, Monday, the five hundred and fourth, political. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the stair and the room over the market. The door at the foot of that stair; the keeper;
  the man of about thirty-one; the woman of about fifty-two.
- **Goal:** to come down to a door standing open with the light coming up the stair, and to say nothing about
  which of the two the room wanted.
- **Business:** a door standing open on a stair with the light coming up it. People come and go and the room
  goes on, and no mouth says which it wanted.
- **Resistance:** the ordinary morning, which has room for an open door without remarking on it.
- **Change:** the stair is lighted from below for the better part of the day and nobody remarks on it.
- **Closing beat:** the steps drying from the top down with the bottom one still dark at the tenth hour — new
  to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the stair and the light on it.

### Card 0820 — day 820, Tuesday, the five hundred and fifth, discovery. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the room over the market and the stair below it. The man of about twenty-seven with his
  bucket; the keeper; the woman of about fifty-two.
- **Goal:** to look at two empty sockets and to know how long they have stood empty without having asked.
- **Business:** what it is to have the sockets empty for a fortnight and not to have asked. The sockets are
  looked at and the fortnight is felt and nobody asks.
- **Resistance:** the morning's work, which has gone on round them without stopping.
- **Change:** the length of it is said out loud in that room for the first time, and nothing is done about it.
- **Closing beat:** nobody coming down that stair after the ninth hour while the room goes on till the tenth —
  new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the bucket work and the morning.
- **No elapsed figure is printed; a fortnight is the day's own business and not a subtraction.**
