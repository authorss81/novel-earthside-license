### Card 0831 — day 831, Saturday, the five hundred and sixteenth day of the Bare Month, character. Adrian: 3.
- **Where and who:** the yard at the salt wharf at the near end of eleven miles of flats, at the store door
  there. Adrian Vale; the man of about thirty-eight with his barrow and his boards; a man carrying a crate
  through that yard on his own business.
- **Goal:** to have that store door propped so a stack could be carried in out of the weather.
- **Business:** the flat of his hand on that door and his shoulder in the opening, and nothing on that wharf
  to hold it with. He gets the door back twice with his weight and it comes to in the wind both times, and it
  is not propped and the chapter does not label it. The man of about thirty-eight brings the stack to that
  door and a barrow will not go through an opening with a man standing in it, so that stack stands in the
  weather. When he comes out of the opening the door swings back to and its edge goes across the near end of
  that load and puts two boards off the stack on to the flags, and the near end of one of them takes the wet
  through. The man of about thirty-eight gets the door open himself with his knee and carries the whole stack
  in past Adrian Vale on his own, and one board of it goes on the barrow with its end black.
- **Resistance:** a door with nothing to prop it, and a barrow that wants the whole of an opening.
- **Change:** he is the cause of two boards going off that stack on to the flags and of the near end of one of
  them standing in the water at the side of that yard, and of a man having to pick them up and go on with the
  load he was carrying.
- **Closing beat:** the near end of that stack standing black and dripping on to dry boards, and a wet ring
  round the end of the one that had been down — a fact new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the barrow, the stack, the awning, the road out to the flats.
- **He performs no working, no threshold is opened, the other world is not named, nobody offers him a workway
  and he asks for none, and nobody in that yard was waiting for him to be useful and he was not useful to
  them. Nobody thanked him and nobody told him he was right.**
