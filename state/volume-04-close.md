# Volume 04 Close Record — *The White Ledger*, Chapters 151–200

Day 1 was the Tuesday Adrian Vale came out of the wall. **Day 200 is the two hundredth day of the flood and a Friday and the forty-fifth day of the Long Month**, and at about the sixth hour of the evening a man of forty-two is standing at the back of his own queue nine miles up the upper road with his hands in his pockets, and there is nothing at the front of it that needs him, and nobody has turned him out. A compact of eleven lines was read out in daylight in a room over a market on the morning of that day, in the local language, with a date in chalk on a door, by a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf who has been on nothing for two hundred days and said so first, and whose name is on the front of it for the first time in nine years and is not on any page you can read, and a girl of seventeen read the whole of it back twice for the people at the back from a slate and not from her own, because her own has her name at the top of it. A man of about forty-one who keeps a book on a trestle read out a number of one hundred and fifty-eight in a room over a yard on the Wednesday and it is on a wall in a column with a date at the top of it. A woman of about thirty-eight gave nine days of a week back in daylight on a flat on the Monday, seventeen days into a year of being angry that has not run out. A boy of eight rang a bell twice. And there have been one hundred and four silences in a row.

This is the one page the next phase has to read before it plans anything.

## 1. What Volume 04 answered

**A crossing closed and a gate came back and neither of them was done by the man who carries the question.** Adrian Vale carried a question in four sentences to a meeting-ground on the fortieth day of the Long Month, in a woman's hand, eleven miles in the dark, because he is a person who can be sent into any room in four hundred miles and be believed and cannot be sent to a gate at all. The raft could not answer it — it holds no days on any gate, and it says so, and the only thing a body that floats can do with a question it cannot answer is the thing it has done for four hundred years, which is to apply a rule that has no name on it and no column in it: every household affected by a change must be heard. **The raft refused to name the household and it was right to and a man who has been arguing against commons for a hundred and ninety-five days said so against himself. The household heard itself, unasked, on its own ground, and gave the days back, and the gate had never gone anywhere, and the only thing that changed is whose turn it is.** Then the racks stopped coming off and the four-mile causeway stood open and empty with no bar on it, and the loss fell on the settlement and not on the Reach, and that sentence is the reason the flat was able to do it. Thirty-nine men under the near gate are a hundred and fifteen days unpaid and that is a different debt and it did not change this week.

**The coalition of the seventeen split into nine and eight, in a yard, out loud, with no roll and no count and no vote, and the split was not Adrian's to repair and he did not try, and the finding is not the split: eight of them read a number they could not understand and decided that the man who can get forty people a week out of a city that cannot get one out is the safer answer to it, and about nine of the sixteen had not realised that they had decided anything.**

**And the resolution is the sixteenth shape, a land-and-labour compact between the Redroot Commons and the Reed Commons, eleven lines, dated, in daylight, in a room, with the ground staying with the households, no land right of any kind transferred, no workway a term of anything, the labour shared and posted, a right of refusal that cannot be bought out, a man of about thirty-nine's own objection to it carried in it in his own words with the date on it because he is a household affected by a change, the salt a common and not a product, the road open to everybody on posted days, the store and its queue on the same posting and not above it, a share on the same posting as every other share, and a date. The line that is a person is the ninth line and it is *a person may be found*, and that is not the fifth line of the compact of eleven lines and may not be confused with it: a document that can be ended by the asking of a person and a document that requires a person to be findable are not the same instrument and neither is a copy of the other. Jonah Pike lost the export, kept the queue, took a share on the same posting as everybody else, said nine sentences of which eight are the best arguments anybody in this city has heard, and the ninth was the word *order*, which a woman of fifty-eight who cannot read said in a yard in a town nine hundred miles from here on the tenth day of the new month, and which a man from another world arrived at from the other end in the sixth week of this flood, and the two of them have now been in a room twice and found out nothing either time. He is not defeated and he does not leave and he is not the end of this.**

**And the person who pays is the woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf, and she named the price before she said yes, in her own words: for nine years nobody in this city has known what she looks like while she is arguing, and from the forty-fourth day of this month there is a date and a stall and a name on a page and anybody with a copy can wait at that wharf and she will be the one who comes out, and she will be findable while she is arguing. She asked for it to be entered that she said no for nine years and that this is the yes and that the two are the same person and that she is not a different woman today. Nobody thanked her and she asked in advance not to be thanked. She is a page for the first time in nine years of refusing to be on one, and the batch record is explicit that her name is not printed on any page and a later writer may not print it and may not have a person in a room say it.**

## 2. What Volume 04 deliberately did not answer

None of these is a gap. Each is a live question and each was protected in prose.

- **What a day of the trestle count is.** The number is one hundred and fifty-eight, counted one a day from the fortieth day of this flood and from nothing else, it was one hundred and fourteen on the sixty-fourth day of the month after this one and eighty-nine on the thirty-ninth, and forty-four days separate the hearing from the room and there are forty-four in the difference. **The man who keeps it does not know what a day of it means and has never pretended to and has never been asked in four hundred days. Nobody in this city can find out. The number is correct and nobody can stop it, and it went onto a wall in the ordinary course in the room it was read in, and about four people in that room had wanted it not to. A correct page is a page nobody can argue with, and a page nobody can argue with is a page nobody can stop, and this volume ends on one.**
- **The line in the same book that is not a number.** It is the only line in it that is not a figure. It is addressed to a person in another hemisphere, it has not been sent, there is no route in this world that a letter can travel, it is in no book, no office in nine hundred miles knows it exists, and the man keeping the number has still not mentioned it and no chapter in this volume had him mention it. **Not resolved, not softened, and Volume 05 may not resolve it.**
- **About four hundred and forty people who dry salt for a buyer nine hundred miles away.** The racks stand in a channel and the channel takes its water from a gate and the gate's days went back to a household, and the people who are not on the settlement's rota have never been on it and are paid in salt and a place at a fire, and there is no route in this world that reaches that buyer and no page that tells him anything. **They were not in either room and they are not in the compact and nobody in four hundred miles can reach them.**
- **A woman of forty-eight in a fourth house at Nine Elms.** The fourth channel is shut, the quarter is gone, the fourth line of a term sheet is in force, and the hole in the Reach's own rule that is shaped exactly like her was found in Chapter 0167 and was not filled on the twenty-fourth of the Long Month and was not filled on any of the seven days after it. **She is not named once in Chapters 0171 to 0200, nobody stood outside the fourth house, and the hole is open and deliberate.**
- **The box, the delegation, the eight names, the six rooms, the page on the back of a stone, the twenty-eight words on a slate, the two words in about nine rooms, and the room at the far end of the Narrow Mark.** Untouched, as in the six batches before it. **The word *knock* does not appear once in Chapters 151–200 and the correct number of times in Volume 04 is also zero.**

## 3. What is live into Volume 05

1. **The trestle count and the letter in the same book**, and the question the volume's close record exists to hand on: **how many people on Earth are disappearing, and who is exploiting the fear.** Partly answered, not closed. The number is one hundred and fifty-eight and nobody knows what a day of it is. **The White Ledger's queue is the only functioning way out of the world, it works, it is on the same posting as everybody else now, and it is still the reason the fear is worth having, and the man who runs it is not exploiting anybody and is not a saint for it.**
2. **Jonah Pike**, who has lost his export, kept his queue, put a share on a public posting where it can be removed by anybody who reads the line, and is standing at the back of his own queue on a Friday afternoon with nothing to do and nowhere to be. **He is a serious rival and Volume 05 may not defeat him and he may not become a villain.**
3. **The sixteen shape, dated the forty-fifth day of the Long Month, and the four copies of it that about nine people in four hundred miles could now find, and a posting bound to it with a date and a price under every line, and nobody in this city knows how a right of refusal that cannot be bought out is administered except the man who says it cannot be.** Nobody has run the compact for a day. **The store's queue is on the same posting as everybody else's and that has not been tested.**
4. **The gate came back and the household is seventeen days into a year of being angry, and a woman of about fifty-nine who keeps that house is the one who has to be in it for the rest of the year.**
5. **Adrian Vale, Stage 2, Passage holder, in the doing, who holds nothing, is the most findable person in this world, said so himself, was asked on the forty-third day of the Long Month whether a day suited him and said no, and will never know who checked a number he was not in the room to hear.** A share with the post taken out has been in a coat for about sixty days and has not been answered. **No chapter of Volume 05 may offer him a workway and he may not ask for one, and no chapter may turn the name he did not choose into a qualification.**
6. **A correct return on a desk nine hundred miles away and a correct number on a wall in a converted salt store, and a third correct number on a fourth wall that agrees with nothing, and the reading of all three is nobody's in this city, and the next quarter is not a date.**
7. **The delegation of four people and a question has not left, nobody has been asked whether they will go, and the only instruction it has is *to open nothing*, and that clause is the woman of twenty-nine's and was said out loud by her in a room on the forty-fourth day of the Long Month and is kept as hers.**
8. **The compact of eleven lines, the provisional recognition, the seat of holder of the pull, the cost column, the fee of eleven ounces, the second list, the measurement column and its un-taken twenty-fifth reading, the woman of forty-eight, the box, the eight names and the room at the far end of the Narrow Mark** — all carried, none discharged, all listed in the *Live into Volume 05, Batch 0001* block of `state/open-threads.md`.

## 4. What must not be re-litigated

- **Adrian does not change stage, and Volume 05 may not change it.** He performed no working in Volume 04 and no threshold was opened and the other world is not named once in fifty chapters. **The gains of Volume 04 are a share, a name he did not choose, a month, a date, a gate that came back, and a woman who went on a page on purpose. None of them is a power. One of them is the price of a power. All of them are heavier than a power.**
- **The keeper of the Redroot gate may not be rescued, talked out of it, given a reason to have done otherwise, or made glad, and *the whole of her* may not be used about her at all.** A year is a year and seventeen days of it are paid.
- **A gate is not owned and what a household has is the days on it, and the sentence was said once, in Chapter 0154, at a junction, in the local language, and it is not said again in the fifty chapters of this volume and may not be said in narration and may not be improved on.** The instrument for a gate's recovery is a community's own four-hundred-year-old rule and it has no name and may not be given one and may not be replaced by anything a document can do.
- **The water-count's record of who may use a water gate is a list of persons, it has kept one for nine years, every name on it is alive, and it was not resolved in this volume and may not be.**
- **Marrow stays publicly useful and unaccused. He reads a number and does nothing else. He has still not mentioned the line in his book, and no chapter may have him mention it, and neither of his two words may be guessed aloud by anybody who has not been given them and he may not explain them.**
- **A weekday is a figure and day 1 is a Tuesday, and every weekday in Chapters 151 to 200 was recomputed from day 1.** The four double days of the volume are 194, 196, 198 and 200 and each of them carries two chapters and one weekday.
- **One date-form set, four forms:** *The Nth day of this flood* (1–40); *The Nth day of the new month* (26–90, N = day − 25); *The Nth day of the month after this one* (91–155, N = day − 90); ***The Nth day of the Long Month* (156–220, N = day − 155)**. **Never *of this month*, *of the month*, *of next month* or *of this new month*, and never a form like *the last day of the month before this one* — the last one of those was written in Batch 0002 and removed.** *March* is a month and is never a date number. The only two month names in this world are *March* and *the Long Month*.
- **An elapsed count is that chapter's own day number subtracted from the day the thing happened, and the day a thing happened has to be opened in a file and not remembered, and where two elapsed counts sit in one sentence the event each is measured from has to be named in the sentence.** The anchors of this volume: the first of March is the eighty-fifth day of this flood, so the unpaid figure is the day less eighty-five; the fourth channel at Nine Elms was shut on day 112 and that junction has not received water since day 69; the trestle count is the day less forty; the quarter ran out on day 189 and it is not replaced; **and the interval since the seventh week of this flood is the only one in Volume 04 that is repeated in more than one chapter, and it is correct in exactly one of them and was carried forward wrongly in twenty places in the last batch and was repaired.**
- **The count of this city's silences is one hundred and four at day 200, derived: ninety-five on the thirty-sixth day of the Long Month, ninety-eight on the thirty-ninth, and one addition for every day the wall says nothing, and the wall said nothing for the last six days of the volume.** A day the wall says something on is not a silence. **The wall has said thirty-four things in this city, five have been refused out loud, and it said nothing at all after day 191.**

## 5. The one thing the next phase should know about the shape of the book

Nobody in this volume got stronger. A woman of about fifty-nine said in her own words that she is the one who has to live in a house with somebody else's anger in it for the next year. A man of fifty-two who digs said the channel will silt and that he cannot say what day the water comes back. A man of about thirty-nine said that a right of refusal that cannot be bought out is a shape a person can hold up in four years and not a rule anybody is going to check, and put his own objection to it into a page he is party to. A woman of about thirty-four said that being findable is the price of being real and not the penalty for it, and then a year later in the same flood paid it herself and asked in advance not to be thanked and was not thanked. **A man from another world gave up the only thing he is good at as a way of being needed, and nobody thanked him, and then he stood at the back of a room for four hours and did not answer a question in his own words, and then he chose not to be in the room where a number was read out, and the gains of the whole volume are a share, a name he did not choose, a month, a date, a gate that came back, and a woman who went on a page on purpose.**

**The last image is a man of forty-two at the back of his own queue with his hands in his pockets, and nobody turns him out, and a compact nobody can check is better than a list anybody can be angry at, and about two hundred and forty people in a market know both of those sentences and neither of them helps.**
