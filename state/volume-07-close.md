## What this volume is, in one line, and the one thing it spent that cannot be got back

**Volume 07 is fifty days on fifty days in which a boundary could not be settled and a word was spent.** A page four hundred miles inland is the only page in the world that says where a line in the ground runs, and the four people who could fetch it have stood still for two hundred and five days and nobody has ever asked any of them whether it will go. A notary named a month on a page in Chapter 0316 and declined to give it a length, and said why, and the reason is hers. A man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall and wrote the ninth line said one word out loud in a room in daylight with a date on the door, and it went on a slate, and thirty-two days later he took it off that slate in chalk with a cloth in the same room in the same light. **Nobody in fifty days got stronger. The antagonist is a column. The only instrument that has not lied to anybody in this book spent a word and cannot get it back.**

**The thing this volume spent, and the reason it needed a close record more than the last four did, is that on day 342 a word was taken off the only page in four hundred miles that had it, and no page in the world will ever carry it again.** A reader knows what a straight line in an orchard about nine hundred paces off a shut gate is called. Four hundred miles of people, including this volume's own cast, do not, and cannot, and the one person who could say it out loud has said in a room that he is not going to say a fifth thing in front of about nine people. **That is a state no earlier volume of this book has been in. A close record that does not say it plainly has missed the volume, and a later writer who plans against it without knowing it is planning against a volume in which a piece of vocabulary was permanently spent.**

## 2. What Volume 07 deliberately did not answer

None of these is a gap. Each is a live question and each was protected in prose for fifty days, and this volume protected the first item on the list by not going near it.

## 4. What must not be re-litigated

- **The sixth form, and the fact that the month has no length.** ***The Nth day of the Bare Month***, the Nth being day less 315, from day 316. Established on a page in **Chapter 0316** by Sera Vail, a notary of fifty, after a refusal whose reason was the refuser's, **with NO LENGTH STATED**, and the reason she gives for declining to state one is hers. **That is the cheapest protection the whole volume bought and it costs one sentence in a notary's mouth.
