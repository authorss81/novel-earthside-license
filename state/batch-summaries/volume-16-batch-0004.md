## THE TEN CARDS, DAYS 781 TO 790

### Card 0781 — day 781, Friday, the four hundred and sixty-sixth day of the Bare Month, political. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** that room. Keeper at the near end with the book; man of about thirty-nine with his board; man of about thirty-four; woman of about fifty-two; man of about twenty-seven; carrier.
- **Goal:** to get through a morning with an empty place at the far end of the bench.
- **Business:** a person noticed not to be in the room and the notice correct and undatable. The man of about thirty-four says the man of about thirty-eight has not come up; the corner where his barrow rests stands bare.
- **Resistance:** nobody can say since which morning the absence began; the room talks round it with weather and flats.
- **Change:** the far end stays empty to the tenth hour; the keeper carries the book to the shelf; the board goes down the stair under an arm.
- **Closing beat:** a jug untouched on the table and a cloth folded twice beneath it.

### Card 0782 — day 782, Saturday, the four hundred and sixty-seventh, discovery. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a room that cannot say how many of them there were and is not asked.
- **Change:** the hour where a thing was said passes in silence; small talk does not cover it; the board is carried far-end to near-end and down.
- **Closing beat:** dust settling back over wood where hands and boards had been.

### Card 0783 — day 783, Sunday, the four hundred and sixty-eighth, character. Adrian: no.
- **Where:** the market end, two men. Man of about thirty-four at his trestle; man of about thirty-one with his satchel shut.
- **Business:** two men at one end of one market and neither asks the other.
- **Change:** a leg mended, rain discussed, nothing asked; the fence of sixteen willow posts and eleven withies stands unmeasured in narration.
- **Closing beat:** canvas dripping onto wood below.

### Card 0784 — day 784, Monday, the four hundred and sixty-ninth, character. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a stool at the side of a bench and nobody sitting on it.
- **Change:** the woman of about fifty-two looks at the stool and does not go to it; a boot rocks it once; the board goes far end and down.
- **Closing beat:** a stool holding its place on a knot with dust on its seat.

### Card 0785 — day 785, Tuesday, the four hundred and seventieth, physical. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a stone lip and a trough and a bucket and a rag in the ordinary course.
- **Change:** lip wiped bare, trough filled, bucket returned to the low place on its side, rag spread damp-side-up, chalk box left with its lid ajar.
- **Closing beat:** a stone lip holding its damp against the evening wind.

### Card 0786 — day 786, Wednesday, the four hundred and seventy-first, political. Adrian: 12.
- **His hands:** the flat of the door at the foot of that stair. **What he wanted it for:** to have that door standing open through the morning so that anybody going past could see up the stair into the room. **Not obtained.**
- **Resistance:** the bar in its sockets on the far side lets the door open a finger's width and no more; a stone wedged at its foot is kicked clear.
- **Change caused by him to somebody else (not a decision, word, document, notice or panel):** the man of about twenty-seven must shift his full bucket to get past on the narrow side, slops water down his coat and boot, sets the bucket down hard, and goes on leaving wet prints; nobody thanks Adrian, nobody tells him he was right, nobody was waiting for him to be useful.
- **Closing beat:** a chalk day above a shut door with no palm-mark in the clean wood beneath it.

### Card 0787 — day 787, Thursday, the four hundred and seventy-second, decision: the panel, and the one question, and a number in an answer. Adrian: no.
- **The wall:** along the far side, one thing, in a block of its own, plain, no mark, not said aloud by anybody, not repeated in any mouth, nobody saying it right or wrong. The man of about thirty-nine is in that room and is not asked whether he agrees with it.
- **The cost:** the woman of about fifty-two asks the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that register one question about that book; she answers it; the answer is a number. She is thanked by nobody, nobody improves on it, nobody asks a second question, nothing said that morning is acted on before the light goes.
- **Change:** the light leaves the far wall bare again; the board goes far end and down; the book goes to the rear shelf ahead of the shaded form.
- **Closing beat:** a folded cloth and a stool with dust, the chalk day holding untouched.

### Card 0788 — day 788, Friday, the four hundred and seventy-third, character. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** the decider in that room and not asked whether he agrees with it — *it* being the panel that went up on day 787, not the wall.
- **Change:** he stands the whole morning at hand on board and then far end, says nothing, is asked nothing; the room talks of ordinary things.
- **Closing beat:** a bench keeping board-warmth briefly after the stair goes quiet.

### Card 0789 — day 789, Saturday, the four hundred and seventy-fourth, physical. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a board on two nails and a wall above its top edge that is not washed.
- **Change:** the board wiped clean round its strokes, frame and nails wiped, trough filled, bucket set in the low place, rag spread, chalk box left ajar; the plaster above untouched by any hand.
- **Closing beat:** a roof-drip finding its old path above a clean board.

### Card 0790 — day 790, Sunday, the four hundred and seventy-fifth, character. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a carrier with both hands empty at the far end of a bench.
- **Change:** the satchel left below; the carrier stands the morning empty-handed at the far end beside the woman of about fifty-two; the board is laid beside him and taken down; the book goes to the rear shelf.
- **Closing beat:** two pale hand-places in dusty wood with the grain showing between them.

Nobody thanked him and nobody told him he was right.
5. **Small durable states moved.** The stool stayed empty and in place; the lip was bared and left damp; the outside board was wiped while the plaster above stayed streaked; the satchel spent a morning below while its carrier stood empty-handed at the far end.
