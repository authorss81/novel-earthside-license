## THE TEN CARDS, DAYS 841 TO 850

### Card 0841 — day 841, Tuesday, BM 526, political. Adrian: no.
Hands: the keeper's hands on the edge of the register to square it, to have the page lie flat for the morning. A stranger comes to the foot of the stair, goes up it, finds the room above with its morning going on and nobody waiting at the top of it. Change: a new pair of boots on the boards and nothing given to them. Closing: a thing taken away.

### Card 0842 — day 842, Wednesday, BM 527, cost. Adrian: no.
Hands: the man of about twenty-seven's hands on the lip of the trough, to have the water still for the bucket. A cost named by a person who is not the person it is about, in daylight, with the date outside, and nobody improves on one word of it. Hands answer while the mouth speaks. Closing: a thing drying.

### Card 0843 — day 843, Thursday, BM 528, character. Adrian: 4.
Hands: Adrian Vale's hands on nothing on a page; what he wanted the thing for is to have said one thing out loud in the room and been let go of afterwards. Obtained no. He comes up the stair, says the cost first in his own words, then says one thing once; the chapter prints neither. At about the tenth hour he goes down the stair and is not on it again on any of the seven mornings that follow. Nobody thanks him, repeats one word back, or asks him a second thing. Cause: a bench end cleared because he stood from it; no chapter says so. Closing: a thing put down.

### Card 0844 — day 844, Friday, BM 529, political. Adrian: no.
Hands: the man of about thirty-four's hands on the face of his own board, to have the damp off it before the middle of the day. A door with nobody coming up it for a whole morning. Change: the morning goes on without a stair taken. Closing: a thing opening.

### Card 0845 — day 845, Saturday, BM 530, recovery. Adrian: no.
Hands: the woman of about fifty-two's hands on the wood of the bench, to hold herself still while she asks. The resolution: she asks the man of about thirty-one one thing, he answers with a morning, and it is the first morning on which the iron at the foot of the stair was not in its sockets. Nobody thanks him and nobody asks him a second thing. The narration does not say he is right and does not say the room has what it wanted. No chapter prints the morning itself. Closing: a thing found out elsewhere.

### Card 0846 — day 846, Sunday, BM 531, physical. Adrian: 5.
Hands: Adrian Vale's hands on a bar lying on the top step; what he wanted the thing for is to have it out of the weather and out of the way of feet. Obtained no. He puts a hand on it and does not get it up and does not put it in its sockets. No working, no threshold opened, the other world not said, no workway offered or asked, not thanked, not told he was right, nobody waiting for him to be useful and he is not useful. The bar is in a hand on exactly one morning of the fifty and this is it. Cause: a man with a barrow steps wide of him and goes on. Closing: feet going on past the stair.

### Card 0847 — day 847, Monday, BM 532, physical. Adrian: no.
Hands: the man of about twenty-seven's hands on the handle of the bucket, to keep it from spilling on the stair. Rain in the stairwell, the bottom step shining, the iron above it where it was. Change: water comes down and the room goes on above it. Closing: water arriving where it had not stood.

### Card 0848 — day 848, Tuesday, BM 533, character. Adrian: no.
Hands: the woman of about fifty-two's hands at the sides of her dress, to stand still at the near end of the bench while she speaks. The new question, asked by her to the man of about thirty-four at the near end of the bench, in the room, in daylight, with the date in chalk outside, and not to the room. He does not answer and nobody thanks him for not answering. The chapter prints no wording of it. Closing: light leaving the bench wood.

### Card 0849 — day 849, Wednesday, BM 534, recovery. Adrian: no.
Hands: the keeper's hands on the sill of the one window, to ease it wider for the air. A morning in which the room says a thing about the weather and nothing else. Change: talk goes round once and stops. Closing: a cloth drying on the sill.

### Card 0850 — day 850, Thursday, BM 535, recovery. Adrian: no.
Hands: the man of about thirty-one's hand on the iron where it lies; to have it known and not to move it. The last image: he puts his hand on the bar where it lies on the top step, lifts it a hand's breadth, sets it down in the same place, and from the iron, from the hand and from the dust there is no way of telling whether it has been done before. No elapsed subtraction on this chapter at all. The chapter does not observe any rhyme and does not improve any. Closing: dust showing nothing either way.
