## THE TEN CARDS, DAYS 751 TO 760

**A card gives the day, the pressure, who is in the room or the yard, the goal, the resistance, the change, and
the closing beat, and a card may not carry anything the plan does not give it. The tenth card carries the
batch's turn and the batch ends on the morning before the volume's first cost.**

### Card 0751 — day 751, a Wednesday, the four hundred and thirty-sixth day of the Bare Month, physical. Adrian: no.

- **Where and who:** the salt wharf at the near end of eleven miles of flats, and then that room over that market. The man of about thirty-one, the man of about thirty-eight at that wharf, the woman of about fifty-two, the man of about thirty-four, the man of about thirty-nine.
- **Goal:** water in that trough, and get there before anybody else uses it.
- **Resistance:** the man of about thirty-eight will not say who filled it and nobody in that yard saw anybody fill it.
- **Change:** the trough is full and it has been used and no hand in that yard knows anything about either.
- **The room's own figure:** said aloud once, by the woman of about fifty-two, in the ordinary course.
- **Closing beat:** the water standing at the level another man left it at, and nothing on that trough that says any of it happened.

### Card 0752 — day 752, a Thursday, the four hundred and thirty-seventh, character. **Adrian, one of eleven.**

- **His hands:** a length of board carried up eleven miles. **What he wanted it for:** to set it down level with the sill of the one window in that room. **Not obtained.**
- **Resistance:** the sill is a hand's breadth deep and the board is not that narrow, and nobody in that room asked him what he wanted it for.
- **The day's discovery:** he watches the whole length of that strip of ground from the bottom step and cannot afterwards say how many he saw.
- **Change:** two lengths of board lean against that wall at the foot of that stair at the tenth hour, and nothing on the wall says which of the two men put which one there first.
- **Closing beat:** one of the two is his and one of them is not.

### Card 0753 — day 753, a Friday, the four hundred and thirty-eighth, physical. Adrian: no.

- **Where and who:** that wharf yard, and then that room. The man of about thirty-one, the man of about thirty-eight, the man of about thirty-four.
- **Goal:** the level of that water, put right and put higher.
- **Resistance:** nobody in that yard sees him do it and nobody will say afterwards that they did.
- **Change:** the trough stands a hand's breadth further from that step than it stood at the fourth hour, and the stone at the low place is wet in a shape that was not there at the fourth hour.
- **The room's own figure:** said aloud once, by the man of about thirty-four.
- **Closing beat:** the second slate on the shelf behind her, untouched, with a day cut across the head of it and nothing under the day.

### Card 0754 — day 754, a Saturday, the four hundred and thirty-ninth, character. **Adrian, two of eleven.**

- **His hands:** the near edge of a board stood on end against the bare back of the stallholder's own board. **What he wanted it for:** to have somebody in that row see that he was in it. **Not obtained.**
- **Resistance:** nobody in that row looked at it, and the stallholder who would have looked at it never turned round.
- **Change:** the man of about thirty-nine does not go up that stair at the end of that morning, for the first time in the whole of this flood, and nobody in that row asked him where he had gone.
- **The room's own figure:** said aloud in that market row, behind him, and he hears it.
- **Closing beat:** Adrian's board put back against the same back at the tenth hour, and the man of about thirty-four looks at it and says nothing.

### Card 0755 — day 755, a Sunday, the four hundred and fortieth, recovery. Adrian: no.

- **Where and who:** a house out past the last named house. The man of about fifty-seven. Nobody else is in that house and there has not been on any morning of this flood.
- **Goal:** a gate in his own yard that does not hang crooked.
- **Resistance:** the hole in the end of the iron will not take the cord, and when he gets it to take it he takes the whole of it back off again.
- **Change:** he goes out along the top of that road about forty paces and turns back before that fence and does not go along it.
- **Closing beat:** the rope on the chair where it lay at the fourth hour, and both of those things done with his hands three feet away from it.

### Card 0756 — day 756, a Monday, the four hundred and forty-first, political. Adrian: no.

- **Where and who:** that room, and the foot of that stair. The woman of about fifty-two, the man of about thirty-four, the man of about thirty-eight at the wharf.
- **Goal:** the room's own figure, and an end to two different versions of it in the same morning.
- **Resistance:** about nine in one mouth and eight in another, and the difference between the two figures is one person and no mouth in that room can put a name to it.
- **Change:** the bar comes out of its sockets on the far side of that door at the sixth hour and the door stands open into the room all morning and nobody in that room knows who opened it.
- **Closing beat:** the bar on the flags inside, the date in chalk outside, the bare piece of board below that date bare, and about four people going past and none of them going up.

### Card 0757 — day 757, a Tuesday, the four hundred and forty-second, discovery. **Adrian, three of eleven.**

- **His hands:** the near rim of that trough at the top of that step. **What he wanted it for:** to fill it before the fourth hour so that nobody in that yard would have to be the one who filled it. **Not obtained.**
- **Resistance:** it was already full when he got there at the fourth hour and he is not going to ask one person in this city who had filled it.
- **The day's discovery:** the man of about thirty-nine says out loud that he cannot put two of his own mornings in an order, and nobody asks him a second question about it.
- **Change:** the man of about thirty-eight has to turn his barrow on its side at the foot of that stair to get past two boards leaning there, and neither man says a word about it.
- **Closing beat:** the trough standing where the other man left it, and the man who filled it having said nothing.

### Card 0758 — day 758, a Wednesday, the four hundred and forty-third, character. Adrian: no.

- **Where and who:** that room. The woman of about twenty-nine, the man of about twenty-seven.
- **Goal:** that page out of the light that comes over the roof at about the ninth hour.
- **Resistance:** the light reaches that bench whatever the book does, and the man of about twenty-seven comes up that stair and looks at that shelf and says nothing at any hour of that day.
- **Change:** the book has been moved to the far end of that bench and the room's own figure is not said by anybody on a morning when about nine people were in it, and that is the first morning of this flood on which that has happened.
- **Closing beat:** the second slate behind her, with a day cut into the head of it and nothing under it, and only her hand near that shelf all flood.

### Card 0759 — day 759, a Thursday, the four hundred and forty-fourth, physical. Adrian: no.

- **Where and who:** the handcart and the fence out past the last named house, and then that room. The man of about sixty, the man of about thirty-eight at the wharf.
- **Goal:** the coil of rope that is not on the cart where it lies every morning.
- **Resistance:** it is hanging on that fence with one of the eleven withies bound on with a piece of it instead of with the cord, and the cut end of it is clean and was cut off somebody's coil.
- **Change:** he takes the piece off, puts the cord back on, ties all eleven withies in the order he keeps them in, draws each knot tight, and does not measure that fence and does not move a post.
- **The room's own figure:** said aloud once, by the woman of about twenty-nine, who has never said it out loud in that room before.
- **Closing beat:** one withy that was bound on with somebody else's rope and is now bound on with the cord, and nobody has asked him.

### Card 0760 — day 760, a Friday, the four hundred and forty-fifth, political. Adrian: no. **THE BATCH'S TURN.**

- **Where and who:** that room, and the foot of that stair. The woman of about fifty-two, the man of about thirty-four, the man of about thirty-eight at the wharf, the man of about thirty-nine, the woman of about twenty-nine.
- **Goal:** to say out loud, in that room, what that figure is and has always been.
- **Resistance:** nobody improves on one word of it, nobody argues with it, and nobody asks where it came from.
- **Change:** the bar goes back into its sockets and the door is shut and nobody in that room knows who shut it; and the man of about thirty-nine says out loud that he has noticed that it has never been written down, and says he is not saying what he is going to do about it.
- **Closing beat:** the five figures in the middle column with a day against each and no sixth entered, and the room's own figure in about nine mouths and on no page at all.
