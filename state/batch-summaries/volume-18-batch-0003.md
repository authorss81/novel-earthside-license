## THE REVIEW REPAIR ON CHAPTERS 0871 TO 0880, AND EVERYTHING IT TOUCHED

A review of this batch (`logs/batch-0003.review.log`) returned eleven findings. This run fixed what the outline and the
bible make fixable and declined what they require it to leave alone. **No chapter was restarted and no event, day,
decision, name, panel or price was changed.**

### FIXED, IN THE CHAPTERS

1. **§17.9, the handle rule — 87 restatements reduced to 10.** Every figure was introduced by its full
   age-plus-trade handle and then described by that same handle for the rest of the chapter. §17.9 requires the full
   handle on a first appearance and permits a short handle after it, and gives *the keeper*, *the stallholder*, the
   bare age and the trade without the location as the short forms. **All ten survivors are first appearances or words
   inside a speech paragraph.** This is also the repair for the review's central complaint about template dominance.
2. **`chapter-0873.md` carried one clause twice.** The sentence ending `and nobody in the room said one word to him at
   any hour of the morning` appeared twice in the same paragraph, the second time after a full stop and in lower case.
   One instance stands, and it now carries a hand in it rather than a bare absence.
3. **`chapter-0871.md` narrated one beat twice.** The man with the satchel going down the stair and squeezing past the
   bench was described in full at the fifth and sixth hour and then re-described in the closing paragraphs. **The
   closing now states only what is different the second time — that he left the strap turned on his arm and the cost
   has moved to a shoulder that the water off the stairwell has got into — and the closing movement named by card one,
   a thing found out by feel in a person's own body, is the stronger for it.**
4. **Two sentences appeared verbatim in two chapters each.** Both were varied at the later chapter: the door at the
   foot of the stair in 0872, and the room being full in 0874. **Repeated measure is now zero across the ten.**
5. **§17.18, two closings shared a construction.** 0876 and 0877 both closed on the board face going the colour of the
   trestle beside it, in nearly the same words, on adjacent days. **0877's closing is now a change rather than a
   state — flags trodden through to stone and wet again where people stood** — which also removes the arguable
   §17.17 case the earlier reading had to publish for that chapter.
6. **§17.19, negation chains.** 0878 carried five constructions of *nobody* in one paragraph and 0873 doubled a
   clause; 0875 and 0879 each carried four and five. **Physical action now carries what the chains were carrying: the
   keeper turning a leaf and the page going on holding figures and days, the man putting his hands behind him and
   leaving them there.** The review is right that *nobody* is this volume's pressure, and §17.19 forbids only a day
   written out of negation, so the pressure is kept and the chains are thinned.
7. **§6.8(iii) and §7.2, the name.** **The chapters call the man who came up that stair by the wet-sleeve handle until
   878 and by his name from 879. 0879 still used the handle on the one day it should have used the name.**
   `chapter-0879.md` now carries it once, in narration, as a word for that man. It is on no surface, nobody asks him
   anything, and it is counted against nothing. **This is the one repair that changes what is printed on a page, and
   the outline asks for it in two places.**
8. **0871's second paragraph was one sentence of about two hundred words** introducing six figures and their props
   with no action. It is now six sentences, each with its own hand on its own thing. **The information is identical.**
9. **0875's midpoint was carrying less weight than the plan gives it.** §6.6 puts a space about two fingers wide at
   the foot of that board's column where a name is not. The chapter did not show it. **It now does, physically, as a
   gap in the writing about two fingers wide, with nobody in that row asking what it is for** — which is §6.6's and
   §7.2's exact position, and the plan does not say what the space is for and this chapter does not either.

    before day 878 and the one new person takes no descriptor at all. §17.13. §6.8. **A repair that gave this room
