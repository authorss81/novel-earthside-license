# Volume 06, Batch 0004 — Chapters 0281–0290, days 281 to 290

**Ten chapters, days 281 to 290, one chapter to a day, the last day a Thursday. These are the last five days of Movement III and the first two of Movement IV, and the hinge they walk toward is a notice that goes out four days before a room and a fifth kitchen in which a household is asked, in daylight, with a date on the door. **The subject of the five days is a person and not a form: a man of forty-one in a very good coat says nine sentences in a room in daylight with a date on the door, the first of which is that a Crown office which gets a form back with a place left empty does not send the form again, and the second of which is that it sends a person. ## 1. The batch goal, and whether it was met

**The goal was: discover that a person and not a page is what comes next, put the notice out four days ahead of nine kitchens in nine days, pay the asking of a Hearthguard of forty-four about his own eight that Chapters 0271 to 0280 owed, and open Movement IV with a person arriving who is a person.**

Seven things were required and all seven are on a page.

1. **The discovery, in a room in daylight with a date on the door, that the third of the four places is a person and that a person is what an office sends.** Chapter 0281. Nine sentences, of which the second is *the office sends a person*, and the ninth is that a person who comes up a road with a column in her hand is going to be offered the days, and there is nothing in that column for her to put them in except the third place, and nobody here is going to be in the third place, so she will walk to the next gate, and there are six gates on that stretch, and at the seventh thing she will write, and what she writes will be correct. **The eighth sentence is the only one about a person and the ninth is the only one about a page, and the man says he stopped on purpose and asked for it to be entered that he stopped on purpose, and he declined to say what the room at the top of that road looks like and asked for that to be entered too.**
2. **The notice, in writing, in each person's own words, four days before the day.** Chapter 0282. 3. **A Hearthguard of forty-four asked about his own eight, for the second time in a hundred and forty-six days, and answered in four words.** Chapter 0283. The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a public register asks him, in a room in daylight with a date on the door, having named the cost first, and she states in the room which of the two events this is: **it repeats the being-asked of the one hundred and thirty-seventh day of this flood, on a day he did not choose, and it is not the day on the fiftieth when he asked to be asked again.** He said *I know which four* and would not give a fifth word, and the second slate says the day, that she asked, and that he answered, and no more than that.
4. **A drain, four feet deep, worked by four pairs of hands where there were nine, and the four hands are four of the nine households being asked.** Chapter 0284. Nobody shut anything, a sheet went out of the room over the market at the seventh hour for a room on the thirty-third, which is four days off and not three, and about four people in that lane have worked out that a document and a drain are the same thing and that neither of them is anybody's fault.
5. **The first kitchen, a Sunday, a household asked, in daylight, with a date in chalk on a door, and the answer is a day nobody put there.** Chapter 0286. A woman of about twenty-eight of Long Rigg, a pot on a fire, a child of about two with a spoon, and a man who came with nothing because the instrument says the question goes to everybody in the room and he is not in the room. **She said yes, and a day, and the day is not the thirty-first and is not any of the nine, and nobody asked her whether the thirty-first suited her, and she suited it.**
6. **A person sent, and a person different from a page.** Chapter 0288. A carter of about thirty-five goes up a lane at the eleventh hour with nothing in his hand, because a page can be refused and a person can only be turned out, and being turned out is worse. **A man of about forty-seven says yes and names the thirty-ninth, which is the ninth of the nine, and nobody in that lane can say what is on the thirty-ninth, and he says the sheet in the asker's coat and the sheet that came up the river in a cart are the same kind of thing with a different sentence at the top of them.**
7. **The person arrives, gives a name and a date, chooses the day, and is answered by nobody being entered.** Chapter 0289. **Ilva Rooke, of about forty-five, stands at a door for four hours with a shut case, is let into a kitchen where a household is being asked, hears the household say yes, and goes away with two of four places filled in.**

**The gains are a shut case, nine unsigned sheets, a four-word answer, a drain and a page found to be the same thing, a yoke standing against a door, a day that was not on anybody's list, a hand that is not a page, a thumbprint of tar on a sill, and a person waiting in the cold because a person cannot be ignored.
