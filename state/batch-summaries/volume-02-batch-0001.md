# Volume 02, Batch 0001 — Chapters 51–60: What the Winter Is For

Ten days. Days 51 to 60, which are the twenty-sixth to the thirty-fifth day of the new month. This is the opening batch of Volume 02, *Salt and Silver*, and it does three things: it finds out that the only door in this world does not go home, it puts a winter on every number in the book, and it breaks the protagonist's best page by discovering that it is a rule about a thing.

---

## 1. The batch's spine

1. **The door is not a road home, and the reason is on page two of the licence.** A controlled opening requires a receiving community that understands the purpose. There is none on the far side of the Narrow Mark, the far side is a room with no window and no door anybody has found, and no instrument in this world can make one from this side. **The first request for an opening in the history of this book is entered as a request, with a name, a date, a person who asked, an answer that is a reason and not a refusal of the person, and a fifth line that is not part of the form.** Eight of the eleven of the second list want the road that goes where they were born. Three do not, and each of the three is named.
2. **Winter is a price, a rail, a wheelbarrow of flour and a shoulder.** Salt up a third. The Brass Measure's fourth term in a new coat. A condemned rail on two hundred steps. A crew one man short, and the fourth page of the instrument used for the first time and working exactly as written.
3. **A bar is a bar and this one was a bar.** Adrian's page of the twenty-sixth day forbids a workway offered by the instrument or by anybody using it. It is the longest page in the licence. **It does not reach a pale field in a room.** Two fields, one refusal, one acceptance, and a city that discovers there is no office in it for a hand that holds things.
4. **A description is a removal with a column for where the person is.** Hettis Roke is asked about his eight graves after nine years, and the answer is a wooden case that has been going down a river for nine hundred years and coming back empty. **One of the eight is on a stone nine miles up a road, and the names were moved and not lost.**
5. **The pull.** A room with no door is being knocked on. Nobody has to open anything for that to happen. And the review is in fifteen days on a slate with a date on it and no questions on it, and a review with no questions on it is a queue.

## 2. What was paid

- **The licence's second paragraph is read out to nineteen signers and the notary enters that she did not ask one of them what it said.** That is the price of a notary and not a schoolmistress and it is the whole of what happened in Volume 01 and it is the whole of what is still happening.
- **The question nobody had asked for nine years is asked and answered, and the answer is a machine and not a person.** That is the cheapest and the most expensive thing in the batch.
- **A boy of nineteen is hurt under the near gate on an ordinary morning at the ordinary volume in no working on ice, and one of Renna Ord's four is paid, and the page written for this day works.** Three are coming. The number four is on the page twice, as a number and as a prediction, neither above the other.
- **Adrian's forty-five days of not saying where the word *player* came from.** He said it. A fourth statement of error was entered against him and he stood by it and could say no more. Nineteen words went over it in chalk.
- **A woman of fifty-eight who cannot read was asked whether she would rather not and said that she would rather be asked, and a man of nineteen put the chalk down rather than write the same word twice, and a docker of fifty-eight picked up a woman's nine years of paper because she got tired of waiting.**

## 3. What the batch gained, and it is all paper

The **receiving page.** The **honest useless form**, sixty-one lines, complete and unreducible and carrying nothing, which is now going to Aurel four times a year in a wooden case with eleven names in it and a true sentence about a room with no door. **The procedure for a holder of a workway**, nine clauses and nine weaknesses, with a date. **The condemned rail and its cost.** **The fifth statement of a fifth Crown instrument** and the last column of a Crown table that is a seat. **A list of nine names who would be angry, with two people on it who got there by being in a room and were not asked.** **Two beds on a page with a date.** **Nineteen words in chalk on a market wall.** **A name cut on the eleventh stone of the upper road in a hand about a hundred and forty years old.** And **four knocks, three and four, both entered, neither deleted.**

**None of these is a power and all of them are heavier than a power.**

## 4. The batch's turn, and it is Adrian's

> **I wrote a rule about a thing and I did not write a rule about the world, and I have spent fifty-seven days telling a city that the difference is pedantry.**

He said it in nine words in front of nine people, and he had his hand over his eyes for four seconds first, and the sentence was said out loud by a woman of forty-one in the same room **before he said it**, and she said it better. **Perry Lask's version:** a rule that only covers the thing you can see is a rule about a thing and not about the world.

The bar is not broken. The bar is perfect. **The page bars the instrument and not the wall, and no scene in this series may make Adrian's bar do something it cannot do, and the failure it is planted for fires in Chapter 0075 at a shrine on a boundary.**

## 5. The rules established in this batch, binding on Batch 0002

1. **A receiving community cannot be made from this side.** No instrument in this world can create one. Any later chapter that opens a route toward a person must either produce a receiving party on the far side with a date on it or say in a room that it cannot be done.
2. **A fact about a shape is a third thing a question can be**, after a fact and a fault. It has no person to give it to and it is entered as a fact and it is not a fault and it is not nothing.
3. **A description is a removal with a column for where the person is**, and the column is the widest one and it is always blank, and the office is nine hundred miles away and has never once sent anybody to fill it in. **No name in Saltwake has come out of one of those cases in nine hundred years.** Any later chapter that sends a roll, a schedule, a return or a description must enter the column and enter the blankness.
4. **A workway is a job, not a power**, and there is **no office in Saltwake for a hand that holds things**, so a workway in this city is a claim on a job that does not exist. **The cost of a workway is not the work. The cost is that a standing is a reason to be angry, and a seat is only ever got rid of by the people who are angry at it.**
5. **A rule about a thing is not a rule about the world, and the protagonist's own page proves it in public.** Any later instrument in this series that bars a *document* and is expected to bar a *practice* is the same page, and the same finding.
6. **The honest useless form**: a form completed honestly by people who have decided to be unhelpful is complete and carries nothing, and **a summary is what an office acts on and a summary is where a decision hides.** Its cost is that compliance is still compliance and it writes down what eleven people want.
7. **A stipend is a wage, a wage is owed to a person, a person is described on a roll** — and now: **a guild is not a person, a guild can be refused, and the fear of the word *list* in this city is a fear of persons and has never once been about guilds.** Jonah Pike has said this out loud in a yard and it is the front door of the White Ledger.
8. **A cost that stops is a cost that has stopped** and is never to be entered as paid. **Used for the first time on the thirty-third day of the new month and it worked.**
9. **A thing that is not done is not done**, and is not entered as deferred, short or carried forward.
10. **A man who cannot be right about a number hears a number, and a join that is dressed does not knock, and both are entered and neither is deleted.** A number going up is not a pattern and is also not nothing. **The knocks are three and four and nobody has to open anything for them.**
11. **Two beds on a page with a date; eleven houses on a list is a roll.** A household may say no, need not give a reason, is not asked a third time, and is not entered anywhere.
12. **The interface has now made nineteen offers across seven batches and has never once registered a refusal of one.** Both of this batch's fields are workways and **both put the whole of the cost on a named person and called that fair and neither contains a lie, and the lie is the position.** A reader should now be able to find the position without being told.

## 6. What must not be re-litigated in Batch 0002

- **Owen Park is dead, buried, and his burial term is formally closed.** Thirty-five days since. **Nobody may write *he died because*, nothing may soften or generalise his death, and a form may not shorten a record because a form is small.**
- **The measurement column is not continuous.** Twenty-four readings, both of the last two eight and seven-eighths, **one ruled line left, and the twenty-fifth reading has not been taken and may not be taken on the wrong water or at the wrong hour.** A dressed join does not narrow; it gets dressed.
- **The chapel stone is silent and unrepaired and may not speak in Batch 0002.** The boards under the chapel have not been lifted since the fortieth day of the flood and are not lifted. **The five-item form cannot close anything and cannot say where a residue goes and may never be made to do either.**
- **The main breach is closed, dressed and silent and has never said a word.** The near gate is where forty men stand. The Narrow Mark is the only licensed route and has opened twice in sixty days.
- **No crowd ever crosses.** One named person per opening, a fresh inspection before every one, two witnesses who are not of the crew, and a receiving community that does not exist.
- **Adrian is Unbound, is on no roster, is party to the trial licence and is given nothing by it, and is still Stage 1.** He has no return mark of his own. **He performed no working in this batch and performed none in Volume 01's closing three.**
- **Two date forms only: the Nth day of this flood up to the fortieth, and the Nth day of the new month after it. Never *of this month*.** Verified: no instance survives in Chapters 51–60. **March is a month in this world's calendar** and is written here as *March*, which is the local word for it.
- **Four Crown instruments and never collapse them**: the Registry's notice of the twenty-eighth day of the flood; the third instrument of the seventeenth day of the new month, a single-author containment held by a Concord clerk answerable only to Aurel, refused in a yard and entered as ninety statements of error; the second answer of the twenty-second, not in the form required; **and the schedule of crossings of the thirty-first, which is a table and not a version of the other three.** The Quiet Choir's design is a fifth shape and is not the Crown's.
- **The name *Single Witness* and the name *First Grammar* are still not used in prose and must not be used before Chapter 0091, in a room, in daylight, with a date on it.** The shape has three descriptions on the page and no name: a rule that only covers the thing you can see, a standing that cannot be refused, and a guild that does not go home.
- **Ottoline Vask's two sentences** — the forty words are the toll and they are the pattern, and a city cannot spend a thing and be its own foundation at the same time — **are unanswered, were not put in front of a room in this batch, and must not be resolved.**
- **Renna Ord's four.** One paid. Three coming. **The number and the prediction are both on the page and neither may be removed.**
- **A person who has never been asked is not a person who has refused.** Roke has now been asked once, in daylight, and said no to the form and yes to a yard, and **that is not the same as having been asked about the other seven and it does not count as an answer to them.**
- **A man is a scar in a person is never a lesson, a consolation or a rule about anybody else.**

## 7. What the next ten chapters owe

- **The review is in fifteen days** and the questions are not on the page, and somebody has to write them, and the only people who can write are the people already in the room.
- **The fee of eleven ounces of silver** is entered as owing with no date, and a clerk of that office has said the office does not charge a fee it cannot collect.
- **Tamsin Quill's nineteen words have not been acted on**: *go and ask the city what a person with no work is for, and take the answer, and do not offer anybody a seat.* And she said it once and said it on a stair.
- **The holder of a workway has not been asked to do a single thing and has not done one, and there is no office for her, and the review date on her procedure is the fourth day of the next month.**
- **Dante Kuo has no scene in ten chapters of a new volume and the rescue argument is thirty chapters overdue.** He is the most conspicuous silence in the book.
- **Two people are on no roll at all** — a girl of sixteen and a woman of thirty-eight — and neither has been asked, and the second list has not gone into a wooden case since the third week of the new month.
- **The name on the eleventh stone, and the other seven, and the person with a knife and nine miles of road.**
- **Nobody has asked what to do about a knock.**

## 8. Notes for the reviewer

- The batch ran **37,935 words across ten chapters**, which is inside the stated 3,300–6,000 range per ordinary chapter.
- **Two System messages in ten chapters**, in Chapters 53 and 57, and **eight silent chapters.** The five-for-five silence pattern holds and is recorded in `bible/power-system.md` §15.10.
- **A self-correction was made during the batch and is worth flagging as a fixed finding:** Chapter 0053 originally attributed the flagstone incident of the twenty-fifth day of the flood to Teodor Blaz. **That incident is Owen Park's and is in his four pages, and it may not be re-attributed.** The chapter now says in terms that the two men were hurt in the same yard on the same morning and that the page that exists about it is four lines long and is about somebody else. A later writer must keep them apart.
- **A second fixed finding:** the first draft of Chapter 0056 put Eli Mercer's four words into the schedule's receiving-party column for the **twentieth-eighth** day of the flood. **The twenty-eighth-day crossing was a different person and was entered as a breach.** The column now says so.
- Verified after writing: no instance of *of this month* or *of the month*; no meta language; no use of *Single Witness* or *First Grammar*; no duplicated paragraph; day/weekday chain 51 Wed through 60 Fri, with day 50 the Tuesday and the new month beginning on day 26.
