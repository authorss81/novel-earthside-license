# Volume 02, Batch 0005 — Chapters 91–100: The Void, Two Houses and a Road

Ten days. Days 91 to 100, the first to the tenth day of the month after this one, and the whole remainder of the volume. **This batch names the shape, voids the false oath on the ground of its own condition, makes a man Stage 2 in the doing and not in a ceremony, takes his road off him, and then goes and builds two houses and a cooperative that are not his, and gives a stone back, and ends on a number and a road.** Nobody is convicted of anything. The undertaking is void and it is still on a nail, unstruck, with the string untouched. A field is not paid. A woman of forty-eight is still owed one question and is not asked. The gains are a name, a spent page, a stage, a lost road, two Wayhouses, one Open Hand, a question in a column of a quarterly description, and eleven households keeping a road.

**This batch has not been reviewed independently. The reviewer agent is configured as a subagent and the review prompt executes under the writer agent, which has now happened in four batches running, and no claim of an independent review is made anywhere in the state files for this batch.** The checks that were run in the writing pass are recorded in §10.
