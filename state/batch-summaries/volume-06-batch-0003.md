**Ten chapters, days 271 to 280, one chapter to a day, the last day a Monday. ## 1. The batch goal, and whether it was met

**The goal was: carry the volume's midpoint on day 275, pay the consent fracture on day 272 without resolving it, cross the hinge into Movement III on day 276, and stand the next batch on a run of ten days that is about wages and figures and columns rather than about a route.**

Six things were required and all six are on a page.

1. **The midpoint, in the plan's own shape.** Chapter 0275. A room over a market in a market town, in daylight, a date in chalk on the door, about nine people in it. **Hettis Roke, forty-four, of the Hearthguard of the outer ward, nineteen years in, refuses the first way on the Hearthguard's behalf in four sentences and the refusal binds the company, dated to the instrument and not to the day, and then gives one more sentence for the route itself.** The room measures the sentences: the woman of about twenty-four says *that was three sentences*, and Roke says he has one more and the fourth is the refusal and it binds, and the fifth is the route. **Nobody in that room told Adrian what he had built, and he worked it out alone in a yard at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and the chapter says he worked it out and does not say he was right.**
2. **The consent fracture, paid and not resolved.** Chapter 0272. Tamsin Quill names the price out loud — a name on a register is a name in a book, a book is kept, a name in a book can be looked up, and the third of the four places on the Crown's page is a holder — and Adrian Vale withdraws the asking before he came up the road, refuses to apologise, refuses forgiveness, and says he will not be forgiven and is not asking. **She then says there is a fifth condition, that there always was, that it is not about the question but about the man asking it, and that she is not going to hand it to him. Nobody in that room said the thing was a misunderstanding and the coda says a thing that has been paid is not the same as a thing that has been mended.**
3. **A body is not a person, and the Reach's own rule has a hole shaped exactly like a body.** Chapter 0274. Adrian's instrument goes to everybody who will be in the room, four days before, in the person's own words, and three of the four places he put it in are a body and a household and a stall and he cannot deliver it to any of the three, **and the rule that has gone from household to household for about four hundred years and has no name has never had anything to say about a body.** The way round the hole is a person.
4. **The wage is real and there is less of it, and then it is not a wage.** Chapters 0276 and 0277. A man of about forty-nine who keeps a store at the north end of that market puts about four hundred coins in a wooden bowl on the boards and it is four parts in five of the last paying day and the same nine, and the cost is named out loud in a market on the Thursday. **On the Friday the bowl does not come out, about eight of the nine do not turn up at a drain, and the reason is that a fifth which is written on nothing can be a fifth which is written on nothing the next time, and nobody did it on purpose and everybody did it.** No chapter invented a word to repair it and a figure about a drain is not a wage.
5. **About four hundred and forty people on eleven miles of flats, and the only page in four hundred miles that has ever mentioned them is a return.** Chapter 0279. Said out loud on the boards of a wharf in daylight on a Sunday by the woman of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by a wharf, whose name is not on this page, and **the counter is a man of about thirty-eight who has sent the number four times a year to an office nine hundred miles away and has never been answered, and he is the enemy's page.** Nobody rescued anybody and nobody turned the finder into a helper.
6. **Two rolls of the same gates, and nobody put them side by side.** Chapter 0280. The Marches keep a roll of gates — a gate, a keeper, a set of days, and nothing else, and no column for a person in it for longer than the oldest thing any of the four men who keep it has. The Crown has sent a roll of people with four places on it and a heading in a word nobody in four hundred miles can repeat. **Both were in one room in daylight with a date on the door and nobody put them together, and a page face up and a page face down on one table is the only picture anybody out past the last named house has of what two Crowns did to the same gates.**

**The gains are a sieve in a left hand, a withdrawn asking, a fifth condition nobody has, a word with two syllables in it, a body that cannot be asked, four sentences and a fifth, a bowl of coins, eight absences, a barrow on its side, a number on boards, and a keeper instead of a holder. None of them is a power. One of them is the price of a power. All of them are heavier than a power.**
