# Volume 13, Batch 0001 Record — Chapters 0601 to 0610, days 601 to 610, and the tenth file of the fifty

## What these ten days were, in one paragraph, and the story comes first

A sheet with a heading and one place ruled under it came up the quarterly round on the six hundred and second day of this flood with nothing whatever in that one place, and it was set flat on the table under the one window of a room over a market with the bone it had been folded around still lying in the near fold of it, and about nine people who were already in that room looked at it for four hours and could not say what that one place was for. On the day before that a man of about thirty-four, who has a board standing face in against his own trestle and a piece of chalk he does not use, said in about nine words that there was nothing on that page, or beside it, or under it, that said what the place was for, and nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him. On the Thursday the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps this city's only public book read that sheet out in the local language in daylight in front of about nine people who had not been told anything, and she said out loud what it was for before anybody asked: one place, to have a word in it and not a figure, and the sheet to go back up that road with the word in it. **There is no eighth notice in this flood and there is not going to be one, so that room found out what it was being asked on the morning it was asked, and the thing it was being asked this time is a word and not a number, and no room in this flood has ever been asked for a word, and the person who keeps the register is the person who will have to find it.** The man of about thirty-nine, who trades nineteen rates on a board under his arm and has a space about as wide as two of his fingers at the foot of the column between the other two where a name is not, was right in about four seconds about one thing in that form, which is that there is no day written on it anywhere, and was thanked by nobody. On the Friday, at the seventh hour at the end of that market, the man of about twenty-seven who washes that board ran the water down it twice and would not say which of the three sets of figures on it is the one to read, and about four people in that row had worked out that a place on a sheet and a column on a page are both places a person might want to put something in and about four had not. On the Saturday the store's own chalk was emptied out of its box onto the flags and the grit was carried off them and put into the channel, and the box went back on its step with its lid where the lid is and the lid did not shut, and a man two stalls along said three things at his own trestle, one of them about a lid that has never shut, and nobody in that market spoke to him and he does not know what is under the rim of that lid. On the Sunday a trough and a bucket and a rag went out along eleven miles of flats in weather and came back by a different way with somebody else's water in them, and nobody counted anything and nobody put a word in anything, and a person went the same ground with a slate under one arm and a tin in her two hands and was given no name and no age and no number and no descriptor and was not asked anything and was thanked by nobody. On the Monday a chair came out from under a shelf in the room over that market and went back, and a man looked at that shelf from the floor and did not find what he came up the stair to look for and has not said what it was. On the Tuesday two pieces of chalk were counted in that room, one out of a box on the step at the back and one in a man's own inside breast pocket, and they were not in one hand and nobody in that room put them together, and the piece of chalk in that pocket has been in it since the two hundred and ninety-eighth day of this flood and nobody in this city has asked him about it in three hundred and twelve days. **The register's column on the right is empty from the head of that page to its foot on all ten of these days and this batch put nothing in it, and the word that this volume spends is spent on a later day of this volume and not on one of these ten.** Nobody in these ten days was thanked. Nobody in these ten days was told he was right. Nobody in these ten days was asked anything about the thing he carries in his pocket.
