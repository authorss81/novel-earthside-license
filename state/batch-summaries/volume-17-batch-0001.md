## THE TEN CARDS, DAYS 801 TO 810

### Card 0801 — day 801, Thursday, the four hundred and eighty-sixth day of the Bare Month, discovery. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the room over the market. Man of about thirty-nine with his board under his arm; keeper at
  the near end with the book open; man of about thirty-four; man of about thirty-one; man of about twenty-seven;
  woman of about fifty-two.
- **Goal:** to get through a morning in which a room cannot find out one thing about its own door.
- **Business:** the decider's hand finds iron on the top step before his boot is on it. He looks down the stair.
  He asks the keeper, low, at the near end, one question about whether the door was shut on another morning, and
  she says she does not know and does not come down that stair till the tenth hour. **Nobody says since when and
  nobody in that room knows, and the narration does not say it either.**
- **Resistance:** the morning goes on; about nine people are doing what they do and none of them is waiting.
- **Change:** the bar is known to be out of its sockets, and the knowledge stops at the top of that stair.
- **Closing beat:** the date in chalk on the outside of that door, whole, with clean wood beneath it and no thumb
  near it.

### Card 0802 — day 802, Friday, the four hundred and eighty-seventh, political. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a room going on with a door it does not know the state of. Two people in that room want to say
  one thing about the door and neither says it.
- **Resistance:** talk on the bench covers the fifth hour; the carrier has a question of his own and gets no
  answer he can use.
- **Change:** the carrier offers, in an ordinary way, to carry the bar down that stair and put it where it was,
  and nobody takes him up on it, and he carries nothing.
- **Closing beat:** the trough on the flags at the top of the market step with yesterday's water still in it.

### Card 0803 — day 803, Saturday, the four hundred and eighty-eighth, character. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the foot of that stair, before the light is on the flags. The woman of about fifty-two.
  Then about four people going past on the paving.
- **Goal:** to look at two empty sockets in a door frame and go away having said nothing to anybody.
- **Resistance:** she has to step aside for feet on the paving, twice, and the second time she is seen.
- **Change:** she is seen. The man of about thirty-eight is seen seeing her and says nothing about it and she
  goes up.
- **Closing beat:** the top of that stair in daylight with nothing on the top step that is iron.

### Card 0804 — day 804, Sunday, the four hundred and eighty-ninth, recovery. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the room, in the ordinary way, with the man of about twenty-seven in it, which he is not
  often.
- **Business:** **nine words** — that he has never had that bar in his hands — and about four people along that
  bench were in the room on the morning of Chapter 0786 and heard the same man say a thing about that door and
  said nothing then and say nothing now.
- **Resistance:** talk covers the moment; nobody asks him a second question.
- **Change:** a fact the room already had is now said out loud in that room and nothing is done with it.
- **Closing beat:** a rag spread on a stone lip and the lip left damp at the tenth hour.

### Card 0805 — day 805, Monday, the four hundred and ninetieth, physical. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** the bar put back into its sockets by somebody, and not left there.
- **Change:** a hand lifts iron off a top step, goes down that stair on it, and seats it in two sockets on the
  far side of the door at the bottom; the door swings to and is shut for the better part of two hours; and at
  about the seventh hour a hand takes it out again and it is back on the top step by the tenth. **No chapter of
  this volume prints whose hand it was and no mouth in that room asks.**
- **Closing beat:** the two sockets with the iron out of them and the dust lying in them where the iron was.

### Card 0806 — day 806, Tuesday, the four hundred and ninety-first, character. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a chalk in an inside breast pocket and a hand on a board, and neither moves.
- **Resistance:** ordinary trading; a rate to be written and a board to be turned; his hand is not free and he is
  not the one who is waiting.
- **Change:** nothing in his pocket is touched and nothing is written on anything, and the narration does not say
  when that chalk was last out of it and may not.
- **Closing beat:** the bare back of that board turned out into the row, unmarked, all morning.

### Card 0807 — day 807, Wednesday, the four hundred and ninety-second, **decision**. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** **this is not the volume's one decision, which is on day 825 and which this batch may not reach.**
  On this day the decider says, in the ordinary course and to nobody in particular, that anybody who wants to be
  in the room when the bar is settled should be in the room. He tells nobody. He writes nothing. He says it once
  and goes back to his board.
- **Resistance:** nobody takes it up; the morning closes over it in about four seconds.
- **Change:** a sentence exists in this city that did not exist on the morning before. **AND THE NARRATION SAYS
  ONCE, IN PLAIN WORDS AND NOT IN A MOUTH, THAT NOBODY WAS SENT FOR AND THERE HAD BEEN NO TIME IN IT, AND THAT
  THE ROOM WAS NOT GOING TO BE GIVEN ANY.**
- **Closing beat:** the board stood face in against a leg at the near end and the rate on it not turned.

### Card 0808 — day 808, Thursday, the four hundred and ninety-third, political. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the market row at the store's end of it, then the room.
- **Business:** that sentence going about a market in somebody's own words, twice, in different words, and not
  coming back up that stair.
- **Resistance:** a third man puts his own version on it and is wrong about who said it.
- **Change:** by the tenth hour the room above has heard it in three shapes and does not know which of them was
  said first, and nobody upstairs knows it was said downstairs at all.
- **Closing beat:** the outside board wiped round three sets of figures with no line of words under any of them.

### Card 0809 — day 809, Friday, the four hundred and ninety-fourth, character. **Adrian: 1.**
- **His hands:** the lid of a box of chalk on a market step, whose lid does not shut.
  **What he wanted the thing for:** to have that lid shut, so the chalk in it would not be walked off that step.
  **Not obtained.**
- **Resistance:** the hinge is not broken and the lid is not missing; the box sits where it has sat and the lid
  does what it does.
- **Change caused by him to somebody else (not a decision, word, document, notice or panel):** a man coming along
  that row with a full bucket has to turn out into the trestles with it, put it down on the flags, go round a
  crouching stranger, come back for it, and take wet marks on two flags that were dry. **Nobody thanks Adrian,
  nobody tells him he was right, nobody was waiting for him to be useful.**
- **Closing beat:** the lid ajar at the same angle it had been at before he touched it.

### Card 0810 — day 810, Saturday, the four hundred and ninety-fifth, physical. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a trough emptied at the top of a step by a person who is not asked why.
- **Change:** the man of about thirty-four empties the trough on the flags himself and does not say so, and the
  water goes across the flags and under a trestle and finds its own way, and nobody in that row asks him.
- **Closing beat:** the trough dry and standing on flags with a wet edge round its feet. **No elapsed figure is
  printed on this page and the row at `outline/volume-17.md` §14.5 that comes out round on this day is not
  touched.**
