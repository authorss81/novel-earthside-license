# Volume 06, Batch 0001 — Chapters 0251–0260, days 251 to 260

**Ten chapters, days 251 to 260, one chapter to one day, the last day a Tuesday.** This is the first batch of the first volume of this book written under an outline, and the finding it is written to answer is a shape and not a plot: **across Chapters 0241 to 0250, 175 of 176 plain speeches were answered by a bolded speech in the next line, in a yard, by a pair of people.** The number for these ten is at §9.1 and it is 78 of 382, or twenty per cent, and that is the whole of what a reader of this record has to know about the difference between the last volume and this one before reading a word of the chapters.
