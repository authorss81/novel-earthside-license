# Volume 06, Batch 0001 — Chapters 0251–0260, days 251 to 260

**Ten chapters, days 251 to 260, one chapter to one day, the last day a Tuesday.** This is the first batch of the first volume of this book written under an outline, and the finding it is written to answer is a shape and not a plot: **across Chapters 0241 to 0250, 175 of 176 plain speeches were answered by a bolded speech in the next line, in a yard, by a pair of people.** The number for these ten is at §9.1 and it is 78 of 382, or twenty per cent, and that is the whole of what a reader of this record has to know about the difference between the last volume and this one before reading a word of the chapters.

**The subject of the batch is a document.** A Crown instrument comes up the river in a case, is correct, is signed by a clerk, does not carry the Regent's name, and requires the days on every gate between this city and the Hearth Marches to be entered in a schedule of four columns — a date, a gate, a holder, the days. It is on a page in Chapter 0251, it is read out in Chapter 0252, nobody in four hundred miles can say who sent it until Chapter 0254, a company to keep the road is provided for in Chapter 0257, the company is entered in Chapter 0258 and cannot be paid, and nobody will carry the case west in Chapter 0259. **It is still on the table and the schedule is not filled in and nothing has been decided.** That is the correct state of Chapter 0260.

## 1. The batch goal, and whether it was met

**The goal was: put a correct document on a table, get a Regent's name into a room by a person who has been in one, and run the calendar out of range on the page so that the fifth form has to exist by Chapter 0256 — without a monster, without a power, and without a chapter in which two people in a yard talk about a day.**

**Nobody got stronger.** Adrian Vale performed no working, opened no threshold, spent no passage privilege, was not offered a workway and asked for none, and no chapter in these ten asked him a question about a gate. The gains of the batch are a case on a table, four columns, a word nobody in this city can define, a name, a refusal, a second reading, a form, a month, a company that cannot be posted, a number of gates nobody has counted, and a question in a hand that is not his. **None of them is a power.
