# Volume 01 Batch 0003 Summary — The Black Scar (Chapters 21–30)

## What happened

Twelve days, a scar in a place, and a debt that got bigger every hour and could not be closed.

The batch turns a post into a season and a hearing into a fight. Nothing opens for four chapters and then everything opens at once, and the thing that opens is not a door.

**Chapters 21–23 are work, procedure and travel.** Chapter 21 is the batch's spine: the keeper's binding has a hole in it every second morning, Vail will not allow a rota to exist, and the only repair available to a man who is not allowed to be at the top of anything is to make *whoever is nearest* have more candidates — which is entered as a widening and not a list. Vail then teaches the lesson the whole batch rests on: **a date in a book is not a plan, it is a place a plan has to be legible in**, and the other half of the seat: when you are at the counter you are also the man who finds out that somebody has not turned up. Adrian's twelve-day list has forty-one names in it and nineteen lines a city can act on, and the difference is that he cannot reach the telephone in any of them. Chapter 22 goes to the Reed Reach, where the Commons does not want Adrian and says so — it wants Owen Park, because a man who has asked for nothing in a room is a man you can send. The Commons hands him a **term sheet** instead of a refusal, and the trap in it is found by a seventy-year-old woman from a village that has already refused twice. Chapter 23 discovers that Owen has stopped asking to go anywhere and started making other people go, and invents the volume's first civic instrument: a form for a claim that cannot be tested, with a refusal clause, a bar on any use against the person, and a clause Hesper Saye writes herself — nobody may be harmed, refused work, refused water, or moved for saying what they saw. The form is used on Adrian himself, and a second person in Saltwake is now documented as being owed an answer.

**Chapter 24 is the discovery.** Twenty-two feet of dressed joint does not end; it turns, and makes the L of a doorway, and stops square under the north wall. The joint is a threshold, not a wall, and Tamsin's boundary table gives the ugly simple answer: **the boundary was written into the ground twice, once as a stone you could lift and once as a joint under a floor you could not, and somebody carried the liftable one a hundred yards downstream in public at a cost of four hundred people's houses and left the other where the water could find it.** It is entered as a description and not a name, because a quarter of a thing is not a thing. The interface offers to let Adrian *name* it. He refuses, because a name in one man is a singleton.

**Chapter 25 is the volume midpoint and it is unambiguous.** The day's working at the Lock of Salt is consented to by a count (thirty-one for, eleven against, the foreman's name entered), costed (eleven measures of opened salt, a day of the day crew, a season of the bottom field), witnessed by four, and performed entirely by locals. Adrian reads and marks and performs nothing and says so when asked. The crew says the maintenance oath **at its ordinary volume**, at a docker's insistence, so that the day cannot later be called a thing a crowd did. **It succeeds**: the pale edge of the main breach steps nine inches *toward the near gate*, in the safe direction, and four people sign. Then, at the eleventh hour and eleven minutes, the boards over the dig rise flat, the water comes up the yard and stops at the threshold slab, and the chapel-floor hairline opens **across** rather than along, for four feet, with a flagstone stood on end — no consent, no witness, no cost, nobody standing in it. **Out of the black line in the chapel stone** — not the floor, not the arch — a flat voice reads out one line of Adrian's own deleted design note, in front of three hundred and ten people, and Vask looks at her own list, item four, and says one word. They had been expected that morning at the **walk-round**, where Renna Ord had four men standing at the foot of the two hundred steps from half past five because a docker had written eleven on a slate on Friday, and they were not there. The joint tears and takes **eleven people** out of the **chapel floor**, four feet beyond the threshold slab, including **Eli Mercer, 19**, and **Wren Halloway, 11**, who was on the standing flagstone. Owen Park, a bystander four feet away, takes the child's weight on his right shoulder and the shoulder goes; it cannot be set, because there is nothing out of place. Mina names the mechanism: **a hold is a working without a witness, and the residue of one goes into the person who did it.** Marrow is the most useful man in the yard and says aloud, in front of witnesses, that this was a good working's residue arriving where it was not sent. The field review is adjourned on the day it was set for, with the reason and both dates entered, and Vail stops two hundred people from opening the seal. The interface says nothing all day. Roke enters Adrian's answer to *is this a game* — *there is no* — in full, and Adrian checks his palm and the black line has not moved one sixteenth of an inch.

**Chapters 26–28 are the aftermath.** The second group is named in the public record as **persons who came through without asking**, in the same words used for the six, on a separate page with its own witnesses and a refusal clause, and Adrian says out loud in a hall that he is staying Unbound and why. **Eli Mercer** refuses, in the record, to join the six and to speak for them. A child goes missing on the reed paths because the twenty-fifth day's working took the water out of four villages' ditches, and the argument about spending the first opening of the narrow route is resolved by six people who are not Adrian. Tamsin says *a door is not a search party*; Renna Ord says an opening is a fresh inspection by four people in the wrong weather; Saye will not have Owen on a stair. Adrian says the hardest thing of the batch out loud, with the child's grandmother in the room: **he does not know whether the mark he gave into the wall in the first week could have brought anybody home.** The first opening is spent on a person with nothing here, chosen by that person, and the child is found at four o'clock on foot by four reed cutters, because she was where the water was not. Chapter 28 enters the account of the other side **with its four disagreements preserved and unreconciled**, translates it word for word in a way that makes Marrow more frightening rather than less, sends it to the Reed Reach in a carrier's hand ahead of the Crown's mail case, and ends on a ratio of heights in a room that is the ratio on **page forty-one of a notebook Adrian has shown to nobody**.

**Chapters 29–30 are the two public scenes.** The review, five days late, passes by the **first count in the Concord's history**, with the debt **continued and enlarged and never closable**, on the grounds that a debt that can be closed is a debt that can be traded out — and Adrian says out loud, in a hall, why he is staying Unbound. The hearing answers its own question with **no**, continues the instrument as a thing nobody may use, and produces a **procedure for being wrong in public** — a statement of error, from which the holder is barred. Owen's Chapter 25 shoulder is entered in the record as the injury that was never set. Marrow takes his own expired chalk off the keeper's board in daylight and asks, in chalk, for the true reason to be written under it in Adrian's own hand, and adds that Adrian will do it.

## Results

- **Power: nothing gained anywhere in the batch.** Adrian is **Stage 1, Witness** at the end exactly as at the start. He cannot perform a working. In Chapter 25 he was put in the lower gallery to read and mark, was asked in front of eleven men whether he was in the working, and said no. In Chapter 27 his only part in the first opening was to read a line and mark it in three places with a stub of chalk in the rain, and Saye made him say that out loud afterwards so that it would be in a book before it was a story. **He is Unbound**, having refused the Wayfinder profile three times, the third time out loud in a hall of three hundred people with the reason given in full and entered, and the roll of strangers still says *Unbound* in his own words with two dates on it.
- **One crossing, one named person, and it was a breach of the instrument's own terms.** The **first and only opening of the Narrow Mark** was made on the twenty-eighth day, at the top of the tide, with the standing terms broken in public, a fresh inspection performed in the wrong weather by four people who did not want to be there, and a cost entered. **Eli Mercer** went and came back in eleven seconds. The route carries one person per opening, needs a fresh inspection before reuse, and has a receiving community that may suspend it — and it is now entered in the ledger as **may not be used at all** until a fresh inspection, no suspension, and an answer.
- **The only return mark in the world belongs to a nineteen-year-old who arrived four days ago.** Adrian has none, and never had one after day 4, and said out loud on day 27, in a room with a grandmother in it, that he does not know whether the one he gave up could have brought anybody home and that the only way to find out is to take back the thing he gave away.
- **Nobody dies.** Owen Park's right shoulder went on day 25 and cannot be set; his scalp has been unclosed since the **first day** and is thirty days old at the end of the batch and is not resolved. Dante Kuo's right shoulder went on day 31 while steadying a door frame. Owen's Chapter 38 death is untouched and must arrive intact and permanent. No respawn, no return, no inheritance of a mark.
- **Day 33 is a Saturday.** Day 1 is a Tuesday, so day 31 is a Thursday, day 32 a Friday and day 33 a Saturday. An earlier draft had day 33 as a Tuesday and had the whole volume's closing image land on the wrong day; the day table in `state/continuity.md` is now authoritative and every weekday from day 32 on is derived from it.
- **Two marks, still separate, and one of them did not move.** The pale fragment-mark is in the maintenance seam. The thin black line in the heel of the palm is **thirty days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch through the whole batch**, including while a chapel floor came apart and including while three hundred and ten people stood in the rain. Adrian's conclusion, written on day 25: *it is not warning me. It is keeping score.*
- **The five lock sites stay apart and one of them is now scarred.** The main breach was stepped nine inches by a working and is still open. The Narrow Mark is the seam in the outer channel wall and was used once. The near gate is the dockers' inner working gate and the day's working was performed with the crew holding it. The chapel-floor hairline seam is the top of the dressed joint and it tore **across**. The chapel stone is the raised threshold slab and it is the voice's only channel, and on day 25 it said twelve words of English in front of three hundred and ten people.
- **Marrow's complicity stays plausible and unproven.** He is the most useful man in the batch and was embarrassingly good at it: tarpaulins, a stretcher, a bonesetter he found before he was needed, Dante Kuo put on paper work to protect a shoulder, and two hours of word-for-word translation with the disagreements left in. He withdrew his own offer in chalk in daylight and asked for a favour. **No public scene convicts him and none may.**
- **The premise surfaces for the first time, in the arrivals' own mouths only.** A boy of nineteen says *lore* on a stair; a woman of forty-one says *this is a closed beta* and asks for it to be entered that she said it first; a stallholder in a market asks *they're players, though, right?* and gets a hole in the shape of the answer; and a market board carries the number eleven by the ninth hour. Adrian says **there is no**, in front of two hundred people, for two minutes and forty seconds, unsoftened, and then says he does not know whether he is part of it. No Veyran asserts that this is a game; a Veyran repeating a word is not a Veyran believing it, and Adrian finds the difference unbearable and is right to. **The word *quest* is not used anywhere in the batch.** Adrian's own vocabulary slips twice and is caught both times — *cohort*, by Priya in public, and *onboarding*, by himself, in a hall, in front of four hundred people.

## Deviations from the batch cards (deliberate, recorded)

1. **The field review is held on day 30, not day 25.** The review was *set* for day 25, the last day of the salt month, and the day's working tore the ground open at the eleventh hour and eleven minutes of that same day, so the review could not be taken. Vail adjourned it on the twenty-seventh and entered the reason and **both dates**, and said the thing that is the batch's whole argument: *a date that moves five days is a date that can move nine.* The six days between the day the review was set for and the hearing are preserved and are the engine; the review is the bill for them. Marrow's chalk expired on day 25 exactly as canon requires and stayed on the board, expired, for five more days, and he took it down himself on day 30.
2. **Chapters 26–28 were reordered in emphasis.** The card's Chapter 27 contains the missing child *and* the first opening; they are both there, in that order, and the child's rescue by locals is the payoff of the argument rather than the alternative to it.
3. **The first opening is not a rescue and the card's ending sentence is honoured literally.** The opening was spent on a person with nothing here, chosen by that person, in order to find out whether a person could go at all. Adrian's part in it was to read a line and mark it, and Nell Bray asked him afterwards, in the rain, in front of a hundred and forty people, what it was for, and he had no answer at the time.
4. **Chapters 25 and 27 run long: 8,556 and 7,091 words, against a 3,300–6,500 range.** Chapter 25 is the volume midpoint and the reversal, and it carries the six terms, the count with the foreman's name, Renna Ord's stop authority, the oath at ordinary volume, the working and its number, the tear, the nine words out of the chapel stone, eleven named arrivals, Owen's injury, Saye's prognosis, Marrow's usefulness and his analysis, the adjournment with both dates, Vail stopping the crowd, and the answer in a hall. Chapter 27 carries the field at four in the morning, the missing child, the mechanism, a five-hour argument resolved by six people who are not Adrian, the eleven refusal to speak for the cohort, the fresh inspection in the wrong weather, six terms, the opening, the return, and the truth told to a grandmother at the foot of a stair. Both were trimmed repeatedly and both were cut by paragraph rather than by rewording, because the length in them is content the cards require and not ornament. **A reviewer should treat these two as deliberate and should check them against the cards rather than against the word count.** A duplicated block of about 380 words was found and removed from Chapter 27 during that pass, and the whole batch was then scanned for duplicated paragraphs at a threshold of thirty-five words and is clean.
5. **Chapters 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 28 and 30 carry no System message.** Batch 0002 carried four, in Chapters 11, 12, 15 and 16, and nothing in 13, 14 or 17–20. **This batch carries three, in Chapters 24, 27 and 29**, and the loudest chapter in the volume carries none — which is the batch's finding, not an omission. The twenty-ninth is the only offer the interface has ever made that is *attractive*, and it is the first one that has tried to buy him out of his own debt. **Chapter 22 is a document, not a prompt:** its four clauses are the Reed Reach's term sheet, read out on a raft, and nothing came up in the air that day. An earlier draft of this summary recorded a fourth message in Chapter 22 and quoted a text that is not in the chapter; that record was wrong and is removed.
6. **The three messages are the same construction, three more times.** 24: *the stone under the floor has a name and the holder may give it.* 27: *a return mark may be set in one hour by a man who has read a seam; the man who reads the seam does not travel and does not change.* 29: *a debt may be closed; a closed debt is a small debt; there is no further cost.* All three are true, none of them is a lie, and all three put the cost in the grammatical position where a cost goes. Adrian has now three of them from this batch and eleven offers across the three batches in a notebook, and he has learned the shape rather than the content, which is the standing test.
7. **Vask is not softened and Mara Vask's four rules are not broken.** The site under the chapel is described and not named; the identification is entered in her words and Quill's; item four of her list is what she says one word about on day 25; and nobody has touched the six rules in state/continuity.md.
8. **The plural principle's first visible act is a notary refusing to reconcile four accounts**, not a character saying something wise about democracy, and the volume's first procedure — the statement of error — is entered with the holder of the seat **barred from using it against himself.** Adrian learned it from a notary and says so.
9. **The Crown's single-author instrument arrives in Chapter 30 as a Registry demand**, not as a coat and a hand. It is **not** the Single Witness offer of Chapters 41–47 and the two must not be collapsed. Aurel's Registry is a bureaucracy; the Choir is a faction. The volume's antagonist is unchanged.
10. **The Cursors are the calendar.** The batch runs day 20 to day 31 and every date on the page derives from the day table in `state/continuity.md`, and the tide table marks the **twenty-fourth, twenty-fifth and twenty-eighth**, and the thirty-third is the first day whose cell in the last column is still empty.

## What the next batch inherits

Everything is dated and everything is armed. **Day 32 is the seventh day of the new month and Marrow has a date for it that he told a boy of nine on a step and nobody else.** (The salt month ended on day 25, so the eighteenth of it was day 18 and is long gone; the date was corrected in Chapter 21 during the repair pass.) **Day 33 is a Saturday, it is the next tide, and it is an ordinary worked day**, which means forty men under the near gate twice, and a word written in the last column of a tide table on the wall of the lower gallery, and about a quarter of an inch in the pale edge of the join in the gate at one in the morning, and a hundred and forty acres four miles away losing another hour. **Day 31 ended at a tide table and a man with a barrow.** The Registry wants a registered holder or a permanent closure before the next quarterly sitting. Hessa Ruun has said in terms that Adrian's own concession will be used against Nine Elms in a month. Peth Lammay is taking the account of the other side to Verrow on day 32, ahead of the Crown's mail case, and Adrian has to go with her and sit in a room in the Reach without a notebook. The nineteenth stones and the eight missing name stones are on the same list and belong to Roke, who has not had a scene of his own. Page forty-one is in a notary's keeping and Adrian has four readings of it and no answer. There is a boy of nineteen in this city holding the only return mark in the world, who has refused to join the six, who is owed nothing, and who has said that a man in a good coat will write a letter about him. The chapel floor is open four feet across under a tarpaulin. Owen Park cannot lift forty pounds with his right arm and does not know it. And on a keeper's board, in chalk, in a good hand, in daylight, there are eight words from a man in a coat that amount to *I expect you to do as I ask*, and the volume has a death in it that has not been set up yet.
