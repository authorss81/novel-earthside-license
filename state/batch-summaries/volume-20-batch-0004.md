**THE THING THE NEXT WRITER MOST NEEDS, AND IT IS THREE.** **1. There is a sheet on that bench.** It was not there on 980.
From 981 it carries three lines — the day, the person it is about, and the putting-to — a hand's width in from its right edge,
and the space below them is bare, and it is the only thing written on any surface on any of the fifty days of this volume.
**2. There is a block of writing on the wall along the far side of that room**, and it was not there at the light on 983, and
nobody has said a word about it. **3. The dry cloth is on the head of the second book's leaf**, laid there on 990 because
Adrian Vale's shoulder was in the way of the keeper's hand, and it was still there at the going of the light.

**AND THE THREE THINGS THAT WENT OUT OF THAT ROOM AND CANNOT BE GOT BACK.** On 981 the keeper put a thing on a page before
she put it to anybody, and the woman of about sixty-nine with her tin read it before she answered, and the answer went out
of that room in her own mouth and is on no surface in this city. On 985 the man who came up that stair said one word out
loud once in that room in daylight, and nobody asked him what it meant and nobody improved on it and nobody repeated it,
and it is not explained in that chapter or in any other. On 989 the woman of about fifty-two put one thing to that same man
about the six strokes in the column, and he answered her, and the chapter prints nothing of what he said and the sixth
place at the foot of that column is still bare wood.

**AND THE THING THE PLAN GOT WRONG, WHICH A LATER WRITER SHOULD NOT REPEAT.** The prompt that wrote this batch stated that
two frames were still undrawn after day 980 and that eight would be left afterwards. **Measured on the three published frame
lists, the union of the three batches behind this one is seventeen of the eighteen frames and only frame 12 was undrawn, and
after this batch nothing is undrawn at all.** A fifth writer of this volume must draw ten distinct frames out of a pool in
which every frame has been used, and must name ten repeats in its card file.
