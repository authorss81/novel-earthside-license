## 1. The batch goal, and whether it was met

**The goal, as the prompt set it: write the first chapters of the next volume without inventing what the next volume is, and if the writer cannot write a chapter without deciding what the volume is about, write the chapter about a *day* and not about a *volume*.**

**It is met, and it is met by a mechanism and not by a refusal. Every one of the ten chapters is a day, and the ten days are consecutive, and the day map was opened from day 1 being a Tuesday and from nothing else. What the ten days have in common is a page — a posting that went up on a wall on the forty-sixth day of the Long Month and had been up nine days by the end of the batch — and the batch's finding is that nine days of a page is a list of costs and not a list of answers.**

- **The posting runs and the running is the finding.** It has a date on it and a price under every line, and the prices are in words, and a man of about twenty-four from the workroom said on the day it went up that a page with a price under every line and no figure under any price is a list of intentions. - **The store and its queue are the ninth and last line and the man who put them there says it was on purpose**, and a queue is a line that nobody decided and a posting is a line that somebody decided, and both sentences are in a mouth in a yard in daylight. Chapter 0204.
- **A right of refusal that cannot be bought out cannot be afforded, and the man who wrote the objection found the second hole in his own sentence on the day he used the right.** Chapter 0206. **A refusal cannot be recorded and cannot be counted, and the page has no line for a number of refusals, and the only instrument left is nine people remembering a thing.**

- **And the batch's one act that is a person and not a document: Adrian Vale said his own name out loud in a room in daylight with a date on the door, was fetched by it the same morning, and could not answer the question he was fetched for.** Chapter 0209. **Nobody got stronger.
