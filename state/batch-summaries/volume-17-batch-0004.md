### Card 0831 — day 831, Saturday, BM 516, character. Adrian: 3.
The yard at the salt wharf at the near end of eleven miles of flats, at the store door there: Adrian Vale, the man
of about thirty-eight with his barrow and boards, a man carrying a crate through that yard on his own business.
Goal: to have that store door propped so a stack could be carried in out of the weather. Beat: his hand on the
face of the door and his shoulder in the opening with nothing to hold it with; the barrow will not go through an
opening with a man standing in it, so the stack stands in the weather; when he comes out of the opening the door
swings back and puts two boards off the stack on to the flags and the near end of one of them takes the wet
through, and the man of about thirty-eight gets the door open himself with his knee and carries the whole stack in
past him on his own. Change: he is the cause of two boards on the flags and of a man picking them up and going on
with his load. Closing: the near end of that stack black and dripping on to dry boards, with a wet ring on the
board underneath. Third party: the barrow, the stack, the awning, the tally-board on the load. **He performs no
working, opens no threshold, names the other world nowhere, is offered no workway and asks for none, is thanked by
nobody, is told he was right by nobody, and nobody in that yard was waiting for him to be useful.**

### Card 0832 — day 832, Sunday, BM 517, physical. Adrian: no.
The wharf yard, then the market row at the store's end of it: the man of about thirty-eight, the man of about
thirty-one with a crate on his shoulder, the keeper at the near end of that bench, the man of about thirty-nine
with his board under his arm. Goal: a loaded barrow in out of the weather, and to find out who has not come up.
Beat: the hollow at the side of that yard dried for several mornings with a hard skin of the stone's own grit
across it that looks like ground; the load goes down it, the skin breaks under the wheel like a board, the barrow
goes over on its side, the boards take the water back into that hollow from four sides; the rest of the skin is
broken off against the step and the wet boards are left at the wall. Then the man of about thirty-one goes past the
foot of that stair without stopping, and the keeper says he has not come up, and the man of about thirty-nine says
he goes past that door every morning and has never once come up it, and the keeper says that is not the same
thing. Change: the hollow has water in it again, and the room above has an absence it cannot put a morning to.
Closing: the piece of the broken skin on the dry crown where the wheel came out of it, unpicked. Third party: the
barrow, the load, the crate, the step. **No elapsed figure, no headcount, and no mouth dates that absence.**
