## THE TEN CARDS, DAYS 791 TO 800

### Card 0791 — day 791, Monday, the four hundred and seventy-sixth day of the Bare Month, political. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** that room. Keeper at near end with book; man of about thirty-nine with board; man of about thirty-four; woman of about fifty-two; man of about twenty-seven; carrier.
- **Goal:** to get through the fifth-hour silence without putting the old morning saying into the air.
- **Business:** a morning in which two people would have said it and neither says it. The man of about thirty-four draws breath at the fifth hour and lets it out; the woman of about fifty-two puts a hand out and brings it back.
- **Resistance:** ordinary talk must cover the quiet; paving and canvas talk comes forward.
- **Change:** board goes near-to-far and down; book goes to shelf behind bench ahead of dark shape.
- **Closing beat:** a table jug with dust-skinned water, not touched all morning.

### Card 0792 — day 792, Tuesday, the four hundred and seventy-seventh, discovery. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** what a decision on a Saturday leaves on a Sunday — here on a Tuesday after it: the room going on without the saying and finding the silence.
- **Change:** trough talk covers the fifth-hour gap; board near-to-far and down; book to back shelf.
- **Closing beat:** rain in the stairwell, the bottom step shining where feet went down.

### Card 0793 — day 793, Wednesday, the four hundred and seventy-eighth, character. Adrian: 13.
- **His hands:** a barrow handle in the yard at the near end of eleven miles of flats. **What he wanted it for:** to have his own barrow back in that yard before the man of about thirty-eight came in with his. **Not obtained.**
- **Resistance:** rain, soft ground, a brimful strange barrow with nowhere to stand it.
- **Change caused by him to somebody else (not a decision, word, document, notice or panel):** the man of about thirty-eight must walk round the strange barrow, take it by both handles, tip the wet out into the flags, drag it out onto the paving to clear working room, and stand his own a hand's breadth further off the wall; nobody thanks Adrian, nobody tells him he was right, nobody was waiting for him to be useful.
- **Closing beat:** two pale grip-prints on strange handles, taken by wind before the tenth hour.

### Card 0794 — day 794, Thursday, the four hundred and seventy-ninth, physical. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a length of board set down square on the top of a stack and nobody measuring it.
- **Change:** length seated flush, sighted along near edge, left square; two grit lines untouched; clean piece untouched.
- **Closing beat:** a knife in a belt untouched all morning with rain beading on the hilt.

### Card 0795 — day 795, Friday, the four hundred and eightieth, recovery: the resolution. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** that room, daylight, date in chalk on door, bar home. Woman of about fifty-two; keeper; man of about thirty-four; carrier; man of about twenty-seven.
- **Business:** she puts both her own hands on the back of the stool and says the man of about thirty-nine is not in the room and she does not know since which morning; nobody asks her how she knows; about four along the bench say in different words they do not know; about four more say nothing at all; nothing lifted, moved or answered.
- **Change:** stool turned a finger's width by the pressure of her hands.
- **Closing beat:** the stool standing where hands held it, turned on its legs.

### Card 0796 — day 796, Saturday, the four hundred and eighty-first, physical. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** eleven miles of flats in weather and a plank across a wet sill.
- **Change:** trough emptied, reset, filled; bucket to low place; rag to lip; plank laid across inner sill to carry drip clear, left all morning; book moved a foot out of run.
- **Closing beat:** the plank dark along its underside where water ran.

### Card 0797 — day 797, Sunday, the four hundred and eighty-second, character. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a man coming up that stair and going back down it and one book being carried the length of a room.
- **Change:** man of about thirty-eight climbs to two steps below top with barrow handles, sees room full, turns and goes down without setting foot on boards; keeper carries book near-to-shelf and back at tenth hour.
- **Closing beat:** a wet mark halfway up the stair where a man turned.

### Card 0798 — day 798, Monday, the four hundred and eighty-third, character: the new question. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** that room, daylight, date in chalk on door. Woman of about fifty-two to man of about thirty-nine at near end of bench beside keeper's page, for him and not for the room.
- **Business:** she asks what a morning is for, when the only thing anybody is going to use it for is to have been in a room in it; he does not answer; nobody along the bench heard a word of it; nobody thanks him; talk further down goes on of rain.
- **Change:** board near-to-far and down; book to back shelf.
- **Closing beat:** prints of two pairs of boots turned toward one another, drying side by side.

### Card 0799 — day 799, Tuesday, the four hundred and eighty-fourth, recovery. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** a morning in which the old saying is not said and one thing is done anyway.
- **Change:** step below washed and wiped while room above talks of weather and trestle; lip holds damp; board on two nails cleaned round strokes; wall above untouched; bucket to low place.
- **Closing beat:** a chalk box a finger's width out of the drip with its lid rocking.

### Card 0800 — day 800, Wednesday, the four hundred and eighty-fifth, recovery: the last image. Adrian: no.
- **Business:** the carrier's hand on the satchel flap, with no way of telling whether it has been done before.
- **Change:** none beyond the standing: he stands three steps up with thumb on flap corner, lifts and sets it down in same place; keeper passes with book; paving passes behind.
- **Closing beat:** leather, hand and light giving no answer whether it was done before.

## WHAT THESE TEN DAYS DID

1. **The sixth-to-fifteenth mornings without the spent figure.** On 791–800 no mouth in that room says it and the narrator does not state it and does not know it. Two mouths reach for it on 791 and stop; the room covers the hour with paving, trough, canvas and cord talk.
2. **The resolution was paid on day 795 in the mouth it belongs to.** Both hands on the stool back, the man of about thirty-nine named as not in the room, no dating, no asking how, about four saying they do not know in different words and about four more saying nothing, nothing acted on.
3. **The new question was asked on day 798 to one man and not answered.** At the near end, low, unheard by the bench, unanswered, unthanked, and asked of nobody else on any other day.
4. **The last image stands on day 800 and nothing else.** A hand on a flap, lifted and set down, flap down as on every morning, no telling whether before.
5. **Adrian Vale failed his thirteenth day on the page.** He got a barrow into the yard before the seventh hour; the man of about thirty-eight tipped it, dragged it out, and worked round his own; the strange barrow stands on the paving at the tenth hour. Nobody thanked him and nobody told him he was right.
6. **Small durable states moved.** A length squared on a stack unmeasured; a plank across a sill all morning; a man turned on a stair leaving a wet mark; a step washed below while the room talked above.
