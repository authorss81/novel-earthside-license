# Volume 02, Batch 0003 — Chapters 71–80: The Shrine

Ten days. Days 71 to 80, which are the forty-sixth to fifty-fifth day of the new month, and which contain **the flood-season review on the fiftieth** and **the volume's midpoint**. This batch does two things and refuses to do either of them cleanly: **a city finds out that the answer to the question that could have taken its door away was already written on the front of a slate, and a man's own perfect page does not reach the thing it was written about.**

---

## 1. The batch's spine

1. **The four who hold the door go down a condemned rail in ice with a gauge and do not agree, the second time, three at a line and one a sixteenth under, and it is entered and neither reading is deleted.** A number on a question is a photograph, and the count on the fourth of the eleven is corrected in front of nine people with the old figure struck and not deleted.
2. **The review's second page travels by hand.** Nine copies in nine hands, written out by a woman of sixty-one who cannot read, at her own cost, because nobody had handed her a page to keep in seventy-eight days. **The copy is not theft and is not a conspiracy, the notary asked for the copies herself, and a document nobody may copy is a document nobody may check.**
3. **The eleventh question is not asking for a noun. It is asking for the heading of a column**, worked out on a pail by a girl of seventeen in nine minutes, and the shape of the answer was already on a slate in a yard in four lines since the forty-first day of the new month.
4. **Renna Ord gives four words in a yard in front of about two hundred and forty people, they go on the front of the slate, the question is on the back, and nobody turns it over for three days.**
5. **The twenty-second field is the twenty-first again with the condition taken off, and a clerk of that office says in nine seconds that a permanent exemption is a standing and a body with a standing is a body that may be written to.** Adrian refuses in under a second, out loud, and gives the sentence back to the clerk.
6. **The review is survived by a refusal, and the licence is weaker for it.** The instrument continues, the door stays shut, Nine Elms' water is not received, a seat is offered a fourth time and the instrument says nobody again, and the last Crown column is answered with a question and not with a name.
7. **A form is added to an instrument for the first time in eighty days for a person who is not a witness, and the day it is added is a Sunday seven days out, and the day is what makes it a question instead of the old form of the oath.**
8. **A workway holder does a work there is no office for, on a boundary, in the wrong weather, with nine people standing in it and nobody asked, and the residue goes into her arm and nobody will ever be asked what happened.**
9. **Adrian's bar fails.** It is perfect. It is scoped to the wrong thing. Nobody used anything and there was no hand on any lever. **He owns it in a room with a date on the notice in his own hand, refuses the way out in four seconds, cannot answer the fourth line of a term sheet, answers it with a page, is not permitted to answer four correct sentences about himself, and is told two things in a yard by a Hearthguard.**
10. **A page goes on the inside of a shrine, and the batch closes on a road with nineteen shapes and a name in a hand about a hundred and forty years old.**

---

## 2. What was paid

- **A licence survived, and surviving it was the worst thing that could have happened to it.** The weakness is not a figure. It is that a review was survived by a refusal, and a refusal that works becomes a thing a room expects a city to do again. The sentence is in a man's private book and is nineteen words long: *the licence survived because a city said no, and a city that says no says it in a room.*
- **A woman of forty-one is not whole and will not be asked what happened**, and the reason is the twentieth field she said yes to in four seconds, which removed the asking.
- **The whole of the Reed Reach's standing charge is still on a man of thirty-two, and the page that would have spread it across four people was refused by the meeting-ground that wrote the fourth line**, and the refusal took about nineteen minutes and its reason was correct.
- **A docker's apprentice of twenty said no in about two hundred and forty people and cost a day's work, and nobody thanked him for it and nobody thanked the man who asked him.**
- **A woman of forty-eight in a village of nine households has been clearing a drain by herself every morning since the twenty-ninth day of the new month because the levy that used to do it is gone, and she does not know a man from another world exists.**

---

## 3. What the batch gained, and it is all paper and stone

**Four words that are not a noun.** A second page in nine hands and nine copies nobody asked for. A line that may be asked once, on a named day, in daylight, in a room, with a refusal that is entered. A form that is worse than the one it replaced and says so on its face. A cross in a cell with nineteen words under it. A consequence written on the back of a field in four hands. A page that asks four people and names none of them, and was refused. A slate turned over. A page in a stone.

**None of these is a power and all of them are heavier than a power.**

---

## 4. The batch's turn, and it is Adrian's

> **The bar says a workway may not be offered to any of the seventeen by this instrument or by anybody using it. It is the longest page in the licence. It is in my own hand and the date is on it. It is not broken. It did not reach.**
>
> **A rule that covers a document is a rule about a document. A pale field in a room is not the instrument. Nobody used anything. There was no hand on any lever. The page is perfect and does not reach, and I wrote it, and I have known what it was since the thirty-second day of the new month and I said it out loud in that room in thirty-one words and then I spent nine days using it about other things.**

The thing it did not reach was a person, on a boundary, on a resonant place with a cut in it, in the wrong weather, and he was four feet back and did nothing.

**Nothing in this batch softens that, and nothing in this batch makes it anybody else's fault, and the batch is built so that it cannot be.** A defence is offered him in the first ninety seconds of the room and he refuses it in four seconds. A woman of forty-one forbids the yard from excusing him. He is the only person in this city who has ever been able to name his own failure accurately, and he did it first, on his own notice, with his own date, in his own hand.

---

## 5. The rules established in this batch, binding on Batch 0004

1. **A review's questions are a decision about what the answer is allowed to be, and the room finds out in front of two hundred and forty people which of the eleven are doors and which is a trap.** Four keep, six close, one neither, one trap. **A later writer may not restate the partition as four-and-six over eleven and may not make the number of traps four.**
2. **The answer to the eleventh question is *nobody has asked them* — four words, not a noun — and it is entered in a column on the same page as the question, not on a separate sheet, and it is read back twice in two languages.** It had been on the front of the slate for three days while the question was on the back.
3. **A licence survived by a refusal is a licence that has written down that this city refuses, and the next review will have that on the table.**
4. **A description is the only thing in this world that has never cost anybody anything until an office reads it.** Adrian's, thirty-two words, and it is not entered by the man it is about.
5. **A document nobody may copy is a document nobody may check, and a page that travels by hand is how a page becomes checkable, and a notary who asks for the copies has entered that she cannot un-ring it.**
6. **A number in a question is a photograph. A figure which cannot be corrected without being deleted is not a figure but a gravestone.**
7. **A person may be asked whether he is able to do a thing and the answer may also be *I can do some and not others*, and the nineteenth line was amended for the worse at the asker's own asking.**
8. **A man who is asked and says no is not a man who did not turn up, and in eighty days of this instrument those had been the same man every time.**
9. **The roll of the forty is thirty-eight, and a prediction about a fraction of a number that is not the number is not a prediction. A prediction which is wrong in the direction it wanted to be is worse than one that is wrong the other way.**
10. **The first line added to an instrument in eighty days for the benefit of a person who is not a witness**, and the date on it is what makes it a question instead of a standing, and **a question that is put on a slate is a question asked of everybody on every day.**
11. **The receiving-party column in ninety years: eleven completions, nine garrisons, never a village, and none of the eleven ever asked a person on the far side.** **A district given a permanent exemption is a body that may be written to without anybody's name being on it.**
12. **A form that repeats itself is not asking twice, it is establishing that it may.** Priya Shah, nineteen words.
13. **A workway is for a promise, and a person who is standing in a working and has not been asked has not agreed to it and is therefore not a witness.** A hold has two of the four things and not three.
14. **The only instrument in this city that could have asked a person what she did is the one that removed the asking, and a consequence written on the back of a document is the only kind anybody here can be angry at.**
15. **A statement of error may be answered in one of three ways and the one Adrian Vale used required no sentence from him, and using it is correct.** Nobody may answer a statement of error against themselves with a speech.
16. **A rule that only covers the thing you can see, and a guild that does not go home** — both of them said about the man who wrote the page, in a yard, in about two hundred and forty people, for the first time.
17. **There is nothing here to resign from, and a man with nothing to resign is not a man being let go.**
18. **A shrine is a record, and a record that cannot be removed, and the first page in this city put somewhere that is not this city's building carries six things and a price written under them and not a seventh.**
19. **A man is not one person**, and a page that asks four people about a charge is a description, and the people whose line it was refused it.
20. **The refusal count in Renner Sallow's second book ends this batch at twenty-two and a field nobody answered is not in it.**

## 6. What must not be re-litigated in Batch 0004

- **Owen Park is dead and buried and his burial term was formally closed on the twenty-fifth day of the new month of Volume 01.** **He is not mentioned once in Chapters 71–80, by name or otherwise, and nothing in Batch 0004 may mention him, soften him, summarise him, re-open him or turn him into a lesson.** Nobody may write *he died because*. **Dante Kuo's shoulder went on the thirty-first day of this flood, was reduced twice, cannot be set, and did not go in this batch and no scene made it whole.** **Ovin Sarr, nineteen, of the day crew, hurt on the thirty-third day of the new month, and Dante Kuo, thirty-five, are two men in two books; neither is the man of the flood crew who complained about a shoulder in the fifth week; the two may never be put in one sentence and a chapter may not carry both.** Chapter 0074 carries Dante and does not carry Ovin, and the struck-off name on the roll of the forty is deliberately unnamed.
- **The measurement column is not continuous.** Twenty-four readings, both of the last two eight and seven-eighths, **two steps in an unnamed mason's own hand before the number was read, on the eighteenth and the twenty-fourth day of the new month**, **one ruled line left, and the twenty-fifth reading has not been taken and was not taken in this batch.** A dressed join does not narrow; it gets dressed. The main breach is closed, dressed, measured and silent and **has never said a word and said nothing in this batch.**
- **The chapel stone is silent and unrepaired and did not speak in this batch.** The ground under the chapel is **SCARRED**, is not repaired, and **the boards have not been lifted since the fortieth day of this flood.** The form on the nail has **four items, the price of the salt written under them, and a fifth item added on the twenty-fourth day of the new month**, and none of the five can close anything or say where a residue goes or say who is in a room. The numbering is Volume 01's and may not be changed.
- **The knocks.** Three on the night of the twenty-ninth day of the new month and four on the night of the thirty-fifth, **both entered and neither deleted.** Neither occurred in this batch, nothing was opened, and **the knocks were not used as an instrument, a signal, a proof, a rescue or a reason to open anything.** The man who reported them is used as a person in Chapter 0071 only, because the second page carries a reason about him and that is why he is not called in to the review.
- **The five lock sites stay apart and nothing happened at three of them.** The main breach; the **Narrow Mark**, the only licensed route, **opened twice in eighty days and not a third time in this batch and the review did not authorise an opening**; the near gate, where forty men stand twice a day and where the rail is condemned; the chapel-floor dressed joint, re-boarded on the fortieth and not opened; the chapel stone, silent.
- **The two marks in the world.** Eli Mercer's return mark belongs to him and to nobody else. Owen Park's is in a grave and does not transfer. **Adrian has none of his own and got none in this batch.**
- **Five Crown instruments and never collapse them:** the Registry's notice of the twenty-eighth day of this flood; the third instrument of the seventeenth day of the new month, a single-author containment held by a Concord clerk answerable only to Aurel, refused in a yard and entered as ninety statements of error; the second answer of the twenty-second, not in the form required; **the schedule of crossings of the thirty-first, a table and not a version of the other three, with a fee of eleven ounces of silver**; **and the fifth thing that office put in a case on the fifty-second day of the new month, which is the fourth shape reissued with one more column and which the chapter says in terms is the fifth sendings and the fourth shape, and those are two different counts.** The answer of the forty-second day of the new month accepting salt is the same instrument as the schedule of crossings and not a fifth. **The Quiet Choir's design is a sixth shape and is not the Crown's**, and it is the one Adrian is frightened of, and it has three descriptions on the page and no name, **and the third of the three is the one that was used on him in this batch.**
- **The name *Single Witness* and the name *First Grammar* are still not used in prose and must not be used before Chapter 0091, in a room, in daylight, with a date on it.**
- **Mina Okoye's sentence is hers and hers alone:** *a hold is a working without a witness, and the residue of one goes into the person who did it.* **No character may claim to have coined it. In Chapter 0078 it is said by her, in a room, in about nine seconds, about a person who is not her, and it is entered that nobody claimed it.**
- **A rescue is a working. A pattern is a toll. A cost is a promise.** A floor that has come open once is a hole and not a pattern; a description is not a place; a quarter of a thing is not a thing; **a rule about a thing is not a rule about the world, and a rule about a document is a rule about a document.**
- **A statement of error's third clause is a real procedure and using it is correct**, it is not a version lock, and a local instrument with a notary, an hour, a person named and a term is not a pale field with no office and no hour.
- **Marrow stays publicly useful and unaccused.** No scene may convict him and none may make him useless. **In this batch he carried a bench, confirmed a shape in one word, and handed this city a dated promise, and asked for all three to be entered. He has still not mentioned the letter to Hattie Park. The count is flood days from the fortieth: thirty-one at day 71, thirty-five at day 75, forty at day 80, forty-five at day 90.**
- **A stipend is a wage, a wage is owed to a person, and a person is described on a roll**; **a guild is not a person, a guild can be refused, and the fear of the word *list* in this city is a fear of persons**; and **a wage off a second page is a wage a person can be removed from, and a page that asks a person whether she will carry a charge is a description in embryo, which is why the meeting-ground refused it.**
- **The calendar is load-bearing.** Day 1 is the Tuesday Adrian came out of the wall. Day 50 is the Tuesday. Day 60 is the Friday. The new month began on day 26, so the Nth day of the new month is day 25 + N. **Verified across the batch: 71 Tue, 72 Wed, 73 Thu, 74 Fri, 75 Sat, 76 Sun, 77 Mon, 78 Tue, 79 Wed, 80 Thu.**
- **Two date forms, and only two.** *The Nth day of this flood* up to the fortieth, *the Nth day of the new month* after it. **Never *of this month*, *of the month*, *of next month*, *of this new month*, or a spelled-out impossible ordinal.** A date after the new month has run out is **the Nth day of the month after this one**, and the workway's review date of the fourth day of that month is the one place it appears. ***March* is a month in this world's calendar**, the season is settled in it, **it is five days off at the end of this batch and it is never a date number.**
- **The word-count device is exact.** Every figure in Chapters 71–80 has been counted against the sentence as it stands and the binding list is in `state/continuity.md` under *Volume 02, Batch 0003*. **Renna Ord's eleven words are Volume 01 canon and are not re-counted.** A figure may not be carried over from a note and a figure may not be reused because it sounds right.
- **The age of the trial licence is that day minus fifty.** Twenty-one on the seventy-first, twenty-five on the seventy-fifth, **thirty at the end of Chapter 0080.**

## 7. Three places where this batch's prompt and the binding state disagreed, and how each was settled

1. **Chapter 0078's field.** The prompt says the batch's field *is the twenty-second* and also that the batch has *two* fields. **Both are now true and the numbering is unambiguous on the page: Chapter 0073's field is the twenty-second and is the one refused, which is the figure the batch's refusal count turns on; Chapter 0078's is the twenty-third and nobody answered it.** The refusals column ends at **twenty-two**; the running number of offers ends at **twenty-three**; and the closing paragraph of Chapter 0078 says all three, and adds that a girl of seventeen counted the field and could not tell anybody which book to write it in.
2. **Chapter 0078's *eleven days ago*.** The twentieth field was accepted on the fortieth day of the new month, which is day 65, and the work is on the fifty-third day of that month, which is day 78, so it is **thirteen days later.** The chapter now states the two dates and never the elapsed count.
3. **The cost column.** The rail's mending was entered as the **eighth** cost of the month on the twenty-seventh day of the new month. Chapter 0071 now says eighth; Chapter 0075 says nine entries and does not attribute the ninth to the rail; Chapter 0076 enters the tenth and says a smaller entry is not a cheaper column.

## 8. Two flags for the reviewer and for the close

- **The volume outline reserves the page-in-a-shrine and the reading of the nineteen shapes for the close in Chapters 96–100.** Chapter 0080 stages both, because the batch card requires the batch to close on the shrine and the outline requires the failure to be paid for at a shrine. **The close must therefore build a different image on the same stones**, and Batch 0004 and Batch 0005 must not repeat the shape-reading.
- **The outline's faction line says the Glass Crown sends four documents by the end of the volume, and Chapter 0077 sends a fifth thing in a case.** The chapter says in terms that the fifth sendings and the fourth shape are two different counts, so both are true, and **Batch 0004 may not turn the receiving-party column into a sixth document.**

## 9. Repair pass after review — what changed and what did not

**Reviewed once, repaired once. No chapter restarted, no scene rewritten, the ten days and their order untouched, no finding softened, nothing in the planned ending or in `outline/volume-02.md` moved.** Sixty findings returned, fifty-one fixed. In short:

- **Twenty-two word counts re-counted and corrected, and nine removed where a count was doing no work.** The binding list is in `state/continuity.md`.
- **Six arithmetic and date errors fixed**, including that the fortieth day of the flood is a **Saturday**, and that the named Sunday is **seven days** after the fifty-first day of the new month and not eleven, in four places.
- **The measurement history in 0075 corrected** to the binding two-step form.
- **Fourteen name and person merges fixed**, two of which any reader would have caught: the four words on the front of the slate are Renna Ord's, not Adrian's; and the girl of seventeen is not one of the two people on no roll.
- **Three internal contradictions inside 0078 fixed** so that the chapter's central finding — two of the four things and not three — is the only account of the working's arithmetic in the chapter.
- **Three incompatible partitions of the eleven questions reduced to the binding four keep, six close, one neither, and one trap.**
- **Four pieces of production meta-language removed** from the prose. **No chapter now contains a batch number, a chapter number or a word-count instruction.**
- **Section openers varied** where a run was found, and the closing roll-call is no longer identical in every chapter.
- **The nine-week Registry hearing moved to eleven weeks** so that there is one eleven-week clock and not two.
- **Ivenn Marrow was given one beat in 0079's yard** after ten days of silence, in which he is the most useful man in the room and hands the city the only weapon it will have against him, and asks for it to be entered.
- **Not changed, deliberately:** the house voice survives at full strength, and **the midpoint reversal was checked line by line and is unsoftened** — the bar is perfect, scoped to the wrong thing, **nobody used anything and there was no hand on any lever**, the page is perfect and does not reach, the thing it did not reach was a person on a boundary, the fourth line is used against him correctly and he cannot answer it, four correct sentences stand against him and he may not answer them, **and a yard of two hundred and forty people refuses to excuse him out loud.**
