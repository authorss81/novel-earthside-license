# Volume 06, Batch 0005 — Chapters 0291–0300, days 291 to 300

**Ten chapters, days 291 to 300, one chapter to a day, and the last of them is the forty-fifth day of the Short Month, a Sunday. These are the last ten days of the volume and they end it. The days go back on the thirty-ninth, a road north of the Marches is shut by nobody and does not open again, a regional answer is read out in a room and is not signed by anybody, a raft goes to nine places on nine days without a page on it, a man who wrote a line on a wall is not thanked, a straight line in the ground is seen from a gate by a person doing her work and is not looked at, and the case is still shut on a table and the schedule is not filled in and the ninth line is where it was and nobody got stronger.**

**The subject of the first four days is a question asked nine times and what came back, and the finding is a count.
