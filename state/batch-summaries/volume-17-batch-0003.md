## THE TEN CARDS, DAYS 821 TO 830

### Card 0821 — day 821, Wednesday, the five hundred and sixth day of the Bare Month, character. Adrian: no.
- **Where and who:** the room over a market, at the side of that bench. The woman of about fifty-two; the
  keeper at the near end; the man of about thirty-four; the man of about thirty-nine.
- **Goal:** to put that stool back against a wall where nobody can get at it.
- **Business:** the stool carried the length of the side of that room and set into the corner behind the near
  leg of the bench, under the shelf, with the table against the wall in front of it. To sit on it a person
  would have to move the table and climb. Nobody moves the table.
- **Resistance:** the corner, and an ordinary morning that wants somewhere to sit.
- **Change:** the room has one fewer place to sit and a man who came up that stair at the seventh hour put his
  hand on the far end of the bench and went back down again without sitting anywhere.
- **Closing beat:** the damp flags keeping the shape of where the stool had stood, still there at the tenth
  hour — a fact new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the bench, the shelf, the table against the wall.
- **No chapter of the ten says why she moves it and no mouth asks.**

### Card 0822 — day 822, Thursday, the five hundred and seventh, physical. Adrian: 2.
- **Where and who:** the yard at the near end of the flats, at the side of it. Adrian Vale on the flags at the
  hollow; the man of about thirty-eight with his barrow and his load of boards.
- **Goal:** to have that hollow dry before a barrow came down it.
- **Business:** water standing in a hollow of the flags. He gets his hands and then a piece of slate into it,
  brings out grit and a filled joint, and the water comes back into the same place. **NOT OBTAINED, and the
  chapter does not label it.** He is the cause of a thing that happens to somebody else: to get past him at the
  near side the man with the barrow has to swing wide over the dry crown, a wheel goes down into the hollow,
  and the near end of his load stands in the water while the run-off off the boards goes back into the same
  hollow. Nobody thanks him, nobody tells him he was right, and nobody in that yard was waiting for him to be
  useful.
- **Resistance:** a filled joint under the flags and a barrow that wants its own line.
- **Closing beat:** the water in that hollow standing deeper than it had at the fourth hour, with the grit of
  the stone in a skin on the top of it — a fact new to the chapter.
- **Third party / task / thing:** the barrow, the load, the low place in the stone at the side of that yard.
- **He performs no working, no threshold is opened, the other world is not named, nobody offers him a workway
  and he asks for none.**
