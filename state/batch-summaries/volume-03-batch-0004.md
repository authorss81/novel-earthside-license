# Volume 03, Batch 0004 — Chapters 131–150: A Room, In Daylight, With a Date On It

**Twenty chapters, and they are the last twenty days of Volume 03 and the whole of the run-up to and including the hearing. This is the batch in which the volume's climax happens, and it is a refusal, and the refusal is not a victory and does not produce the compact.** The hearing was held on day 154, the hundred and fifty-fourth day of the flood, in a room over a market, in daylight, with a date in chalk on the door, and about nine people were in it and about two hundred and forty people could hear out of the hall and the landing and the market. A man of thirty-two refused a Crown appointment of interpreter with unlimited authority in the local language and read his own declination out. A woman of twenty-nine who keeps a public register stood up without being asked and read eleven lines out loud and became the account of them. A notary of fifty was asked, in a room, in daylight, with a date on it, by a person, for the first time in thirty-three days, and said no — not to being asked, and no to the opening. The arrivals were recorded as **named guests** under a compact that may be ended by the asking of a person, the crossing was recognised **provisionally**, and four people and a question were sent to the First Landing under a clause that says *to open nothing*. **And a man of thirty-two answered the volume's question in a form, and it is unpaid, and the eight names at the foot of a hundred-and-forty-year-old page were not read.**

The gains are a compact, a refusal, a recognition, a delegation, a reading out loud, a page on the back of a stone, a wage, a room, a box that is still shut on purpose, and the mechanism by which names leave a place. **None of them is a power.** The three costs are a man who is now outside every office in this world and has said out loud that that makes him the most findable person in it, a reader who is a person on a public page and will be written down by a clerk in about four weeks, and a notary who has finally answered something and can never say she was not asked.

## 5. The rules established in this batch, binding on Volume 04

1. **THE HEARING IS A ROOM, IT WAS HELD, AND IT IS OVER.** Day 154, the hundred and fifty-fourth day of the flood, in a room over a market, in daylight, with a date in chalk on the door, about nine people in it, about two hundred and forty able to hear out of the hall, the landing and the market. There was one hearing and one clock. **The next time this city can put anything at all in front of the office that held it is about five weeks from the hearing, which is the quarter.**
2. **A HEARING IS A ROOM AND A ROOM IS THE ONLY SHAPE IN FOUR HUNDRED MILES THAT IS NOT A PAGE, and a building is the only thing anybody in this city has built that is meant to be sat in by somebody who is not from this city.** A room is gone in a day. A page travels. **This does not.**
3. **A TABLE IS A PAGE YOU CAN SIT AT. A BENCH FACING A DOOR IS A SEAT, AND A SEAT IS A PLACE TO BE ANGRY AT, AND A STEP IS A THING A FLOOR HAS AND NOT A THING A ROOM OFFERS.** The table went down the stair and came back four days later and nobody agreed to that.
4. **A WALL IN A ROOM IS THE CHEAPEST THING IN THIS CITY AND THERE IS ALREADY ONE, SO A HEARING ROOM HAS NOTHING ON ITS WALLS AND THE DATE GOES ON THE DOOR, BECAUSE A DOOR IS NOT A PAGE.**
5. **A COMPACT WITH A PERSON'S NAME IN EVERY PLACE IN IT IS A DESCRIPTION OF A DISTRICT, AND IT IS JUST LONGER AND IT IS SIGNED.** A compact that is meant to travel cannot have a person in every place, and a compact with a number in every place can be removed by an office, and **both halves are the finding and the batch did not resolve them.**
6. **A COUNT IN ONE PLACE AND AN ASSENT IN ANOTHER, AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME PLACE.** Two lines, a hundred and forty years apart, and the cost of the two lines is that no office in nine hundred miles can check either of them. **This is the volume's argument as a shape and it is now binding: no later shape may put a count and an assent in one place without the chapter saying what it costs.**
7. **THE FIFTH LINE IS THE ONLY LINE THAT IS A PERSON, AND IT IS NINE WORDS, AND THAT OFFICE CANNOT ACT ON IT, AND THE REASON IS THAT THAT OFFICE REVOKES THINGS AND DOES NOT RECEIVE THINGS THAT STOP BY THEMSELVES, AND THE FORM FOR AN ENDING HAS A COMPULSORY REASON IN IT.** Everything else in a compact may be a rule about a document. Line five is a person. **And line five is the reason a compact is a compact and not a description, and it is the reason this batch's resolution is a resolution and not a description.**
8. **A DELEGATION IS A NUMBER WITH LEGS. YOU CAN SEND A NUMBER AND YOU CANNOT SEND A PAGE, and when four comes back there will be no person in the account to be cross with.** A person who is asked whether they will go is a person on a list, and a list of who will stand in a room is a removal with a column for where the person is, and a delegation is the same page with a longer column and a worse place to be found in. **A man of about thirty-nine said *I will go* and then said he would not be named and asked for the second half to be written down and not the first.**
9. **A NAME SAID OUT LOUD ONCE IN A ROOM IS A PERSON, AND A NAME ON A PAGE IS A PLACE A PERSON CAN BE FOUND IN, AND ABOUT TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY PEOPLE CAN HEAR OUT OF A ROOM, A HALL, A LANDING AND A MARKET.** That is the instrument this volume built and it is four hundred years old and it is gone in a day. **No page in this city carries the nine names and the page with a count of nine on it and nobody at the top of it is still on the page, and the two disagree, and both of them are true.**
10. **A REFUSAL OF AN OFFER IS A THING THAT COSTS THE PERSON WHO MAKES IT, and the cost is named out loud and entered, and the third refusal moved the count in a clerk's second book from twenty-four to twenty-five for the third time in a hundred and fifty-four days, and the count at Aurel is thirteen, and the difference is twelve, and it was eleven, and it was ten for about a hundred days, and it was nine before the first registered refusal, and it has never been made smaller.**
11. **THE ONE SHAPE A MAN WHO CAN READ TWO SYSTEMS OF WRITING MAY NOT BE GIVEN, RESTATED: a name, a title, a standing, a workway, a mark, a seat, a wage, a roster, a column, or any arrangement in which his reading is the price of somebody else's place.** And the sentence that came with it: **a reading that is the price of a place is a reading that is the price of a place, and a thing that cannot be argued with because it is a day is a chair with no date on it.**

A POST CHANGES WHO FILLS IN THE PLACES.** And a compact is a form and a form exists whether or not anybody will stand in it, and if he had said yes the compact would be exactly the eleven lines it is with his voice in it instead of hers. **No later chapter may present the refusal as the cause of anything.**
