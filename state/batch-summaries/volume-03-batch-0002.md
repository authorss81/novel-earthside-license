# Volume 03, Batch 0002 — Chapters 111–120: A Case That Came Up

Ten days. Days 111 to 120, the twenty-first to the thirtieth day of the month after this one, and the second ten days of Volume 03. **This batch is the one in which something came back up a river.** A wooden box with a strap and a wax and a second strap came up the water on the twenty-sixth day for the first time in a hundred and forty years, crossed in mid-channel with the case that went down on the eighth day, and was not opened, and could not be checked. The batch then took a document a hundred and forty years old out of a chest in a village, had the only man in this city who can tell a form from a sentence read it out loud in a room, and found out that **a translation is a description and a description is a removal with a column for where the person is and the column is him.** Nobody was convicted of anything. A village refused a fourth time and nobody can say what a person's fourth refusal is. Thirty-nine men turned up unpaid. A boy of nine's answer was written down sixty-eight days late and nobody improved on it. **The gains are a clock, a case that cannot be opened, a number with nobody at the top of it, a boundary form, a fifth clause of a document this city did not write, and twenty-eight words on a slate. None of them is a power and the volume's reversal is in what one of them cost.**

**This batch has been reviewed once and repaired once, and the review ran under the writer agent because the reviewer is configured as a subagent, so it is a strong pass and not an independent sign-off, and no claim of an independent review is made anywhere in the state files for this batch.** The record is `reviews/volume-03/batch-0002.md` and the finding list, including one finding that was **rejected** because the page was right and the review had read a Volume 03 path for a Volume 02 chapter, is §13.
