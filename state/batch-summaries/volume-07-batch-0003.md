1. **May not reduce any of the four notices to three days, may not put a name at the top of any of them, may not print the string, and may not write a fifth notice on any day other than the twentieth of the Bare Month for a room on the twenty-fourth.**
2. **May not use the word on the second slate, and may not define the line, explain it, improve on it or put it in a second mouth.** It is on a page in 0310 and comes off in 0342.
3. **May not send anybody to the woman of about fifty at the shut gate for any reason, and may not ask her anything, and may not ask the man of about thirty-eight with the barrow whether she knows what the line is** — that is the volume's climax and it is not this batch's.
4. **May not put a rod in anything, may not clear or measure either of the two holes nine feet apart, may not make a third hole, may not touch the line, and may not send anybody to look at it.** The man of about fifty-two who digs is on a page in 0321 with a rod he does not carry, and a chapter before 0321 may not invent it.
5. **May not name him anything but Nev Cutler, may not give him a surname, and may not put his name at the top of a page.**
6. **May not ask any of the four whether it will go, may not send anybody to ask them, and may not give them a word for what they are.** They are four people.
7. **May not put Tamsin Quill and the woman of about twenty-nine in one room** — the room in Chapter 0330 is a room over a shop on the low ground and the register-keeper is not in it — **may not resolve whether they are one woman, may not mend the consent fracture, and may not give the fifth condition.**
8. **May not fetch the girl of seventeen, may not resolve her, may not give her a reading out, and may not put her on a roll or at an address.**
9. **May not say the day-154 sentence, may not put *scar* or any of the three sentences this manuscript has used about it into a mouth, and may not use *unwritten* of a settlement, a people, a record, a place or a life, may not use *class* outside a prohibition, and may not use *one version* of a history, a road, a record or a person.** The volume's noun is one account.
10. **May not print *knock* on the stem, the string *to open nothing*, or an ordinal for a month, and may not put a figure on the Marches or convert a hundred and forty years or a season into days.**
11. **May not lay the two rolls of the same gates side by side, may not define *holder*, may not print the request to strike it out of any list again, and may not open, fill in or withdraw the Crown's schedule.**
