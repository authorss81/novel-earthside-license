# Volume 03 Close Record — *The Crown's Ledger*, Chapters 101–150

Day 1 was the Tuesday Adrian Vale came out of the wall. **Day 154 is the hundred and fifty-fourth day of the flood and a Monday**, and at about the seventh hour of the evening the room over the market is a room again: a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall has put the table back, because nine people were in it for four hours and the people who come in afterwards need something to stand at, and nobody agreed to that and he did it anyway. There is a step at the back with no name on it, a shutter open, and a date in chalk on a door that will still be there in about four months. A woman of twenty-nine is walking down a hill with a page on her and a girl of seventeen is carrying a slate. A notary of fifty said no to being asked and no to the opening, in the same four hours. A box is upstairs with its strap untied and the writing round the edge of its wax unread by anybody in a hundred and fifty-four days. Eight names sit at the foot of a page in a fold of oilcloth in a book that cannot travel, and nobody read them. And there have been sixty silences in a row.

This is the one page the next phase has to read before it plans anything.

## 1. What Volume 03 answered

**The plan's climax happened and it did not produce the compact.** Adrian Vale refused an appointment of Crown-appointed interpreter for a district with authority without limit — nine terms and two eighths — in a room, in daylight, in the local language, on the sixty-fourth day of the month after this one, in front of about nine people and about two hundred and forty who could hear him, and he read his own declination out because there is no one else in the room who can. **The compact was drafted the day before the post was put on a table, and the batch says on the page that if he had said yes the compact would be exactly the eleven lines it is with his voice in it instead of hers.** A post does not change a form; a post changes who fills in the places, **and no later chapter may present the refusal as the cause of anything.**

**The compact is the eleventh shape and it is eleven lines, and the fifth line is the only line of the eleven that is a person**: a guest may ask to stop being a guest, the asking is the whole of it, it requires no reason, and it is not asked twice. The eighth line is the reader. The count of the guests is in the second place and the assent of the guests is in the third place, and they are not the same place, and the reason is that no office in nine hundred miles can check either of them. **A man of forty-one in a very good coat spent nine days looking for a form his office could act on and there is not one, because that office revokes things and does not receive things that stop by themselves, and the form for an ending has a compulsory reason in it.** The compact is a compact because about nine people were in a room with it, and the eleventh line says so.

**The arrivals were recorded as named guests, and named means that about nine people said their own names out loud, once each, in that room, in daylight, and not one of them was asked and not one of the nine names was written down by anybody anywhere.** A woman of twenty-nine who keeps a public register read the eleven lines out and became the account of them; a girl of seventeen read the whole of it back twice in the hall for the people at the back, from a slate and not from her own, because her own has her name at the top of it. **The page in this city with a count of nine on it and nobody at the top of it, and the nine names in no page, are both true and they disagree, and a volume may not have it both ways and a chapter may not resolve the disagreement.**

**The crossing is recognised provisionally, in the local language, in no book, and provisional is revocable**, and a district recognised provisionally is a district that can be described as not recognised, and there is a form for that too which a man from that office has not seen. **A delegation of four people and a question is to go four hundred miles inland to the ground people call the First Landing, to say what is standing on it and whether there is a seam in the ground, to open nothing, to come back, and to say it in a room in daylight in the local language with a date on it.** Nobody has been asked whether they will go, because a person who is asked is a person on a list, and a delegation is a number with legs: you can send a number and you cannot send a page.

**The volume's question was answered, in a form, and it is unpaid.** What happened to the people whose names were removed from the first records is not that somebody took them off a list. It is that a form has one place for a count and one place for an assent, and it has had that for a hundred and forty years, and a man who was taken goes in the count and does not go in the assent, **and there is no column anywhere in nine hundred miles for a man who was taken.** That is not a crime, it is not a conspiracy and it is not a person. It is a shape, and a clerk filling it in on the forty-fourth day of the month after this one was doing the ordinary work of a Tuesday. He said it in a room, in daylight, in the local language, with a clerk of that office in it, on the day he gave up the only office anybody ever offered him, having no standing to act on it; he had about nine more sentences and did not say them and nobody asked him for them. **The eight names were not read. The case with the question in its eighth column is on a desk nine hundred miles away, may not be answered here, may not be intercepted and may not be taken back, and what comes back from it in about five weeks is a number, and the reading of that number is not his.** A correct number about a district, made by a waterboard in the ordinary course, is already on a wall in a converted salt store and cannot be stopped by anybody in this city.

**What Adrian lost, in the order the volume took it, item by item.** First, on the twenty-ninth day of the month after this one, he read a boundary page out loud in the local language in a room, and the finding of that room was that a translation is a description and a description is a removal with a column for where the person is, **and the column is him**; that is the last place his reading was a private act and the volume took it. Second, on the thirty-third day, he was asked to read out a page with a girl's name at the top of it and said no in about four seconds, and entered that he has not asked one person one question in a hundred and twenty-nine days. Third, the appointment, and then the honest version of it — one reading, one document, one day, no standing, no roster, no seat, no wage — refused in about four seconds for the same reason, stated once and not improved on, because a reading that is the price of a place is a reading that is the price of a place, and a thing that cannot be argued with because it is a day is a chair with no date on it. **He is now outside every office in this world, holding no mark, no roster, no standing, no seat, no wage, no title, no land right and no workway, and he said out loud, in front of about two hundred and forty people, after he had given the thing up and not before, that a man with no mark and on no roster is the most findable person in this world, and that this is not a safety and he knows it.** And nobody thanked him and he said in advance that nobody would. **What did not come back and was not coming back: the knowing, lost in about four minutes in a room on the second day of the month after this one, sixty-two days ago, and the way home goes through a room with no door.** He said that out loud too, so that about four people in this city would stop thinking that saying no brings it back.

## 2. What Volume 03 deliberately did not answer

None of these is a gap. Each is a live question and each was protected in prose.

- **What is in the box.** It came up a river on the twenty-sixth day of the month after this one and it is thirty-eight days in a room with a door, the strap on it is untied, and the writing round the edge of the wax has not been read by anybody in a hundred and fifty-four days, though one man in this city could have and did not on four days and said nothing on a fifth. A notary of fifty was asked, in a room, in daylight, with a date on it, by a person, for the first time in thirty-three days, and said no — not to being asked, and no to the opening — because a thing that is opened in a hearing is a thing the hearing is about. **The second sharpest thing in the book is a document a man of thirty-two could read and has declined to read five times.**
- **Where the eight men went.** The mechanism is answered and the men are not located. The eight names at the foot of a boundary page of about a hundred and forty years ago are in a fold of oilcloth in a book that cannot travel, and no page in this city carries them. **And three eights in four hundred miles are still three, and a man who wants them to be one is a man who wants and not a man who knows.**
- **What is at the First Landing.** Three things, none of them on a page: one line in a register about a market, about three centuries old, with salt in it; a tradition in about nine rooms of which there are three incompatible versions; and ground with stone on it that nobody in this city has been to. **The delegation has not left, nobody has been asked whether they will go, and the only instruction it has is *to open nothing*.** It is not a door, a monster, a court, or a place where anybody in this book was already wrong, and the delegation is not a rescue.
- **How a page that ends a document came to end one.** Six rooms, a seventh thing tried, about four minutes, a hand flat on a table, and a count of rooms that is not being made seven, because a count that moves is a score and this is not a score. The one sentence that came closest was not written down, a girl of seventeen had a slate under her hand and did not put it on the table, and not one of the nine people in that room wanted the page to have not worked, out loud. **The mechanism is not on the page and may not be put there before Volume 06.**
- **And the fifth, and it is the series' engine: the room at the far end of the Narrow Mark, the boards under the chapel, the chapel stone, the scar, and the knocks are exactly where they were four batches ago.** The boards have not been lifted since the fortieth day of this flood. The chapel stone is silent, unrepaired, and has said nothing in a hundred and fifty-four days. The room has no window and no door anybody has found and has not been looked at in four batches. Three knocks on the night of the twenty-ninth day of the new month and four on the thirty-fifth stand entered and neither deleted, **and the word *knock* does not appear once in Chapters 131–150.** *Active, and it is the series' engine, and it is untouched.*

## 4. What must not be re-litigated

- **Owen Park is dead and buried and is not mentioned once in Chapters 101–150, and the correct number of times in Volume 04 is also zero.** Nothing may mention him, soften him, summarise him, re-open his burial term or turn him into a lesson, and **nobody may write *he died because*.** A form may not shorten a record because a form is small. **Dante Kuo's right shoulder went on the thirty-first day of this flood, was reduced twice, cannot be set, did not go, was not made whole, and is on a page in a stone as a debt with no line that will mend it — and that page is now wrong and is going to stay wrong on purpose.** The struck-off name on the roll of the forty is deliberately unnamed and may stay that way, and Ovin Sarr may not be put in a sentence with Dante Kuo. **Eight people exist who may never be put in one sentence** — a man of about twenty-six refused a bed at a Wayhouse, a man in a back room who is dying, a man of about thirty-one with a hand that will not close, a man of about thirty-five whose shoulder cannot be set, a man of about forty-four who keeps a stall, a man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board, a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall, and a man of sixty-one who copies other men's sentences. **This list is eight and not nine: the ninth entry that stood here until this close was *a man of about thirty-four who keeps a stall by the wharf*, and that person is a woman, and the ninth entry was a phantom produced by one wrong word in Chapter 0149, corrected on the page in this phase. ## 5. The one thing the next phase should know about the shape of the book

Nobody in this volume got stronger. A woman of seventy said a plain sentence about luck, in a kitchen, on a day she did not choose, and what she said was that the strongest refusal this city has is held up by luck. A girl of seventeen read her own page out badly in the local language in front of about nine people, and nobody corrected her, and the cost of it was her. A man of sixty-eight said he would carry a person and would not write the household down. A notary of fifty was asked six times in thirty-eight days and answered once, and it was no. A woman of about sixty-one with a barrow said she would have to look at a wrong page for forty years, and she is not going to be asked about it, and she offered that as the only thing she had. A Hearthguard of forty-four was asked properly, on a day he did not choose, after twenty-seven days, said no in about four seconds, entered that the four seconds are the same four seconds as on the thirtieth day of the new month, and asked in advance not to be thanked, and nobody thanked him. **And a man from another world gave up the only thing he is good at as a way of being needed, in front of about two hundred and forty people, and nobody thanked him, and the gains of the whole volume are a name, a page on the back of a stone, a form, a room, a wage and a mechanism.**

**The gains are a name said aloud and written nowhere, a page on the back of a stone that is wrong on purpose, a form for a compact of eleven lines of which the fifth is a person, a room with a table put back in it, a wage with the payer's name on it, and a mechanism — a form with one place for a count and one place for an assent, and no column anywhere in nine hundred miles for a man who was taken. None of them is a power. One of them is the price of a power. All of them are heavier than a power.**
