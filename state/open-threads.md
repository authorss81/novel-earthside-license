# Open Threads

Status after Batch 0005 (Chapters 41–47). A promise becomes active when the prose pays it and is discharged when the book or the event closes it. **Owen Park's death is paid, not open, and his burial is paid too.**

## Central mystery

- **Active, untouched in this batch.** On the thirty-eighth day Owen Park, on a table, in a room, said the truest thing anybody said in nine hours: the light he has seen since the first morning is **not a relic, it is the same shape and the same proportion as the mark on Adrian's right palm, and he has been looking at Adrian.** It is entered as *what a person said*, corroborated by nothing, and a woman of fifty-eight has said since that the fourth reading of a mystery is available to anybody. Adrian has not acted on it and has not read it. **The account is now buried and the four pages are in the fourth volume and a mason of thirty-four years has still never been able to get her hands on the stones.** *Active.*
- **Active and advanced twice in this batch, and not one more time in forty-three days.** The interface has made **sixteen offers across five batches** — four, four, three, three and now two — and has **never once registered a refusal of one**, and still has no memory of any of them. **Two messages in this batch, in Chapters 42 and 46**, and **none in 41, 43, 44, 45 or 47**, and **a room of two hundred and forty people committing a man to a thing for the rest of his life produced no field at all.** Both messages are the same construction seen for a sixteenth time and both are the new versions of it: the first **puts nothing at all in the position where a cost goes** and calls the city's not-being-asked free; the second **names a process instead of a person** and makes the whole of the plural condition reversible by one man, in the shape of the Crown's own *a position may be filled by a person who can hold it*. Adrian refused both in under a second and could say why both times. **A clerk from the Registry has been counting the fields in a book at Aurel since the eighth day of this flood — eight so far, all in a stranger's language, none in anybody else's — and has never been asked to stop and is never going to be.** *Active.*
- **Not yet surfaced in prose.** What is the First Grammar, and how did the First Sundering split it? The name is not used in Chapters 1–47. **Neither is the name Single Witness used in prose, and Chapter 48–50 must not use it either.**
- **Advanced to its sharpest point and still unnamed, on purpose.** Marrow's sentence of the fortieth has been understood as a **shape** and refused twice in public — his own version in a room on the nineteenth day of the new month, and his method, which Adrian named in a yard as *one true reason, in one hand, in the one place a city keeps things, and then the same move again about a different person*. The shape is now in the book and there are **three instruments of it in Saltwake in five weeks and only two of them are the Crown's**, and the third one is not from a court and not from an office. **Adrian has said out loud that he does not have a name for it and is not going to invent one in a room, because a quarter of a thing is not a thing.** Roke has entered that the man's face did not do anything at the end of nine hundred words. *Active, and the volume's next problem is now a problem of naming rather than of detection.*
- **Closed in the worst way and not repaired.** The voice's channel is the **black line in the chapel stone** and it **did not speak in this batch**: on the twenty-second day of the new month Adrian stood on the slab at about the tenth hour of the night and waited about four minutes and nothing came. It is not repaired and it is not going to be. Its one long message in this world is still the twelve words of the twenty-fifth day, and **Adrian now believes those twelve words describe the Lock of Salt, and has told a notary once and no one else, and did not say it in a yard.** *Active, and the reason is entered: a sentence that can only be confirmed by the man it is about is a version of himself and is not a record.*
- **Active, and now the shape of the volume's answer.** The ordinary maintenance of the lock steps the join in the gate, a working's residue reaches a place the working could not touch, and a working's residue opening a scar under a floor is what took a man in on the thirty-eighth. **Chapter 0045 has now named the third term of the triangle: the forty words under the near gate are the toll and they are the pattern, and a city cannot spend a thing and be its own foundation at the same time.** *Active, and the remedy remains working the lock differently and not less.*

## Immediate Volume 01 promises

- **The main breach is open and is stepping.** **Twenty-three readings. Eight and seven-eighths.** The step on the forty-third day is **a quarter of an inch in one step** and it is entered as a fact with the decision behind it written by a mason of thirty-four years in his own hand at the seventh hour and in the book before the number was read, with Vask entering it and Adrian not asking her to. **The next tide is the forty-ninth day, a Monday, its box is empty, and the working is on the board for that day and has not been done.** *Not discharged. This is the volume's whole remaining question and the next three chapters are it.*
- **The distributed working is committed, sealed and not started.** The instrument's ninth page is entered and signed on the twenty-second day of the new month, with its six conditions, its cost, and a flood-season review with a date on the page before the day. **Adrian is on no roster of it, at his own request, in his own hand.** **The city is the debtor, and the day the bottom field is not paid in March the shift stops and the join stops being worked.** *Active, and not performed.*
- **The Registry's demand is live and has been answered twice and answered in the form required neither time.** Three Crown instruments are now in this city's book. The letter's next quarterly sitting is at about a hundred and ten days from the date of the letter. *Active, and the only instrument with a date on it.*

- **The Narrow Mark is continued, unusable, costed and refusable.** Its one use is entered as a breach and is not a precedent. *Not discharged, and the trial licence of Chapters 48–50 is the only thing in the series allowed to be a standing route.*

- **The black line in the chapel stone is not repaired and did not speak in this batch. The black line in Adrian's palm is forty-six days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch.** *Active, and the distinction between the two is load-bearing.*

Vask's four items are on the other side of the door and are not washed. *Discharged as an event; the board remains.*

He asked in the room to be removed from the terms of the post and **was not**, and the notary entered that she cannot enter the request and why. *Active, and it expires in two days.*
