# Open Threads

Status after Batch 0002 (Chapters 11–20). A promise becomes active when the prose pays it and is discharged when the book or the event closes it.

## Central mystery

- **Active, opened Chapter 1.** Why does the System use Adrian's familiar game vocabulary before he has taught it that vocabulary?
- **Advanced in Chapter 19, and worse.** The voice said a sentence in the chapel stone that Adrian wrote at four in the morning in a file at Morrowglass and then **deleted the section of**. So it is not on the laptop and there is no file. Either something has been inside him since Tuesday, or something read a document that no longer exists. He wrote the second reading out in a keeper's own words and had it chalked on the chapel door, under Vask's list, where anybody who comes up into that yard has to read it. *Active and load-bearing.*
- **Advanced in Chapters 12 and 16.** Three separate suspicions of the interface and no proof: its costs are edited (12, 16), it is *current to the hour* (16), and it has **no memory of a refusal** — ten days and two refusals apart it still says *it is not yet accepted* (16). Adrian's whole working assumption that a permission is a thing with a state is wrong. *Active.*
- **Not yet surfaced in prose.** What is the First Grammar, and how did the First Sundering split it? The name is not used in Chapters 1–20.
- **Opened in Marrow's mouth, Chapter 7; advanced Chapter 12.** What did the earlier Bright Pilgrims cross, and why does Marrow have Adrian's own unpublished sentence? Now two of them, through two different channels, and only one of those channels is a man in a good coat.
- **Opened Chapter 7 and unresolved.** Ivenn knows more about the interface than he explains. In Chapter 12 he is reported to have told nine people in a room, on the Wednesday of the first week, that a genuine three-hundred-and-ten-year-old customs document is a fraud, and let them believe the whole of it was fake, when he had found it himself in the first week. **He is the only man in Saltwake with both reason and access. No public scene may convict him. He must remain publicly useful to the end of the volume.** *Active, dangerous, unproven.*
- **Opened Chapter 8, advanced Chapter 15.** Something is standing in the lock join using the old form of the oath with a **keeper's word** in the middle of it — a word that has never been in the oath, said every morning at the arch by Nyla Ferrow, her mother, and her mother's mother. In Chapter 15 the voice repeated it a half-second after the keeper said it, which means it is standing inside the binding. *Active.*
- **Advanced Chapter 19, Owen's account.** It is not the stone, it is the four words forty men say twice a day under the near gate; the man in the join did not build the pattern, he found it. **The field is a toll. Somebody is being charged.** The only question left is by whom, and whether they have agreed to it. *Active, and it is the batch's central argument.*
- **Not yet surfaced.** Can a plural bridge remain stable without a single authority? The Chapter 10 instrument is the first attempt, it has been formally declared unrecognised, and it is empty.

## Immediate Volume 01 promises

- **The main breach is still open and three inches wider than on arrival, and it is *stepping* rather than cracking.** Twenty nightly readings, four steps, all four on days the lock did unusual work. Nobody can act on the conclusion without drowning eleven villages. *Not discharged.*
- **The bottom field's lost season is a live clock and the number is still rising.** The review is the last day of the salt month, day 25, six days out, and it is the day a woman in Corrow decides how much winter grain to put in. Adrian understands now that a debt is not a fine but a relationship. *Active.*
- **Nine Elms' refusal is now a standing commitment**, given in public, in their own words, and may be withdrawn on any day. Tamsin's prediction has been paid: the instrument is formally unrecognised until a receiving community accepts the whole of the area, and that may be never. *Active and probably permanent.*
- **The Concord hearing on a first license is dated: three days after the next tide. The next tide is day 28 and the hearing is day 31.** Twelve days from the end of Chapter 20. *Active, on a clock.*
- **Ivenn's Wayfinder offer is still open, and it is now a *shape* with a date on it**, in his own hand in chalk on the keeper's board, expiring on the last day of the salt month. Adrian has refused it twice. *Active.*
- **The displaced Anchorstone under the chapel's yard-side landing is still out of place** and the boundary table now explains it: the stone was moved a hundred yards downstream by the three jurisdictions together, at a public act, in living memory, and the cost column says four hundred people were rehoused. **There is no "back".** *Active, and reframed.*
- **The ground under the chapel is open and boarded, and it continues under the river bank where nobody has ever dug.** The dig stopped on Renna Ord's arithmetic, not on fear. Digging the bank is a cofferdam, a manned pump, three more men, and a season. *Active.*
- **Owen Park's eventual death, planned for Chapter 38, must be permanent** and must carry Adrian's responsibility, the Chapter 25 shoulder that was never set, and Owen's own choice. He is alive at the end of Batch 0002. His scalp wound from the wall in Chapter 5 is now diagnosed as **unclosed** — clean skin over it and nothing happening underneath, a promise with a witness on it that has not been taken up. **His shoulder is untouched and remains reserved for Chapter 25.** Do not give him a leg, a collapsed lung, or any other lasting injury before then. *Deferred, not open.*
- **The black line in the chapel stone is not repaired in Volume 1.** It is on the page, unhealed, and it keeps talking. *Active.*
- **The black line in Adrian's palm is not repaired in Volume 1** and does not deepen into a black scar in a person; that is Owen's return mark in Chapter 38. It is an overload warning, it is separate from the pale fragment-mark, it opened on the second night, and it has not moved since. *Active.*
- **The keeper's standing labour is live**: four words and the fifth, twice a day, in the open, in any weather, by whoever is nearest, for as long as the ground is open, on a rota with no name at the top. Owen Park does it. It does not work the first time of the morning. *Active, and it is a season's obligation.*
- **Adrian's post is live**: for as long as the ground is open, the ground does not open without him. Every beginning. It is not money, it is not useful, and there is no end of it in sight. *Active.*
