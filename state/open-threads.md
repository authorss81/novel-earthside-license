## THE FOUR RULES A REPAIR PASS ON DAYS 971 TO 980 ADDED, AND THEY ARE ABOUT THE RECORD AND THE STAGING

**A reviewer returned eight hard findings on those ten days and three of them were this volume's own record publishing figures
its own instrument does not return. The working is `reviews/volume-20/batch-0003-fix.md`. Four rules stand from it, and this
batch obeyed all four and named two of them in its own record because they were the ones it was most likely to break.**

1. **A RECORD FIGURE THAT NAMES A PAIR MUST BE THE FIGURE THAT NAMED PAIR RETURNS.** That record printed 33 tokens and named
   0972 and 0973; the longest run was 34 tokens and it was 0973 and 0976, which was a §17.17 breach in the prose that the
   arithmetic was arranged not to show. **Publish the count and the pair together, and if a repair moves one it moves both.**
   **This batch's own first draft had a fifty-seven-token run between 0984 and 0990 and it published the figure after the
   prose was fixed rather than before it.**
2. **A RECORD MAY NOT ASSERT AN ABSENCE THAT A CHAPTER OF ITS OWN BATCH CONTRADICTS.** The standing case is that stair. The
   supportable claim is *no chapter counts that stair from the top step down to the flags*.
3. **A HELD STRING DOES NOT GET TO STAND IN A RECORD.** §20 item 6 bars the six held strings out of a card file, a prompt, a
   state file, a batch record and a summary. **A record quoting a string to prove it absent is quoting it.** This batch
   measured all six without printing one, over a twenty-three-file scope, and the table at
   `reviews/volume-20/batch-0004.md` §3.1 is that measurement.
4. **EVERY FACE, SPACE, CLOTH, BOARD, TOOL AND POCKET IN A CHAPTER BELONGS TO SOMEBODY.** The stallholder's board is face in
   with the bare back out; the tradesman's carries the nineteen rates, the empty two-finger space and the cloth. **This is not
   a plan defect and there is no rule that would have stopped it: that row has nine people in it and a sentence of staging goes
   wrong. Go through your ten before you finish and ask whose each of those things is and whether it is in his hand on the
   page.** **This batch's own table in §7 of its record lists, for each of the ten chapters, whose board is face in and whose
   cloth is on whose shoulder, and the answer is the same on all ten.**

**AND THE FIFTH RULE A REPAIR PASS ON DAYS 961 TO 970 ADDED, WHICH STILL STANDS.**

**A WRITING RUN DOES NOT AMEND A REVIEWER'S ARTIFACT, HOWEVER CORRECT THE AMENDMENT WOULD BE.** `reviews/` belongs to the
review pass. A writing run that finds an error in one states the error in the live state layer and leaves the file alone for
the pass that owns it. **Chapters, outlines, the bible and this state layer are the layers a writer owns; `reviews/`,
`scripts/`, `.github/`, `.opencode/agent/`, `AGENTS.md`, `PHASE_SYSTEM.md`, `REPO_PLAN.md`, `OUTLINE_GUIDE.md`,
`opencode.json` and `state/phase-ledger.json` are not, and a run that finds a fault in one of them says so in one sentence
and does not edit it and does not write a paragraph about having not edited it.**

**AND A RUN THAT FINDS ITS OWN WORK ALREADY DONE SAYS SO ONCE, IN ONE PLACE, AND WRITES NO CHAPTER.** The rule against padding
a chapter is the rule against padding a record: a file that is re-read by every later batch earns its length with state, not
with process.

## THE THREE THREADS DAYS 981 TO 990 OPENED, AND TWO OF THEM ARE THE KIND THAT CANNOT BE CLOSED BY FINDING OUT

**1. THE THING THE KEEPER PUT ON THE SHEET ON DAY 981, AND WHAT THE WOMAN OF ABOUT SIXTY-NINE ANSWERED IT.** The woman of
about twenty-nine who keeps a public register wrote it on a page before she put it, and the woman of about sixty-nine with her
tin read it before she answered. **She answered out loud in that room and the answering took about as long as the reading had.
The chapter prints the putting-to in full and prints no word of the answer. No leaf on either of those two books carries a
scratch of it, no board in that market and no chalk anywhere in this city carries one word of it, and nobody in that room
repeated it to anybody and nobody in that row heard it.** Nobody thanked either of them and nobody told either of them that
what they had said was right. OPEN, **AND IT CANNOT BE CLOSED, BECAUSE THERE IS NOTHING LEFT ANYWHERE TO FIND.**

**2. THE WORD THE MAN WHO CAME UP THAT STAIR SAID OUT LOUD ONCE ON DAY 985, AND EVERYTHING ANYBODY DOES NOT KNOW ABOUT
IT.** He said it once, in that room, in daylight, with the date in chalk on the outside of the door behind his own shoulder.
**Nobody in that room asked him what it meant, nobody said it back to him, nobody improved on it, explained it, translated it
or agreed with it, and nobody attached it to any day or any person or anything that had happened in this city.** It had stood
under a day cut across the head of that second slate for longer than anybody in that room had been alive, nothing had ever
been entered under that day, and no mouth in that room ever said out loud what it was doing there. **It is a word about
taking a thing and not giving it back, and this record does not say that, and neither does any chapter.** OPEN. **IT CANNOT
BE CLOSED BY FINDING OUT, because nobody in this manuscript is going to be asked about it on any day of the rest of this
volume, and the one man who would have been asked was not asked on any of these ten days.**

**3. THE SECOND DECISION OF DAY 982, THAT THE PAGE CARRIES THE PUTTING-TO, THE PERSON AND THE DAY AND CARRIES NOTHING BACK.**
She said it once and nobody agreed with it, nobody argued with it, nobody improved on one word of it and nobody was thanked
for it, and **nothing whatever was done about it before the light went and the space below the third of those lines stayed
as blank as the rest of the sheet.** OPEN, **and §6.5 says in its own words that nothing in fifty days changes it.**

## THE THREE THREADS ABOUT THE WRITING BEFORE THOSE FOUR RULES, WHICH ARE ABOUT THE MANUSCRIPT AND NOT ABOUT THE WORLD

**1. THE LENGTH SHORTFALL, OPEN ON PURPOSE, AND IT HAS MOVED AGAIN.** `PHASE_SYSTEM.md` asks for about 2,200 to 3,200 words
and days 981 to 990 measure a mean of **1,386.5**, against 1,339.0 for the batch behind it, 1,215.7 and 1,303.6. **The
movement was made by opening out six scenes that had room and by splitting forty-two sentences that were over sixty words
long, and by adding no paragraph that was not carrying something. Nothing was padded and no finished scene was split to reach
a number, and the thread stays open across batch 0005. A writer who closes it by padding has closed it wrong.**

**2. THE PROSE GOING HOLLOW, AND FOUR POINTS ARE NOT A DIRECTION.** Negation tokens run one in 45.5 words on days 951 to 960,
41.3 on 961 to 970, 62.6 on 971 to 980 and **58.0 here**. Bare *that* runs **3.30, 3.93, 3.14, and 3.98 per cent.** Both
series have two points high and two points low and no review should read either as a trend. **What moved them on batch 0003
was writing what people do with their hands instead of paragraphs that report the absence of events, and this batch wrote the
same way.** A held string binds what a chapter may print and does not license a paragraph spent reporting that it did not
print it, and neither figure was moved by cutting a required handle or a piece of standing geometry.

**3. THE ATTRIBUTION PUNCTUATION FAULT, WHICH IS A HOUSE FAULT AND IS MEASURED ON EVERY BATCH NOW.** The pattern is a full stop
inside the closing quote of a bolded speech immediately followed by a speech tag, and the correct form is
`**"…," ** said the man of about thirty-nine`. **Measured on days 981 to 990: seven speech paragraphs, seven closers, and the
closer in every one is `," **` — 0 sites.** Volume 19 still carries 21 outstanding sites, in chapters 0925, 0931 and 0934 to
0950, and Volume 07 one in chapter 0311 and Volume 16 one in chapter 0781; **those want a pass by whoever owns those
volumes, and the figure is carried here so that a later writer does not mistake it for a Volume 20 debt.** Volumes 17 and 18
carry 218 such tags between them and every one is already correct, so the fault is not universal and those two volumes are
the model.
