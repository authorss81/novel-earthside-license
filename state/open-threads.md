## Live into Volume 02

- **March.** The bottom field is **forty-seven days old**, entered **seven times**, with no final figure, and a woman in a village four miles away has to decide how much of her life to put in the ground. **Before March, four of the forty will be hurt doing the ordinary work of this lock, one at a time, on an ordinary morning, and no instrument in this world can stop it.** The day the field is not paid, the shift stops and the join stops being worked. *Active, and it is the clock of the next two volumes.*
- **The Registry's demand.** Three Crown instruments in this city's book — the notice of the twenty-eighth day of the flood, the third instrument of the seventeenth day of the new month, and the second answer of the twenty-second — **and none of them has been answered in the form required.** The quarterly sitting is at about a hundred and ten days from the date of the letter. *Active, and the only instrument with a date on it.*
- **Nine Elms' refusal stands** and the whole of the Reach's standing charge is on Adrian personally, and the door stays shut. *Actively adverse, and it must not be reversed without the process being dramatised.*
- **The trial licence in its first months.** Two witnesses per opening, one named living person, a fresh inspection before every opening in whatever weather there is, a flood-season review with a date on the page before the day, nine named persons who may stop it and one of whom is named by herself. *Active, and a trial arrangement, not an institution.*
- **The gauge.** One mark, cut on the twenty-seventh day of this flood by a man who did not say why, and a docker of fifty-eight who has been arguing for nine days that a river does not keep one number, and **she was right and was not answered.** *Active and unanswered.*
