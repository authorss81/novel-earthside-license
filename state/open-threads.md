# Open Threads

Status after Batch 0001 (Chapters 1–10). A promise becomes active when the prose pays it and is discharged when the book or the event closes it.

## Central mystery

- **Active, opened Chapter 1.** Why does the System use Adrian's familiar game vocabulary before he has taught it that vocabulary?
- **Not yet surfaced in prose.** What is the First Grammar, and how did the First Sundering split it? The name is not used in Chapters 1–10.
- **Opened in Marrow's mouth, Chapter 7.** What did the earlier Bright Pilgrims cross, what happened to them, and why is Ivenn's copy of Adrian's own sentence coming *through a wall*?
- **Opened Chapter 7.** Ivenn knows more about the interface than he explains, and he is the only person in the batch who is *not* frightened by it.
- **Opened Chapter 8.** Something is standing in the lock join using the old form of the oath, learning an older fifth syllable, spending the bottom field an hour at a time, and it knows Adrian's name.
- **Not yet surfaced.** Can a plural bridge remain stable without a single authority? The Chapter 10 instrument is the first attempt and it is empty.
