# Open Threads

Status after Batch 0003 (Chapters 21–30). A promise becomes active when the prose pays it and is discharged when the book or the event closes it.

## Central mystery

- **Active, opened Chapter 1, advanced Chapter 19, advanced again Chapter 25 and Chapter 28.** In Chapter 25 the chapel stone said twelve words of English in a flat reading voice — *one. A place people already agree on, used already, for another reason* — out of the black line, in a yard, in front of about three hundred and ten people. In Chapter 28 it was established that those twelve words are **the first line of page forty-one of Adrian's paper notebook**, which he wrote at four in the morning on the fourth night of the flood and has shown to nobody. He wrote the four readings down in order of how much they frighten him: something read a file that does not exist; the thing has been inside him since Tuesday; it is somebody else's room; **he wrote it down and has been carrying it and did not know it was the same thing.** He has not chosen. He handed the page to Sera Vail on day 29. *Active, and the fourth reading is the one he cannot rule out and the one he is most afraid of, because it requires no conspiracy and only him.*
- **Advanced Chapters 24, 27 and 29.** The interface offered a way to name a stone (24), a way to establish a return mark in an hour (27), and — for the first time — a way to **close** the debt (29). It has **no memory of any of them**. Across the three batches it has made eleven offers and it has never once registered a refusal of one. **Chapter 22 carried no message at all**; its four clauses are the Reed Reach's term sheet, a document, and a later writer must not record it as a prompt. *Active.*
- **Not yet surfaced in prose.** What is the First Grammar, and how did the First Sundering split it? The name is not used in Chapters 1–30.
- **Opened in Marrow's mouth, Chapter 7; advanced Chapter 12.** What did the earlier Bright Pilgrims cross, and why does Marrow have Adrian's own unpublished sentence? Now three of them, through three different channels, and two of those three are Adrian's own memory and Adrian's own notebook. *Active.*
- **Opened Chapter 7 and unresolved.** Ivenn knows more about the interface than he explains. In Chapter 12 he was reported to have told nine people in a room that a genuine three-hundred-and-ten-year-old customs document is a fraud, and let them believe the whole of it was fake. In Chapter 28 he translated a frightened group's account **word for word, kept the four disagreements in, and added one sentence of his own saying he had not removed them.** He is now openly useful and openly the most frightening version of himself. **No public scene may convict him. He must remain publicly useful to the end of the volume.** *Active, dangerous, unproven.*
- **Opened Chapter 8, advanced Chapter 15, and now Chapter 25.** Something is standing in the lock join using the old form of the oath with a keeper's word in the middle of it. Owen's account stands: **it is not the stone, it is the four words forty men say twice a day under the near gate; the man in the join found the pattern and did not build it. The field is a toll and somebody is being charged.** On day 25 the gate crew said the oath at its ordinary volume and the main breach stepped **nine inches toward the near gate** in the safe direction, and the day's residue opened a scar under a chapel three hundred yards away. *Active, and the mechanism is now proven twice in different directions.*
- **Not yet surfaced.** Can a plural bridge remain stable without a single authority? **The first visible act of the plural principle in the volume is on the page and it is small: on day 31 Sera Vail entered four contradictory accounts of the same room side by side and refused to reconcile them, and on the same day a man of twenty-two entered the first statement of error in the history of the Concord.** *Active, and it is now institutional rather than thematic.*

## Immediate Volume 01 promises

- **The main breach is open and stepped.** Nine inches toward the near gate on day 25, in the safe direction, by a working that was consented to, costed, witnessed and performed by locals. Four and a half inches of the original three inches were bought with a season of a field and a scar in a place. **The only known remedy remains stopping the ordinary work of the lock, which drowns eleven villages.** *Not discharged, and now the remedy has been shown to be worse than the disease at the exact scale of the disease.*
- **The bottom field's lost season is a live clock.** The review was held on **day 30**, five days late, and the debt was **continued and enlarged with no final figure.** A new cost column governs the seat: the yard's labour twice a day with the holder as the nearest, and a new page in the ledger for the eleven. Elsa Skell's number goes *down* and Adrian's goes *up* and the book now says so. *Active, and the debt can no longer be closed by anybody, which is the point and the humiliation.*
- **Nine Elms' refusal is a standing commitment**, given twice, in their own words, and it is now entered **on the same page and at the same level** as the instrument's own refusal. Hessa Ruun has warned in terms that a standing charge on the instrument would turn that refusal into a **non-payment**, and Adrian has been handed the term sheet that would do it. *Active, and about to be tested by somebody using his own concession.*
- **The Concord hearing on a first license was held on day 31 and answered.** **No.** The instrument is continued, unusable, with both refusals entered together. The hearing's real product is a **procedure for being wrong in public.** *Discharged as a question, answered as a refusal, and deliberately non-terminal.*
- **The Crown has answered, twice, and neither letter is polite.** The second one, from Anwen Kroon, requires before the next quarterly sitting **either a registered holder with a Crown-recognised workway and a Crown advocate, or a statement of permanent closure**, and says that a seat with no workway is not a form the Registry can accept and that **a position may be filled by a person who can hold it.** *Active, and it is the first Crown instrument with teeth and it is dated to a quarter.*
- **Ivenn's Wayfinder offer is withdrawn, not refused, in his own hand, in chalk, in daylight, on the keeper's board on day 30** — and in withdrawing it he asked Adrian to write the true reason underneath it in his own hand, and wrote *he will do it* beneath that. **That is a promise with a shape in it, and it is dated to the seventh day of the new month, which is day 32.** *Active and the batch's sharpest hook.*
- **The displaced Anchorstone under the chapel's yard-side landing is still out of place** and the boundary table now explains the whole of it: the boundary was written into the ground **twice**, once as a stone you could lift and once as a joint under a floor you could not, and the liftable half was carried a hundred yards downstream in public at a cost of four hundred people's houses while the other half was left where the water could find it. **There is no "back."** *Active, and reframed again.*
- **The ground under the chapel is open and boarded, and it is no longer a mystery.** It is a record. It is also, since day 25, **scarred**, and it is the reason the volume's problem is now a floor and not a wall. *Active.*
- **Owen Park's eventual death, planned for Chapter 38, must be permanent** and must carry Adrian's responsibility, the Chapter 25 shoulder that was never set, and Owen's own choice. He is alive at the end of this batch. His scalp has been **unclosed since the first day and is thirty days old at the end of day 31**, and it is not resolved. **His right shoulder went on day 25, cannot be set, and is the volume's injury.** Do not give him any other lasting injury before Chapter 38. *Deferred, not open.*
- **The black line in the chapel stone is not repaired in Volume 1.** It went from black to the colour of a wet road during the tear and it still carries, and on day 25 it said twelve words of English out loud in front of three hundred and ten people. *Active.*
- **The black line in Adrian's palm is not repaired in Volume 1** and does not deepen into a black scar in a person; that is Owen's return mark in Chapter 38. It is **thirty days old and has not moved one sixteenth of an inch**, including while a floor was coming apart. *Active.*
- **The keeper's standing labour is live and is no longer a rota**: the words are said twice a day in the open by whoever is nearest, and **as many people as will learn them have learned them**, so that the rule has more than one candidate. Owen no longer does it alone. *Active, and it is the one thing in the volume that is getting better.*
- **Adrian's post is live**: for as long as the ground is open, the ground does not open without him. It has now survived a working at a gallery, an excavation, a tear, a council, a hearing, and a boy walking into a wall, and he was present for every beginning, and he has noticed that the post has quietly become the reason the instrument is operated at all. *Active.*

## Relationship and political threads

- **Adrian and Tamsin: working partnership, no romantic milestone, and the check has become mutual.** She made him learn his own vocabulary publicly in English in Chapter 26 and corrected him in a yard in Chapter 29. He **did not** hand her page forty-one: on day 29 he handed it to Sera Vail, and the notebook says so, and Tamsin has not been given it.
