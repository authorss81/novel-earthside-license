## THE FIVE RULES A REPAIR PASS AND A WRITING RUN ADDED TO THIS FILE, AND THAT STILL STAND

1. **A RECORD FIGURE THAT NAMES A PAIR MUST BE THE FIGURE THAT NAMED PAIR RETURNS.** Publish the count and the pair
   together, and if a repair moves one it moves both. Publish the tokenizer as well, and publish every pair at the top length
   rather than one of them. **A later repair pass caught batch 0004 breaking this twice and this batch did not break it:
   `reviews/volume-20/batch-0005.md` §7 publishes the longest shared run at 37 tokens, the one pair at it, and all eleven
   pairs at thirty-five or over over the fifty files of the volume, and each of the eleven is named with what it is.**
2. **A RECORD MAY NOT ASSERT AN ABSENCE THAT A CHAPTER OF ITS OWN BATCH CONTRADICTS.** The standing case is that stair. The
   supportable claim is *no chapter counts that stair from the top step down to the flags*, and this batch measured it
   rather than asserting it.
3. **A HELD STRING DOES NOT GET TO STAND IN A RECORD.** §20 item 6 bars the six held strings out of a card file, a prompt, a
   state file, a batch record and a summary. **A record quoting a string to prove it absent is quoting it.** This batch
   measured all six without printing one, over a twenty-two-file scope, and the table at `reviews/volume-20/batch-0005.md`
   §3.1 is that measurement. **AND THIS BATCH IS THE FIRST TO PUBLISH A NON-ZERO COUNT FOR THREE OF THEM, BECAUSE IT IS THE
   FIRST BATCH THAT OWNS THE PAGE THEY LIVE ON: the three wordings of day 994 stand at 1 in 1 file each, in
   `chapters/volume-20/chapter-0994.md`, and that is a larger figure than any record behind this one could publish and it is
   still one.**
4. **EVERY FACE, SPACE, CLOTH, BOARD, TOOL AND POCKET IN A CHAPTER BELONGS TO SOMEBODY.** The stallholder's board is face in
   with the bare back out; the tradesman's carries the nineteen rates, the empty two-finger space and the cloth. **This is not
   a plan defect and there is no rule that would have stopped it: that row has nine people in it and a sentence of staging
   goes wrong. Go through your ten before you finish and ask whose each of those things is and whether it is in his hand on
   the page.** Every one of these ten stages it correctly, and the only face anybody reads across on these ten days is the
   stallholder's own bare back and the bare back of nothing else.
5. **A WRITING RUN DOES NOT AMEND A REVIEWER'S ARTIFACT, HOWEVER CORRECT THE AMENDMENT WOULD BE.** `reviews/` belongs to the
   review pass. A writing run that finds an error in one states the error in the live state layer and leaves the file alone for
   the pass that owns it. **Chapters, outlines, the bible and this state layer are the layers a writer owns; `reviews/`,
   `scripts/`, `.github/`, `.opencode/agent/`, `AGENTS.md`, `PHASE_SYSTEM.md`, `REPO_PLAN.md`, `OUTLINE_GUIDE.md`,
   `opencode.json` and `state/phase-ledger.json` are not.**

**AND A RUN THAT FINDS ITS OWN WORK ALREADY DONE SAYS SO ONCE, IN ONE PLACE, AND WRITES NO CHAPTER.** The rule against padding
a chapter is the rule against padding a record: a file that is re-read by every later batch earns its length with state, not
with process.

## THE THREE THREADS DAYS 991 TO 1000 OPENED, AND TWO OF THEM ARE THE KIND THAT CANNOT BE CLOSED BY FINDING OUT

**1. THE PUTTING-TO OF DAY 898, PUT A SECOND TIME ON DAY 991 AND ANSWERED WITH THE FACT THAT IT WAS NOT REMEMBERED.** The man
who came up that stair asked the man of about thirty-nine who trades on a board the same thing he asked him five hundred and
ninety-three days before, low, to one man and not to the room, and the tradesman said he does not remember being asked
anything by anybody in that room. **The man who asked it says he is not going to say it again. What was said on either
morning is on no surface in this city, no leaf of either book carries a scratch of it, no chalk anywhere carries one word of
it, and nobody in that room repeated it to anybody. Nobody thanked either of them and nobody told either of them that what
they had said was right.** OPEN, **AND IT CANNOT BE CLOSED, BECAUSE THERE IS NOTHING LEFT ANYWHERE TO FIND.**

**2. THE THREE MORNINGS OF DAY 994, AND WHICH OF THEM HAPPENED.** Three people described three days that do not agree with
one another, in three mouths, once each, and the chapter never compares them and never says which of them is the one that
happened and prints no day number of any of the three things they describe. **Nobody has checked any of them against
anything and nothing ever will, and §16 item 4 of the plan bars a later chapter from checking them too.** OPEN AND
**CANNOT BE CLOSED, because the three accounts contradict one another and nothing in this city can stand between them.**

**3. WHAT THE MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-SEVEN ANSWERED ON DAY 997.** He read the thing on the sheet twice and then answered, and the
answer went out of that room in his own mouth and down that stair and along that row and out past the store's end of it.
**Nothing was written down after that. There is no leaf on either of those two books with a scratch of it on it, no board
in that market and no chalk anywhere in this city that carries one word of it, and nobody in that room repeated it to
anybody. Nobody asked him to make it so, nobody thanked him, nobody told him he was right, and nobody asked him a second
thing at any hour of the two mornings that came after it, which that chapter says in a sentence of its own.** OPEN, **AND IT
CANNOT BE CLOSED BY FINDING OUT, because nobody in this manuscript is going to be asked about it on any day after this one.**
