## DAYS 961 TO 970 CLOSED NO THREAD, ANSWERED NO STANDING QUESTION, MENDED NOTHING AND PAID NOTHING

Four threads changed their state and two opened, and **a state changing is not a thread being answered.**

## THE TWO THREADS DAYS 961 TO 970 OPENED, AND BOTH ARE THE KIND THAT CANNOT BE CLOSED BY FINDING OUT

**1. THE THING PUT TO THE MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-SEVEN IN THE WHARF YARD ON DAY 961 IS A STANDING THREAD, AND IT IS ANSWERED
AND THAT IS THE WORST OF IT.** A man at that salt wharf put one thing to another man about the trough at the top of that
market step and the heat nobody fetches for it, in a yard with about nine people standing in it, and it was answered in the
ordinary course. **It is not written down. The chapter does not print the words of the putting and does not print one word
of the answer. Nobody thanked either man and nobody told either of them that what they had said was right. It is in nobody's
record and there is no surface in this city that could hold one word of it. OPEN, AND IT CANNOT BE CLOSED, because there is
nothing left anywhere to find.**

**2. THE REFUSAL OF DAY 965 IS A STANDING THREAD AND IT IS THE SECOND OF THE SIX COST DAYS OF THIS VOLUME.** The woman of
about fifty-two said, at her own far wall, in daylight, with the date in chalk on the outside of the door behind her own
shoulder, before anything else that morning, that she is not going to have this written down and has known that since
before she came up those stairs and is going to ask it anyway. She then put one thing to the man of about thirty-nine who
trades on a board about nine feet off. **He answered. Nobody in that room was told what the answer was. The chapter does
not print it. Nothing was written down. She did not say it a second time before the light went or after, and he did not
say his a second time, and nobody in that room asked him for it again.** OPEN. **On no day of this volume does anybody ask
him again, ask her why she refused, or suggest that the refusal could be undone, and a chapter that does any of those
three has broken §6.10.**

## THE THREAD THAT 969 MADE WORSE INSTEAD OF BETTER, AND IT IS THE SAME SHAPE AS 958

**THE ANSWER SHE HAS BEEN CARRYING SINCE DAY 598.** She said out loud on 969 that somebody put a thing to her on the five
hundred and ninety-eighth day of that flood, that she has had the answer to it that whole week, and that she did not know
until that morning that she had it. **She did not say what the answer is. Nobody can check it. The standing question of day
598 stands, and it now stands with a person in this manuscript who says she has the answer to it and cannot produce it.**
The man of about thirty-eight at that salt wharf, who was in that yard on that morning, said he does not remember that morning
at all, and said that is not an answer to what she has said and is not not-an-answer either. **Nobody in that room compared
one of those two mornings with the other of them, and nobody said that she had remembered it right or that she had
remembered it wrong, or that he had remembered right or that he had remembered wrong, and §16 item 3 clause (vi) is what
holds all four of those out of every chapter of this volume.** OPEN, and **no chapter of days 970 to 1000 prints what the
answer was, and day 970 is on disk and does not.**

## THE FOUR STATES THAT MOVED, WHICH ARE THREADS' STATES AND NOT THREADS ANSWERED

The stool is a foot further out from that wall and there is a clean square on the flags where its near leg was. The gap at
the head of the keeper's leaf is closed and the dry cloth has nowhere to go in it. The empty second hole in the outside wall
has taken a day of rain and gone soft about the width of a nail all round its lip, and no nail went into it. A clean arc
about the width of a hand is on that gate's hinge where somebody pushed it once and it came back. **All four are threads
about a thing, and a later chapter stages them and does not undo them.**

## THE THREADS THIS VOLUME MAY NOT SETTLE, AND DAYS 961 TO 970 DID NOT TOUCH ONE OF THEM

**The consent fracture is not mended and the fifth condition is not given, and neither of their names goes in any column of
either book.** The standing questions of days **346, 396, 445, 498, 548, 598, 648, 698, 748, 798 and 848** stand unanswered,
and 598 is on one of these ten days in a new way and is still unanswered. **The question of day 898** stands unanswered and is
asked a second time on 991, which is not this batch. **The five questions the woman of about sixty-nine has asked stand, and
her sixth is 997 and is not this batch, and she is not asked about the lines in the grit at the store's end of that row on
any of these ten days and no chapter of this volume may ask her.** **The mark of day 440, the hand that moved it and the
woman of about fifty-four are untouched, and the woman of about fifty-four is at zero across all ten of these bodies.** The
two shapes in the dust of that sill are undated. The bar is out of its two sockets and goes back on no day. The trough gets
no heat and its ice is not broken. The fence is unmeasured, no post moves, and the new rope on the chair is not lifted. The
satchel and the tin stay shut, and the tin is in none of these ten chapters. The tally-board is not turned over. **The
question of 958 stands unanswerable because nobody in that room can say what was asked and the two women of twenty-nine are
not established as one woman or two, and that is a finding and not a gap to be filled.**

## THE TWO THREADS ABOUT THE WRITING, WHICH ARE ABOUT THE MANUSCRIPT AND NOT ABOUT THE WORLD

**1. THE LENGTH SHORTFALL, OPEN ON PURPOSE.** `PHASE_SYSTEM.md` asks for about 2,200 to 3,200 words and days 961 to 970
measure a mean of **1,215.7**. **Nothing was padded and no finished scene was split to reach a number, and the thread stays
open across batch 0003. A writer who closes it by padding has closed it wrong.**

**2. THE PROSE GOING HOLLOW, WHICH THE FIX PASS MEASURED, NAMED AND COULD NOT REWRITE.** Across three sets measured with
one instrument, negation tokens being *nothing, nobody, no, not, never, cannot, neither* run **one in 51.2 words on Volume
19's last twenty, one in 45.4 on days 951 to 960, and one in 41.2 on days 961 to 970.** Bare *that* runs **2.41 per cent of
words, then 3.31, then 3.94.** Both drift the wrong way across three batches, and a reviewer has named the shape as
chapters that narrate the absence of events rather than staging a scene: **on 961 a whole paragraph went on the fact that
nothing on any surface holds what two men said to each other, and on 965 two paragraphs went on establishing that nobody
will ever learn what the question was.** **Rewriting ten finished chapters to fix that would be restarting the batch, so it
is carried as a measured number into `workspace/volume-20/batch-0003/PROMPT.md` instead, where the rule is that a held
string binds what a chapter may print and does not license a paragraph spent reporting that it did not print it.**

**AND THE THIRD, WHICH THE FIX PASS ADDED, AND IT IS A MANUSCRIPT-WIDE DEBT AND NOT A VOLUME-20 ONE.** **The attribution
punctuation fault this volume was written with is a house fault and it is not confined to Volume 20.** The pattern is a full
stop inside the closing quote of a bolded speech immediately followed by a speech tag. Measured over every chapter file on
disk, with each match classified by whether the text after the marker is a speech tag or the next narration sentence:

- **Volume 20 — 39 sites, which is all 39 of the volume's tagged speeches. All 39 are corrected by this fix pass and none
  remain.**
- **Volume 19 — 21 sites, in chapters 0925, 0931 and 0934 to 0950. Not corrected by this phase.**
- **Volume 07 — 1 site, chapter 0311. Volume 16 — 1 site, chapter 0781. Not corrected by this phase.**
- **23 real sites outstanding outside Volume 20.** Volumes 17 and 18 carry 218 such tags between them and every one of them
  is already correct, so the fault is not universal and those two volumes are the model to point at.
- Two further matches, in `chapter-0301.md` and `chapter-0797.md`, are **not** defects: the period there ends an internal
  sentence of a multi-sentence bolded speech and the sentence after the marker is narration, not a tag.

**A card file or a prompt should carry the correct form forward so that a later volume does not inherit it again, and the 23
outstanding sites want a pass of their own by whoever owns Volumes 07, 16 and 19.**
